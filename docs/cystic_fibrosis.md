# Cystic fibrosis

Cystic fibrosis is an autosomal recessive disease caused by mutation in the CFTR (cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator) gene.

CFTR is basically a chloride channel.

Normally:

* chloride leaves cells into mucus
* water follows chloride
* mucus stays thin and slippery

In cystic fibrosis:

* defective CFTR means chloride cannot move properly
* water does not follow into the mucus layer
* mucus becomes thick, dry, and sticky

So the core idea is:

> bad chloride movement = dehydrated secretions.

---

### Why the findings happen

Lungs:

* thick mucus blocks airways
* bacteria get trapped
* recurrent lung infections happen
* chronic inflammation causes bronchiectasis (permanent airway dilation)

Pancreas:

* thick secretions block pancreatic ducts
* pancreatic enzymes cannot reach the intestine
* fat malabsorption happens
* this causes steatorrhea (fatty, foul-smelling stool) and failure to thrive

Sweat:

* sweat glands cannot reabsorb chloride normally
* sodium follows chloride
* sweat becomes salty

That is why the sweat chloride test is positive.

---

### Classic Step 1 associations

* recurrent pneumonia
* bronchiectasis (permanent airway dilation)
* pancreatic insufficiency
* fat-soluble vitamin deficiency: A, D, E, K
* male infertility from congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens (missing sperm-carrying ducts)
* nasal polyps
* meconium ileus (newborn intestinal obstruction from thick stool)

---

## One-line intuition

Cystic fibrosis makes secretions too thick because broken CFTR channels fail to move chloride and water correctly.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Most common mutation is delta F508, meaning deletion of phenylalanine at position 508 in the CFTR protein.
