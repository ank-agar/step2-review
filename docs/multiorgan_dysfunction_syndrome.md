## Multiorgan dysfunction syndrome

Multiorgan dysfunction syndrome (MODS) means **2 or more organ systems start failing in an acutely ill patient**.

Usually this happens after something severe like:

* sepsis (life-threatening organ dysfunction from a dysregulated immune response to infection)
* shock (poor tissue perfusion)
* major trauma
* severe pancreatitis
* massive burns

---

### Core idea

The body has a huge inflammatory/stress response.

That causes:

* vasodilation (blood vessels widen)
* capillary leak (fluid leaves blood vessels into tissues)
* microthrombi (tiny clots)
* poor tissue perfusion (poor blood flow to organs)
* mitochondrial dysfunction (cells cannot use oxygen well)

So even if oxygen is in the blood, organs may not receive/use it well.

Result:

* lungs fail → acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS; inflammatory fluid-filled lungs)
* kidneys fail → oliguria (low urine output), increased creatinine
* liver fails → jaundice, increased liver enzymes
* brain affected → confusion, altered mental status
* blood system affected → disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC; widespread clotting that uses up clotting factors)
* heart/circulation fails → hypotension (low blood pressure), increased lactate

---

### Why it becomes a vicious cycle

One failing organ makes other organs worse.

Example:

* septic shock causes low blood pressure
* low blood pressure decreases kidney perfusion
* kidney injury causes acid/electrolyte problems
* acid/electrolyte problems stress the heart
* poor heart output worsens perfusion everywhere

So MODS is like the body losing homeostasis (stable internal balance) across multiple systems at once.

---

## One-line intuition

MODS is when severe systemic inflammation + poor perfusion cause multiple organs to stop keeping up.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, connect:

**sepsis → cytokines → endothelial damage/capillary leak → hypotension + microthrombi → tissue hypoxia → lactic acidosis + organ dysfunction.**
