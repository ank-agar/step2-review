### Core idea

Hashimoto thyroiditis is autoimmune destruction of the thyroid gland.

Autoimmune means the immune system attacks your own body.

So in Hashimoto:

* immune cells attack thyroid follicles
* thyroid tissue slowly gets damaged
* thyroid hormone production drops
* patient develops hypothyroidism

Hypothyroidism means low thyroid hormone.

---

### What antibodies show up?

Classic antibodies:

* anti-TPO antibodies
* anti-thyroglobulin antibodies

TPO (thyroid peroxidase) is an enzyme needed to make thyroid hormone.

Thyroglobulin is the protein scaffold used to build thyroid hormone.

Important intuition:

These antibodies are great markers of disease, but the main tissue damage is from T cells.

T cells are immune cells that can directly injure thyroid tissue.

---

### Why does it cause hypothyroidism?

The thyroid normally makes:

* T3 (triiodothyronine)
* T4 (thyroxine)

In Hashimoto, immune attack destroys thyroid follicles, so the gland cannot make enough T3/T4.

Low T3/T4 removes negative feedback on the pituitary gland.

So the pituitary increases TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) to yell at the thyroid:

> "Make more hormone."

That gives the classic lab pattern:

* high TSH
* low free T4

---

### Symptoms

Think low metabolism:

* fatigue
* weight gain
* cold intolerance
* constipation
* dry skin
* bradycardia
* heavy menstrual bleeding

Bradycardia means slow heart rate.

---

### Histology

On biopsy, Hashimoto shows:

* lymphocytic infiltration
* germinal centers
* Hurthle cells

Lymphocytic infiltration means lots of lymphocytes, which are immune cells, entering the thyroid.

Germinal centers are areas where B cells are actively reacting and multiplying.

Hurthle cells are damaged thyroid follicular cells with abundant pink granular cytoplasm.

---

## One-line intuition

Hashimoto is the immune system slowly burning out the thyroid, causing low thyroid hormone and high TSH.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Hashimoto thyroiditis is associated with increased risk of primary thyroid lymphoma.

Primary thyroid lymphoma means lymphoma starting in the thyroid, usually from chronic lymphocytic inflammation.
