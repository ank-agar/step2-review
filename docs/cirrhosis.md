## Cirrhosis

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17884/N01013-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/Gross_pathology_of_alcoholic_liver_cirrhosis.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/Cirrhosis_high_mag.jpg/1280px-Cirrhosis_high_mag.jpg)

Cirrhosis = end-stage chronic liver damage where normal liver tissue gets replaced by:

* fibrosis (scar tissue)
* regenerative nodules (clumps of hepatocytes trying to regrow)

So the liver becomes hard, nodular, and bad at doing normal liver jobs.

---

### Core idea

Think:

> repeated liver injury → scarring → distorted blood flow + poor liver function

Common causes:

* alcohol use disorder
* chronic hepatitis B or C (long-term viral liver infection)
* MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis; fatty liver inflammation from metabolic disease)
* autoimmune or biliary disease

---

### Why the findings happen

The liver normally has soft, organized blood channels.

In cirrhosis, scar tissue blocks and distorts those channels.

That causes portal hypertension (high pressure in the portal venous system, which drains gut blood into the liver).

Portal hypertension causes:

* ascites (fluid in the abdomen)
* esophageal varices (dilated esophageal veins that can bleed)
* splenomegaly (big spleen)

The liver also loses synthetic function (ability to make proteins), so you get:

* low albumin (blood protein that keeps fluid inside vessels) → edema/ascites
* low clotting factors → easy bleeding, increased PT/INR (prothrombin time/international normalized ratio; blood clotting tests)

The liver also loses detox function, so ammonia can build up and cause hepatic encephalopathy (confusion from liver failure toxins affecting the brain).

---

### Compensated vs decompensated

Compensated cirrhosis = scarred liver, but still mostly keeping up.

Decompensated cirrhosis = liver can no longer keep up.

Big decompensation signs:

* ascites
* variceal bleeding
* jaundice (yellow skin/eyes from bilirubin buildup)
* hepatic encephalopathy

---

## One-line intuition

Cirrhosis is a liver that has been injured so many times that healing turned into scarring, and the scarring blocks blood flow and destroys liver function.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cirrhosis increases risk of HCC (hepatocellular carcinoma; primary liver cancer), so patients need screening with liver ultrasound, often with AFP (alpha-fetoprotein; tumor marker), every 6 months.
