# Deep venous thrombosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/62/Blausen_0290_DeepVeinThrombosis.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/21/Deep_vein_thrombosis_of_the_right_leg.jpg)

![Image](https://us-southeast-1.linodeobjects.com/wikem-images/images/Ultrasonography_of_deep_vein_thrombosis_of_the_femoral_vein.jpg)

Deep venous thrombosis, or **DVT (deep venous thrombosis)**, is a **blood clot inside a deep vein**, usually in the leg.

Deep veins are the big veins buried under muscle, not the superficial veins right under the skin.

---

### Core idea

A clot forms in a deep leg vein and blocks venous return, meaning blood has trouble flowing back to the heart.

So the affected leg can become:

* swollen
* painful
* warm
* red
* tender

Often it is **unilateral (one-sided)**, which is a big clue.

---

### Why it happens

Think **Virchow triad**:

* **Stasis (slow blood flow):** immobility, surgery, long flights, hospitalization
* **Endothelial injury (vessel wall injury):** trauma, surgery, inflammation
* **Hypercoagulability (increased tendency to clot):** cancer, pregnancy, estrogen therapy, inherited thrombophilias

Intuition:

Blood that sits still is more likely to clot, like sludge forming when flow slows down.

---

### Why DVT is dangerous

The scary complication is **PE (pulmonary embolism)**.

That means:

* clot forms in deep leg vein
* piece breaks off
* travels through venous blood to the right heart
* lodges in pulmonary arteries (lung arteries)

This can cause sudden:

* dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* pleuritic chest pain (chest pain worse with breathing)
* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* hemoptysis (coughing blood)

---

### Diagnosis

Best initial test is usually **compression ultrasound**.

Normal vein:

* compresses when pressed by ultrasound probe

DVT vein:

* does **not** compress because a solid clot is inside

---

### Treatment idea

Treat with **anticoagulation (blood-thinning medication)**.

Important: anticoagulants do not directly dissolve the clot. They mainly stop the clot from growing while the body breaks it down.

---

## One-line intuition

DVT is a deep leg vein clot caused by slow flow/injury/hypercoagulability, and the big danger is that it can embolize to the lungs.

---

## Pearl 🔥

DVT and PE are both forms of **VTE (venous thromboembolism)**: DVT is the clot sitting in the vein, PE is the clot after it travels to the lungs.
