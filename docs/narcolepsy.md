## Narcolepsy

![Image](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/36072/xml-images/fendo-04-00018-g001.webp)

![Image](https://pub.mdpi-res.com/children/children-09-00974/article_deploy/html/images/children-09-00974-g001.png?1656502610=)

![Image](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/215003/xml-images/fneur-07-00130-g001.webp)

Narcolepsy is a chronic sleep-wake disorder where the brain cannot keep wakefulness and REM sleep (rapid eye movement sleep) properly separated.

Core problem:

* loss of orexin/hypocretin neurons in the lateral hypothalamus
* orexin/hypocretin normally stabilizes wakefulness
* without it, REM sleep features can suddenly intrude into wakefulness

Think:

> narcolepsy = unstable sleep-wake switch.

---

### Classic symptoms

* Excessive daytime sleepiness
* Cataplexy
* Sleep paralysis
* Hypnagogic hallucinations
* Fragmented nighttime sleep

Cataplexy means sudden loss of muscle tone while awake, usually triggered by emotion, especially laughter.

Why?

During REM sleep (rapid eye movement sleep), your body normally has muscle atonia (temporary muscle paralysis) so you do not act out dreams.

In narcolepsy, REM physiology leaks into wakefulness:

* REM sleepiness leaks in -> sudden sleep attacks
* REM atonia leaks in -> cataplexy
* REM paralysis leaks in while falling asleep/waking up -> sleep paralysis
* REM dreaming leaks in while falling asleep -> hypnagogic hallucinations

---

### Type 1 vs type 2

Narcolepsy type 1:

* cataplexy is present
* low CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) hypocretin/orexin

Narcolepsy type 2:

* no cataplexy
* hypocretin/orexin is usually normal or not clearly low

---

### Diagnosis idea

Diagnosis often uses:

* PSG (polysomnography), an overnight sleep study
* MSLT (multiple sleep latency test), a daytime nap test

Classic MSLT (multiple sleep latency test) finding:

* short sleep latency, meaning the patient falls asleep quickly
* SOREMPs (sleep-onset rapid eye movement periods), meaning REM sleep starts too early

Normal person:

* sleep -> NREM sleep (non-rapid eye movement sleep) first
* REM sleep usually later

Narcolepsy:

* sleep -> REM can happen almost immediately

---

## One-line intuition

The brain loses orexin/hypocretin, so the boundary between awake and REM sleep becomes leaky.

---

## HY pearl

Narcolepsy type 1 is strongly associated with HLA-DQB1*06:02 (human leukocyte antigen DQB1*06:02), supporting an autoimmune mechanism destroying orexin/hypocretin neurons.
