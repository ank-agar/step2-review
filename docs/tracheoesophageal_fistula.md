# Tracheoesophageal fistula

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3f/Tracheoesophageal_Fistula_Types.svg/1920px-Tracheoesophageal_Fistula_Types.svg.png?utm_campaign=index&utm_content=thumbnail&utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org)

![Image](https://modules.oc4d.org/en-medline_plus/ency/images/ency/fullsize/10315.jpg)

Tracheoesophageal fistula, or **TEF (tracheoesophageal fistula)**, is an abnormal connection between:

* trachea = windpipe/air tube
* esophagus = food tube

So food, saliva, or stomach contents can accidentally enter the airway.

---

### Core idea

The trachea and esophagus are supposed to separate during embryologic development.

In TEF, they do not separate correctly, so there is a "wrong tunnel" between breathing tube and food tube.

---

### Why symptoms happen

* **Coughing/choking with feeds:** milk enters the trachea instead of staying in the esophagus
* **Cyanosis (blue discoloration from low oxygen):** aspiration blocks/irritates the airway
* **Excess saliva/drooling:** often because TEF is associated with EA (esophageal atresia), meaning the esophagus ends blindly and cannot pass contents normally
* **Abdominal distension:** air from the trachea can pass through the fistula into the stomach/intestines

---

### Most common anatomy

Most common type:

* proximal esophagus ends in a blind pouch
* distal esophagus connects to the trachea

That is why a newborn can have:

* feeding tube coiled in the upper esophageal pouch
* gas in the stomach/intestines on X-ray

Gas in the abdomen means air is getting from trachea into distal esophagus, then into stomach.

---

### Important association

TEF/EA is associated with **VACTERL (vertebral defects, anal atresia, cardiac defects, tracheoesophageal fistula, renal anomalies, limb abnormalities)**.

So if you see TEF on Step 1, think:

* check heart
* check kidneys
* check limbs/spine/anus

---

## One-line intuition

TEF is a bad shortcut between the air tube and food tube, so feeds go into the lungs and air can go into the stomach.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In a newborn with choking/coughing during feeds + excessive drooling, suspect **TEF with EA (tracheoesophageal fistula with esophageal atresia)**, especially if an NG tube (nasogastric tube) cannot pass into the stomach.
