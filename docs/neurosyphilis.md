## Neurosyphilis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b2/Spinal_cord_tracts_-_English.svg/960px-Spinal_cord_tracts_-_English.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/Tabes_Dorsalis.jpg/960px-Tabes_Dorsalis.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/Treponema_pallidum.jpg)

Neurosyphilis means syphilis infection involving the CNS (central nervous system).

Cause:

* Treponema pallidum, a spirochete (spiral-shaped bacterium)

Core idea:

* untreated syphilis can eventually invade the brain, meninges (brain/spinal cord coverings), or spinal cord
* this causes neurologic symptoms instead of just skin/genital findings

---

### Major Step 1 forms

Early neurosyphilis:

* meningitis (inflammation of meninges)
* cranial nerve problems
* meningovascular syphilis = inflamed CNS blood vessels → stroke-like symptoms

Late neurosyphilis:

* tabes dorsalis
* general paresis

---

### Tabes dorsalis

Tabes dorsalis is degeneration of the dorsal columns (posterior spinal cord tracts that carry vibration and proprioception).

So patients lose:

* vibration sense
* proprioception (sense of body position)
* reflexes

Why the gait gets weird:

* if you cannot feel where your feet are, walking becomes unsteady
* this causes sensory ataxia (poor coordination from loss of sensory input)

Classic symptoms:

* lightning-like leg pains
* wide-based gait
* positive Romberg sign (worse balance when eyes are closed)

---

### General paresis

General paresis is brain involvement causing:

* dementia
* personality changes
* psychiatric symptoms

Basically:

> syphilis damages the brain itself, so cognition and behavior decline.

---

### Classic pupil finding

Argyll Robertson pupil:

* accommodates but does not react

Meaning:

* constricts when focusing on a near object
* does not constrict well to light

One-line memory:

> Argyll Robertson = accommodates but does not react.

---

## One-line intuition

Neurosyphilis is untreated syphilis getting into the nervous system: dorsal columns → ataxia/lightning pains, brain → dementia/personality changes, vessels → stroke.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Tabes dorsalis hits the dorsal columns, so think loss of vibration/proprioception + positive Romberg sign + lightning pains.
