## Berylliosis

Berylliosis is a lung disease caused by inhaling **beryllium** dust/fumes.

Beryllium is a lightweight metal used in things like:

* aerospace
* electronics
* ceramics
* nuclear/defense industries
* metal machining

---

### Core idea

The lung sees beryllium as a foreign trigger, and certain immune cells become sensitized to it.

Then the immune system keeps reacting and forms:

* **noncaseating granulomas** = tight immune-cell clusters without cheesy necrosis
* **interstitial fibrosis** = scarring in the lung tissue between air spaces

So it looks a lot like **sarcoidosis**.

---

### Why it causes symptoms

The granulomas and fibrosis make the lung stiff.

Stiff lung = harder to expand = **restrictive lung disease**.

That causes:

* dyspnea = shortness of breath
* dry cough
* fatigue
* decreased diffusion capacity = oxygen has a harder time crossing from alveoli into blood

If severe/chronic, it can lead to:

* pulmonary hypertension = high pressure in lung blood vessels
* cor pulmonale = right-sided heart failure from lung disease

---

### Why it mimics sarcoidosis

Both berylliosis and sarcoidosis form **noncaseating granulomas**.

The difference is the trigger:

* sarcoidosis = unknown cause
* berylliosis = beryllium exposure

So occupational history is the big clue.

---

### Diagnosis

The key test is **BeLPT** = beryllium lymphocyte proliferation test.

Meaning:

* take lymphocytes = immune white blood cells
* expose them to beryllium
* if they proliferate strongly, the person is sensitized to beryllium

That supports berylliosis.

---

## One-line intuition

Berylliosis is basically **sarcoid-like granulomas in the lung, but caused by workplace beryllium exposure**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 clue: if a question sounds like sarcoidosis but mentions **aerospace/electronics/ceramics/beryllium exposure**, think **berylliosis** and confirm with **BeLPT**.
