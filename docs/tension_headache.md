# Tension headache

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19247.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3f/Pain_distribution_primary_headache_types.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/08/Large_headache_diagram.png)

A tension headache is the classic **“band-like” headache**.

It feels like:

* dull pressure
* tight squeezing
* both sides of the head
* forehead, temples, scalp, or back of neck

Think:

> a tight headband squeezing around the skull.

---

### Core idea

Tension headache is usually from increased sensitivity/pain input from **pericranial muscles** (muscles around the skull), especially in the:

* scalp
* neck
* shoulders
* jaw region

So the pain is usually **muscle/soft tissue-type pain**, not a brain lesion.

---

### Why it happens

The brain itself does not feel pain well.

Pain comes from pain-sensitive structures around it, like:

* muscles
* fascia (connective tissue covering muscles)
* blood vessels
* meninges (brain coverings)

In tension headache, tight or sensitized head/neck muscles send pain signals through trigeminal/cervical pathways.

That is why stress, posture, jaw clenching, and neck tension can trigger it.

---

### How to distinguish it from migraine

Tension headache:

* bilateral = both sides
* pressing/tight quality
* mild to moderate pain
* not worse with routine activity
* usually no nausea/vomiting
* no major photophobia/phonophobia

Photophobia = light sensitivity.  
Phonophobia = sound sensitivity.

Migraine is more likely:

* unilateral = one side
* throbbing/pulsatile
* worse with activity
* nausea/vomiting
* light or sound sensitivity

---

## One-line intuition

A tension headache is a **muscle-tension/sensitization headache** that feels like a dull, tight band around both sides of the head.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 distinction:

* **Tension headache:** bilateral, band-like, no nausea/vomiting
* **Migraine:** unilateral, throbbing, nausea/vomiting, photophobia/phonophobia
* **Cluster headache:** unilateral orbital pain + lacrimation/rhinorrhea

Lacrimation = tearing.  
Rhinorrhea = runny nose.
