### Core idea

Bronchiectasis is permanent dilation of the bronchi (large airways in the lungs).

Think:

> damaged floppy airways get widened, mucus pools there, bacteria grow, and infections keep coming back.

---

### Why the airways dilate

Normally, bronchi have elastic support that helps them stay open and clear mucus.

In bronchiectasis:

* chronic infection/inflammation damages the bronchial wall
* elastin/smooth muscle support gets destroyed
* the airway becomes abnormally widened
* mucus gets trapped because the airway cannot clear secretions well

Then trapped mucus causes more infection and inflammation.

So it becomes a vicious cycle:

**infection -> inflammation -> airway damage -> mucus stasis -> more infection**

---

### Classic symptoms

* chronic productive cough
* lots of foul-smelling sputum (mucus/phlegm coughed up from the lungs)
* recurrent lung infections
* dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* hemoptysis (coughing up blood)
* possible clubbing (bulbous enlargement of fingertips from chronic low oxygen/inflammation)

---

### Common causes

Anything that causes chronic airway infection, obstruction, or impaired mucus clearance:

* cystic fibrosis, aka CF (autosomal recessive chloride channel disease causing thick mucus)
* primary ciliary dyskinesia (defective cilia, so mucus cannot be cleared)
* recurrent pneumonia
* tuberculosis, aka TB (mycobacterial lung infection)
* allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis, aka ABPA (hypersensitivity reaction to Aspergillus in asthmatics)
* airway obstruction from tumor or foreign body

---

### Imaging idea

On CT (computed tomography), bronchi are abnormally wide.

Classic sign:

* signet ring sign = dilated bronchus is bigger than the nearby pulmonary artery

Why this matters:

normally the bronchus and artery travel together and are similar size, but in bronchiectasis the bronchus becomes too wide.

---

## One-line intuition

Bronchiectasis = permanently stretched-out bronchi that trap mucus, causing recurrent infections.

---

## Pearl

Kartagener syndrome = primary ciliary dyskinesia + chronic sinusitis + bronchiectasis + situs inversus (organs mirrored left-right).
