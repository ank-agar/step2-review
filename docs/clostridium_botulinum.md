# Clostridium botulinum

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/PHIL_Images/09132002/00001/PHIL_2107_lores.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ca/Clostridium_botulinum_zoom.jpg)

### Core idea

Clostridium botulinum is a gram-positive, spore-forming, anaerobic rod.

* gram-positive = has thick peptidoglycan cell wall
* spore-forming = can make tough dormant survival forms
* anaerobic = grows best without oxygen
* rod = bacillus-shaped bacterium

It produces botulinum toxin, which blocks acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction.

* acetylcholine = neurotransmitter used to tell muscle to contract
* neuromuscular junction = connection between a motor neuron and skeletal muscle

So the muscle does not get the "contract" signal.

Result:

* flaccid paralysis

Flaccid means weak and limp.

---

### Why it causes weakness

Normally:

* nerve terminal releases acetylcholine
* acetylcholine binds muscle
* muscle contracts

Botulinum toxin blocks SNARE proteins.

SNARE proteins = proteins that help neurotransmitter vesicles fuse with the nerve terminal membrane.

If vesicles cannot fuse:

* acetylcholine cannot be released
* skeletal muscle cannot contract
* paralysis becomes flaccid

This is the opposite of Clostridium tetani.

* C. tetani blocks inhibitory neurotransmitters, causing spastic paralysis
* C. botulinum blocks acetylcholine release, causing flaccid paralysis

---

### Classic presentations

Foodborne botulism:

* from preformed toxin in improperly canned foods
* causes descending weakness

Infant botulism:

* spores are ingested, classically from honey
* bacteria grow in the gut and produce toxin
* can cause constipation, poor feeding, weak cry, and floppy baby

Wound botulism:

* spores enter a wound
* toxin is produced in the wound

---

### Key symptoms

Think:

* bilateral cranial nerve palsies
* blurry vision/diplopia
* dysphagia
* dry mouth
* descending symmetric weakness
* respiratory failure in severe cases

Dysphagia = trouble swallowing.

Diplopia = double vision.

---

## One-line intuition

Clostridium botulinum makes a toxin that prevents acetylcholine release, so muscles lose the signal to contract and become weak/floppy.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Botulinum toxin causes descending flaccid paralysis, while tetanus toxin causes spastic paralysis by blocking release of GABA and glycine.

GABA = gamma-aminobutyric acid, an inhibitory neurotransmitter.

Glycine = inhibitory neurotransmitter especially important in the spinal cord.
