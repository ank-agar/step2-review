# Lactation problems

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ee/Breast_anatomy_drawing.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/31/Breastfeeding_-_Incorrect_vs_Correct_Latch-On.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/24468.jpg)

Lactation problems = problems with making milk, releasing milk, or getting milk from breast to baby.

Main categories:

* low milk supply
* poor latch
* nipple pain/cracking
* engorgement
* plugged duct
* mastitis

---

### Core idea

Breastfeeding is basically:

* prolactin = makes milk
* oxytocin = ejects milk
* baby latch/suck = removes milk

So problems happen when either:

* milk is not made enough
* milk is not removed well
* milk sits in the breast and causes inflammation/infection

---

### Why common problems happen

**Poor latch** = baby is not taking enough areola (pigmented skin around nipple) into the mouth.

Why it matters:

* shallow latch pulls mainly on nipple
* nipple gets compressed/traumatized
* milk transfer is worse
* breast does not empty well

That can lead to nipple cracks, low supply, engorgement, and mastitis.

---

**Engorgement** = breast is overly full with milk and fluid.

Why it hurts:

* pressure builds up inside breast tissue
* ducts get compressed
* milk removal gets harder

So fullness itself can worsen milk flow.

---

**Plugged duct** = localized painful lump from poor milk drainage/inflammation in a duct area.

Classic:

* tender breast lump
* no fever
* usually one localized area

---

**Mastitis** = inflammation of breast tissue, sometimes with bacterial infection.

Classic:

* breast pain
* redness/warmth
* swelling
* fever or flu-like symptoms

Why it happens:

* milk stasis (milk sitting still) causes inflammation
* cracked nipple can let bacteria enter
* bacteria can then multiply in trapped milk

---

### Low milk supply

Low supply often means milk removal is not happening enough.

The breast works by supply-demand:

* more emptying → more milk production
* less emptying → less milk production

So poor latch, infrequent feeding, sleepy baby, or ineffective pumping can all lower supply.

---

## One-line intuition

Most lactation problems come from bad milk transfer: if milk does not leave well, supply drops and the breast gets inflamed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Prolactin makes milk, but oxytocin causes milk letdown (milk ejection). So stress/pain can impair letdown because oxytocin release is sensitive to sympathetic tone.
