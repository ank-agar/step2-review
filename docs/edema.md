# Edema

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK53443/bin/fig1.1.gif)

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/pitting-edema.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Combinpedal.jpg)

Edema = excess fluid trapped in the interstitial space (space between cells/tissues).

So the basic idea is:

> too much fluid leaves blood vessels, or not enough fluid gets removed.

---

### Why edema happens

Fluid movement is controlled by Starling forces (pressures that push or pull fluid across capillary walls):

* hydrostatic pressure = pushes fluid out of blood vessels
* oncotic pressure = protein-pulling pressure that pulls fluid back into blood vessels
* lymphatic drainage = cleanup system that returns extra interstitial fluid to circulation

Edema happens when this balance is disrupted.

---

### Big causes

* Increased hydrostatic pressure: fluid gets pushed out  
  Example: CHF (congestive heart failure), venous obstruction

* Decreased oncotic pressure: not enough protein pulling fluid back in  
  Example: nephrotic syndrome (kidney protein loss), cirrhosis (liver scarring causing low albumin)

* Increased vascular permeability: leaky capillaries  
  Example: inflammation, burns, sepsis (life-threatening systemic inflammation from infection)

* Lymphatic obstruction: fluid cannot drain  
  Example: tumor, surgery, filariasis (parasite blocking lymphatics)

---

### Pitting vs non-pitting

Pitting edema = you press the swollen area and it leaves a dent.

Why?

The extra fluid is mostly free fluid in the tissue, so pressure can temporarily displace it.

Non-pitting edema = pressing does not leave a dent.

Classic causes:

* lymphedema (lymphatic fluid buildup)
* myxedema (skin/tissue swelling from hypothyroidism due to glycosaminoglycan deposition)

---

### One-line intuition

Edema is tissue swelling because the capillary/lymph system is losing the tug-of-war that normally keeps fluid inside vessels or drains it away.

---

### HY pearl

Low albumin causes edema because albumin creates plasma oncotic pressure, which normally pulls water back into blood vessels.
