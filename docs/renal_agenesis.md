## Renal agenesis

Renal agenesis means the kidney never forms.

Break it down:

* renal = kidney
* agenesis = failure of an organ to develop

So renal agenesis = congenital absence of one or both kidneys.

---

### Types

* **Unilateral renal agenesis** = one kidney is absent
* **Bilateral renal agenesis** = both kidneys are absent

Unilateral is often compatible with life because the other kidney can enlarge and compensate.

Bilateral is usually fatal because there is no kidney function and severe lack of amniotic fluid.

---

### Why low amniotic fluid happens

In the fetus, urine is a major source of amniotic fluid.

So:

* no kidneys -> no fetal urine
* no fetal urine -> oligohydramnios (low amniotic fluid)
* low amniotic fluid -> lungs cannot expand/develop normally
* poor lung development -> pulmonary hypoplasia (underdeveloped lungs)

That is why bilateral renal agenesis causes severe respiratory failure after birth.

---

### Classic associated finding

Bilateral renal agenesis causes Potter sequence.

Sequence means one initial problem causes a chain of downstream defects.

In Potter sequence:

* renal agenesis causes oligohydramnios
* oligohydramnios compresses the fetus
* compression causes limb deformities and flattened facial features
* lack of fluid also causes pulmonary hypoplasia

---

## One-line intuition

Renal agenesis is when the kidney-building process fails, and if both kidneys are missing, the fetus cannot make urine-based amniotic fluid, so the lungs fail to develop.

---
