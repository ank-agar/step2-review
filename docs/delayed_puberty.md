# Delayed puberty

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg/960px-Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.wikidoc.org/images/2/26/Delayed-bone-age.jpg)

Delayed puberty means puberty starts later than expected.

Classic cutoffs:

* girls: no breast development by age 13
* boys: no testicular enlargement by age 14

Core idea:

Puberty needs the HPG axis (hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis) to turn on.

Flow:

* hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* pituitary releases LH (luteinizing hormone) and FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* gonads make sex hormones:
  * testes make testosterone
  * ovaries make estrogen

Those sex hormones cause secondary sexual characteristics.

So delayed puberty is basically:

> the puberty hormone pathway is late, under-stimulated, or the gonads cannot respond.

---

### Main categories

#### 1. Constitutional delay

This is the most common cause.

It means the kid is a "late bloomer."

They usually have:

* short stature for age
* delayed bone age
* family history of delayed puberty
* otherwise normal health

Why bone age is delayed:

Sex hormones help mature growth plates. If puberty starts late, the skeleton also matures late, so bone age lags behind chronological age.

---

#### 2. Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism

Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism means:

* low gonadotropins = low LH (luteinizing hormone) and low FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* low sex hormones

So the problem is upstream:

* hypothalamus
* pituitary

Intuition:

The brain/pituitary is not telling the gonads to start puberty.

Causes include:

* chronic illness
* malnutrition
* excessive exercise
* pituitary/hypothalamic disease
* Kallmann syndrome

Kallmann syndrome = GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone) deficiency plus anosmia (loss of smell).

---

#### 3. Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism

Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism means:

* high gonadotropins = high LH (luteinizing hormone) and high FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* low sex hormones

So the problem is in the gonads:

* testes
* ovaries

Intuition:

The pituitary is yelling at the gonads, but the gonads are not responding.

Examples:

* Turner syndrome in girls
* Klinefelter syndrome in boys
* primary ovarian or testicular failure

---

### One-line intuition

Delayed puberty = puberty has not started on time because the HPG axis is either late to activate, not sending enough signal, or the gonads cannot respond to the signal.

---

## Pearl

Low LH (luteinizing hormone) and low FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) = central problem.

High LH and high FSH = gonadal failure.

Think:

* hypo-gonadotropic = brain/pituitary problem
* hyper-gonadotropic = gonad problem
