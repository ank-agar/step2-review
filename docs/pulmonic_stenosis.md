# Pulmonic stenosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/Pulmonary_valve_stenosis.svg/960px-Pulmonary_valve_stenosis.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.goredforwomen.org/-/media/Images/Health-Topics/Congenital-Heart-Defects/50_1683_48_PS.jpg?sc_lang=en)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/8221/3327016/e819787f5336/APC-5-47-g001.jpg)

Pulmonic stenosis means narrowing of the pulmonic valve or outflow tract.

Pulmonic valve = valve between the right ventricle and pulmonary artery.

So blood has trouble going:

* right ventricle
* through pulmonic valve
* into pulmonary artery
* to lungs

---

### Core idea

The right ventricle has to squeeze harder to push blood through a narrowed exit.

This causes:

* increased right ventricular pressure
* right ventricular hypertrophy (thickening of the right ventricle muscle)
* systolic murmur

Systolic = during ventricular contraction.

---

### Why the murmur happens

The narrowed valve makes blood shoot through a small opening.

Fast, turbulent blood flow = murmur.

Classic murmur:

* systolic ejection murmur
* best heard at the left upper sternal border

Left upper sternal border = upper left edge of the sternum/chest bone.

---

### Why symptoms happen

Mild cases are often asymptomatic (no symptoms).

Severe cases can cause:

* dyspnea (shortness of breath) because less blood reaches lungs efficiently
* fatigue because cardiac output (blood pumped to the body per minute) can fall
* syncope (fainting) with exertion because the heart cannot increase forward flow enough
* cyanosis (bluish skin) if severe and associated with a shunt

---

### Associations

Pulmonic stenosis is usually congenital (present from birth).

Important associations:

* Noonan syndrome
* Tetralogy of Fallot
* congenital rubella syndrome

Tetralogy of Fallot = ventricular septal defect, overriding aorta, right ventricular hypertrophy, and pulmonic stenosis.

Ventricular septal defect = hole between the ventricles.

---

## One-line intuition

Pulmonic stenosis is like a blocked exit door for the right ventricle, so the right ventricle gets muscular from pushing against resistance.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Right-sided murmurs usually get louder with inspiration because breathing in increases venous return (blood returning to the heart) to the right side of the heart.
