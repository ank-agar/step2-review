Bacillus anthracis is a gram-positive rod-shaped bacterium that causes anthrax.

Break it down:

* Bacillus = rod-shaped bacterium
* anthracis = anthrax-causing species

Classic microbiology description:

* large gram-positive rods
* spore-forming
* nonmotile
* forms chains that look like boxcars
* has a capsule

The big idea:

Bacillus anthracis is dangerous because it makes:

* spores
* capsule
* toxins

---

### Why the findings happen

**Spores** are dormant survival forms.

So the organism can survive in soil or animal products for a long time, then enter the body through:

* skin cuts
* inhalation
* ingestion

**Capsule** prevents phagocytosis, meaning immune cells have a harder time eating the bacteria.

Phagocytosis = immune cell engulfing/eating a microbe.

**Toxins** cause tissue damage.

The main toxin components are:

* protective antigen
* edema factor
* lethal factor

Protective antigen helps the toxins enter cells.

Edema factor increases cAMP (cyclic adenosine monophosphate), which causes major edema/swelling.

Lethal factor damages cells and contributes to necrosis (cell/tissue death).

---

### Clinical forms

Cutaneous anthrax:

* painless black eschar
* eschar = dead black scab-like tissue
* lots of surrounding edema

Inhalational anthrax:

* spores inhaled into lungs
* macrophages carry them to mediastinal lymph nodes
* can cause hemorrhagic mediastinitis

Mediastinitis = inflammation of the mediastinum, the central chest space between the lungs.

---

## One-line intuition

Bacillus anthracis is a spore-forming gram-positive rod whose capsule blocks immune killing and whose toxins cause edema + necrosis.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 clue: Bacillus anthracis is **nonmotile**, unlike many other Bacillus species, and classically causes a **painless black eschar** in cutaneous anthrax.
