### Core idea

Prematurity means birth before 37 weeks gestation.

The baby is born before multiple organ systems are fully mature, especially:

* lungs
* brain blood vessels
* retina
* gut
* immune system
* temperature/glucose control systems

So the main problem is not one single defect.

It is generalized physiologic immaturity.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Respiratory distress syndrome (RDS = neonatal respiratory distress syndrome):** immature type II pneumocytes make low surfactant, so alveoli collapse
* **Intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH = bleeding into brain ventricles):** fragile germinal matrix vessels rupture easily
* **Necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC = intestinal ischemia/necrosis):** immature gut barrier + poor blood flow regulation
* **Retinopathy of prematurity (ROP = abnormal retinal blood vessel growth):** abnormal oxygen exposure disrupts normal retinal vascular development
* **Hypothermia:** thin skin + low fat stores + high surface area
* **Hypoglycemia:** low glycogen stores and immature glucose control

---

### Lung intuition

Surfactant lowers alveolar surface tension.

Without enough surfactant:

* alveoli collapse
* lung compliance decreases
* baby has to work hard to breathe
* CXR (chest x-ray) can show ground-glass opacities and air bronchograms

So prematurity → low surfactant → collapsed stiff lungs.

---

### Important management association

If preterm delivery is likely, give maternal corticosteroids.

Examples:

* betamethasone
* dexamethasone

These accelerate fetal lung maturation and increase surfactant production.

---

## One-line intuition

Prematurity = baby born before organ systems are ready, so the classic complications come from immature lungs, fragile brain vessels, immature retina, and immature gut.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Premature infant with respiratory distress shortly after birth + ground-glass CXR = neonatal RDS from surfactant deficiency.
