# Otitis media

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19324.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549830/bin/Otitis_media_entdifferenziert2.jpg)

### Core idea

Otitis media means inflammation/infection of the **middle ear**.

Break the word apart:

* otitis = ear inflammation
* media = middle

So:

> inflammation of the middle ear space, usually behind the tympanic membrane (eardrum).

---

### Why it happens

Usually starts after a URI (upper respiratory infection).

The URI causes swelling around the Eustachian tube (tube connecting the middle ear to the nasopharynx, which is the back of the nose/throat).

If the Eustachian tube gets blocked:

* middle ear cannot drain
* fluid builds up
* bacteria/viruses grow in the trapped fluid
* pressure increases behind the eardrum

That pressure is why the tympanic membrane (eardrum) becomes:

* red
* bulging
* painful
* less mobile

---

### Classic bugs

Common causes:

* Streptococcus pneumoniae
* nontypeable Haemophilus influenzae
* Moraxella catarrhalis

---

### Why children get it more

Kids have Eustachian tubes that are:

* shorter
* more horizontal
* easier to block

So infections from the nasopharynx (back of nose/throat) can reach the middle ear more easily.

---

### Symptoms

Can cause:

* ear pain
* fever
* hearing loss
* irritability in infants
* tugging at the ear

Hearing loss happens because fluid blocks normal sound conduction through the ossicles (malleus, incus, and stapes: the tiny middle-ear bones).

---

## One-line intuition

Otitis media is basically a clogged, infected middle-ear space that fills with fluid and pushes the eardrum outward.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Bulging tympanic membrane is more specific for acute otitis media than just redness, because crying or fever alone can make the eardrum look red.
