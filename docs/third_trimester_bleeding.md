# Third-trimester bleeding

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ca/Placenta_previa.svg/1280px-Placenta_previa.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/77/Blausen_0737_PlacentalAbruption.png)

![Image](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/mmanual-ssq1ci05/media/home/images/g/y/n/gyn_vasa_previa_he.gif?sc_lang=en-us)

Third-trimester bleeding means vaginal bleeding after about 28 weeks of pregnancy.

Big idea:

* late pregnancy bleeding is placental/uterine until proven otherwise
* the main dangerous causes are placenta previa, placental abruption, vasa previa, and uterine rupture

---

### Most important causes

### Placenta previa

Placenta previa = placenta covers or is near the cervical opening.

Classic clue:

* painless, bright red bleeding
* soft, nontender uterus

Why bleeding happens:

As the lower uterus and cervix stretch near delivery, the placenta gets pulled away from its attachment site, so maternal blood vessels tear.

Do **not** do a digital vaginal exam until placenta previa is ruled out by ultrasound, because fingers can disrupt the placenta and trigger major bleeding.

---

### Placental abruption

Placental abruption = placenta separates from the uterine wall before delivery.

Classic clue:

* painful vaginal bleeding
* abdominal pain
* tender, firm uterus
* fetal distress

Why bleeding hurts:

Blood collects behind the placenta and irritates the uterus, making it contract and become tense.

Bleeding can be hidden, so the amount of visible blood may underestimate how sick the patient is.

---

### Vasa previa

Vasa previa = fetal blood vessels run across the cervical opening, unsupported by the umbilical cord or placenta.

Classic clue:

* painless bleeding right after rupture of membranes
* fetal distress very quickly

Why it is so dangerous:

The bleeding is fetal blood, not maternal blood. The fetus has a tiny blood volume, so even a small bleed can be catastrophic.

---

### Uterine rupture

Uterine rupture = tearing of the uterine wall, usually in someone with a prior C-section (cesarean section) scar.

Classic clue:

* sudden severe abdominal pain
* fetal distress
* loss of fetal station
* abnormal uterine shape or contractions

Intuition:

The uterus is the container. If it tears, the fetus and placenta can lose normal support and blood flow suddenly.

---

## One-line intuition

Third-trimester bleeding is scary because it often means the placenta, fetal vessels, or uterus is being disrupted near the birth canal.

---

## Quick distinction

* Painless bright red bleeding = think placenta previa
* Painful bleeding with tender uterus = think placental abruption
* Bleeding after membrane rupture + fetal distress = think vasa previa
* Prior C-section scar + sudden pain/fetal crash = think uterine rupture

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1/2 pearl: placenta previa bleeding is usually maternal blood, but vasa previa bleeding is fetal blood.
