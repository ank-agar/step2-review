# Pelvic inflammatory disease

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Blausen_0732_PID-Sites.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/Blausen_0719_PelvicInflammatoryDisease.png)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil///PHIL_Images/17605/17605_lores.jpg)

Pelvic inflammatory disease, or PID (pelvic inflammatory disease), is an infection that ascends upward from the lower genital tract into the:

* uterus
* fallopian tubes
* ovaries
* pelvic peritoneum (lining of the pelvic cavity)

Most classic causes:

* Chlamydia trachomatis
* Neisseria gonorrhoeae

But PID can also be polymicrobial (caused by multiple organisms), including anaerobes (bacteria that grow well without oxygen).

---

### Core idea

Bacteria start lower, usually in the cervix (opening of the uterus), then climb upward.

So think:

> cervicitis → endometritis → salpingitis → tubo-ovarian abscess

Definitions:

* cervicitis = inflammation of the cervix
* endometritis = inflammation of the endometrium (inner uterine lining)
* salpingitis = inflammation of the fallopian tubes
* tubo-ovarian abscess = pus-filled infection involving the fallopian tube and ovary

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Lower abdominal/pelvic pain:** inflamed uterus, tubes, ovaries, and pelvic lining are irritated
* **Cervical motion tenderness:** moving the cervix tugs on inflamed upper reproductive organs
* **Fever:** systemic inflammatory response to infection
* **Abnormal discharge:** associated cervicitis/vaginal infection
* **Dyspareunia (pain with sex):** pelvic organs are inflamed and tender

---

### Why PID causes infertility

The fallopian tubes are supposed to be open, delicate tunnels that let the egg travel toward the uterus.

PID inflames and scars the tubes.

That can cause:

* tubal blockage → infertility
* partial blockage → ectopic pregnancy
* chronic pelvic pain → adhesions (scar tissue sticking organs together)

---

## One-line intuition

PID is an ascending infection that climbs from the cervix into the upper reproductive tract and scars the fallopian tubes.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Fitz-Hugh-Curtis syndrome is PID causing perihepatitis (inflammation around the liver capsule), leading to RUQ (right upper quadrant) pain even though the liver itself is not infected.
