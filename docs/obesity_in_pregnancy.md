## Obesity in pregnancy

![Image](https://stop.publichealth.gwu.edu/sites/g/files/zaxdzs4356/files/2022-06/maternal-child_intergenterational_transmission_of_obesity.png)

![Image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/54cce6f8e4b02ab7cb244c17/1659468914622-825NG2JOZE4CHPXQYN4K/pregnancy.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e7/Medical_Complications_of_Obesity.svg/1920px-Medical_Complications_of_Obesity.svg.png)

Obesity in pregnancy means the pregnant patient has obesity, usually defined as:

* BMI (body mass index) 30 or higher

Core idea:

* more adipose tissue = more insulin resistance, inflammation, and baseline cardiovascular strain
* pregnancy already causes insulin resistance and increased blood volume
* so obesity + pregnancy can push the body into metabolic/vascular complications

---

### Main maternal risks

Obesity in pregnancy increases risk of:

* gestational diabetes mellitus, meaning diabetes first recognized during pregnancy
* gestational hypertension, meaning high blood pressure during pregnancy
* preeclampsia, meaning hypertension plus end-organ dysfunction after 20 weeks
* thromboembolism, meaning pathologic blood clots
* cesarean delivery
* wound infection after delivery

Why?

Adipose tissue is hormonally active. It increases inflammatory cytokines (immune signaling proteins), free fatty acids, and insulin resistance. That makes glucose control worse and puts more stress on vessels/placenta.

---

### Main fetal/baby risks

Can increase risk of:

* macrosomia, meaning large birth weight
* shoulder dystocia, meaning the fetal shoulder gets stuck during delivery
* congenital anomalies, especially neural tube defects and cardiac defects
* stillbirth
* childhood obesity/metabolic disease later

Why macrosomia happens:

* maternal insulin resistance causes higher maternal glucose
* glucose crosses the placenta
* fetal pancreas responds with more insulin
* insulin is a growth factor
* baby grows larger

Insulin itself does not cross the placenta well, but glucose does.

---

## One-line intuition

Pregnancy already stresses glucose metabolism and blood vessels; obesity adds more insulin resistance and inflammation, so diabetes, preeclampsia, clotting, and large baby risk all go up.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Maternal obesity increases the risk of neural tube defects, so folate (vitamin B9) counseling is especially important before conception.
