# Latent autoimmune diabetes in adults

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK604182/bin/preventionttype1-Image001.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ac/2424_Exocrine_and_Endocrine_Pancreas.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d7/Type_1_Diabetes_Mellitus.jpg)

Latent autoimmune diabetes in adults is basically **slow adult-onset type 1 diabetes**.

It is often called **LADA (latent autoimmune diabetes in adults)** or sometimes **type 1.5 diabetes** because it looks mixed:

* like type 1 diabetes: autoimmune destruction of pancreatic beta cells
* like type 2 diabetes: starts in adults and may not need insulin right away

Beta cells are the pancreatic islet cells that make **insulin (the hormone that moves glucose from blood into tissues)**.

---

### Core idea

In LADA, the immune system slowly attacks pancreatic beta cells.

So early on:

* some beta cells still work
* insulin is still being made
* the patient may look like they have type 2 diabetes

But over time:

* beta cells keep dying
* insulin production drops
* the patient eventually needs insulin

---

### Why it is “latent”

Latent means hidden or delayed.

The autoimmune process is present, but it progresses slowly, so the person does not present like classic childhood type 1 diabetes with sudden severe insulin deficiency.

That is why LADA can be missed and labeled as **T2DM (type 2 diabetes mellitus)** at first.

---

### Diagnosis clues

Think LADA when an adult has diabetes but seems “too type 1-ish”:

* adult onset, often >30 years old
* not very obese
* poor response to oral diabetes meds
* low or falling **C-peptide (marker of endogenous insulin production)**
* positive islet autoantibodies

Common antibody:

* **anti-GAD antibody (anti-glutamic acid decarboxylase antibody)**

Anti-GAD is high yield because it points toward autoimmune beta-cell destruction.

---

## One-line intuition

LADA is type 1 diabetes moving in slow motion in an adult, so it can masquerade as type 2 diabetes early.

---

## Pearl 🔥

If an adult “type 2 diabetic” is lean and rapidly fails oral meds, check **GAD antibodies (glutamic acid decarboxylase antibodies)** and **C-peptide (endogenous insulin marker)** for LADA.
