# Breech presentation

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19158.jpg)

![Image](https://assets.mayoclinic.org/content/dam/hecs-ccms/global/images/pc-articles/2024/03/25/ART-20546850-pr00080-buttocks-first-en.jpg)

![Image](https://assets.mayoclinic.org/content/dam/hecs-ccms/global/images/pc-articles/2024/03/25/ART-20546850-pr00080-feet-first-en.jpg)

Breech presentation means the fetus is positioned so the buttocks or feet are closest to the cervix instead of the head.

Normally, near delivery, the fetus should be:

* head down
* head presenting first

That is called cephalic presentation (head-first fetal position).

In breech presentation:

* the fetal head is up near the uterine fundus
* the fetal buttocks or feet are down near the birth canal

---

### Main types

* **Frank breech:** hips flexed, knees extended  
  Baby is basically in a pike position: butt first, feet near the head.

* **Complete breech:** hips flexed, knees flexed  
  Baby is sitting cross-legged/crouched.

* **Footling breech:** one or both feet present first  
  This is higher risk because the feet do not fill the cervix well.

---

### Why it matters

Breech is riskier because the biggest part, the head, comes last.

So delivery can have problems like:

* head entrapment, meaning the body delivers but the head gets stuck
* umbilical cord prolapse, meaning the cord slips down before the baby and can get compressed
* birth trauma
* need for C-section (cesarean section)

The intuition:

> Head-first is safest because the largest, firmest part opens the path first. Breech is harder because the smaller butt/feet may come out first, then the larger head has to pass last.

---

### External cephalic version

External cephalic version (ECV) is when the doctor tries to turn the fetus from breech to head-down by pushing on the mother's abdomen from the outside.

Break it down:

* external = from outside the body
* cephalic = head
* version = turning

So ECV means:

> externally turning the baby into a head-down position.

---

## One-line intuition

Breech presentation = baby is bottom-first or feet-first instead of head-first.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Breech presentation is associated with developmental dysplasia of the hip (abnormal development/instability of the hip joint), especially frank breech, because the fetal hips are flexed for a long time.
