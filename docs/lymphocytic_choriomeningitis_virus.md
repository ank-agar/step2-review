## Lymphocytic choriomeningitis virus

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Viruses-04-02973-g002.webp/960px-Viruses-04-02973-g002.webp.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b9/Lymphocytic_choriomeningitis_virus.jpg/500px-Lymphocytic_choriomeningitis_virus.jpg)

### Core idea

Lymphocytic choriomeningitis virus (LCMV) is a rodent-borne arenavirus (enveloped, negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus) that classically causes:

* aseptic meningitis (meningitis not caused by typical bacteria)
* encephalitis (brain inflammation)
* congenital infection in pregnancy

Break the name apart:

* lymphocytic = lots of lymphocytes (white blood cells like T cells and B cells)
* chorio = choroid plexus (brain structure that makes cerebrospinal fluid)
* meningitis = inflammation of the meninges (brain and spinal cord coverings)

So the name basically means:

> a virus that causes lymphocyte-heavy inflammation around the meninges/choroid plexus.

---

### How you get it

Main reservoir:

* house mice
* sometimes pet/lab rodents like hamsters

Humans get infected by exposure to rodent:

* urine
* feces
* saliva
* aerosolized particles (tiny particles inhaled into the lungs)

So the classic clue is:

> meningitis-type illness + rodent exposure.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

LCMV infects the body first, then the immune response can inflame the central nervous system (CNS, brain and spinal cord).

That gives:

* fever
* headache
* muscle aches
* nausea/vomiting
* meningitis signs later, like neck stiffness and photophobia (light sensitivity)

The cerebrospinal fluid (CSF, fluid around the brain and spinal cord) often looks like viral meningitis:

* lymphocytic pleocytosis (increased lymphocytes in CSF)
* increased protein
* usually normal glucose

---

### Pregnancy association

If LCMV infects a fetus, it can damage the developing brain and eye.

Congenital LCMV can cause:

* hydrocephalus (too much cerebrospinal fluid in the brain ventricles)
* chorioretinitis (inflammation/scarring of the choroid and retina in the eye)
* intracranial calcifications (calcium deposits in the brain)
* seizures/developmental delay

Why?

The fetal brain and retina are actively forming, so viral injury there can permanently disrupt normal structure.

---

## One-line intuition

LCMV is a mouse-borne viral meningitis bug that becomes especially high-yield when it infects a fetus and causes brain + eye abnormalities.

---

## Pearl 🔥

LCMV can mimic congenital toxoplasmosis because both can cause hydrocephalus + chorioretinitis + intracranial calcifications, but LCMV points toward rodent exposure, while toxoplasmosis points toward cat feces or undercooked meat.
