## Hypospadias

Hypospadias is a congenital defect (present at birth) where the urethral meatus (external opening of the urethra, where urine exits) is on the ventral side (underside) of the penis instead of at the tip.

### Core idea

The urethral opening is too low.

It can be:

* near the glans (head of penis)
* along the shaft
* near the scrotum
* in the perineum (area between scrotum and anus)

More proximal (closer to the body) usually means more severe.

---

### Why it happens

During male external genital development, the urethral folds normally fuse in the midline to form the penile urethra.

In hypospadias:

* urethral folds fail to fuse completely
* the urethral tube does not extend all the way to the tip
* the opening stays on the underside

So the anatomy is basically:

> incomplete ventral closure of the urethra.

---

### Associated finding

Often associated with chordee (ventral curvature of the penis).

Why?

The ventral tissues are underdeveloped/shortened, so the penis can curve downward.

---

## One-line intuition

Hypospadias = the urethral opening is on the underside because the ventral urethral folds did not fully close.

---

## HY Step 1 pearl

Do **not** circumcise an infant with hypospadias, because the foreskin may be needed later for surgical repair.
