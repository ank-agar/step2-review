### Core idea

Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome is brain injury from **thiamine deficiency**.

Thiamine = vitamin B1.

It has 2 parts:

* **Wernicke encephalopathy** = acute, reversible-ish emergency
* **Korsakoff syndrome** = chronic, often irreversible memory problem

Think:

> low B1 injures memory + eye movement + balance circuits.

---

### Why thiamine deficiency hurts the brain

Thiamine is needed for glucose metabolism, meaning cells use it to turn glucose into energy.

Brain cells need tons of glucose energy, so if B1 is low:

* neurons cannot make enough ATP (energy)
* high-demand brain regions get injured
* especially the mammillary bodies, thalamus, periaqueductal gray, and cerebellar pathways

Mammillary bodies are part of memory circuitry, which is why memory gets hit hard.

---

### Wernicke encephalopathy

Classic triad:

* confusion
* ophthalmoplegia (eye movement weakness)
* ataxia (unsteady gait/coordination problem)

But the full triad is often not all present, so do not wait for all 3.

This is treated urgently with:

* IV thiamine

Important: give thiamine before glucose.

Why?

Glucose metabolism uses thiamine. If you give glucose first to someone who is thiamine-deficient, you can worsen the B1 shortage and worsen brain injury.

---

### Korsakoff syndrome

This is the chronic memory syndrome after untreated or severe Wernicke encephalopathy.

Classic findings:

* anterograde amnesia (cannot form new memories)
* retrograde amnesia (loss of old memories)
* confabulation (unintentionally making up stories to fill memory gaps)

Confabulation is not lying.

It is the brain trying to fill in missing memory without realizing the memory is missing.

---

### Common cause

Most commonly associated with chronic alcohol use disorder.

Why alcohol causes it:

* poor nutrition lowers thiamine intake
* alcohol decreases thiamine absorption in the gut
* liver disease decreases thiamine storage

But it can also happen with severe malnutrition, hyperemesis gravidarum (severe vomiting in pregnancy), eating disorders, bariatric surgery, or cancer.

---

## One-line intuition

Wernicke-Korsakoff = **low vitamin B1 starves memory/balance/eye-movement brain circuits of energy**, causing acute confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia, then chronic amnesia/confabulation if untreated.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 loves: **give thiamine before glucose** in alcohol use disorder or malnutrition, because glucose metabolism consumes thiamine and can precipitate/worsen Wernicke encephalopathy.
