## Major depressive disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Depression_biological_factors.jpg)

Major depressive disorder (MDD) is a mood disorder with:

* depressed mood and/or anhedonia (loss of interest or pleasure)
* lasting at least 2 weeks
* causing functional impairment

To diagnose a major depressive episode:

* at least 5 symptoms for at least 2 weeks
* must include depressed mood or anhedonia

Classic symptom memory aid:

* sleep change
* interest loss/anhedonia
* guilt or worthlessness
* energy loss
* concentration problems
* appetite or weight change
* psychomotor slowing/agitation (movement or speech becomes slowed or restless)
* suicidal ideation (thoughts of self-harm or death)

---

### Core idea

Depression is not just "being sad."

It is a pathologic low-mood state where the brain circuits for:

* mood
* reward
* sleep
* appetite
* concentration
* energy

are persistently dysregulated.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Low mood:** impaired monoamine signaling, especially serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine
* **Anhedonia:** reduced reward pathway activity, especially dopamine-related motivation/reward circuits
* **Sleep/appetite changes:** hypothalamic and circadian rhythm dysregulation
* **Low energy/concentration:** reduced arousal and attention signaling
* **Psychomotor slowing:** severe depression can slow thinking, speech, and movement

Monoamines = neurotransmitters with one amine group, including serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine.

---

### Step 1 treatment intuition

Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) increase serotonin in the synaptic cleft (space between neurons).

Why they take weeks:

* serotonin rises quickly
* but receptor adaptation and downstream brain circuit changes take longer

So symptom improvement is delayed.

---

## One-line intuition

MDD is a sustained shutdown of mood/reward/energy circuits, causing low mood or loss of pleasure plus body-wide symptoms.

---

## HY pearl

MDD can have neurovegetative symptoms, meaning body-function symptoms like:

* sleep change
* appetite change
* low energy
* psychomotor slowing

Also, always distinguish MDD from bipolar disorder because giving antidepressants alone can trigger mania (abnormally elevated mood, high energy, decreased need for sleep).
