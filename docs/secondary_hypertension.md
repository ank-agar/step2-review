# Secondary hypertension

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone_system.svg/960px-Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone_system.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/18103/N01399-H.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f3/Pheochromocytoma2.jpg)

Secondary hypertension = high blood pressure caused by a specific underlying problem.

So unlike primary hypertension, where there is no single obvious cause, secondary hypertension means:

> something else is driving the blood pressure up.

Common Step 1 causes:

* kidney disease
* renal artery stenosis
* primary hyperaldosteronism
* pheochromocytoma
* Cushing syndrome
* obstructive sleep apnea
* thyroid disease
* drugs, like NSAIDs (nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs), OCPs (oral contraceptive pills), steroids, cocaine

---

### Core idea

Blood pressure is basically pushed up by:

* more fluid in the vessels
* more vessel constriction
* more sympathetic tone

Secondary hypertension is when a disease causes one of those.

---

### Why the big causes raise BP

Renal artery stenosis:

* renal artery is narrowed
* kidney thinks blood pressure is low
* kidney releases renin
* RAAS (renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system) turns on
* angiotensin II constricts vessels
* aldosterone retains sodium/water
* BP goes up

Primary hyperaldosteronism:

* adrenal gland makes too much aldosterone
* aldosterone increases sodium and water reabsorption
* volume goes up
* BP goes up
* potassium goes down

Pheochromocytoma:

* adrenal medulla tumor releases catecholamines (epinephrine/norepinephrine)
* sympathetic tone shoots up
* causes episodic hypertension, headache, sweating, palpitations

Kidney disease:

* damaged kidneys cannot regulate salt/water well
* fluid retention increases blood volume
* BP goes up

---

### When to suspect it

Think secondary hypertension when BP is:

* severe
* sudden onset
* resistant to multiple medications
* in a young patient
* associated with hypokalemia (low potassium)
* associated with episodic sweating/headache/palpitations

---

## One-line intuition

Secondary hypertension means the BP is high because another disease is pressing the “raise blood pressure” button.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Renal artery stenosis causes high renin hypertension because the kidney is underperfused and thinks the whole body has low BP.
