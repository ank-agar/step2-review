## Blastomycosis

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil///PHIL_Images/493/493_lores.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/blastomycosis/media/images/usablastoplasmosismap_3.jpg)

Blastomycosis is a systemic fungal infection caused by **Blastomyces**.

Systemic means it can spread through the body, not just stay in one local spot.

Classic Step 1 idea:

* inhaled spores from soil
* starts in the lungs
* can spread to skin and bone
* microscopy shows **broad-based budding yeast**

---

### Core idea

Blastomyces is a **dimorphic fungus**.

Dimorphic fungus means:

* mold in the cold/environment
* yeast in the heat/body

So think:

> mold in soil gets inhaled, then turns into yeast in the lungs.

---

### Where it lives

Classically associated with:

* Ohio River Valley
* Mississippi River Valley
* Great Lakes region

It likes moist soil and decaying organic matter, like wood and leaves.

---

### What it causes

Usually starts like pneumonia:

* fever
* cough
* chest pain
* shortness of breath

But if it disseminates, meaning spreads through blood, it can cause:

* verrucous skin lesions
* bone lesions
* genitourinary disease

Verrucous means wart-like.

Genitourinary means involving the genital and urinary tract.

---

### Why the skin/bone findings happen

The fungus enters through the lungs first.

Then yeast can survive in tissue and spread hematogenously, meaning through the blood.

That is why blastomycosis can look like:

* pneumonia plus skin lesions
* pneumonia plus bone pain
* a chronic infection that can mimic cancer

---

### Microscopy clue

The buzzword is:

> broad-based budding yeast

Broad-based budding means the daughter yeast cell is attached to the parent yeast by a wide neck.

This is different from narrow-based budding, where the daughter cell pinches off through a skinny connection.

---

## One-line intuition

Blastomycosis is a dimorphic fungus from wet soil that gets inhaled into the lungs, becomes yeast, and can spread to skin and bone.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Blastomycosis = **broad-based budding**.

If a question stem says Mississippi/Ohio River region + pneumonia + verrucous skin lesions, think Blastomyces.
