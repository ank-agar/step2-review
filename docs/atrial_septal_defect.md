## Atrial septal defect

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3b/Blausen_0069_AtrialSeptalDefect_02.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/18119.jpg)

### Core idea

Atrial septal defect (ASD) is a congenital heart defect where there is a hole in the atrial septum (wall between the right atrium and left atrium).

So blood can move:

* left atrium -> right atrium
* oxygenated blood goes back to the lungs instead of going fully out to the body

---

### Why blood goes left to right

Left atrial pressure is normally higher than right atrial pressure.

So the blood gets pushed from:

* left side = higher pressure
* right side = lower pressure

This causes a left-to-right shunt (abnormal blood flow from the left heart to the right heart).

---

### What happens over time

Extra blood enters the right side of the heart, so you get:

* right atrial enlargement
* right ventricular enlargement
* increased pulmonary blood flow

Basically, the right heart and lungs get volume overloaded.

---

### Classic finding

ASD classically causes:

* fixed split S2

S2 (second heart sound) is the closing of the aortic and pulmonic valves.

Why fixed split S2 happens:

* more blood keeps flowing into the right heart
* the pulmonic valve closes late
* because the shunt is always adding blood to the right side, the split does not vary much with breathing

---

## One-line intuition

ASD is a hole between the atria that lets left-sided blood overload the right heart and lungs.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Most common type of ASD is ostium secundum ASD, caused by excessive resorption of septum primum or underdevelopment of septum secundum.
