## Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/pneumocystis/modules/Pneumocystis_LifeCycle.gif)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/pneumocystis/images/2/Pneumocystis_HB1.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/pneumocystis/images/1/Pneumocystis_BAL_Giemsa1.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/74/PCPxray.jpg)

Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia, often called PCP (Pneumocystis pneumonia), is an opportunistic fungal lung infection.

Opportunistic means it mainly causes disease when the immune system is weak.

Classic patient:

* HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) with CD4 (cluster of differentiation 4 helper T-cell count) < 200
* transplant patient
* chronic corticosteroid or chemotherapy use

---

### Core idea

Pneumocystis jirovecii lives in the alveoli (tiny air sacs where gas exchange happens).

It fills the alveoli with foamy material, so oxygen cannot diffuse well from air into blood.

That gives:

* progressive dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* dry/nonproductive cough
* fever
* hypoxemia (low blood oxygen)

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Diffuse bilateral infiltrates:** many alveoli are affected throughout both lungs
* **Ground-glass appearance:** the alveoli are partially filled, not completely consolidated like classic lobar bacterial pneumonia
* **High A-a gradient (alveolar-arterial oxygen gradient):** oxygen reaches the alveoli, but cannot cross into blood efficiently
* **Elevated LDH (lactate dehydrogenase):** lung tissue injury releases this enzyme

Think:

> Air gets in, but oxygen transfer across the alveolar wall is blocked.

---

### Diagnosis

Look for the organism in induced sputum or BAL (bronchoalveolar lavage, fluid collected from the lungs during bronchoscopy).

Classic stain:

* silver stain shows cup-shaped cysts

---

### Treatment

First-line treatment:

* TMP-SMX (trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole)

Add corticosteroids if severe hypoxemia is present, because killing the organism can trigger lung inflammation and worsen oxygenation.

---

## One-line intuition

PCP is a fungal pneumonia in immunocompromised patients where foamy organisms clog the alveoli and cause a diffusion problem.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In HIV (human immunodeficiency virus), PCP prophylaxis (prevention) is TMP-SMX (trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole) when CD4 (cluster of differentiation 4 helper T-cell count) drops below 200.
