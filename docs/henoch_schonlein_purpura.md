Henoch-Schonlein purpura is now called **IgA vasculitis**.

IgA (immunoglobulin A) vasculitis = small blood vessel inflammation caused by IgA immune complex deposition.

Classic patient:

* child
* recent upper respiratory infection
* palpable purpura
* abdominal pain
* joint pain
* kidney involvement

Palpable purpura = raised purple-red spots you can feel.

---

### Core idea

IgA immune complexes deposit in small vessels, especially in:

* skin
* joints
* gastrointestinal tract
* kidneys

This causes inflammation of those small vessels.

So the symptoms are basically:

* skin vessels leak blood → purpura
* gut vessels get inflamed → abdominal pain, GI (gastrointestinal) bleeding
* joint vessels get inflamed → arthralgia (joint pain) or arthritis (joint inflammation)
* kidney glomeruli get inflamed → hematuria (blood in urine) and proteinuria (protein in urine)

---

### Why the rash is on the legs/buttocks

The purpura is classically on dependent areas:

* legs
* buttocks

because gravity and small-vessel inflammation make blood leakage more noticeable there.

Important: platelet count is usually normal.

So this is **nonthrombocytopenic purpura**:

* nonthrombocytopenic = not due to low platelets
* purpura = purple bleeding spots in skin

---

### Kidney connection

In the kidney, IgA deposits in the glomerulus.

Glomerulus = tiny filtering unit of the kidney.

That is why Henoch-Schonlein purpura can look like IgA nephropathy:

* hematuria
* proteinuria
* sometimes hypertension

Intuition:

> IgA vasculitis is like IgA nephropathy plus systemic symptoms.

IgA nephropathy mainly hits the kidney.

IgA vasculitis hits kidney + skin + gut + joints.

---

## One-line intuition

Henoch-Schonlein purpura is **IgA immune complexes clogging/inflaming small vessels**, causing the Step 1 tetrad:

* palpable purpura
* abdominal pain
* arthritis/arthralgia
* renal disease

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association:

**Henoch-Schonlein purpura = type III hypersensitivity reaction.**

Type III hypersensitivity reaction = immune complex deposition causing inflammation.

That is why IgA immune complexes deposit in vessels and cause vasculitis.
