## Malignant hyperthermia

### Core idea

Malignant hyperthermia is a life-threatening reaction to certain anesthetic drugs where skeletal muscle gets stuck in a hypermetabolic state.

Triggered by:

* volatile anesthetics, like halothane/sevoflurane/isoflurane
* succinylcholine, a depolarizing paralytic drug

Usually due to an inherited **RYR1 mutation** (ryanodine receptor 1 mutation).

---

### What is actually going wrong?

The ryanodine receptor is the calcium release channel on the sarcoplasmic reticulum (calcium storage organelle in muscle).

In malignant hyperthermia:

* anesthesia trigger hits susceptible muscle
* abnormal RYR1 channel opens too much
* too much calcium floods into the muscle cell cytoplasm
* muscle contracts intensely and cannot relax well
* ATP (adenosine triphosphate, cellular energy) gets burned rapidly

So the body starts producing lots of:

* heat
* CO2 (carbon dioxide)
* acid
* muscle breakdown products

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Muscle rigidity:** too much calcium keeps actin-myosin contraction turned on
* **High CO2:** muscles are burning tons of ATP, so metabolism produces excess carbon dioxide
* **Metabolic acidosis:** intense muscle metabolism produces lactic acid
* **Hyperthermia:** all that ATP use releases heat
* **Hyperkalemia:** damaged muscle cells leak K+ (potassium)
* **Rhabdomyolysis:** muscle cells break down and release myoglobin, which can injure kidneys

Important: fever is often a later sign.

Early clue is usually:

* rising end-tidal CO2 (carbon dioxide measured in exhaled air)
* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* muscle rigidity, especially masseter rigidity (jaw muscle rigidity)

---

### Treatment

Stop the triggering anesthetic and give:

* **dantrolene**

Dantrolene works by blocking calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum, so the muscle can stop contracting so aggressively.

Also give supportive care:

* cooling
* oxygen
* correct acidosis
* treat hyperkalemia
* protect kidneys from rhabdomyolysis

---

## One-line intuition

Malignant hyperthermia is an anesthesia-triggered calcium leak in skeletal muscle, causing runaway contraction, heat production, acidosis, high CO2, and muscle breakdown.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Malignant hyperthermia is **autosomal dominant** (one mutated copy can cause disease), and the classic Step 1 treatment is **dantrolene**.
