### Core idea

Tonsillitis = inflammation of the tonsils.

The tonsils are lymphoid tissue (immune tissue that samples germs) in the back of the throat.

So tonsillitis is basically:

> infected/swollen immune tissue in the back of the mouth.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Sore throat:** inflamed tonsils irritate the throat
* **Painful swallowing:** food/liquid rubs against swollen tonsils
* **Fever:** immune cytokines (inflammatory signaling proteins) reset the body's temperature upward
* **Red, swollen tonsils:** increased blood flow + immune cell buildup
* **White exudate:** pus-like material from dead neutrophils (bacteria-fighting white blood cells), bacteria, and debris
* **Tender neck nodes:** cervical lymph nodes (neck immune filters) enlarge because they are draining the infection

---

### Causes

Most cases are viral.

Important bacterial cause:

* Group A Streptococcus, aka GAS (Group A Streptococcus)

GAS matters because untreated strep throat can lead to immune complications like rheumatic fever.

---

### Viral vs bacterial intuition

Viral tonsillitis often comes with viral symptoms:

* cough
* runny nose
* hoarse voice
* conjunctivitis (eye inflammation)

Bacterial strep tonsillitis is more likely with:

* fever
* tonsillar exudates
* tender anterior cervical lymphadenopathy (painful front-neck lymph node swelling)
* no cough

---

### Complication to know

Peritonsillar abscess = pus collection next to the tonsil.

Think:

> tonsillitis spreads locally and forms a pocket of pus.

Classic clues:

* muffled “hot potato” voice
* uvula deviation
* trismus (difficulty opening the mouth)

---

## One-line intuition

Tonsillitis is when the tonsils act like immune filters, catch an infection, and become red, swollen, painful, and sometimes covered with exudate.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, GAS pharyngitis/tonsillitis is treated partly to prevent rheumatic fever, which is caused by molecular mimicry (antibodies against strep cross-reacting with human tissue), especially the heart.
