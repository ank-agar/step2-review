## Brachial plexus injury

### Core idea

The brachial plexus is the nerve network from **C5-T1** (cervical spinal nerves 5 through thoracic spinal nerve 1) that controls:

* shoulder
* arm
* forearm
* hand

A brachial plexus injury means these nerves get stretched, compressed, torn, or avulsed (ripped away from the spinal cord).

---

### Intuition

Think of the brachial plexus like a big electrical cable bundle going from the neck to the arm.

If the neck and shoulder get pulled apart too hard, the wires stretch or tear, so the muscles and skin supplied by those nerves lose signal.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Weakness/paralysis:** motor nerves to muscles are damaged
* **Numbness/tingling:** sensory nerves from skin are damaged
* **Burning pain:** injured nerves fire abnormally
* **Atrophy later:** denervated muscles lose nerve input and shrink

---

### Classic Step 1 patterns

**Upper brachial plexus injury = Erb-Duchenne palsy**

Usually involves **C5-C6**.

Classically caused by:

* excessive lateral traction on the neck
* birth trauma
* motorcycle/shoulder trauma

Arm position:

* adducted (held toward body)
* internally rotated
* extended elbow
* pronated forearm

This is the classic **waiter's tip** posture.

Why?

* C5-C6 help power shoulder abduction/external rotation and elbow flexion
* so loss of those actions leaves the arm hanging inward and extended

---

**Lower brachial plexus injury = Klumpke palsy**

Usually involves **C8-T1**.

Classically caused by:

* upward traction on the arm
* grabbing something during a fall

Findings:

* hand weakness
* claw hand
* possible Horner syndrome

Horner syndrome = ptosis (droopy eyelid), miosis (constricted pupil), and anhidrosis (loss of sweating).

Why Horner syndrome?

T1 fibers can carry sympathetic nerve fibers to the eye, so a lower plexus injury can disrupt that pathway.

---

## One-line intuition

Brachial plexus injury = damage to the neck-to-arm nerve bundle, causing arm weakness and sensory loss based on which roots are hit.

---

## Pearl 🔥

**Erb palsy = C5-C6 = waiter's tip.**

**Klumpke palsy = C8-T1 = claw hand + possible Horner syndrome.**
