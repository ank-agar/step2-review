Bronchiolitis obliterans organizing pneumonia, or **BOOP** (bronchiolitis obliterans organizing pneumonia), is now usually called **cryptogenic organizing pneumonia** when no cause is found.

Break it down:

* **bronchiolitis** = inflammation of bronchioles (small airways)
* **obliterans** = the airway lumen gets plugged/blocked
* **organizing pneumonia** = old lung injury is “healing” by growing granulation tissue (new repair tissue made of fibroblasts and collagen)

So BOOP is basically:

> lung injury where repair tissue plugs small airways and alveoli.

---

### Core idea

This is not a classic bacterial pneumonia.

In bacterial pneumonia, alveoli (tiny air sacs) fill with neutrophils/pus.

In BOOP/COP (cryptogenic organizing pneumonia), alveoli and small bronchioles get filled with **Masson bodies** (plugs of granulation tissue).

Masson bodies = fibroblast plugs inside airspaces.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Dry cough and shortness of breath:** airspaces are plugged, so gas exchange is harder
* **Fever and fatigue:** lung inflammation causes systemic symptoms
* **Patchy opacities on chest imaging:** plugged/inflamed alveoli look white because air is replaced by tissue/inflammatory material
* **Does not improve with antibiotics:** the problem is inflammatory/repair tissue, not active bacteria
* **Improves with corticosteroids:** steroids reduce inflammation and fibroblast-driven repair

---

### Causes/associations

It can be idiopathic, meaning no known cause.

It can also happen after:

* viral infection
* connective tissue disease, like rheumatoid arthritis
* drugs, like amiodarone
* radiation
* transplant rejection

If no cause is found, call it **COP** (cryptogenic organizing pneumonia).

---

## One-line intuition

BOOP/COP is like pneumonia that the lung tried to repair, but the repair response overdid it and plugged the tiny airways/alveoli with granulation tissue.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY distinction:

* **BOOP/COP:** plugs inside alveoli/bronchioles; often steroid-responsive
* **bronchiolitis obliterans:** small airways are scarred/narrowed; classically associated with transplant rejection

Do not mix them up just because both have “bronchiolitis obliterans” in the name.
