### Core idea

Fecal incontinence means involuntary leakage of stool.

Basically:

* stool comes out when the person does not want it to
* can be gas + stool staining
* can be liquid stool
* can be complete loss of solid stool

Continence depends on a few things working together:

* rectum senses stool
* rectum can stretch and store stool
* internal anal sphincter stays tight automatically
* external anal sphincter can squeeze voluntarily
* stool is formed enough to hold

Sphincter = ring-like muscle that closes an opening.

---

### Why leakage happens

Think of the anus like a valve.

Fecal incontinence happens when either:

* the valve is weak
* the nerves controlling the valve are damaged
* the rectum cannot store stool well
* stool is too loose/liquid to hold
* stool is impacted and liquid leaks around it

That last one is called overflow incontinence:

* constipation causes a hard stool mass
* liquid stool leaks around the blockage
* so constipation can paradoxically cause stool leakage

Paradoxically = seems opposite of what you expect.

---

### Common causes

Major causes include:

* diarrhea
* chronic constipation with overflow
* anal sphincter injury after childbirth
* pelvic surgery
* rectal prolapse
* diabetes mellitus causing nerve damage
* spinal cord injury
* inflammatory bowel disease, or IBD (inflammatory bowel disease)
* radiation injury causing a stiff rectum

Why diarrhea matters:

* liquid stool is harder to hold than formed stool
* even normal sphincters can get overwhelmed

Why nerve damage matters:

* the rectum may not sense fullness properly
* the external sphincter may not squeeze effectively

---

## One-line intuition

Fecal incontinence is a failure of the rectum-anus continence system: storage, sensation, sphincter squeeze, and stool consistency are not working together.

---

## HY (high-yield) Step 1 pearl

External anal sphincter = voluntary skeletal muscle controlled by the pudendal nerve from S2-S4.

So a lesion affecting S2-S4 or the pudendal nerve can cause:

* fecal incontinence
* urinary problems
* decreased anal wink reflex

Anal wink reflex = contraction of the external anal sphincter when the skin around the anus is stroked.
