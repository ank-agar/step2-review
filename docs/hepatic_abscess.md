# Hepatic abscess

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/24341.jpg)

![Image](https://med.aws.emory.edu/departments/emergency-medicine/_images/ultrasound/hepaticabscess.jpg)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/PHIL_Images/27028/27028_lores.jpg)

### Core idea

A hepatic abscess is a walled-off pocket of pus inside the liver.

Break it down:

* hepatic = liver
* abscess = localized pus collection from infection

So:

> infected material gets trapped inside liver tissue and forms a pus-filled cavity.

---

### Why it happens

The liver gets exposed to infection because it receives blood/drainage from the gut and biliary system.

Common routes:

* **Biliary tract spread:** infection ascends from bile ducts, especially with obstruction/cholangitis (infection of bile ducts)
* **Portal vein spread:** gut infections like appendicitis or diverticulitis drain to the liver through the portal vein
* **Amebic spread:** Entamoeba histolytica (a protozoan parasite) travels from the colon to the liver

---

### What you see clinically

Classic findings:

* fever
* RUQ pain (right upper quadrant abdominal pain)
* tender/enlarged liver
* nausea/anorexia (low appetite)
* elevated alkaline phosphatase sometimes

Intuition:

The liver capsule stretches/inflames, causing RUQ pain, while pus/infection causes fever.

---

### Pyogenic vs amebic

**Pyogenic hepatic abscess** = bacterial pus-forming liver abscess.

Often polymicrobial (multiple organisms), especially from biliary or gut sources.

**Amebic hepatic abscess** = caused by Entamoeba histolytica.

Classically after travel/exposure in endemic areas and may follow amebic colitis (colon infection).

---

### Treatment idea

Usually:

* antibiotics
* drainage if large or not improving

Why drainage?

Antibiotics may not penetrate a big pus pocket well, so physically removing the pus helps source control.

---

## One-line intuition

A hepatic abscess is basically an infected pus pocket in the liver, usually from bacteria traveling through bile ducts or portal blood, or from Entamoeba histolytica.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Amebic liver abscess classically has **“anchovy paste”** aspirate and is treated with metronidazole plus a luminal agent (a drug that clears organisms from the intestinal lumen) like paromomycin.
