Trypanosomiasis means infection by **Trypanosoma** parasites, which are flagellated protozoa (single-celled organisms with a whip-like tail).

There are 2 big Step 1 versions:

* **African trypanosomiasis** = sleeping sickness
* **American trypanosomiasis** = Chagas disease

---

### African trypanosomiasis

Caused by:

* **Trypanosoma brucei gambiense**
* **Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense**

Spread by:

* **tsetse fly**

Classic idea:

* parasite enters blood/lymph
* later crosses into the CNS (central nervous system)
* causes sleep-wake disturbance, confusion, coma

That is why it is called **sleeping sickness**.

Important clue:

* posterior cervical lymphadenopathy (swollen lymph nodes in the back of the neck) = **Winterbottom sign**

---

### American trypanosomiasis / Chagas disease

Caused by:

* **Trypanosoma cruzi**

Spread by:

* reduviid bug / kissing bug

Classic idea:

* bug bites
* bug defecates near bite
* parasite in feces enters through skin or mucosa (wet epithelial surface, like conjunctiva)

Acute finding:

* **Romana sign** = painless periorbital swelling (swelling around the eye)

Chronic findings:

* dilated cardiomyopathy (big weak heart)
* megaesophagus (dilated esophagus)
* megacolon (dilated colon)

Why?

T. cruzi damages autonomic ganglia (nerve cell clusters controlling involuntary function), especially in the heart and GI (gastrointestinal) tract.

So the organs lose normal tone/motility and become dilated.

---

## One-line intuition

Trypanosomiasis is a flagellated protozoal infection: **T. brucei goes to the brain and causes sleeping sickness; T. cruzi damages heart/GI nerves and causes Chagas disease.**

---

## Pearl

African trypanosomes have **antigenic variation** (they keep changing surface proteins), which helps them evade antibodies (immune proteins that bind pathogens).
