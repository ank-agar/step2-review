### Core idea

Bulimia nervosa is an eating disorder with:

* binge eating
* loss of control during the binge
* compensatory behavior afterward

Compensatory behavior means trying to "undo" the calories, like:

* self-induced vomiting
* laxative use
* diuretic use
* fasting
* excessive exercise

Usually BMI (body mass index) is normal or high.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Vomiting exposes teeth to stomach acid** → enamel erosion and dental caries
* **Repeated vomiting irritates salivary glands** → parotid gland enlargement
* **Fingers scrape against teeth during vomiting** → Russell sign, which is calluses/scars on the knuckles
* **Vomiting loses hydrogen chloride from the stomach** → hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis
* **Volume loss activates RAAS (renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system)** → kidney wastes potassium → hypokalemia

So the classic electrolyte pattern is:

> hypokalemic, hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis

---

### Key distinction

Bulimia nervosa:

* binge + purge
* usually normal/high BMI (body mass index)

Anorexia nervosa, binge-eating/purging type:

* binge + purge
* significantly low BMI (body mass index)

So the weight status helps separate them.

---

## One-line intuition

Bulimia nervosa is a binge-purge cycle where purging causes the classic acid/base and physical exam findings.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Best medication is an SSRI (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor), especially fluoxetine, and the most HY lab clue is hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis from vomiting.
