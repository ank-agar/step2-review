# Seborrheic dermatitis

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532846/bin/en_haut_seborrhoisches_ekzem_gegenueberstellung_weisse-und-dunkle-haut.jpg)

![Image](https://www.penndermspecialists.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/seborrheic-dermatitis-1-300x290.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/2398.jpg)

Seborrheic dermatitis is chronic inflammation of oily skin areas.

Break it down:

* seborrheic = related to sebum, which is skin oil
* dermatitis = skin inflammation

So the core idea is:

> oily areas of skin get inflamed and flaky.

Classic locations:

* scalp, where it can look like dandruff
* eyebrows
* nasolabial folds, which are the creases from the nose to the mouth
* ears
* central chest

It usually causes:

* greasy yellow-white scale
* red or lighter inflamed patches
* mild itching

---

### Why it happens

The big association is **Malassezia**, which is a normal yeast/fungus that lives on oily skin.

Malassezia likes sebum-rich areas. In susceptible people, it triggers an inflammatory reaction.

That inflammation makes the epidermis, which is the outer skin layer, turn over faster than normal.

Fast turnover = skin cells shed in clumps = flakes/scales.

So the intuition is:

> oil feeds yeast, yeast irritates skin, irritated skin flakes.

---

### Important associations

Seborrheic dermatitis can be worse in:

* HIV (human immunodeficiency virus)
* Parkinson disease
* immunosuppression, meaning a weakened immune system

Why HIV matters:

If immune control is poor, Malassezia and skin inflammation can become more exaggerated.

---

### Treatment intuition

Treatment usually targets the yeast and inflammation:

* ketoconazole shampoo/cream = antifungal medicine
* selenium sulfide or zinc pyrithione shampoos = anti-dandruff/anti-yeast effect
* low-potency topical steroids = calm inflammation during flares

Do not think of it as simply “dry skin.”

It often looks flaky, but the underlying problem is usually oily skin + yeast-driven inflammation.

---

## One-line intuition

Seborrheic dermatitis is dandruff-like inflammation in oily areas because Malassezia yeast triggers scaling and irritation.

---

## HY Step 1 pearl

Seborrheic dermatitis is associated with **Malassezia** and is classically seen in oily areas like the scalp, eyebrows, nasolabial folds, and chest; severe or widespread disease should make you think about HIV (human immunodeficiency virus).
