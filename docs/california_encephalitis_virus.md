California encephalitis virus is part of the **California serogroup** of **Orthobunyaviruses**.

Orthobunyavirus = enveloped, negative-sense, segmented RNA virus.

The high-yield human disease in this group is usually **La Crosse encephalitis virus**, which is closely related and tested more often.

---

### Core idea

This is a mosquito-borne **arbovirus**.

Arbovirus = arthropod-borne virus, meaning it is spread by an arthropod like a mosquito or tick.

Basic cycle:

* mosquito bites small mammal
* virus amplifies in small mammals like chipmunks/squirrels
* mosquito bites human
* human can develop CNS infection

CNS = central nervous system, meaning brain and spinal cord.

Humans are usually **dead-end hosts**.

Dead-end host = the human gets infected, but does not make enough virus in the blood to pass it back to mosquitoes.

---

### What it causes

Most infections are mild or asymptomatic.

If symptomatic:

* fever
* headache
* nausea/vomiting
* fatigue

Severe cases can cause:

* meningitis (inflammation of the meninges, the coverings around the brain/spinal cord)
* encephalitis (inflammation of brain tissue)
* seizures
* altered mental status

Severe neuro disease is classically more common in children.

---

### Why the brain symptoms happen

After a mosquito bite, the virus first replicates locally, then enters the blood.

If it reaches the CNS, it triggers inflammation in brain tissue.

That inflammation disrupts neuron signaling, causing:

* confusion/altered mental status
* seizures
* headache
* neurologic deficits

So the issue is not just “virus in blood”; the dangerous part is viral inflammation inside the brain.

---

### Diagnosis and treatment

Diagnosis is usually by serology:

* IgM antibody testing in serum or CSF

IgM = immunoglobulin M, an early antibody suggesting recent infection.

CSF = cerebrospinal fluid, the fluid around the brain and spinal cord.

CSF in viral encephalitis often shows:

* lymphocytic pleocytosis (increased lymphocytes in CSF)
* normal glucose
* mildly elevated protein

Treatment is supportive:

* fluids
* fever/pain control
* seizure management if needed
* respiratory support if severe

No routine antiviral cure.

---

## One-line intuition

California encephalitis virus is a mosquito-borne Orthobunyavirus group virus that cycles between mosquitoes and small mammals, but can accidentally infect humans and inflame the brain.
