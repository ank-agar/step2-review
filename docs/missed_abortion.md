# Missed abortion

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/12/Gestational_sac.svg/250px-Gestational_sac.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5c/Ultrasound_of_embryo_at_5_weeks%2C_colored.svg/960px-Ultrasound_of_embryo_at_5_weeks%2C_colored.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Anembryonic_gestation.jpg)

### Core idea

Missed abortion means:

* the embryo/fetus has died
* but the products of conception are still retained in the uterus
* and the cervix is closed

Products of conception = pregnancy tissue, including gestational sac, embryo/fetus, placenta, and membranes.

So the word "missed" means the body has not expelled it yet.

---

### Intuition

In a complete abortion, the pregnancy tissue is gone.

In an incomplete abortion, some tissue is gone and some remains.

In a missed abortion, the pregnancy has stopped developing, but the tissue is still sitting inside the uterus.

---

### What ultrasound shows

Classic findings:

* embryo/fetus with no cardiac activity
* gestational sac that is too large with no embryo
* pregnancy measuring smaller than expected for dates

Gestational sac = fluid-filled sac surrounding the early pregnancy.

Cardiac activity = heartbeat.

---

### Why symptoms may be subtle

The cervix stays closed, so there may be:

* little or no bleeding
* little or no cramping
* persistent pregnancy symptoms for a while

Why?

Because trophoblast tissue (early placental tissue) can still make hCG (human chorionic gonadotropin), so the body may still "feel pregnant" even though the embryo/fetus is no longer viable.

---

## One-line intuition

Missed abortion = fetal/embryonic death that the uterus has not expelled yet.

---

## Pearl

Most first-trimester spontaneous abortions are due to chromosomal abnormalities, especially aneuploidy (abnormal number of chromosomes).
