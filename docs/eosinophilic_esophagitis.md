# Eosinophilic esophagitis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8d/Illu_esophagus.jpg)

![Image](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/mmanual-ssq1ci05/media/professional/images/e/o/s/eosinophilic-esophagitis-rings-strictures-lynch-sized.jpg?mw=2048&sc_lang=en-us)

![Image](https://www.endoscopy-campus.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/KLassifikation_Bs_1_1-eosinophile-Oesophagitis.jpg)

Eosinophilic esophagitis is chronic allergic inflammation of the esophagus.

Break it down:

* eosinophilic = lots of eosinophils
* eosinophils = allergy/parasite-fighting white blood cells
* esophagitis = inflammation of the esophagus

So:

> eosinophils are attacking/inflaming the esophageal lining.

---

### Core idea

Usually triggered by food allergens.

The immune system reacts in the esophagus, causing eosinophils to build up in the mucosa (inner lining).

This causes:

* dysphagia = difficulty swallowing
* food impaction = food gets stuck
* chest discomfort
* reflux-like symptoms

---

### Why rings/strictures happen

Chronic inflammation makes the esophageal wall heal with fibrosis.

Fibrosis = scar tissue.

Scar tissue makes the tube:

* stiff
* narrow
* ringed

That is why endoscopy can show a "ringed esophagus" or "trachealization" (the esophagus starts looking like the trachea with rings).

---

### Diagnosis

Diagnosis is by endoscopy with biopsy.

Biopsy shows:

* increased eosinophils in the esophageal epithelium
* classically **>=15 eosinophils/HPF**

HPF = high-power field, meaning one microscope view at high magnification.

---

### Important association

Patients often have atopy.

Atopy = allergic tendency, like:

* asthma
* eczema
* allergic rhinitis
* food allergies

---

## One-line intuition

Eosinophilic esophagitis is basically a food-allergy-type immune reaction in the esophagus that makes it inflamed, stiff, and ringed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 loves: **dysphagia + food impaction + history of asthma/eczema/food allergies = think eosinophilic esophagitis.**
