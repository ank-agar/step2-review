### Core idea

Short stature means height is abnormally low for a person's:

* age
* sex
* population background

Medically, it usually means height is below the 3rd percentile or more than 2 SD (standard deviations) below the mean.

---

### Big Step 1 split

Think:

* proportionate short stature = whole body is small
* disproportionate short stature = some body parts are much shorter than others

Proportionate causes include:

* familial short stature
* constitutional growth delay
* GH (growth hormone) deficiency
* hypothyroidism
* chronic disease or malnutrition
* Turner syndrome

Disproportionate causes include:

* achondroplasia
* other skeletal dysplasias (abnormal bone/cartilage development disorders)

---

### Why growth slows

Linear growth mostly happens at the growth plate, also called the epiphyseal plate.

The growth plate makes cartilage first, then that cartilage is replaced by bone.

So if anything decreases:

* chondrocyte (cartilage cell) proliferation
* GH (growth hormone)
* IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1)
* thyroid hormone
* nutrition
* normal cartilage signaling

then the long bones do not lengthen normally.

That gives short stature.

---

### Classic examples

**GH deficiency:**

Low GH means low IGF-1, so the growth plate is not stimulated enough.

Result:

* proportionate short stature
* delayed bone age
* increased fat mass sometimes

**Hypothyroidism:**

Thyroid hormone is needed for normal growth and skeletal maturation.

Result:

* short stature
* delayed bone age
* fatigue, constipation, cold intolerance

**Turner syndrome:**

Turner syndrome = partial or complete loss of one X chromosome in a phenotypic female.

Classic:

* short stature
* webbed neck
* broad chest with widely spaced nipples
* streak ovaries
* coarctation of the aorta

**Achondroplasia:**

Achondroplasia is due to activating FGFR3 (fibroblast growth factor receptor 3).

FGFR3 normally puts the brakes on cartilage growth.

Activating mutation = too much braking at the growth plate.

Result:

* short limbs
* normal trunk size
* large head/frontal bossing
* disproportionate short stature

---

## One-line intuition

Short stature means the growth curve is too low, and the cause is usually either normal-family pattern, delayed puberty pattern, hormone/nutrition/chronic disease problem, or abnormal growth plate/cartilage problem.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Achondroplasia is AD (autosomal dominant), but most cases are new mutations in FGFR3 (fibroblast growth factor receptor 3), and patients usually have normal intelligence.
