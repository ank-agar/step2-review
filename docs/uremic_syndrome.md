### Core idea

Uremic syndrome means symptoms from buildup of uremic toxins (waste products normally cleared by the kidney) in severe kidney failure.

So the basic chain is:

* kidney fails
* blood is not filtered well
* urea and other toxins accumulate
* many organs get irritated/dysfunctional

---

### What you see

Common findings:

* nausea/vomiting
* anorexia (loss of appetite)
* fatigue
* confusion or encephalopathy (brain dysfunction)
* pruritus (itching)
* pericarditis (inflammation of the sac around the heart)
* platelet dysfunction causing bleeding tendency
* uremic frost in severe cases

Uremic frost = white crystals on skin from urea coming out in sweat, then drying.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Nausea/vomiting:** toxins irritate the gastrointestinal tract
* **Confusion:** toxins affect neurons in the brain
* **Pruritus:** retained waste products and phosphate irritate skin/nerves
* **Pericarditis:** toxins inflame the pericardium (heart lining)
* **Bleeding:** uremia causes platelet dysfunction, even if platelet count is normal
* **Uremic frost:** very high urea leaves through sweat and crystallizes on skin

---

### Important distinction

Uremic syndrome is not the same as HUS (hemolytic uremic syndrome).

HUS (hemolytic uremic syndrome) = microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (red blood cells shredded in small vessels) + thrombocytopenia (low platelets) + acute kidney injury.

Uremic syndrome = systemic symptoms from kidney failure toxin buildup.

---

## One-line intuition

The kidneys are supposed to clean the blood, so when they fail, the blood becomes chemically dirty and starts irritating the brain, gut, skin, heart lining, and platelets.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Uremic pericarditis is an indication for urgent dialysis because it means kidney failure toxins are severe enough to inflame the pericardium.
