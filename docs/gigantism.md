### Core idea

Gigantism = excess GH (growth hormone) before the epiphyseal plates (growth plates at the ends of long bones) close.

Most often due to a pituitary adenoma (benign tumor of the pituitary gland) secreting too much GH.

GH then increases IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1) from the liver.

IGF-1 is what actually drives a lot of the body growth.

---

### Why the patient gets very tall

In children, growth plates are still open.

So excess GH/IGF-1 keeps stimulating long bone growth:

* femur gets longer
* tibia gets longer
* humerus gets longer
* overall height increases a lot

That is why gigantism causes increased linear growth.

Linear growth = growth in height/length.

---

### Gigantism vs acromegaly

Same hormone problem:

* too much GH (growth hormone)
* too much IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1)

Difference is timing:

* **Before growth plate closure:** gigantism
* **After growth plate closure:** acromegaly

Once growth plates close, long bones cannot get longer.

So adults with GH excess do not usually get taller. Instead, they get enlargement of:

* hands
* feet
* jaw
* forehead
* soft tissues

---

### One-line intuition

Gigantism is basically “acromegaly before puberty finishes,” so the bones can still grow longer.

---

### Diagnosis idea

Think:

* very rapid growth
* very tall for age
* high IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1)
* GH (growth hormone) that does not suppress after oral glucose

Normally, glucose suppresses GH.

So if GH stays high after glucose, that suggests autonomous GH secretion, usually from a pituitary adenoma.

---

### Treatment idea

Main treatment is usually transsphenoidal surgery (surgery through the sphenoid sinus to reach the pituitary gland) to remove the pituitary adenoma.

Other options can include:

* somatostatin analogs, like octreotide, which decrease GH release
* GH receptor antagonists, like pegvisomant, which block GH action
* dopamine agonists, like cabergoline, in some cases

---

## Pearl 🔥

GH (growth hormone) acts partly by increasing IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1), so **IGF-1 is the better screening test** because it is more stable throughout the day than GH, which is pulsatile.
