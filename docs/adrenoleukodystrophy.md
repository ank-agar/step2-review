## Adrenoleukodystrophy

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/ea27/11271253/c83df88f2cf5/nihms-2005969-f0001.jpg)

![Image](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/864358/xml-images/fnut-09-864358-g001.webp)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ee/Adrenoleukodystrophy.jpg)

Adrenoleukodystrophy means:

* **adreno-** = adrenal glands
* **leuko-** = white matter
* **dystrophy** = abnormal degeneration

So the name literally tells you:

> adrenal gland problems + white matter degeneration.

Most Step 1 relevant form is **X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy**.

---

### Core defect

Mutation in **ABCD1** gene causes defective **ALDP** (adrenoleukodystrophy protein).

ALDP normally helps move **VLCFAs** (very long-chain fatty acids) into **peroxisomes** (organelles that break down very long fatty acids).

If ALDP is defective:

* VLCFAs cannot be broken down well
* VLCFAs accumulate
* myelin gets damaged
* adrenal cortex gets damaged

---

### Why the brain is affected

**Myelin** is the fatty insulation around nerves.

VLCFAs build up in myelin and destabilize it, causing **demyelination** (loss of myelin).

That leads to:

* behavioral changes
* cognitive decline
* vision/hearing problems
* seizures
* progressive neurologic deterioration

Intuition:

The brain wiring loses its insulation, so signals slow down or fail.

---

### Why adrenal insufficiency happens

The **adrenal cortex** (outer adrenal gland layer that makes steroid hormones) is also sensitive to VLCFA buildup.

Damage there can cause **primary adrenal insufficiency** (adrenal gland itself cannot make enough cortisol/aldosterone).

Findings can include:

* fatigue
* weight loss
* hypotension
* hyperpigmentation
* salt craving

Hyperpigmentation happens because low cortisol causes high **ACTH** (adrenocorticotropic hormone), and ACTH can stimulate melanocytes (pigment-producing cells).

---

### Inheritance

Usually **X-linked recessive**.

So it classically affects boys because they only have one X chromosome.

Females can be carriers and may have milder adult symptoms due to **X-inactivation** (random silencing of one X chromosome in each cell).

---

## One-line intuition

Adrenoleukodystrophy is a peroxisomal fatty acid breakdown problem where VLCFAs build up and damage myelin + adrenal cortex.

---

## HY pearl

Think:

**X-linked + peroxisome disorder + VLCFA accumulation + adrenal insufficiency + demyelination = adrenoleukodystrophy.**
