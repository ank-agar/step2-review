# Hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer

![Image](https://nci-media.cancer.gov/pdq/media/images/733730.jpg)

![Image](https://visualsonline.cancer.gov/retrieve.cfm?dpi=300&fileformat=jpg&imageid=9843)

Hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer, also called **Lynch syndrome**, is an inherited cancer syndrome that increases risk of:

* colorectal cancer
* endometrial cancer
* ovarian cancer
* stomach/small bowel cancers
* urinary tract cancers

Break down the name:

* **hereditary** = inherited
* **nonpolyposis** = not thousands of polyps
* **colorectal cancer** = cancer of colon/rectum

So the key idea is:

> inherited colon cancer risk without massive polyp burden.

---

### Core mechanism

Lynch syndrome is due to mutations in **DNA mismatch repair genes**.

Common genes:

* **MLH1**
* **MSH2**
* **MSH6**
* **PMS2**
* sometimes **EPCAM** deletion

**MMR (mismatch repair)** normally fixes small DNA copying mistakes.

If MMR is broken:

* DNA replication errors accumulate
* especially in repeated DNA regions called **microsatellites**
* this causes **MSI (microsatellite instability)**
* mutations build up faster
* cancer develops earlier

---

### Why it causes cancer

Think of MMR as the cell’s spell-checker.

Normally:

* DNA gets copied
* mistakes happen
* MMR fixes them

In Lynch syndrome:

* one bad MMR gene copy is inherited
* the second copy gets knocked out later in a colon/endometrial cell
* now the cell cannot fix DNA copying errors
* mutations pile up
* cancer risk increases

This is a **two-hit tumor suppressor** pattern.

---

### Why "nonpolyposis" matters

This contrasts with **FAP (familial adenomatous polyposis)**.

FAP:

* APC gene mutation
* hundreds to thousands of colon polyps

Lynch syndrome:

* MMR gene mutation
* few polyps
* but polyps can progress to cancer faster

So Lynch does not mean "no polyps ever."

It means:

> colon cancer without the massive carpet of polyps seen in FAP.

---

### Classic clues

Think Lynch when you see:

* colon cancer at young age
* right-sided colon cancer
* family history across generations
* colon cancer + endometrial cancer in same family
* MSI-high tumor

**Autosomal dominant** means one mutated copy can be passed from an affected parent to a child.

---

## One-line intuition

Lynch syndrome is inherited loss of DNA spell-checking, causing early colorectal and endometrial cancers without tons of colon polyps.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Lynch syndrome = **MMR defect → MSI (microsatellite instability)**, while FAP = **APC mutation → hundreds/thousands of polyps**.
