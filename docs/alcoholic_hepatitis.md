## Alcoholic hepatitis

Alcoholic hepatitis = liver inflammation caused by heavy alcohol use.

Break the term apart:

* alcoholic = due to alcohol
* hepatitis = inflammation of the liver

So this is not just “fat in the liver.”

It is:

* fat buildup in hepatocytes (liver cells)
* hepatocyte injury/death
* inflammation, especially neutrophils (acute inflammatory white blood cells)

Classic histology:

* ballooning degeneration = swollen, injured hepatocytes
* Mallory-Denk bodies = damaged cytokeratin intermediate filaments clumped inside hepatocytes
* neutrophilic infiltrate = neutrophils surrounding injured liver cells

Why alcohol causes this:

Alcohol is metabolized in the liver into acetaldehyde, which is toxic.

Acetaldehyde + oxidative stress damage hepatocytes, so the immune system responds with inflammation.

Also, alcohol metabolism increases NADH (reduced nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), which shifts metabolism toward fat production and fat trapping in the liver.

That is why alcohol can cause:

* fatty liver first
* alcoholic hepatitis next
* fibrosis/cirrhosis later

Typical presentation:

* jaundice = yellow skin/eyes from high bilirubin
* RUQ pain = right upper quadrant abdominal pain
* fever
* tender hepatomegaly = enlarged painful liver

Labs:

* AST (aspartate aminotransferase) > ALT (alanine aminotransferase)
* often AST:ALT ratio > 2
* elevated bilirubin
* elevated GGT (gamma-glutamyl transferase), which supports alcohol-related liver injury

Why AST > ALT?

Alcohol damages mitochondria, and AST is found in mitochondria. Alcohol use can also cause vitamin B6 deficiency, which lowers ALT activity more.

One-line intuition:

Alcohol poisons hepatocytes, injured hepatocytes swell and accumulate damaged protein clumps, and neutrophils rush in to inflame the liver.

HY pearl 🔥
Mallory-Denk bodies are eosinophilic (pink-staining) cytokeratin intermediate filament aggregates classically associated with alcoholic hepatitis, but they are not perfectly specific because they can also appear in NASH (nonalcoholic steatohepatitis).
