### Core idea

Female infertility = inability to get pregnant after:

* 12 months of regular unprotected sex
* or 6 months if age 35 or older

Basically, pregnancy needs:

* ovulation = egg released
* open fallopian tubes = sperm and egg can meet
* receptive uterus/endometrium = embryo can implant

If any step fails, infertility can happen.

---

### Main causes

* **Ovulatory dysfunction:** no egg is released regularly
  * classic cause: PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)
* **Tubal blockage:** egg and sperm cannot meet
  * often from PID (pelvic inflammatory disease), usually due to Chlamydia trachomatis or Neisseria gonorrhoeae
* **Endometriosis:** endometrial-like tissue outside the uterus causes inflammation/scarring
* **Uterine problems:** fibroids, adhesions, or abnormal uterine shape can impair implantation
* **Decreased ovarian reserve:** fewer remaining eggs, often age-related

---

### Why the findings happen

* **PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome):** high androgens (male-type sex hormones) disrupt follicle maturation, so ovulation becomes irregular.
* **PID (pelvic inflammatory disease):** infection scars the fallopian tubes, so sperm cannot reach the egg.
* **Endometriosis:** ectopic endometrial tissue bleeds/inflames cyclically, causing adhesions that distort pelvic anatomy.
* **Older age:** oocyte (egg cell) number and quality decline, so fertilization and implantation become less likely.

---

### Tests you may see

* **Pregnancy test first:** rule out pregnancy.
* **TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) and prolactin:** endocrine problems can suppress ovulation.
* **FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone):** high FSH can suggest low ovarian reserve.
* **HSG (hysterosalpingogram):** dye study to see if fallopian tubes are open.

---

## One-line intuition

Female infertility is usually a problem with egg release, tube transport, or uterine implantation.
