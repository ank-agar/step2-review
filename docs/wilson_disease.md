### Core idea

Wilson disease is an autosomal recessive (need 2 bad copies) disorder where the body cannot get rid of copper properly.

The defect is in:

* ATP7B (copper-transporting ATPase beta)

ATP7B normally helps the liver:

* put copper into ceruloplasmin (the main copper-carrying protein in blood)
* excrete extra copper into bile

So if ATP7B is broken:

* copper cannot leave through bile
* copper does not get loaded well onto ceruloplasmin
* copper builds up in liver, brain, and eyes

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Low ceruloplasmin:** copper is not loaded onto ceruloplasmin properly, so ceruloplasmin gets degraded
* **Liver disease:** copper accumulates in hepatocytes (liver cells) and causes toxic damage
* **Neurologic symptoms:** copper deposits in basal ganglia (movement-control brain region), causing tremor, dystonia (abnormal muscle contractions), parkinsonism (slow movement/rigidity), and psychiatric symptoms
* **Kayser-Fleischer rings:** copper deposits in Descemet membrane (deep corneal layer) around the iris
* **Hemolytic anemia:** free copper can damage red blood cells, causing them to break apart

---

### Classic clues

Think:

* young patient
* liver disease
* neuro/psych symptoms
* Kayser-Fleischer rings
* low ceruloplasmin
* increased urinary copper

Break the name intuition:

* **hepato-** = liver
* **lenticular** = lentiform nucleus/basal ganglia region
* **degeneration** = tissue damage

So hepatolenticular degeneration means:

> copper damages the liver + basal ganglia.

---

### Treatment

Main treatments:

* penicillamine or trientine = copper chelators (bind copper so it can be excreted)
* zinc = blocks intestinal copper absorption

---

## One-line intuition

Wilson disease is a copper disposal problem: the liver cannot put copper into bile, so copper backs up into the liver, brain, and cornea.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Wilson disease is one of the big causes of liver disease + movement disorder in a young person, and the Step 1 buzzword is **low ceruloplasmin with Kayser-Fleischer rings**.
