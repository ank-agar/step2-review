## Adrenal adenoma

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/88/Adrenal_Gland_detail_-_English.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/50/Adrenal_Cortex_Pathology.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/22/CT_of_adrenal_adenoma.jpg)

An adrenal adenoma is a benign tumor of the adrenal cortex (outer steroid-producing layer of the adrenal gland).

Break it down:

* adrenal = adrenal gland, which sits on top of the kidney
* adenoma = benign glandular tumor

So:

> adrenal adenoma = benign growth from adrenal gland cells.

---

### Core idea

Most adrenal adenomas are:

* small
* benign
* found incidentally on imaging

That is why they are often called adrenal incidentalomas (unexpected adrenal masses found on imaging done for another reason).

---

### Why symptoms can happen

The adrenal cortex normally makes steroid hormones.

So an adenoma can be:

* nonfunctional = does not secrete excess hormone
* functional = secretes too much hormone

If functional, it can cause:

* too much cortisol -> Cushing syndrome
* too much aldosterone -> Conn syndrome, aka primary hyperaldosteronism
* too much androgen -> virilization (development of male-pattern features)

---

### High-yield hormone patterns

Too much cortisol causes:

* central obesity
* thin skin/easy bruising
* proximal muscle weakness
* hypertension
* hyperglycemia

Too much aldosterone causes:

* hypertension
* hypokalemia (low potassium)
* metabolic alkalosis (high blood pH from increased bicarbonate)

Why?

Aldosterone makes the kidney reabsorb sodium and water, which raises blood pressure. In exchange, the kidney dumps potassium and hydrogen ions, causing hypokalemia and metabolic alkalosis.

---

## One-line intuition

An adrenal adenoma is usually a benign adrenal cortex bump, but if it makes hormones, it acts like a tiny autonomous steroid factory.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 distinction:

* adrenal cortical adenoma -> steroid hormone symptoms, like cortisol or aldosterone excess
* pheochromocytoma -> adrenal medulla tumor that makes catecholamines (epinephrine/norepinephrine), causing episodic headache, sweating, palpitations, and hypertension
