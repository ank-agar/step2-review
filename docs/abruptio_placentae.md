# Abruptio placentae

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/77/Blausen_0737_PlacentalAbruption.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/Placental_Abruption_%2852079142525%29.jpg/960px-Placental_Abruption_%2852079142525%29.jpg)

Abruptio placentae = **placental abruption**.

It means the placenta separates from the uterine wall **before delivery**.

Break it down:

* abruptio = tearing away / separation
* placentae = of the placenta

So the key idea is:

> the placenta detaches too early.

---

### Why this is dangerous

The placenta is how the fetus gets:

* oxygen
* nutrients
* blood flow support

If it peels off the uterus early, blood collects behind the placenta, forming a **retroplacental hematoma** (blood clot behind the placenta).

That causes:

* **painful vaginal bleeding**
* **uterine tenderness/rigidity**
* **fetal distress** because oxygen delivery drops
* possible maternal hemorrhage

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* third-trimester bleeding
* painful bleeding
* tender, firm uterus
* fetal heart rate abnormalities

This is different from **placenta previa** (placenta covering/near the cervical opening), which classically causes **painless** vaginal bleeding.

---

### Why DIC can happen

DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation) = widespread clotting that uses up platelets and clotting factors, causing bleeding.

In placental abruption, damaged placenta/decidua releases tissue factor into maternal blood.

Tissue factor activates clotting everywhere, so the body burns through clotting factors.

Result:

* low fibrinogen
* bleeding
* shock risk

---

### Risk factors

Big ones:

* hypertension
* preeclampsia (pregnancy hypertension with end-organ findings like proteinuria)
* cocaine use
* smoking
* abdominal trauma
* prior placental abruption

---

## One-line intuition

The placenta is supposed to stay attached until birth; in abruptio placentae, it peels off early, so the baby loses oxygen supply and the mom can bleed heavily.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Painful third-trimester bleeding = think **placental abruption**; painless third-trimester bleeding = think **placenta previa**.
