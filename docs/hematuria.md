## Hematuria

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/79/Sources_of_Hematuria_by_Anatomic_Location.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e9/HematuriaGross.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Hematuria_Microscopy.jpg)

Hematuria means **blood in the urine**.

Break it down:

* heme = blood
* -uria = urine

So:

> hematuria = red blood cells in urine

---

### Two main types

* **Gross hematuria:** blood is visible, so urine looks pink/red/brown
* **Microscopic hematuria:** urine looks normal, but red blood cells are seen on urinalysis/microscopy

**Urinalysis** means lab testing of urine.

---

### Where can the blood come from?

Blood can enter anywhere along the urinary tract:

* kidney
* ureter
* bladder
* urethra
* prostate in males

Intuition:

The urinary tract is basically a drainage pipe system. If any part of that system is irritated, inflamed, infected, injured, or invaded by tumor, red blood cells can leak into the urine.

---

### Common causes

Think:

* **UTI (urinary tract infection):** inflamed bladder/urethra bleeds a little
* **Kidney stone:** sharp stone scrapes the urinary tract
* **Glomerulonephritis:** inflamed glomeruli leak red blood cells
* **Trauma:** direct injury causes bleeding
* **Tumor:** abnormal tissue can bleed into urine

**Glomeruli** are the tiny kidney filtering units.

---

### Big Step 1 distinction

If hematuria is from the **glomerulus**, you often see:

* red blood cell casts
* proteinuria
* dysmorphic red blood cells

**Proteinuria** means protein in the urine.

**Dysmorphic** means abnormally shaped.

Why?

Because red blood cells are being forced through a damaged kidney filter, so they get squeezed/deformed and can clump into casts inside renal tubules.

---

## One-line intuition

Hematuria means red blood cells got into the urine somewhere between the kidney filter and the urethral exit.

---

## Pearl 🔥

**Red blood cell casts** strongly point to a **glomerular** source of hematuria, especially glomerulonephritis.
