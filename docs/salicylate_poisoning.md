Salicylate poisoning = toxicity from aspirin or other salicylates.

Classic Step 1 clue:

* tinnitus
* nausea/vomiting
* fever
* hyperventilation
* mixed acid-base disorder

---

### Core acid-base idea

Salicylates cause **both**:

* respiratory alkalosis
* anion gap metabolic acidosis

Respiratory alkalosis means low carbon dioxide from breathing too much.

Anion gap metabolic acidosis means acid buildup in the blood with an increased anion gap (unmeasured acids).

---

### Why respiratory alkalosis happens first

Salicylates directly stimulate the medullary respiratory center.

Medulla = brainstem area that controls breathing.

So the patient hyperventilates.

Hyperventilation blows off carbon dioxide.

Less carbon dioxide means less acid in blood, so pH goes up.

That is **respiratory alkalosis**.

---

### Why metabolic acidosis happens later

Salicylates uncouple oxidative phosphorylation.

Oxidative phosphorylation = mitochondrial process that makes ATP (cell energy).

Uncoupling means:

* mitochondria burn fuel
* but do not efficiently make ATP
* energy is wasted as heat

So you get:

* fever/hyperthermia
* increased lactic acid
* increased ketoacids
* anion gap metabolic acidosis

---

## One-line intuition

Aspirin overdose makes you breathe too much early, then poisons mitochondria and builds up acids later.

---

## Treatment idea

Give sodium bicarbonate.

Why?

* alkalinizes blood and urine
* traps salicylate in ionized form
* ionized salicylate crosses into the central nervous system (CNS, brain and spinal cord) less easily
* ionized salicylate is excreted in urine more easily

Severe cases may need hemodialysis (machine filtering of blood).

---

## HY pearl

Salicylate poisoning is a classic cause of **mixed respiratory alkalosis + anion gap metabolic acidosis**.

If you see tinnitus + hyperventilation + weird acid-base labs, think aspirin toxicity.
