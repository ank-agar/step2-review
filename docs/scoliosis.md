### Core idea

Scoliosis = abnormal **side-to-side curvature of the spine**, usually with some **rotation** of the vertebrae (spinal bones).

So it is not just “bad posture.”

The spine actually curves laterally, often making an:

* S-shape
* C-shape

---

### Why the body looks uneven

Because the vertebrae rotate, they pull the ribs and trunk with them.

That can cause:

* uneven shoulders
* uneven hips
* one shoulder blade sticking out more
* rib hump on forward bending

The rib hump happens because the rotated spine twists the rib cage backward on one side.

---

### How it is measured

Doctors measure scoliosis with the **Cobb angle** (angle used on X-ray to measure spinal curve severity).

Basic idea:

* bigger Cobb angle = bigger spinal curve
* **10 degrees or more** is usually considered scoliosis

---

### Common causes/types

Most common:

* **adolescent idiopathic scoliosis** = scoliosis in teenagers with no clear cause

Idiopathic means the cause is unknown.

Other causes:

* congenital vertebral defects (abnormal spine formation before birth)
* neuromuscular disease (nerve or muscle disorders), like cerebral palsy
* connective tissue disorders

---

## One-line intuition

Scoliosis is the spine bending sideways and rotating, so the back/ribs/shoulders can become asymmetric.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: scoliosis can be associated with **neurofibromatosis type 1** (NF1; genetic disorder with cafe-au-lait spots, neurofibromas, and Lisch nodules).
