## Undescended testicle

![Image](https://content.healthwise.net/resources/14.8/en-us/media/medical/hw/h9991609_001_2.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279106/bin/cryptorchid-hypospad-Image001.jpg)

![Image](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/images/f/f6/Cryptorchidism.jpg)

An **undescended testicle** = **cryptorchidism** (a testis that did not move down into the scrotum).

Normally, the testes form high up in the abdomen during fetal life, then descend through the inguinal canal into the scrotum.

If descent stops early, the testicle may stay in the:

* abdomen
* inguinal canal
* just above the scrotum

---

### Core idea

The testicle is stuck somewhere along its normal migration path.

So instead of sitting in the cooler scrotum, it remains higher up in the body.

---

### Why this matters

The scrotum is cooler than the abdomen, and sperm-producing cells are temperature-sensitive.

So an undescended testicle has increased risk of:

* infertility
* testicular cancer
* testicular torsion (twisting of the spermatic cord)
* inguinal hernia

Reasoning:

* **Infertility:** warmer body temperature damages sperm production
* **Cancer risk:** abnormal development/location increases risk of germ cell tumor
* **Torsion:** abnormal position means the testis may be less securely anchored
* **Hernia:** failed descent can be associated with a patent processus vaginalis (persistent embryologic connection between abdomen and scrotum)

---

### Important distinction

Do not confuse with a **retractile testis**.

* **Undescended testis:** cannot stay down in the scrotum
* **Retractile testis:** can be pulled into the scrotum, but the cremasteric reflex pulls it back up

The **cremasteric reflex** is when stroking the inner thigh causes the testicle to rise.

---

### Treatment idea

Treatment is usually **orchiopexy** (surgery to move and fix the testicle into the scrotum).

This helps fertility potential and makes the testicle easier to examine for cancer later.

---

## One-line intuition

An undescended testicle is a testis that got stuck on the way down, and staying too warm increases infertility and cancer risk.

---

## HY pearl

Cryptorchidism is associated with increased risk of **testicular germ cell tumor**, especially **seminoma**.
