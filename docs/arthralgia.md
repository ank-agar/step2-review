### Core idea

**Arthralgia = joint pain.**

Break it down:

* **arthro-** = joint
* **-algia** = pain

So arthralgia literally means:

> pain in a joint.

---

### Key distinction

**Arthralgia** is a symptom.

**Arthritis** means joint inflammation.

So:

* arthralgia = joint hurts
* arthritis = joint is inflamed, usually with swelling/warmth/stiffness

You can have arthralgia without arthritis.

---

### Why joint pain happens

Pain can come from irritation of structures around the joint, like:

* joint capsule
* synovium (joint lining)
* ligaments
* tendons
* nearby bone

Cartilage itself usually does **not** hurt much because it has poor innervation (nerve supply).

That is why osteoarthritis (degenerative joint disease) hurts when cartilage loss causes stress on nearby bone and soft tissues.

---

### Common causes

Arthralgia can be caused by:

* viral infection
* autoimmune disease
* osteoarthritis
* rheumatoid arthritis
* gout
* trauma/overuse
* medication side effects

---

## One-line intuition

Arthralgia just means the joint hurts; arthritis means the joint is actually inflamed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, **parvovirus B19** can cause symmetric arthralgia/arthritis in adults, especially women, and can mimic rheumatoid arthritis.
