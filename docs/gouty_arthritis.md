### Core idea

Gouty arthritis is joint inflammation caused by deposition of monosodium urate crystals (crystals made from uric acid) in the joint.

Classic presentation:

* sudden severe joint pain
* red, hot, swollen joint
* usually the 1st metatarsophalangeal joint, meaning the big toe joint

This big toe gout attack is called podagra (gout of the big toe).

---

### Why it happens

Uric acid is the breakdown product of purines (DNA/RNA building blocks).

If uric acid gets too high in blood, it can crystallize.

Those sharp needle-like crystals deposit in joints and trigger inflammation.

So the sequence is:

* ↑ uric acid
* monosodium urate crystal deposition
* macrophages eat the crystals
* NLRP3 inflammasome activation (innate immune inflammatory complex)
* IL-1β release (interleukin-1 beta, a pro-inflammatory cytokine)
* painful neutrophil-rich arthritis

---

### Why the big toe?

Urate crystals form more easily in cooler areas.

The big toe is far from the body core, so it is relatively cold.

That is why gout loves the 1st metatarsophalangeal joint.

---

### Diagnosis

Joint aspiration shows:

* needle-shaped crystals
* negatively birefringent crystals under polarized light

Birefringent means the crystals change color/brightness under polarized light.

Negative birefringence in gout:

* yellow when parallel
* blue when perpendicular

---

### Chronic gout

Long-standing gout can form tophi (chalky deposits of monosodium urate crystals).

Tophi can cause:

* joint deformity
* bone erosions
* chronic arthritis

---

## One-line intuition

Gout is basically sharp uric acid crystals in the joint causing an intense innate immune reaction.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do not rule out gout just because serum uric acid is normal during an acute flare; the best diagnosis is synovial fluid (joint fluid) showing negatively birefringent needle-shaped crystals.
