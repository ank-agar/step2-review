### Core idea

Graft-versus-host disease, or GVHD (graft-versus-host disease), is when immune cells from the donor graft attack the recipient host.

Most classic setting:

* allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant, or HSCT (stem cell transplant from another person)
* especially bone marrow transplant

So the problem is:

> donor T cells see the patient’s tissues as foreign and attack them.

---

### Why this happens

Break the name apart:

* graft = transplanted donor tissue/cells
* versus = attacking
* host = recipient patient

This is the opposite direction of regular transplant rejection.

Regular rejection:

* host immune system attacks graft

GVHD:

* graft immune cells attack host

The key cells are donor T lymphocytes (T cells, immune cells that recognize antigens).

---

### Main organs hit

GVHD especially attacks epithelial surfaces, meaning barrier tissues lining the body.

Classic targets:

* skin
* gastrointestinal tract, or GI tract (digestive tract)
* liver

So symptoms can include:

* maculopapular rash (flat and raised red skin lesions)
* diarrhea
* abdominal pain
* jaundice (yellow skin/eyes from bilirubin buildup)
* elevated liver enzymes

---

### Why skin, gut, and liver?

These tissues are exposed to lots of inflammatory signals after transplant conditioning.

Conditioning means the chemo/radiation given before transplant.

That tissue injury releases cytokines (immune signaling proteins), which activate antigen-presenting cells, or APCs (cells that show antigens to T cells).

Then donor T cells get activated and start killing host tissues.

Simple chain:

* chemo/radiation damages host tissue
* cytokines and APCs activate donor T cells
* donor T cells attack host skin, gut, and liver

---

### Acute vs chronic

Acute GVHD:

* usually earlier after transplant
* classically skin rash, diarrhea, liver injury
* T-cell driven inflammation

Chronic GVHD:

* later and more autoimmune-like
* can cause dry eyes, dry mouth, skin thickening, lung disease, and liver disease

Autoimmune-like means the immune system behaves as if the patient’s own tissues are foreign.

---

## One-line intuition

GVHD is donor immune cells waking up inside the recipient and attacking the recipient’s body.

---

## Pearl

GVHD is a type IV hypersensitivity reaction (delayed T-cell-mediated immune reaction), because donor T cells directly damage host tissues.
