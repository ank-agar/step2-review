# Collagenous colitis

![Image](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/-/media/ksw-images/mechanismofinjuryincollagenouscolitis290489.jpg)

![Image](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/-/media/ksw-images/pathogenesisofdiarrheaincollagenousandlymphocyticcolitisanormal.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/Histopathology_of_collagenous_colitis.jpg)

Collagenous colitis is a type of microscopic colitis, meaning:

* colonoscopy often looks normal
* but biopsy under the microscope shows inflammation

The key finding is a thick collagen band under the surface epithelium.

Break it down:

* collagenous = collagen buildup
* colitis = inflammation of the colon

So:

> inflammation of the colon with excess collagen deposited right under the lining.

### Why it causes watery diarrhea

Normally, the colon absorbs water from stool.

In collagenous colitis:

* inflammation damages the colonic epithelium
* collagen builds up under the epithelium
* water absorption becomes impaired
* inflammatory mediators increase secretion into the lumen

Result:

* chronic watery diarrhea
* usually non-bloody diarrhea
* crampy abdominal pain can happen

Why no blood?

Because the mucosa is usually not deeply ulcerated. This is not like ulcerative colitis, where the lining can be visibly ulcerated and bloody.

### Diagnosis

You diagnose it with colon biopsy.

Classic biopsy finding:

* thickened subepithelial collagen band

Subepithelial means under the epithelium, which is the surface lining of the colon.

### One-line intuition

Collagenous colitis is "normal-looking colonoscopy, watery diarrhea, biopsy shows a thick collagen layer under the colon lining."

### Pearl

Microscopic colitis has 2 main types:

* collagenous colitis = thick subepithelial collagen band
* lymphocytic colitis = increased intraepithelial lymphocytes

Intraepithelial lymphocytes means lymphocytes, which are immune cells, sitting inside the epithelial lining.
