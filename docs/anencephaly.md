### Core idea

Anencephaly is a severe neural tube defect where the baby is missing a major part of the:

* brain
* skull
* scalp

It happens because the cranial neuropore (head-end opening of the early neural tube) fails to close.

---

### Timing

The neural tube normally closes very early:

* around week 4 of embryologic development
* classically days 23-26

So anencephaly is an early pregnancy developmental problem.

---

### Why the findings happen

The neural tube is supposed to become the central nervous system (brain and spinal cord).

If the head-end part does not close:

* the forebrain does not form normally
* the skull vault does not form over it
* brain tissue is exposed and degenerates

That is why anencephaly causes absent cranial vault and absent/poorly developed brain tissue.

---

### Labs/screening

Anencephaly causes increased maternal serum AFP (alpha-fetoprotein).

Why?

* AFP (alpha-fetoprotein) is made by the fetus
* the open neural tube defect lets fetal proteins leak into amniotic fluid
* then AFP rises in maternal blood

So:

> open defect = more AFP leaks out.

---

### Risk factor

Low folate (vitamin B9) increases risk of neural tube defects.

That is why folic acid supplementation before pregnancy and early in pregnancy helps prevent:

* anencephaly
* spina bifida

---

## One-line intuition

The "head end" of the neural tube fails to close, so the brain and skull roof never develop properly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Anencephaly is usually lethal, and on Step 1 the classic association is increased AFP (alpha-fetoprotein) due to an open neural tube defect.
