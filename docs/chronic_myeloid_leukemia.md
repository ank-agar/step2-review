# Chronic myeloid leukemia

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c6/Schematic_of_the_Philadelphia_Chromosome.svg/960px-Schematic_of_the_Philadelphia_Chromosome.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fc/Chronic_Myeloid_Leukemia_smear_2009-04-09.JPG/960px-Chronic_Myeloid_Leukemia_smear_2009-04-09.JPG)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/1445.jpg)

Chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) is a cancer of myeloid white blood cell precursors.

Break it down:

* chronic = slower onset, more mature cells
* myeloid = granulocyte lineage, especially neutrophils, basophils, and eosinophils
* leukemia = malignant white blood cells in blood/bone marrow

Classic cause:

* t(9;22) translocation (chromosome 9 and chromosome 22 swap pieces)
* creates the Philadelphia chromosome
* makes the BCR (breakpoint cluster region)-ABL (Abelson tyrosine kinase) fusion gene

BCR-ABL is a constitutively active tyrosine kinase (an enzyme that keeps adding phosphate signals).

So the cell gets a constant "grow and divide" signal.

---

### Why the findings happen

* Very high white blood cell count: myeloid cells keep proliferating
* Left shift: lots of immature granulocytes spill into blood
* Basophilia: basophils are myeloid-lineage cells, so they also increase
* Splenomegaly: spleen filters the excess abnormal blood cells and may restart blood formation outside the marrow
* Fatigue/weight loss/night sweats: high cell turnover and malignancy cytokines (inflammatory signaling proteins)

---

### Big test clue

CML often has:

* high white blood cell count
* left-shifted granulocytes
* increased basophils
* low leukocyte alkaline phosphatase (LAP) score

LAP (leukocyte alkaline phosphatase) is low because these neutrophils are clonal (copied from one cancer cell) cells, not reactive neutrophils responding normally to infection.

---

## One-line intuition

CML is basically a chromosome 9 and 22 swap that creates BCR-ABL, which leaves the myeloid cell growth switch stuck ON.

---

## Pearl

Treat CML with tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) like imatinib, which block the BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase.
