# Hemoptysis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Hemoptysis.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Tuberculosis_image1.jpg)

Hemoptysis means coughing up blood from the respiratory tract (airways/lungs).

Break the word apart:

* hemo- = blood
* -ptysis = spitting/coughing up

So hemoptysis = blood coming out with a cough.

Important distinction:

* hemoptysis = blood from lungs/airways
* hematemesis = vomiting blood from the GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)

---

### Why it happens

Blood vessels in the airway or lung tissue get irritated, inflamed, invaded, or damaged.

Then coughing pushes that blood out through the mouth.

Common causes:

* bronchitis = inflamed bronchi (large airways)
* pneumonia = infected lung tissue
* TB (tuberculosis) = chronic infection that can cavitate/erode vessels
* lung cancer = tumor can invade airway blood vessels
* PE (pulmonary embolism) = clot in pulmonary artery causing infarction/bleeding
* bronchiectasis = permanently dilated airways that bleed easily

---

### What it looks like

Usually:

* blood-streaked sputum
* frothy red sputum
* coughing before blood comes out

This helps separate it from hematemesis, where there is usually nausea/vomiting and dark “coffee-ground” blood.

---

## One-line intuition

Hemoptysis is airway bleeding that gets coughed out.

---

## Pearl

Massive hemoptysis is dangerous mainly because the patient can drown/aspirate blood into the airway, not just because of blood loss.
