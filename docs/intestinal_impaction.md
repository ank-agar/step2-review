## Intestinal impaction

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17840/N00912-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/Journal.pmed.1000092.g002_fecal_impaction.png)

Intestinal impaction usually means fecal impaction:

> a hard, stuck mass of stool in the colon or rectum that cannot be passed normally.

Think:

* constipation gets severe
* stool sits too long
* colon absorbs too much water
* stool becomes dry/hard
* hard stool gets stuck

---

### Why it happens

The colon normally absorbs water from stool.

If stool moves slowly through the GI tract (gastrointestinal tract), it spends extra time in the colon, so:

* more water gets absorbed
* stool gets drier
* stool gets harder
* the rectum/colon cannot push it out effectively

So the core mechanism is:

> slow transit + water absorption = hard stool plug.

---

### Classic symptoms

* abdominal pain or bloating
* constipation
* rectal pressure/fullness
* straining
* nausea/vomiting if severe obstruction occurs
* overflow diarrhea

Overflow diarrhea is high-yield:

> liquid stool leaks around the hard stool mass, so it can look like diarrhea even though the real problem is constipation.

---

### Common causes/risk factors

* chronic constipation
* opioids
* anticholinergic drugs
* dehydration
* low fiber diet
* immobility
* neurologic disease affecting bowel motility
* elderly patients

---

### Diagnosis/treatment idea

Diagnosis is often by DRE (digital rectal exam), where you can feel hard stool in the rectum.

Treatment is disimpaction, meaning removal/breakup of the stool:

* enemas
* stool softeners/laxatives
* manual disimpaction if reachable

If there are severe obstruction signs, fever, peritoneal signs, or concern for perforation, that becomes urgent.

---

## One-line intuition

Intestinal impaction is basically constipation that has hardened into a stool plug.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Opioids can cause fecal impaction because they activate mu opioid receptors in the gut, which decreases peristalsis (wave-like intestinal muscle contractions that move stool forward).
