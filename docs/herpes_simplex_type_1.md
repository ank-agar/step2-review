### Core idea

Herpes simplex virus type 1, or HSV-1 (herpes simplex virus type 1), is an enveloped double-stranded DNA virus that classically causes:

* cold sores / fever blisters around the mouth
* gingivostomatitis (painful inflammation of gums and mouth)
* keratitis (corneal infection)
* encephalitis (brain inflammation), especially temporal lobe

It can also cause genital herpes, but HSV-2 (herpes simplex virus type 2) is classically more associated with genital lesions.

---

### Why it recurs

HSV-1 infects epithelial cells first, causing painful grouped vesicles.

Then it travels up sensory nerves and becomes latent in the trigeminal ganglion.

Latent means:

> the virus is hiding quietly inside nerve cells without actively making lots of new virus.

Later, stress, fever, sunlight, illness, or immunosuppression can reactivate it.

That is why cold sores tend to come back in the same general area.

---

### What the lesions look like

Classic description:

* painful grouped vesicles on an erythematous base

Erythematous means red/inflamed.

Vesicles means small fluid-filled blisters.

So the image in your head should be:

> little painful blisters sitting on a red base near the lip.

---

### Why acyclovir works

Acyclovir is activated by viral thymidine kinase.

Thymidine kinase is a viral enzyme that helps phosphorylate, or activate, the drug.

Once activated, acyclovir inhibits viral DNA polymerase.

DNA polymerase means the enzyme that copies DNA.

So:

> HSV activates its own poison, and then the drug blocks viral DNA replication.

This is why acyclovir is selective for infected cells.

---

### Diagnosis clue

A Tzanck smear can show multinucleated giant cells.

Multinucleated means one large fused cell with multiple nuclei.

But Tzanck smear cannot distinguish HSV-1 from HSV-2 or VZV (varicella-zoster virus).

PCR (polymerase chain reaction) is more specific because it detects viral DNA.

---

## One-line intuition

HSV-1 is the cold sore virus: it infects oral mucosa, hides in the trigeminal ganglion, and reactivates when the immune system is stressed.

---

## Pearl

HSV-1 encephalitis classically affects the temporal lobe and can cause fever, altered mental status, seizures, and focal neurologic deficits.
