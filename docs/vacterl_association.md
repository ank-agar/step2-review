### Core idea

VACTERL association is a nonrandom group of congenital anomalies (birth defects present from birth).

It is called an **association** because these findings occur together more than expected by chance, but there is no single known cause like in a classic syndrome.

VACTERL stands for:

* **V** = vertebral defects (abnormal spine bones)
* **A** = anal atresia (absent or blocked anal opening)
* **C** = cardiac defects (heart defects)
* **TE** = tracheoesophageal fistula (abnormal connection between trachea/windpipe and esophagus/food pipe) and esophageal atresia (esophagus ends blindly)
* **R** = renal anomalies (kidney abnormalities)
* **L** = limb defects, especially radial defects (thumb-side forearm defects)

Usually, diagnosis requires **at least 3** of these features.

---

### Why these things happen together

The intuition:

early in embryologic development, multiple organs are forming at the same time.

So if early body-patterning/development is disrupted, you can get defects in several nearby/developing systems:

* spine
* GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)
* heart
* trachea/esophagus
* kidneys
* limbs

That is why it looks like a “multi-system birth defect pattern,” not one isolated organ problem.

---

### Classic presentation

A newborn may have:

* imperforate anus (no normal anal opening)
* feeding difficulty, choking, or coughing with feeds from TE fistula (tracheoesophageal fistula)
* inability to pass an NG tube (nasogastric tube) into the stomach due to esophageal atresia
* murmur from VSD (ventricular septal defect, a hole between the ventricles)
* abnormal kidneys or hydronephrosis (urine backup into the kidney)
* absent thumb/radius or other limb defect
* vertebral/rib anomalies

---

## One-line intuition

VACTERL = several embryologic structures formed abnormally together, so think: **spine + anus + heart + trachea/esophagus + kidneys + limbs**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

VACTERL is **not one specific genetic syndrome**; it is an **association**, and Step 1 loves the acronym plus the rule that patients usually have **3 or more** component defects.
