# Postpartum sepsis

Postpartum sepsis = sepsis (life-threatening whole-body inflammatory response to infection) occurring after delivery.

Most often, the infection starts in the uterus:

* endometritis (infection/inflammation of the endometrium, the inner uterine lining)
* myometritis (infection/inflammation of the myometrium, the uterine muscle)
* parametritis (infection/inflammation of tissue around the uterus)

---

### Core idea

After delivery, the uterus is basically a large healing wound where the placenta detached.

So bacteria can ascend from the vagina/cervix into the uterus → infect the damaged uterine lining → enter bloodstream → sepsis.

Think:

> postpartum uterus + ascending bacteria + systemic inflammation

---

### Classic findings

Postpartum patient with:

* fever
* chills
* uterine tenderness
* lower abdominal/pelvic pain
* foul-smelling lochia (postpartum vaginal discharge)
* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* hypotension (low blood pressure) if severe
* confusion, low urine output, or organ dysfunction if progressing to septic shock

Septic shock = sepsis with dangerously low blood pressure and poor organ perfusion (blood flow to organs).

---

### Why the risk is higher after C-section

C-section (cesarean delivery) increases risk because:

* uterine incision gives bacteria more access to tissue
* more tissue trauma
* more manipulation of the uterus
* surgery itself increases infection risk

So C-section is a major risk factor for postpartum endometritis and postpartum sepsis.

---

### Bugs to remember

Usually polymicrobial (many different microbes together), especially:

* anaerobes (bacteria that grow without oxygen)
* gram-negative rods like E. coli
* group B Streptococcus
* group A Streptococcus, aka Streptococcus pyogenes

Group A Streptococcus is especially scary because it can cause rapid sepsis and toxic shock syndrome (toxin-mediated shock with fever, rash, hypotension, and organ injury).

---

## One-line intuition

Postpartum sepsis is when a uterine infection after delivery spills into the bloodstream and becomes a whole-body emergency.

---

## HY Step 1 pearl

Postpartum fever + uterine tenderness + foul-smelling lochia = think postpartum endometritis, and if there is hypotension/tachycardia/organ dysfunction, think postpartum sepsis.
