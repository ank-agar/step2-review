# Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1f/Hematopoiesis_%28human%29_diagram_en.svg/1920px-Hematopoiesis_%28human%29_diagram_en.svg.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/1221.jpg)

### Core idea

Acute lymphoblastic leukemia, or **ALL (acute lymphoblastic leukemia)**, is a cancer of immature lymphoid cells called **lymphoblasts (immature B-cell or T-cell precursors)**.

Think:

* **acute** = lots of immature blasts
* **lymphoblastic** = from lymphoid-lineage precursors
* **leukemia** = malignant cells mainly in bone marrow and blood

So ALL is basically:

> immature lymphocytes get stuck in blast form, rapidly multiply, and crowd out normal marrow.

---

### Why the findings happen

The bone marrow normally makes:

* **RBCs (red blood cells)** -> carry oxygen
* **WBCs (white blood cells)** -> fight infection
* **platelets** -> help clotting

In ALL, lymphoblasts fill the marrow, so normal blood cell production drops:

* low RBCs -> fatigue, pallor
* low neutrophils (infection-fighting WBCs) -> infections
* low platelets -> bruising, bleeding, petechiae (tiny skin bleeds)

The blasts can also spread outside the marrow:

* lymph nodes -> lymphadenopathy (enlarged lymph nodes)
* liver/spleen -> hepatosplenomegaly (enlarged liver and spleen)
* CNS (central nervous system) -> headache, neurologic symptoms
* testes -> testicular involvement

---

### Classic Step 1 associations

Most common childhood cancer.

Usually **B-ALL (B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia)** in kids.

Markers often include:

* **TdT (terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase)** = marker of immature lymphoblasts
* **CD10 (cluster of differentiation 10)** = CALLA (common acute lymphoblastic leukemia antigen)
* **CD19 (cluster of differentiation 19)** = B-cell marker

T-ALL (T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia) can present as an anterior mediastinal mass because T cells mature in the thymus.

---

## One-line intuition

ALL is a rapidly growing pile-up of immature lymphoid blasts that crowds out normal marrow and can spread to the CNS and testes.

---

## Pearl

**TdT-positive blasts** point toward acute lymphoblastic leukemia because TdT is a marker of immature lymphoid cells.
