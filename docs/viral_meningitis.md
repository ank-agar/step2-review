### Core idea

Viral meningitis = inflammation of the meninges (protective membranes around the brain and spinal cord) caused by a virus.

Most common cause:

* enteroviruses

Other causes:

* HSV (herpes simplex virus)
* VZV (varicella-zoster virus)
* HIV (human immunodeficiency virus)
* mumps virus
* West Nile virus

---

### What symptoms you expect

Classic meningitis symptoms:

* fever
* headache
* neck stiffness
* photophobia (light sensitivity)
* nausea/vomiting

Why neck stiffness happens:

* inflamed meninges get stretched when the neck flexes
* that stretch causes pain and reflex muscle stiffness

So the patient does not want to bend their neck forward.

---

### CSF findings

Diagnosis is usually with LP (lumbar puncture), which samples CSF (cerebrospinal fluid).

Viral meningitis CSF usually shows:

* increased lymphocytes
* normal glucose
* mildly increased protein
* normal or mildly increased opening pressure
* negative Gram stain

Reasoning:

* viruses live inside cells, so the immune response is more lymphocyte-heavy
* glucose is usually normal because viruses do not consume glucose the way bacteria/fungi can

---

### Viral vs bacterial intuition

Viral meningitis is usually less severe than bacterial meningitis.

Bacterial meningitis tends to be more dangerous because bacteria trigger a stronger neutrophil-heavy inflammatory response in the CSF (cerebrospinal fluid), which can rapidly increase pressure and damage the brain.

Viral meningitis is still serious clinically, but Step 1 usually wants:

* lymphocytes
* normal glucose
* supportive treatment

---

### Treatment idea

Most viral meningitis treatment is supportive:

* fluids
* pain control
* fever control

But if HSV (herpes simplex virus) is suspected:

* give acyclovir

because HSV can involve the CNS (central nervous system) and can be dangerous.

---

## One-line intuition

Viral meningitis is a virus irritating the meninges, causing headache + fever + stiff neck, with CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) showing lymphocytes and normal glucose.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: viral meningitis classically has lymphocytic CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) with normal glucose, while bacterial meningitis classically has neutrophilic CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) with low glucose.
