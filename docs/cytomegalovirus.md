Cytomegalovirus, usually called **CMV (cytomegalovirus)**, is a **herpesvirus**.

Its human form is:

* **HHV-5 (human herpesvirus 5)**
* enveloped
* double-stranded DNA virus
* can become latent, meaning it hides quietly in the body and can reactivate later

---

### Core idea

CMV usually causes mild or no disease in healthy people.

But it becomes important in:

* fetuses/newborns
* transplant patients
* AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome)
* other immunocompromised patients

So think:

> CMV is a quiet herpesvirus unless immunity is weak or the fetus is infected.

---

### Why it is called cytomegalovirus

Break the word apart:

* cyto- = cell
* megalo- = big
* virus = virus

So:

> CMV makes infected cells become enlarged.

That is why pathology shows big cells with classic **owl-eye inclusions**.

An inclusion is viral material inside the cell.

---

### Classic pathology finding

CMV causes:

* enlarged cells
* intranuclear inclusions, meaning inclusions inside the nucleus
* “owl-eye” appearance

Why “owl-eye”?

The viral inclusion sits in the middle of the nucleus with a clear halo around it, so it looks like an eye.

---

### Congenital CMV

Congenital CMV means CMV infection passed from mother to fetus during pregnancy.

Classic findings:

* sensorineural hearing loss, meaning hearing loss from inner ear or nerve damage
* petechiae, meaning tiny red-purple bleeding spots
* jaundice, meaning yellow skin from high bilirubin
* hepatosplenomegaly, meaning enlarged liver and spleen
* periventricular calcifications, meaning calcium deposits around the brain ventricles
* seizures or developmental delay

Important:

CMV is one of the most common congenital infections and a major infectious cause of hearing loss.

---

### Immunocompromised disease

In weak immune systems, CMV can cause:

* retinitis, meaning inflammation/infection of the retina
* colitis, meaning colon inflammation
* esophagitis, meaning esophagus inflammation
* pneumonitis, meaning lung inflammation

Retinitis is especially high-yield in AIDS.

Patients can have floaters, blurry vision, or vision loss.

---

## One-line intuition

CMV is a herpesvirus that hides in the body, makes cells huge with owl-eye inclusions, and causes serious disease in babies and immunocompromised patients.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Congenital CMV classically causes **periventricular calcifications**, while congenital toxoplasmosis classically causes **diffuse intracranial calcifications**.
