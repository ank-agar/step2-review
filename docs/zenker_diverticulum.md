## Zenker diverticulum

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/zenkers-diverticulum)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK532858/bin/zenker.jpg)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/1aa7/4191700/10ab93e51008/PAMJ-17-267-g002.jpg)

Zenker diverticulum is an outpouching of the mucosa/submucosa at the back of the throat, just above the upper esophageal sphincter.

In simpler terms:

> A little pouch forms above the esophagus and traps food.

---

### Core idea

Food is supposed to go:

* mouth
* pharynx (throat)
* esophagus
* stomach

But in Zenker diverticulum, the cricopharyngeus muscle does not relax well.

Cricopharyngeus muscle = the muscle that forms the upper esophageal sphincter, which is the "gate" from throat into esophagus.

So pressure builds up during swallowing, and the throat wall balloons outward through a weak spot.

That weak spot is called Killian triangle.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Dysphagia (difficulty swallowing):** food has trouble passing cleanly into the esophagus
* **Regurgitation of undigested food:** old food sits in the pouch and can come back up later
* **Bad breath/halitosis:** trapped food decays in the pouch
* **Coughing or aspiration:** food can spill from the pouch into the airway
* **Neck gurgling/mass:** the pouch can fill with air/food

Key phrase:

> Older patient + dysphagia + bad breath + regurgitation of undigested food = Zenker diverticulum.

---

### Important anatomy

Zenker diverticulum is a **false diverticulum**.

False diverticulum = only mucosa and submucosa herniate out, not the full wall.

It occurs:

* above the cricopharyngeus muscle
* through Killian triangle
* posteriorly, meaning toward the back

---

### Diagnosis

Best initial test is usually a barium swallow.

Barium swallow = patient swallows contrast material while x-rays show where it goes.

Why this works:

* the pouch fills with barium
* you can see the contrast sitting outside the normal esophageal path

Be careful with endoscopy because the scope can accidentally enter the pouch and cause perforation.

Perforation = making a hole through the wall.

---

### Treatment idea

Treatment is usually surgical/endoscopic repair if symptomatic.

The key move is often cricopharyngeal myotomy.

Myotomy = cutting a muscle.

Why?

Because the real problem is the tight/non-relaxing cricopharyngeus muscle creating pressure.

---

## One-line intuition

Zenker diverticulum is a food-trapping pouch above the esophagus caused by a tight upper esophageal sphincter.

---

## HY Step 1 pearl

Zenker diverticulum is a **pulsion diverticulum**, meaning it forms because high pressure pushes the wall outward.
