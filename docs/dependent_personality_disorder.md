## Dependent personality disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/eb/Signs_of_DPD_1.png)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK590043/bin/ch10basic-Image009.jpg)

Dependent personality disorder is a **Cluster C personality disorder** (anxious/fearful personality disorder group) where the person has an excessive need to be taken care of.

Core pattern:

* submissive
* clingy
* fears separation/abandonment
* has trouble making decisions without reassurance
* wants others to take responsibility for major parts of life

---

### Intuition

Think:

> "I cannot handle life alone, so I need someone else to guide/protect me."

The key is **dependence on another person for security and decision-making**.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Because they feel incapable alone, they may:

* avoid disagreeing with others because they fear losing support
* quickly seek another relationship when one ends
* volunteer for unpleasant tasks just to keep someone close
* struggle to start projects because they do not trust their own judgment

So the behavior looks "clingy," but the underlying driver is **fear of being unable to function without support**.

---

### How to distinguish it

* **Dependent personality disorder:** "Please take care of me."
* **Avoidant personality disorder:** "I want relationships, but I am afraid of rejection."
* **Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder:** "I need control, order, and perfection."

All 3 are Cluster C because they are anxiety/fear-based.

---

## One-line intuition

Dependent personality disorder = chronic fear of being alone leading to submissive dependence on others.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cluster C personality disorders are the **anxious/fearful** cluster:

* Avoidant
* Dependent
* Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder

Mnemonic: **"C = Cowardly/anxious"** for Step 1.
