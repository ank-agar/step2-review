### Core idea

Primary amenorrhea = the patient has **never had a period**.

Classically:

* no menses by age 15 if secondary sexual development is present
* or no menses by age 13 if secondary sexual development is absent

Secondary sexual development = puberty signs like breast development and pubic/axillary hair.

---

### What has to work for a period to happen?

Think of menstruation as a chain:

* hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* pituitary releases FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) and LH (luteinizing hormone)
* ovaries make estrogen
* uterus/endometrium responds
* outflow tract lets blood exit

If any part of that chain fails, no visible period happens.

---

### Big causes

Primary amenorrhea can come from:

* no estrogen signal from the brain/pituitary/ovaries
* no functioning uterus/endometrium
* blocked outflow tract
* chromosomal or gonadal development problems

Examples:

* Turner syndrome: streak ovaries → low estrogen → no puberty/period
* Müllerian agenesis: absent uterus/upper vagina → normal puberty but no bleeding
* androgen insensitivity syndrome: 46,XY with androgen receptor defect → breast development, little pubic hair, no uterus
* imperforate hymen/transverse vaginal septum: uterus makes blood, but blood cannot exit

---

### Why breast development matters

Breast development means estrogen has been present.

So:

* breasts present → ovaries are probably making estrogen
* breasts absent → think low estrogen from ovarian failure or hypothalamic/pituitary failure

This is why the workup asks:

* Are breasts present?
* Is the uterus present?
* What are FSH/LH levels?

---

### FSH logic

FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) is the pituitary yelling at the ovary.

* high FSH = pituitary is yelling, but ovary is not responding → ovarian failure
* low/normal FSH = pituitary/hypothalamus is not yelling enough → central problem

Intuition:

If the ovary fails, the brain turns up FSH because estrogen feedback is low.

---

## One-line intuition

Primary amenorrhea means the first period never came, so ask: **is puberty happening, is a uterus present, and is there an open exit path?**

---

## Pearl 🔥

Normal breast development + absent uterus = think **Müllerian agenesis vs androgen insensitivity syndrome**.

Key split:

* Müllerian agenesis: 46,XX, normal ovaries, normal pubic hair
* androgen insensitivity syndrome: 46,XY, testes make AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone) → no uterus, and androgen resistance → sparse/absent pubic hair
