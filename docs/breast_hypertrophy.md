### Core idea

Breast hypertrophy means excessive enlargement of breast tissue.

It is also called:

* macromastia
* gigantomastia, if very severe

Hypertrophy = increased size of tissue.

So breast hypertrophy = the breast tissue becomes abnormally large.

---

### What tissue is growing?

The breast contains:

* glandular tissue = milk-producing lobules and ducts
* adipose tissue = fat tissue
* connective tissue = structural support tissue

In breast hypertrophy, one or more of these tissue components enlarges too much.

---

### Why it happens

Often, the breast tissue is overly sensitive to hormones such as:

* estrogen
* progesterone
* prolactin

Prolactin = hormone from the anterior pituitary gland that promotes milk production.

This is why breast hypertrophy can happen during:

* puberty
* pregnancy
* medication exposure
* sometimes idiopathically

Idiopathic = no clear known cause.

---

### Why symptoms happen

Large breast mass pulls on the chest wall and shoulders, causing:

* neck pain
* back pain
* shoulder grooving from bra straps
* posture problems
* skin irritation under the breast folds

The key idea is mechanical weight.

It is not primarily a cancer process. It is usually benign overgrowth of normal breast tissue.

---

## One-line intuition

Breast hypertrophy is basically too much normal breast tissue, often hormone-sensitive, creating symptoms mostly because of weight and pulling.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember: prolactin comes from anterior pituitary lactotrophs and is inhibited by dopamine from the hypothalamus.
