# Anal abscess

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459167/bin/perirectal__abscess.jpg)

![Image](https://coloproctologie.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Abces-anal-English.jpg)

![Image](https://coloproctologie.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Abces-anal-28.jpg)

An anal abscess is a pocket of pus near the anus.

Abscess = localized collection of pus from infection.

Most commonly, it starts when an anal gland gets blocked and infected.

Think:

* anal gland gets plugged
* bacteria multiply
* pus builds up
* painful swollen lump forms near the anus

---

### Classic presentation

Usually:

* severe anal/perianal pain
* tender swollen bump near the anus
* redness/warmth
* pain worse with sitting or bowel movements
* fever sometimes

Perianal means around the anus.

---

### Why it hurts so much

The abscess is trapped pus under pressure.

So the tissue stretches and inflames nearby nerves, causing throbbing pain.

That is why it often feels much worse than a hemorrhoid.

Hemorrhoid = swollen anal/rectal vein.

---

### Treatment

Main treatment is:

* incision and drainage

Incision and drainage means cutting it open so the pus can come out.

Antibiotics alone usually do not fix an anal abscess because antibiotics cannot drain a closed pocket of pus well.

Antibiotics are added if there is:

* fever/systemic illness
* cellulitis (spreading skin infection)
* diabetes mellitus (high blood sugar disease)
* immunosuppression (weakened immune system)

---

### Important complication

Anal abscess can lead to an anal fistula.

Fistula = abnormal tunnel between two surfaces.

Here, the tunnel can connect:

* anal canal
* skin near the anus

So the sequence is:

> blocked anal gland → abscess → drainage/rupture → possible fistula tract

---

## One-line intuition

An anal abscess is basically an infected blocked anal gland that fills with pus and needs drainage.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 trap: if they describe a very painful tender perianal mass, treatment is drainage, not just antibiotics.
