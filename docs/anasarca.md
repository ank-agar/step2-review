Anasarca = severe generalized edema (swelling from fluid buildup) throughout the body.

So instead of just ankle swelling, the patient can have swelling in many places:

* legs
* arms
* face
* abdominal wall
* scrotum/vulva
* sometimes body cavities too, like ascites (fluid in the peritoneal cavity) or pleural effusions (fluid around the lungs)

---

### Core idea

Anasarca means fluid has left the blood vessels and is sitting in the interstitial space (space between cells) all over the body.

Think:

> blood vessels are leaking/overfilled or not holding fluid well, so water moves into tissues everywhere.

---

### Why it happens

Fluid balance depends mainly on:

* hydrostatic pressure = pressure pushing fluid out of vessels
* oncotic pressure = protein pull, mainly from albumin, pulling fluid into vessels
* lymphatic drainage = cleanup system that returns extra fluid to blood

Anasarca happens when this balance is badly disrupted.

Common mechanisms:

* heart failure -> increased venous hydrostatic pressure, so fluid backs up and gets pushed out
* nephrotic syndrome (heavy protein loss in urine) -> low albumin, so less oncotic pull keeps fluid in blood
* cirrhosis (liver scarring) -> low albumin production + portal hypertension (high pressure in portal venous system)
* kidney failure -> salt and water retention
* severe malnutrition -> low protein/albumin

---

### Classic exam finding

Often causes pitting edema.

Pitting edema = when you press the swollen skin and it leaves a dent.

Why?

Because the tissue is waterlogged, so pressure pushes fluid away from that spot, leaving a temporary indentation.

---

## One-line intuition

Anasarca is whole-body fluid overload in the tissues because the vessels either have too much pressure, too little protein pull, too much retained salt/water, or poor drainage.

---

## Pearl

For Step 1, connect anasarca with low albumin states:

* nephrotic syndrome = albumin lost in urine
* cirrhosis = albumin not made well
* malnutrition = not enough protein substrate

Low albumin -> low oncotic pressure -> fluid leaves blood vessels -> generalized edema/anasarca.
