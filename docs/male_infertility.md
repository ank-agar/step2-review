Male infertility means the male partner has trouble contributing to pregnancy, usually due to a problem with:

* sperm production
* sperm transport
* sperm function

Think:

> Can the testes make sperm, can the sperm get out, and can the sperm swim/fertilize?

---

### Core idea

The main test is semen analysis (lab test that checks sperm in ejaculate).

It looks at:

* sperm count/concentration = how many sperm
* motility = how well sperm move
* morphology = sperm shape
* volume = how much semen comes out

Low numbers, poor movement, or abnormal shape can all reduce fertility.

---

### Why the findings happen

Sperm need 3 big things:

* enough production in seminiferous tubules (sperm-producing tubes in testes)
* enough testosterone (main male androgen, or male sex hormone)
* cooler temperature than core body temperature

That is why testes sit in the scrotum:

* sperm production works best a few degrees cooler than the body
* heat damages spermatogenesis (sperm production)

So anything that raises testicular temperature can impair fertility.

Classic example:

* varicocele = dilated veins in the pampiniform plexus (testicular venous network)
* feels like a "bag of worms"
* usually left-sided
* increases testicular heat
* can decrease sperm count and motility

---

### Hormone logic

Hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone).

Anterior pituitary releases:

* LH (luteinizing hormone) -> stimulates Leydig cells -> testosterone
* FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) -> stimulates Sertoli cells -> sperm production support

Sertoli cells also release inhibin B (hormone that inhibits FSH).

So:

* low FSH/LH can cause low sperm production
* low testosterone inside the testes can cause poor spermatogenesis
* anabolic steroids suppress GnRH/LH/FSH, so they can shrink testes and cause infertility

---

### Major cause buckets

Pre-testicular:

* hormonal problem before the testes
* example: low GnRH, low LH, low FSH

Testicular:

* testes cannot make sperm well
* examples: varicocele, cryptorchidism (undescended testis), Klinefelter syndrome, mumps orchitis (testicular inflammation after mumps), chemotherapy

Post-testicular:

* sperm are made but cannot exit properly
* examples: vas deferens obstruction, ejaculatory duct obstruction, retrograde ejaculation (semen goes backward into bladder)

---

## One-line intuition

Male infertility is usually a problem with sperm quantity, sperm movement, sperm shape, hormones, heat regulation, or blocked sperm delivery.

---

## HY pearl

Varicocele is the classic potentially correctable cause of male infertility: left-sided "bag of worms" due to dilated pampiniform plexus veins causing increased testicular temperature and impaired spermatogenesis.
