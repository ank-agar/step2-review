# Pancreatitis in pregnancy

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17492/N00042-H.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/Ultrasound_image_of_gallbladder_stone_Gallstone_091937515.jpg)

Pancreatitis in pregnancy = inflammation of the pancreas during pregnancy.

The big Step 1 idea:

* pregnancy increases estrogen
* estrogen increases cholesterol in bile
* progesterone slows gallbladder emptying
* bile sits there longer
* cholesterol stones/sludge form
* a gallstone can block the ampulla of Vater (opening where the common bile duct and pancreatic duct drain into the duodenum)
* pancreatic enzymes back up and activate inside the pancreas
* pancreas starts digesting itself

So the most common cause is usually gallstones.

---

### Presentation

Think acute pancreatitis symptoms:

* severe epigastric pain (upper middle abdominal pain)
* pain radiates to the back
* nausea/vomiting
* worse after eating
* elevated lipase/amylase

Lipase is more specific for pancreatitis than amylase.

---

### Why pregnancy causes gallstones

Pregnancy hormones make the gallbladder a perfect setup for stones:

* estrogen makes bile more cholesterol-rich
* progesterone relaxes smooth muscle, so the gallbladder contracts poorly

That means concentrated, stagnant bile.

One-line intuition:

> pregnancy makes bile thicker and gallbladder emptying slower, so stones form and can block pancreatic drainage.

---

### Diagnosis

First-line imaging is usually RUQ ultrasound (right upper quadrant ultrasound), because it can look for gallstones without radiation.

Avoid CT (computed tomography) if possible in pregnancy because of fetal radiation exposure, unless the clinical situation really requires it.

---

### Treatment idea

Initial management is supportive:

* IV fluids (intravenous fluids)
* pain control
* bowel rest
* treat the cause

If gallstones are causing recurrent/severe disease, cholecystectomy (surgical gallbladder removal) can be done during pregnancy when needed, often safest in the 2nd trimester.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Acute pancreatitis classically causes hypocalcemia (low calcium) because pancreatic enzymes digest fat, and calcium binds the released fatty acids in a process called fat saponification.
