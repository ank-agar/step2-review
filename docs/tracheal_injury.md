# Tracheal injury

### Core idea

A tracheal injury is a tear, cut, rupture, or disruption of the trachea (windpipe).

The trachea is the tube that carries air from the larynx (voice box) down to the bronchi (airways entering the lungs).

So if it gets injured:

* air may not reach the lungs properly
* air can leak into the neck/chest tissues
* breathing can become dangerous quickly

---

### Common causes

* Blunt trauma, like a car crash or neck/chest impact
* Penetrating trauma, like a stab wound or gunshot wound
* Iatrogenic injury, meaning caused by a medical procedure
* Endotracheal intubation, meaning placing a breathing tube through the mouth into the trachea
* Tracheostomy, meaning surgically making an airway opening in the trachea

---

### Why the findings happen

If the trachea tears, air escapes out of the airway.

That leaked air can cause:

* **Subcutaneous emphysema:** air under the skin, often causing neck/chest swelling or crackly skin
* **Pneumomediastinum:** air in the mediastinum (central chest space between the lungs)
* **Pneumothorax:** air in the pleural space (space around the lung), which can collapse the lung
* **Hemoptysis:** coughing up blood
* **Respiratory distress:** difficulty breathing

Intuition:

> The trachea is supposed to be a sealed air pipe. If the pipe tears, air leaks into places it should not go.

---

### Important anatomy

The trachea has:

* C-shaped cartilage rings anteriorly (in front)
* a softer membranous wall posteriorly (in back)

That posterior membranous wall is vulnerable during intubation because a tube, stylet, or cuff can tear it.

Stylet = stiff insert placed inside an endotracheal tube to help guide it.

---

### Diagnosis

Best test:

* bronchoscopy

Bronchoscopy means putting a camera into the airway to directly see the trachea and bronchi.

CT (computed tomography) can show indirect clues like:

* pneumomediastinum
* pneumothorax
* subcutaneous emphysema
* visible airway wall defect

---

## One-line intuition

Tracheal injury = a broken windpipe, so air leaks out of the airway instead of smoothly traveling into the lungs.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Bronchoscopy is the gold standard for diagnosing tracheal injury because it directly visualizes the airway tear.
