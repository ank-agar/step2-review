## Bartholin gland abscess

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d6/Bartholin_cyst_svg_hariadhi.svg)

![Image](https://s3.amazonaws.com/static.wd7.us/f/fe/Bartholin%27s_gland_abscess003.jpg)

### Core idea

A Bartholin gland abscess is an infected, pus-filled swelling of a Bartholin gland.

Bartholin glands are mucus-secreting glands near the vaginal opening, classically at the:

* 4 o'clock position
* 8 o'clock position

Their job is lubrication.

If the duct gets blocked:

* mucus cannot drain
* a cyst forms
* bacteria infect the trapped fluid
* it becomes an abscess

Abscess means a walled-off collection of pus.

---

### Cyst vs abscess

Bartholin cyst:

* blocked duct
* fluid-filled
* often painless

Bartholin abscess:

* infected cyst/gland
* pus-filled
* painful, red, swollen, tender

So the abscess is basically the painful infected version.

---

### Why it hurts so much

The gland is right near the vaginal opening, so swelling stretches sensitive vulvar tissue.

That causes pain with:

* sitting
* walking
* sex

And because it is infected, inflammation brings:

* redness
* warmth
* tenderness
* possible fever

---

### What causes it

Usually polymicrobial, meaning multiple bacteria are involved.

Common organisms include:

* E. coli
* Staphylococcus aureus
* Streptococcus species
* anaerobes, which are bacteria that grow in low-oxygen environments

STIs (sexually transmitted infections) like gonorrhea or chlamydia can sometimes be involved, especially in younger or higher-risk patients.

---

### Treatment intuition

The key problem is pus trapped under pressure.

So treatment is mainly:

* drainage
* keeping it open long enough to keep draining

Common options:

* incision and drainage, meaning making a small cut to let pus out
* Word catheter, which is a small balloon catheter left in place so the duct stays open
* marsupialization, which creates a more permanent drainage opening for recurrent cases

Antibiotics are not always enough by themselves because antibiotics have trouble sterilizing a closed pus pocket.

---

## One-line intuition

A Bartholin gland abscess is a blocked vulvar mucus gland that gets infected and turns into a very painful pus pocket near the vaginal opening.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In a patient over age 40 with a new Bartholin gland mass, think biopsy to rule out Bartholin gland carcinoma, because a "cyst/abscess" in that age group can rarely hide malignancy.
