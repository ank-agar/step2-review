### Core idea

Postpartum cardiomyopathy, also called peripartum cardiomyopathy, is new-onset heart failure near the end of pregnancy or within about 5 months after delivery.

Cardiomyopathy means disease of the heart muscle.

The key problem is:

* the left ventricle becomes weak
* ejection fraction drops
* blood backs up into the lungs/body

Ejection fraction means the percent of blood the left ventricle pumps out with each beat.

---

### Why it happens

Pregnancy already increases blood volume and cardiac workload.

In postpartum cardiomyopathy, the heart muscle cannot handle that stress and becomes dilated/weak.

So it behaves like dilated cardiomyopathy:

* big floppy ventricle
* poor contraction
* low cardiac output
* fluid backup

That is why symptoms look like heart failure.

---

### Classic symptoms

Think new mom with heart failure symptoms:

* dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* orthopnea (shortness of breath when lying flat)
* paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, or PND (waking up gasping for air)
* leg edema (swelling)
* fatigue
* palpitations
* pulmonary edema (fluid in lungs)

The tricky part:

Some fatigue and mild swelling can be normal in pregnancy/postpartum, but orthopnea, pulmonary edema, severe dyspnea, or low ejection fraction is not normal.

---

### Diagnosis

Main test:

* echocardiogram, or echo (heart ultrasound)

Echo shows:

* reduced left ventricular ejection fraction, or LVEF
* often LVEF less than 45%
* dilated left ventricle sometimes

Chest x-ray may show:

* cardiomegaly (big heart)
* pulmonary edema
* pleural effusions (fluid around lungs)

---

### Why blood clots can happen

A weak, dilated ventricle has sluggish blood flow.

Slow blood flow can clot.

So postpartum cardiomyopathy can cause:

* left ventricular thrombus (clot inside the left ventricle)
* embolic stroke (clot travels to brain)
* systemic emboli (clots travel to body organs)

This is the same basic idea as stasis in Virchow triad.

Virchow triad = stasis, endothelial injury, and hypercoagulability causing thrombosis.

---

### Treatment idea

Treat it like heart failure, but pregnancy/breastfeeding safety matters.

Common postpartum meds may include:

* diuretics to remove extra fluid
* beta blockers to reduce cardiac workload
* ACE inhibitors after delivery
* anticoagulation if severe low ejection fraction or ventricular clot

ACE inhibitors (angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors) are avoided during pregnancy but can be used postpartum in appropriate patients.

---

## One-line intuition

Postpartum cardiomyopathy is pregnancy-triggered dilated cardiomyopathy: the left ventricle gets weak and dilated, so blood backs up and the patient develops heart failure after delivery.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association:

Postpartum cardiomyopathy is especially associated with preeclampsia, multiple gestation, advanced maternal age, and African ancestry.
