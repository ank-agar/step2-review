## Thyrotoxicosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/25/Myxedema.jpg)

### Core idea

Thyrotoxicosis = too much thyroid hormone effect on the body.

Thyroid hormone mainly means:

* T3 (triiodothyronine)
* T4 (thyroxine)

So the body acts like the metabolic “gas pedal” is being pushed too hard.

---

### Thyrotoxicosis vs hyperthyroidism

These are close, but not exactly the same:

* **Thyrotoxicosis:** too much thyroid hormone effect, from any cause
* **Hyperthyroidism:** the thyroid gland is actively making too much hormone

So:

> hyperthyroidism can cause thyrotoxicosis, but not all thyrotoxicosis is hyperthyroidism.

Example:

* Graves disease = thyroid is overproducing hormone → hyperthyroidism + thyrotoxicosis
* thyroiditis (thyroid inflammation) = stored hormone leaks out → thyrotoxicosis, but not true overproduction
* taking too much levothyroxine (synthetic T4 thyroid hormone) → thyrotoxicosis, but thyroid itself is not overactive

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Too much thyroid hormone increases metabolism and increases sensitivity to catecholamines (epinephrine/norepinephrine, “fight-or-flight” hormones).

That causes:

* weight loss despite appetite → burning more energy
* heat intolerance/sweating → higher metabolic heat production
* tremor/anxiety → increased catecholamine sensitivity
* tachycardia (fast heart rate) and palpitations → beta-adrenergic stimulation
* diarrhea or frequent stools → increased gut motility
* muscle weakness → catabolic state, meaning the body breaks down tissue for energy

---

### Labs

Usually:

* low TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
* high free T4 (thyroxine)
* high T3 (triiodothyronine)

Why low TSH?

The pituitary gland sees high T3/T4 and says:

> “we already have too much thyroid hormone”

So it suppresses TSH.

---

### Common causes

* Graves disease: autoimmune stimulation of the TSH receptor
* toxic multinodular goiter: multiple autonomous thyroid nodules
* toxic adenoma: one autonomous thyroid nodule
* thyroiditis: inflammation releases preformed hormone
* excess thyroid medication

---

## One-line intuition

Thyrotoxicosis is when the body is flooded with too much thyroid hormone, so everything runs hot, fast, shaky, and catabolic.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Radioactive iodine uptake helps separate causes:

* high uptake = thyroid is making too much hormone, like Graves disease
* low uptake = hormone is leaking out or coming from outside, like thyroiditis or excess levothyroxine
