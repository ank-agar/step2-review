### Core idea

Escherichia coli, or E. coli, is a Gram-negative rod-shaped bacterium that normally lives in the colon.

Gram-negative means it has:

* thin peptidoglycan cell wall
* outer membrane
* lipopolysaccharide, or LPS (endotoxin on the outer membrane)

Most E. coli is normal gut flora.

But some strains are pathogenic, meaning disease-causing.

---

### What E. coli commonly causes

Big Step 1 associations:

* urinary tract infection, or UTI (infection of bladder/kidney urinary tract)
* neonatal meningitis (infection of meninges in newborns)
* gastroenteritis (infectious diarrhea)
* sepsis (dangerous bloodstream inflammatory response)

E. coli is the most common cause of uncomplicated UTI.

Reason:

* E. coli lives in the colon
* it can contaminate the periurethral area (area around the urethra)
* it ascends into the bladder

So the anatomy explains why it causes UTIs so often.

---

### Why it is Gram-negative

E. coli has an outer membrane with LPS.

LPS has lipid A, which is the toxic part.

Lipid A triggers macrophages (immune cells that eat microbes) to release inflammatory cytokines (immune signaling proteins), especially:

* IL-1 (interleukin-1)
* IL-6 (interleukin-6)
* TNF-alpha (tumor necrosis factor-alpha)

That is why severe Gram-negative infection can cause:

* fever
* hypotension (low blood pressure)
* septic shock

---

### Diarrhea strains

Different E. coli strains cause different diarrhea patterns:

* ETEC (enterotoxigenic E. coli): traveler's diarrhea, watery diarrhea
* EHEC (enterohemorrhagic E. coli): bloody diarrhea, can cause HUS
* EPEC (enteropathogenic E. coli): watery diarrhea in infants
* EIEC (enteroinvasive E. coli): dysentery-like illness with fever/blood

HUS means hemolytic uremic syndrome:

* hemolytic anemia (red blood cells get destroyed)
* thrombocytopenia (low platelets)
* acute kidney injury

Why EHEC causes HUS:

* EHEC makes Shiga-like toxin
* toxin damages endothelial cells (cells lining blood vessels), especially in kidney
* platelets get consumed and red blood cells shear through damaged vessels

---

## One-line intuition

E. coli is a normal colon Gram-negative rod that becomes high-yield when it gets into the wrong place: bladder, blood, meninges, or gut with toxin-producing strains.

---

## HY pearl

EHEC is associated with undercooked beef and causes bloody diarrhea, but do not give antibiotics because increased toxin release can increase risk of HUS.
