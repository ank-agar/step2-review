# Cubital tunnel syndrome

![Image](https://com-neurosurgery-a2.sites.medinfo.ufl.edu/files/2012/04/Cubital-Tunnel-anatomy.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/62/Compressed_ulnar_nerve-ar.svg/960px-Compressed_ulnar_nerve-ar.svg.png)

![Image](https://com-neurosurgery-a2.sites.medinfo.ufl.edu/files/2012/04/dorsal_ulnar.jpg)

![Image](https://com-neurosurgery-a2.sites.medinfo.ufl.edu/files/2012/04/ulnar-pad.jpg)

Cubital tunnel syndrome is compression of the **ulnar nerve** at the elbow.

Ulnar nerve = the nerve that gives sensation/motor function to the:

* pinky
* ulnar half of ring finger
* many small hand muscles

Cubital tunnel = the tunnel on the medial elbow, near the **medial epicondyle** (the bony bump on the inner elbow).

---

### Core idea

The ulnar nerve gets squeezed where it passes behind the elbow.

This is basically:

> "funny bone" nerve compression.

That is why symptoms shoot into the pinky/ring finger.

---

### Why symptoms happen

When the ulnar nerve is compressed:

* sensory fibers get irritated -> numbness/tingling in pinky + ring finger
* motor fibers get damaged -> weak grip and weak finger spreading
* chronic injury -> intrinsic hand muscle wasting
* severe injury -> ulnar claw

Intrinsic hand muscles = small muscles inside the hand that control fine finger movements.

---

### Why elbow flexion makes it worse

Bending the elbow stretches and tightens the ulnar nerve through the cubital tunnel.

So symptoms often worsen with:

* sleeping with elbows bent
* leaning on elbows
* talking on the phone with elbow flexed
* repetitive elbow bending

Intuition:

The nerve is already passing through a narrow tunnel, and elbow flexion makes that tunnel tighter.

---

### Classic findings

* numbness/tingling in the 5th digit and medial half of 4th digit
* medial elbow pain
* weak finger abduction/adduction
* positive Tinel sign at elbow
* Froment sign in severe cases

Tinel sign = tapping over a nerve causes tingling in that nerve distribution.

Froment sign = patient uses thumb flexion to hold paper because the ulnar-innervated adductor pollicis is weak.

---

## One-line intuition

Cubital tunnel syndrome is the ulnar nerve getting pinched at the elbow, causing "funny bone" symptoms down into the pinky side of the hand.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cubital tunnel syndrome affects the **ulnar nerve at the elbow**, while carpal tunnel syndrome affects the **median nerve at the wrist**.
