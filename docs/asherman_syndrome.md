# Asherman syndrome

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/16561-ashermans-syndrome)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/75/Hysteroscopy_of_Asherman%27s_Syndrome_cropped.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/HSG_Ashermans_syndrome.jpg)

Asherman syndrome is scar tissue inside the uterus.

More specifically:

* intrauterine adhesions = scar bands inside the uterine cavity
* synechiae = adhesions, meaning abnormal tissue bridges between surfaces

So the uterine walls can partially stick together.

---

### Core idea

Normally, the endometrium (uterine lining) grows and sheds every cycle.

In Asherman syndrome, the basal layer of the endometrium is injured.

Basal layer = deep regenerative layer that regrows the uterine lining.

If that layer is damaged, the uterus heals with fibrosis (scar tissue) instead of normal lining.

---

### Classic cause

Most commonly after dilation and curettage (D&C), especially after:

* miscarriage
* delivery
* retained placenta
* abortion

D&C = dilation and curettage, a procedure where the cervix is opened and tissue is scraped/suctioned from inside the uterus.

Why pregnancy-related D&C is risky:

* the postpartum or recently pregnant endometrium is more vulnerable
* scraping can damage the basal layer
* opposing raw surfaces heal by sticking to each other

---

### Symptoms

Think: blocked/scarred uterine cavity.

Findings:

* infertility
* recurrent pregnancy loss
* hypomenorrhea (light menstrual bleeding)
* secondary amenorrhea (absence of periods after previously having periods)
* cyclic pelvic pain if blood is trapped behind adhesions

Why infertility happens:

* embryo needs healthy endometrium to implant
* scarred endometrium is a bad implantation surface
* adhesions can physically distort or narrow the uterine cavity

---

### Diagnosis and treatment

Best diagnostic test:

* hysteroscopy = camera placed through the cervix to directly look inside the uterus

Treatment:

* hysteroscopic adhesiolysis

Adhesiolysis = cutting/removing adhesions.

After treatment, estrogen may be used to help regrow endometrium, and a balloon or barrier may be placed to keep the uterine walls from sticking together again.

---

## One-line intuition

Asherman syndrome = uterine cavity gets scarred shut after endometrial trauma, causing light/no periods plus infertility or recurrent pregnancy loss.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Secondary amenorrhea after a D&C is Asherman syndrome until proven otherwise.
