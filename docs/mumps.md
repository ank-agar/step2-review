Mumps is a contagious viral infection caused by mumps virus, a paramyxovirus (enveloped, negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus).

Classic finding:

* painful parotitis (inflammation of the parotid glands)

Parotid glands = salivary glands in front of and below the ears.

So the classic “puffy cheeks” happen because the virus infects/swells the salivary glands.

---

### Core idea

Mumps loves glandular tissue, especially:

* parotid glands
* testes
* ovaries
* pancreas

That is why complications can include:

* orchitis (testicular inflammation)
* oophoritis (ovarian inflammation)
* pancreatitis (pancreas inflammation)
* aseptic meningitis (meningitis without typical bacterial infection)

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Jaw/cheek swelling:** inflamed parotid glands enlarge near the angle of the jaw
* **Pain with chewing/sour foods:** saliva production squeezes an already swollen gland
* **Fever/malaise:** systemic viral illness
* **Orchitis after puberty:** virus spreads to testicular tissue and causes inflammation

---

### Prevention

Prevented with the MMR vaccine:

* measles
* mumps
* rubella

MMR = live attenuated vaccine (weakened live virus vaccine).

---

## One-line intuition

Mumps is the “paramyxovirus that makes the parotid glands puff up.”

---

## Pearl 🔥

Mumps orchitis can cause testicular swelling/pain, but sterility is rare.
