# Genital warts

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK321770/bin/50a.gif)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK321770/bin/51a.gif)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441884/bin/condylomata__acuminatum.jpg)

Genital warts are benign, wart-like growths on the genital or anal skin caused by HPV (human papillomavirus).

Most commonly:

* HPV 6
* HPV 11

These are low-risk HPV types, meaning they cause warts but are much less associated with cancer than high-risk HPV types.

---

### Core idea

HPV infects squamous epithelial cells.

Squamous epithelial cells = flat surface-lining cells, like skin and mucosa.

The virus makes those cells proliferate too much, creating soft, cauliflower-like papules or plaques.

So the intuition is:

> HPV pushes surface cells to overgrow, so you see visible warty bumps.

---

### Why they look like warts

HPV infects the basal layer of epithelium through tiny breaks in skin or mucosa.

Then as the infected cells move upward and mature, the virus replicates inside them.

This causes:

* epithelial thickening
* finger-like projections
* a rough/verrucous surface
* sometimes koilocytes

Koilocytes = HPV-infected squamous cells with wrinkled nuclei and a clear halo around the nucleus.

---

### Clinical features

Genital warts are usually:

* painless
* flesh-colored or pink
* soft
* raised
* cauliflower-like

They can occur on:

* penis
* vulva
* vagina
* cervix
* perianal skin
* anus

They may itch, bleed, or cause discomfort, but many patients mainly notice the appearance.

---

### Transmission

Spread is by skin-to-skin sexual contact.

Important: condoms reduce risk but do not fully eliminate it because HPV can infect exposed skin not covered by a condom.

---

### Cancer association

Genital warts are classically caused by low-risk HPV 6 and 11.

Cervical, anal, penile, vulvar, vaginal, and oropharyngeal cancers are more associated with high-risk HPV types, especially:

* HPV 16
* HPV 18

So do not equate "visible genital warts" with "high cancer risk."

---

## One-line intuition

Genital warts are low-risk HPV-driven overgrowths of squamous epithelium, most often from HPV 6 and 11.

---

## Pearl

HPV vaccine protects against HPV 6 and 11, the classic genital wart types, plus high-risk types like HPV 16 and 18.
