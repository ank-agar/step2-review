## Neural tube defects

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19087.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/birth-defects/media/images/anencephaly.png)

![Image](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/ncbddd/birthdefects/surveillancemanual/photo-atlas/nervous_html_files/Fig.-6.-Spina-bifida.jpg)

Neural tube defects are birth defects caused by failure of the neural tube to close properly.

Neural tube = embryologic structure that becomes the:

* brain
* spinal cord

So if it does not close, the future central nervous system (brain and spinal cord) is exposed or malformed.

---

### Core idea

Think:

> neural tube should zip closed early in development.

If the zipper fails:

* top/head end problem -> anencephaly
* bottom/spine end problem -> spina bifida

---

### Main types

* **Anencephaly:** failure of the cranial/anterior neuropore (head-end neural tube opening) to close
  * absent major parts of brain/skull
  * incompatible with life

* **Spina bifida:** failure of the caudal/posterior neuropore (tail-end neural tube opening) to close
  * vertebral arches do not close properly
  * meninges (brain/spinal cord coverings) and/or spinal cord can protrude

* **Encephalocele:** skull defect with herniation of brain tissue/meninges

---

### Why folate matters

Folate (vitamin B9) is needed for DNA synthesis and rapid cell division.

Early embryo cells are dividing extremely fast, so low folate means poor neural tube closure.

That is why folic acid supplementation before pregnancy and early pregnancy lowers risk.

---

### Labs/screening

Open neural tube defects usually cause increased:

* maternal serum AFP (alpha-fetoprotein)
* amniotic fluid AFP
* amniotic fluid acetylcholinesterase

Why?

AFP (alpha-fetoprotein) leaks out from the fetus into amniotic fluid and maternal blood when the defect is open.

---

## One-line intuition

The embryo's future brain/spinal cord tube fails to zip closed, causing exposed or malformed brain/spinal cord structures.
