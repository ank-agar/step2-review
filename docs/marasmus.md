![Image](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/courses-images/wp-content/uploads/sites/2569/2017/10/23193725/100000000000015A000001F42176D3CD.jpg)

Marasmus is severe protein-energy malnutrition (PEM = inadequate protein and calorie intake), mainly from **total calorie deficiency**.

Think:

* not enough carbs
* not enough fat
* not enough protein
* not enough total energy

So the body starts eating itself for fuel.

---

### Core idea

Marasmus = **starvation-type malnutrition**.

The body uses up:

* glycogen (stored glucose)
* fat stores
* muscle protein

So the patient becomes extremely thin with:

* severe wasting
* loss of subcutaneous fat (fat under the skin)
* muscle wasting
* prominent ribs
* “old man” or shriveled appearance

---

### Why there is usually no edema

This is the key Step 1 contrast with kwashiorkor.

In marasmus, the body is globally calorie-starved, but the liver can often still make enough albumin (main blood protein that keeps fluid inside blood vessels).

So:

* albumin relatively preserved
* oncotic pressure (protein-pulling force that keeps fluid in vessels) relatively preserved
* no major edema

Kwashiorkor = low protein worse than calories → low albumin → edema.

Marasmus = total calorie starvation → wasting without edema.

---

### Why the body looks wasted

If no food energy is coming in, the body needs another fuel source.

So it breaks down:

* fat → for energy
* muscle → amino acids (protein building blocks) for glucose production

That is why marasmus looks like:

> skin and bones.

---

## One-line intuition

Marasmus is total calorie starvation, so the body burns its own fat and muscle but usually does **not** get edematous.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 loves:

* **Marasmus:** severe calorie deficiency → wasting, no edema
* **Kwashiorkor:** protein deficiency → edema, fatty liver, “flaky paint” skin
