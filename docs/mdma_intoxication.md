### Core idea

MDMA (3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine; ecstasy/molly) intoxication is basically:

* stimulant toxicity
* serotonin excess
* overheating risk

It causes a big release of serotonin (mood neurotransmitter), norepinephrine (fight-or-flight neurotransmitter), and dopamine (reward/movement neurotransmitter).

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Euphoria/empathy:** increased serotonin and dopamine
* **Tachycardia/hypertension:** increased norepinephrine
* **Mydriasis:** sympathetic activation dilates pupils
* **Hyperthermia:** increased muscle activity + dancing/exertion + impaired heat regulation
* **Agitation/confusion:** excess CNS (central nervous system) stimulation
* **Sweating/diarrhea/clonus/hyperreflexia:** serotonin excess, similar to serotonin syndrome

Clonus means rhythmic jerking after stretching a muscle, and hyperreflexia means exaggerated reflexes.

---

### Big dangerous complications

Most important ones:

* **Hyperthermia:** can lead to rhabdomyolysis (muscle breakdown), AKI (acute kidney injury), DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation), and death
* **Hyponatremia:** low blood sodium
* **Seizures:** often from severe hyponatremia or serotonin toxicity

Why hyponatremia happens:

MDMA can increase ADH (antidiuretic hormone), which makes the kidney retain water. People may also drink lots of water because they feel hot/thirsty. So sodium gets diluted.

---

## One-line intuition

MDMA floods the synapse with serotonin plus catecholamines, so the patient looks like a mix of serotonin syndrome + sympathomimetic intoxication.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 clue: MDMA intoxication with **fever + agitation + dilated pupils + hyperreflexia/clonus** should make you think **serotonin excess**, and severe **hyponatremia** can cause seizures because water shifts into brain cells, causing cerebral edema.
