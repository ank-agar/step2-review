## Coronary artery spasm

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/43be/6403432/195646c2bd20/pjab-95-053-g001.jpg)

Coronary artery spasm = temporary sudden narrowing of a coronary artery (artery that supplies the heart muscle).

So the artery is not necessarily blocked by a clot or plaque.

It just clamps down.

That causes transient ischemia (low blood flow/oxygen to tissue) of the myocardium (heart muscle).

---

### Core idea

Think:

* normal artery = open pipe
* spasm = pipe suddenly squeezes shut
* less blood to heart muscle = chest pain/ischemic ECG changes

This causes vasospastic angina, also called Prinzmetal angina.

Angina = chest pain from myocardial ischemia (heart muscle not getting enough oxygen).

---

### Why it happens

The smooth muscle in the coronary artery wall becomes too reactive.

Triggers can include:

* smoking
* cocaine/amphetamines
* cold exposure
* stress
* endothelial dysfunction

Endothelium = inner blood vessel lining.

Normally, endothelium releases nitric oxide (NO), which relaxes vessels.

If endothelial function is bad, there is less vasodilation (vessel widening), so the artery is more likely to constrict.

---

### Classic presentation

* chest pain at rest
* often at night or early morning
* transient ST elevation on ECG (electrocardiogram)
* improves with nitrates

ST elevation happens because the spasm can temporarily cause transmural ischemia (ischemia across the full thickness of the heart wall), similar-looking to an MI (myocardial infarction/heart attack).

But unlike a classic MI, the artery may open back up once the spasm relaxes.

---

### Treatment idea

Use drugs that relax coronary arteries:

* calcium channel blockers
* nitrates

Avoid beta blockers if pure vasospastic angina is suspected, because blocking beta effects can leave alpha-mediated vasoconstriction (vessel tightening) unopposed.

---

## One-line intuition

A coronary artery spasm is a temporary “squeeze shut” of a heart artery, causing brief low oxygen to the heart muscle even without a fixed blockage.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Prinzmetal angina classically causes episodic chest pain at rest with transient ST elevation, and it is treated with calcium channel blockers and nitrates.
