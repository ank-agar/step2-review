## Transposition of the great vessels

![Image](https://www.mayoclinic.org/-/media/kcms/gbs/patient-consumer/images/2013/08/26/11/07/ds00733-mcdc7_transpositionthu_jpg.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538434/bin/Transposition__of__the__Great__Vessels__5.jpg)

![Image](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/mmanual-ssq1ci05/media/professional/images/t/r/a/transposition-of-great-arteries-baffa-high.jpg?mw=1920&sc_lang=en)

Transposition of the great vessels means the 2 big arteries leaving the heart are switched:

* aorta comes from the right ventricle
* pulmonary artery comes from the left ventricle

Aorta = artery that sends blood to the body.

Pulmonary artery = artery that sends blood to the lungs.

---

### Core idea

Normally:

* right ventricle sends blue/deoxygenated blood to lungs
* left ventricle sends red/oxygenated blood to body

In transposition:

* right ventricle sends blue blood straight to the body
* left ventricle sends red blood back to the lungs

So the body keeps getting poorly oxygenated blood.

---

### Why this is dangerous

The circulation becomes 2 separate loops:

* body -> right heart -> body
* lungs -> left heart -> lungs

That means oxygenated and deoxygenated blood do not mix well.

The baby survives only if there is some mixing through:

* VSD (ventricular septal defect = hole between ventricles)
* ASD (atrial septal defect = hole between atria)
* PDA (patent ductus arteriosus = fetal vessel connecting pulmonary artery to aorta)

---

### Classic presentation

Newborn with:

* cyanosis (blue skin from low oxygen)
* respiratory distress
* poor feeding
* little improvement with oxygen

Chest X-ray can show:

* egg-on-a-string appearance

because the heart looks globular and the upper mediastinum (middle chest area between the lungs) looks narrow.

---

## One-line intuition

The heart plumbing is reversed, so blue blood keeps going to the body and red blood keeps going back to the lungs.

---

## Pearl

Initial treatment is prostaglandin E1, which keeps the PDA (patent ductus arteriosus) open so oxygenated and deoxygenated blood can mix before definitive surgical repair.
