## Rectal bleeding

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17502/N00052-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Internal_and_external_hemorrhoids.png/1280px-Internal_and_external_hemorrhoids.png)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537245/bin/Gray1080.jpg)

Rectal bleeding means blood coming out from the anus/rectum, often noticed as:

* bright red blood on toilet paper
* blood coating stool
* blood in the toilet bowl

Medical term:

* **hematochezia** = passage of fresh red blood per rectum

---

### Core idea

Bright red blood usually means the bleeding source is low in the GI (gastrointestinal) tract:

* anus
* rectum
* sigmoid colon
* colon

Why?

Because blood from a low source exits quickly, so it stays red.

---

### Common causes

* **Hemorrhoids** = swollen veins in/around the anus or rectum  
  Usually painless, bright red blood.

* **Anal fissure** = small tear in the anal canal  
  Classically painful bleeding with bowel movements.

* **Diverticulosis** = outpouchings of the colon wall  
  Can cause painless, large-volume lower GI (gastrointestinal) bleeding.

* **Inflammatory bowel disease** = chronic immune-mediated gut inflammation  
  Can cause bloody diarrhea, urgency, and abdominal pain.

* **Colorectal cancer** = malignancy of the colon or rectum  
  Think especially with weight loss, anemia, or change in stool caliber.

---

### Why color matters

* **Bright red blood:** usually lower GI (gastrointestinal) or anorectal source
* **Maroon stool:** often colon or distal small bowel
* **Melena** = black, tarry stool from digested blood  
  Usually upper GI (gastrointestinal) bleeding, like stomach/duodenum

So:

> red = closer/faster exit  
> black/tarry = blood sat around and got digested

---

### Red flags

Rectal bleeding is not automatically dangerous, but these need evaluation:

* dizziness/fainting
* lots of blood
* black tarry stool
* weight loss
* iron-deficiency anemia
* persistent change in bowel habits
* age >45 with new bleeding

---

## One-line intuition

Rectal bleeding is a symptom, not a diagnosis: the color, pain, stool changes, and amount of blood help localize where the bleeding is coming from.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Painless bright red blood on toilet paper = think internal hemorrhoids; painful bright red blood with bowel movement = think anal fissure.
