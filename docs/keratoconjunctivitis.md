### Core idea

Keratoconjunctivitis = inflammation of both the cornea and conjunctiva.

Break the word apart:

* kerato- = cornea, the clear front “window” of the eye
* conjunctiv- = conjunctiva, the thin membrane over the white of the eye and inner eyelids
* -itis = inflammation

So keratoconjunctivitis literally means:

> inflamed cornea + inflamed conjunctiva.

---

### Why it matters more than simple conjunctivitis

Conjunctivitis alone mainly affects the conjunctiva, so it causes:

* red eye
* tearing/discharge
* irritation

But keratoconjunctivitis also involves the cornea.

That is important because the cornea is:

* highly innervated, so inflammation causes pain and photophobia (light sensitivity)
* transparent, so swelling/scarring can blur vision
* the main focusing surface of the eye, so damage can threaten vision

---

### What it looks/feels like

Classic findings:

* red eye from conjunctival injection (dilated inflamed surface blood vessels)
* watery or mucous discharge
* foreign body sensation
* photophobia (light sensitivity)
* blurry vision if the cornea is involved
* corneal staining with fluorescein (yellow-green dye used to highlight epithelial defects)

Intuition:

> conjunctivitis makes the eye red and irritated; adding keratitis makes it painful, light-sensitive, and potentially vision-affecting.

---

### Common causes

Keratoconjunctivitis can be caused by:

* viral infection, especially adenovirus
* allergy, especially vernal keratoconjunctivitis in young patients
* dry eye, called keratoconjunctivitis sicca
* autoimmune disease, especially Sjogren syndrome (autoimmune destruction of moisture-producing glands)
* bacterial infection, especially if there is contact lens use or corneal injury

---

### Big Step 1 association

Keratoconjunctivitis sicca = dry eye severe enough to inflame the cornea and conjunctiva.

This is classically seen in Sjogren syndrome, which causes:

* dry eyes = keratoconjunctivitis sicca
* dry mouth = xerostomia
* autoimmune attack on lacrimal glands (tear glands) and salivary glands

---

## One-line intuition

Keratoconjunctivitis is “pink eye plus corneal involvement,” so think red irritated eye with more pain, light sensitivity, and possible blurry vision.

---

## HY pearl

Adenovirus classically causes epidemic keratoconjunctivitis, which is very contagious and can cause preauricular lymphadenopathy (tender lymph node in front of the ear).
