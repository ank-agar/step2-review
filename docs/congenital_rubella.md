### Core idea

Congenital rubella means fetal infection with rubella virus during pregnancy.

Congenital = present at birth.

Rubella = German measles, an enveloped positive-sense RNA virus.

Big Step 1 association:

* maternal rubella infection in the first trimester
* baby born with cataracts, deafness, and heart defects

---

### Classic triad

Think:

* cataracts
* sensorineural deafness
* patent ductus arteriosus

Sensorineural deafness means hearing loss from inner ear or cranial nerve VIII damage.

Patent ductus arteriosus means the fetal vessel connecting the pulmonary artery to the aorta stays open after birth.

---

### Why the findings happen

Rubella crosses the placenta and infects developing fetal tissues.

Early pregnancy is dangerous because organs are actively forming.

So if the virus disrupts development:

* eye development gets damaged → cataracts
* inner ear development gets damaged → sensorineural deafness
* heart development gets damaged → patent ductus arteriosus or pulmonary artery stenosis
* skin/blood formation gets abnormal → blueberry muffin rash

Blueberry muffin rash means purple-blue skin spots from extramedullary hematopoiesis, which is blood cell production outside the bone marrow.

---

### One-line intuition

Rubella in mom is mild, but rubella in the fetus is an organ-development disaster.

---

## Pearl

Congenital rubella is a TORCH infection.

TORCH means toxoplasmosis, other infections, rubella, cytomegalovirus, and herpes simplex virus.

HY clue: congenital cataracts + deafness + PDA = congenital rubella until proven otherwise.
