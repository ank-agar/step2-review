## Premature ovarian failure

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/66/Follicle_development_stages.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/40/Graafian_Follicle_Labelled.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/Steroidogenesis_in_theca_and_granulosa_cells.png)

Premature ovarian failure means the ovaries lose normal function before age 40.

Better term: **primary ovarian insufficiency**.

Why "insufficiency" is better:

* the ovaries are underfunctioning
* but they may not be completely dead
* some patients can still intermittently ovulate

---

### Core idea

The ovary cannot make enough:

* estrogen
* progesterone
* inhibin

So the pituitary gland tries harder by increasing:

* FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* LH (luteinizing hormone)

This is why it is a form of **hypergonadotropic hypogonadism**:

* hypergonadotropic = high gonadotropins, meaning high FSH/LH
* hypogonadism = low gonadal sex hormone function

---

### Why FSH is high

Normally, ovarian follicles make:

* estrogen
* inhibin B

These tell the pituitary:

> "We are working, so lower FSH."

In premature ovarian failure, the follicles are depleted or dysfunctional, so estrogen/inhibin fall.

No negative feedback means the pituitary releases more FSH.

So the classic labs are:

* high FSH
* low estrogen

---

### What it causes

Low estrogen causes:

* amenorrhea (no periods)
* infertility/subfertility
* hot flashes
* vaginal dryness
* decreased bone density

Amenorrhea happens because the endometrium (uterine lining) is not getting enough estrogen stimulation to build up normally.

---

### Causes to remember

Important causes include:

* Turner syndrome, 45,X
* FMR1 premutation, associated with fragile X syndrome
* autoimmune ovarian damage
* chemotherapy or radiation
* idiopathic, meaning no clear cause found

---

## One-line intuition

The ovary acts "older than expected," so estrogen drops and the pituitary yells at it with high FSH.

---

## HY pearl

Premature ovarian failure is **high FSH + low estrogen before age 40**.

Contrast with hypothalamic/pituitary causes of amenorrhea, where FSH and LH are low or inappropriately normal.
