### Core idea

A fibroadenoma is a benign breast tumor made of:

* fibrous stroma (connective tissue support)
* glandular breast tissue

Break the word apart:

* fibro- = fibrous connective tissue
* adeno- = gland
* -oma = tumor/growth

So fibroadenoma literally means:

> benign fibrous + glandular breast growth.

---

### Classic presentation

Usually in young women, often age 15-35:

* firm
* rubbery
* well-circumscribed
* mobile breast mass
* usually painless

Mobile is the key intuition: it is often called a "breast mouse" because it slips around under the fingers.

---

### Why it feels mobile

Fibroadenomas are usually well-circumscribed, meaning they have a clear border from surrounding tissue.

So instead of invading into nearby breast tissue, they push against it.

That is why they feel:

* smooth
* round/oval
* movable

This is different from invasive cancer, which can tether to nearby tissue and feel fixed.

---

### Hormone connection

Fibroadenomas are estrogen-sensitive.

That means they may:

* enlarge during pregnancy
* enlarge with estrogen exposure
* shrink after menopause

Intuition:

Breast gland/stromal tissue responds to estrogen, so this benign tumor can also respond to estrogen.

---

### Diagnosis idea

Ultrasound often shows a solid, oval, well-defined mass.

But imaging cannot always perfectly prove benign vs malignant, so biopsy may be done if the mass is atypical, growing, large, or clinically concerning.

---

## One-line intuition

A fibroadenoma is a benign, estrogen-sensitive, rubbery, mobile breast lump in a young woman.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: fibroadenoma is the most common benign breast tumor in young women, while invasive breast carcinoma is more concerning in older women, especially if the mass is hard, irregular, fixed, or associated with skin/nipple retraction.
