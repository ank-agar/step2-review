Pulmonary anthrax = inhalation anthrax.

It is a severe lung/chest infection caused by breathing in **Bacillus anthracis** spores.

Break it down:

* **pulmonary** = related to the lungs
* **anthrax** = infection by Bacillus anthracis
* **Bacillus anthracis** = gram-positive, spore-forming rod-shaped bacterium
* **spores** = dormant, tough bacterial survival forms

---

### Core idea

The spores are inhaled into the lungs, then macrophages carry them to the chest lymph nodes.

Then the spores germinate and release toxins.

So the big disease is not just "pneumonia." It is more like:

* toxic infection in the mediastinum
* hemorrhagic lymph node inflammation
* systemic toxicity

**Mediastinum** = the central chest area between the lungs, where the heart, great vessels, trachea, esophagus, and lymph nodes sit.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Flu-like early symptoms:** early infection can look nonspecific, like fever, malaise, cough, and body aches
* **Chest pain/shortness of breath:** inflammation and fluid build up in the chest
* **Widened mediastinum:** infected chest lymph nodes become swollen and hemorrhagic
* **Pleural effusion:** fluid collects around the lungs
* **Shock/death:** anthrax toxins cause severe systemic illness

**Pleural effusion** = fluid in the pleural space, which is the thin space around the lungs.

---

### Toxins to know

Bacillus anthracis has 3 toxin components:

* **Protective antigen** = lets toxin enter cells
* **Edema factor** = increases cAMP (cyclic adenosine monophosphate), causing edema
* **Lethal factor** = damages cells and causes tissue necrosis

High-yield intuition:

Protective antigen is like the doorway. Edema factor and lethal factor are the damaging passengers.

---

## One-line intuition

Pulmonary anthrax is inhaled spores that get carried to mediastinal lymph nodes, causing toxic hemorrhagic mediastinitis with widened mediastinum.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Classic Step 1 clue: **widened mediastinum + flu-like illness after exposure to animal hides/wool or bioterrorism** = inhalation anthrax.
