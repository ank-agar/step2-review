### Core idea

Condylomata acuminata = genital warts.

They are caused by HPV (human papillomavirus), usually:

* HPV 6
* HPV 11

These are low-risk HPV types, meaning they cause warts but are not the classic cervical cancer types.

---

### What they look like

They are usually:

* soft
* flesh-colored
* raised
* cauliflower-like
* on genital, perianal, or anal mucosa

Acuminata means pointed, so think:

> pointed/raised wart-like growths.

---

### Why HPV causes warts

HPV infects squamous epithelial cells, meaning the flat surface-lining cells of skin and mucosa.

The virus makes those epithelial cells proliferate, so the surface grows outward into papillary, cauliflower-like projections.

On histology, you can see:

* papillomatosis = finger-like upward growth of epithelium
* acanthosis = thickened squamous epithelium
* koilocytes = HPV-infected squamous cells with perinuclear clearing

Perinuclear clearing means a pale halo around the nucleus.

---

### Important distinction

Do not confuse:

* condylomata acuminata = HPV 6/11 genital warts
* condylomata lata = secondary syphilis

Condylomata lata are broad, flat, moist lesions and are caused by Treponema pallidum.

---

## One-line intuition

HPV 6/11 makes genital squamous epithelium overgrow into soft cauliflower-like warts.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: HPV 6 and 11 cause condylomata acuminata and recurrent respiratory papillomatosis, while HPV 16 and 18 are the high-risk cancer-associated types.
