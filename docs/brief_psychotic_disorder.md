### Core idea

Brief psychotic disorder is a sudden, short episode of psychosis.

Psychosis = impaired reality testing.

Meaning the person may have:

* delusions (fixed false beliefs)
* hallucinations (perceiving something that is not there)
* disorganized speech
* grossly disorganized behavior
* catatonia (abnormal motor behavior, like immobility or strange posturing)

The key is duration:

* at least 1 day
* less than 1 month
* then full return to baseline functioning

---

### Why it happens

Think of it as the brain temporarily losing normal reality-filtering.

So thoughts/perceptions that should be checked against reality can become:

* false beliefs = delusions
* false perceptions = hallucinations
* chaotic thinking = disorganized speech/behavior

It can happen after a major stressor, like trauma, grief, or severe life stress.

But it should not be due to:

* substances
* medications
* medical illness
* mood disorder with psychotic features

---

### How to separate it from similar disorders

The easiest way is time:

* brief psychotic disorder = 1 day to less than 1 month
* schizophreniform disorder = 1 to 6 months
* schizophrenia = more than 6 months

So the word **brief** is doing a lot of work here.

---

## One-line intuition

Brief psychotic disorder is a short burst of psychosis that lasts under 1 month and then fully clears.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 distinction:

**Brief psychotic disorder returns to baseline**, while schizophrenia is chronic and requires symptoms/signs for more than 6 months.
