### Core idea

Acne vulgaris is inflammation of the pilosebaceous unit (hair follicle + sebaceous gland).

Think:

* follicle gets plugged
* sebum (oil) builds up
* Cutibacterium acnes (skin bacteria formerly called Propionibacterium acnes) grows
* inflammation creates pimples

---

### Why it happens

The main problem starts with a clogged follicle.

Keratinocytes (skin cells that make keratin) stick together too much, so they do not shed normally. That makes a keratin plug.

Then:

* **Androgens (sex hormones like testosterone)** increase sebum production
* **Sebum** feeds Cutibacterium acnes
* **Cutibacterium acnes** triggers inflammation
* **Inflammation** causes papules, pustules, nodules, and sometimes scars

So acne is not just “dirty skin.”

It is:

> plugged follicle + oil + bacteria + inflammation.

---

### Lesions

* **Open comedone:** blackhead
* **Closed comedone:** whitehead
* **Papule:** small inflamed bump
* **Pustule:** inflamed bump with pus
* **Nodule/cyst:** deeper painful inflammatory lesion

Blackheads are black because the keratin/sebum plug is exposed to air and oxidizes, not because it is dirt.

---

### Where it shows up

Commonly affects:

* face
* chest
* upper back

because those areas have many sebaceous glands (oil glands).

---

## One-line intuition

Acne vulgaris is puberty/oil-driven plugging of hair follicles that lets Cutibacterium acnes trigger inflammation.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Isotretinoin (vitamin A derivative) is highly effective for severe nodulocystic acne because it shrinks sebaceous glands and decreases sebum, but it is teratogenic (causes birth defects), so pregnancy prevention is critical.
