## Vitamin B6 deficiency

### Core idea

Vitamin B6 = pyridoxine.

Its active form is **PLP (pyridoxal phosphate)**.

PLP is a cofactor, meaning a helper molecule enzymes need to work.

So vitamin B6 deficiency = low PLP = enzymes that depend on PLP slow down.

---

### What B6 is used for

PLP is especially important for:

* amino acid metabolism
* neurotransmitter synthesis
* heme synthesis

Big Step 1 idea:

> B6 helps make heme because PLP is required for ALA synthase.

ALA synthase = aminolevulinic acid synthase, the rate-limiting enzyme in heme synthesis.

---

### Why anemia happens

Low B6 means low PLP.

Low PLP means ALA synthase works poorly.

So the body cannot make heme well.

If iron cannot be incorporated into heme, it gets stuck in mitochondria around the nucleus of red blood cell precursors.

That causes:

* sideroblastic anemia
* ring sideroblasts
* microcytic anemia

Microcytic anemia = small red blood cells.

Intuition:

> iron is present, but the cell cannot use it properly to build heme.

---

### Classic causes

Most important cause for Step 1:

* **isoniazid**

Isoniazid is a tuberculosis drug that can deplete vitamin B6.

Other causes:

* alcoholism
* malnutrition
* pregnancy
* certain drugs

---

### Symptoms

Because B6 is involved in skin, nerves, blood, and neurotransmitters, deficiency can cause:

* peripheral neuropathy
* seizures
* irritability/confusion
* glossitis
* cheilosis
* seborrheic dermatitis
* sideroblastic anemia

Peripheral neuropathy = nerve damage causing numbness, tingling, or burning, usually in hands/feet.

Glossitis = inflamed tongue.

Cheilosis = cracked lips or mouth corners.

Seborrheic dermatitis = greasy/scaly rash, often on the face/scalp.

---

### Why seizures can happen

PLP is needed to make **GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid)**.

GABA is the main inhibitory neurotransmitter, meaning it calms neuronal firing.

Low B6 → low GABA → neurons become too excitable → seizures.

---

## One-line intuition

Vitamin B6 deficiency breaks PLP-dependent reactions, especially heme synthesis and neurotransmitter synthesis, causing sideroblastic anemia + neurologic symptoms.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Isoniazid can cause vitamin B6 deficiency, so patients taking isoniazid are often given pyridoxine to prevent peripheral neuropathy and sideroblastic anemia.
