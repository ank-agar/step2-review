# Virilization

Virilization means development of male-pattern features due to excess androgens (male sex hormones like testosterone and dihydrotestosterone).

In females, this can look like:

* hirsutism (male-pattern terminal hair growth)
* deep voice
* acne
* increased muscle mass
* clitoromegaly (enlarged clitoris)
* menstrual irregularity

---

### Core idea

Too much androgen pushes tissues toward a male-pattern phenotype.

Think:

> androgen excess = masculinizing changes

---

### Why it happens

Androgens stimulate androgen receptors in:

* hair follicles → coarse facial/chest/abdominal hair
* larynx (voice box) → deeper voice
* sebaceous glands (oil glands) → acne
* external genital tissue → clitoromegaly
* hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis (brain-ovary hormone loop) → irregular menses

Deep voice and clitoromegaly are especially important because they suggest more severe androgen excess than simple hirsutism.

---

### Common Step 1 causes

* congenital adrenal hyperplasia, or CAH (inherited enzyme defect in adrenal steroid synthesis)
* androgen-secreting adrenal tumor
* androgen-secreting ovarian tumor
* exogenous androgens (outside testosterone/anabolic steroid exposure)

In CAH, especially 21-hydroxylase deficiency, cortisol synthesis is blocked.

So precursors get shunted into androgen production:

> low cortisol → high ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone) → adrenal hyperplasia → excess androgens

---

## One-line intuition

Virilization is what happens when androgen levels are high enough to cause male-pattern body changes, especially in a female patient.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Rapid-onset virilization is a red flag for an androgen-secreting tumor, while slower hirsutism is more commonly from PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome).
