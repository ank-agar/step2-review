Balanitis = inflammation of the glans penis.

Glans penis = the head/tip of the penis.

If the foreskin is also inflamed, that is called balanoposthitis:

* balano- = glans
* posthitis = foreskin inflammation

So:

> balanitis = inflamed head of the penis.

---

### Core idea

The glans gets irritated/inflamed, causing:

* redness
* itching or burning
* pain/tenderness
* swelling
* discharge or odor sometimes

This is more common in uncircumcised patients because the foreskin can trap moisture, smegma, urine, and microbes against the glans.

Smegma = buildup of shed skin cells, oils, and moisture under the foreskin.

---

### Why it happens

Common causes:

* Candida infection
* poor hygiene
* diabetes mellitus
* irritants like soaps/fragrances
* sexually transmitted infections, aka STIs
* inflammatory skin disease

Why diabetes matters:

* high glucose in urine/skin environment helps Candida grow
* impaired immunity makes infections more likely

Candida = yeast/fungus.

---

### One-line intuition

Balanitis is basically a red, irritated, inflamed glans, often because moisture + microbes get trapped under the foreskin.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Recurrent Candida balanitis should make you think about diabetes mellitus, because high glucose predisposes to yeast infections.
