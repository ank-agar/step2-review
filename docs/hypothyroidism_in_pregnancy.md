# Hypothyroidism in pregnancy

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17772/N00726-H.jpg)

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/-/media/Images/Health-Information/Endocrine-Diseases/ThyroidAnatomyLowRes_300x324.jpg)

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/-/media/Images/Health-Information/Endocrine-Diseases/PregnantTakingPill_330x220.jpg)

Hypothyroidism in pregnancy means the thyroid gland is not making enough thyroid hormone for both:

* the mother
* the developing fetus

Thyroid hormone mainly means:

* T4 (thyroxine)
* T3 (triiodothyronine)

TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) is the pituitary signal that tells the thyroid to make more hormone.

So classic primary hypothyroidism is:

* high TSH
* low free T4

because the pituitary is yelling at a thyroid that is not responding well.

---

### Why pregnancy makes this important

Early in pregnancy, the fetus depends heavily on maternal T4 (thyroxine), especially for brain and nervous system development.

The fetal thyroid starts working around 12 weeks, but it does not make enough thyroid hormone until around 18-20 weeks.

So untreated maternal hypothyroidism is most dangerous early because the baby cannot fully compensate yet.

---

### Common cause

Most commonly:

* Hashimoto disease

Hashimoto disease is an autoimmune disease where antibodies attack the thyroid gland, causing chronic thyroid inflammation and decreased hormone production.

---

### Why the bad outcomes happen

Low maternal thyroid hormone can cause:

* miscarriage
* preeclampsia
* anemia
* low birth weight
* impaired fetal neurodevelopment

Reason:

Thyroid hormone is like a metabolic and developmental signal. If the fetus does not get enough T4 early, brain development can be affected.

---

### Treatment

Treat with:

* levothyroxine

Levothyroxine is synthetic T4 (thyroxine).

This matters because T4 can enter the fetal brain and then be converted into T3 (triiodothyronine), the more active thyroid hormone.

T3-only or animal thyroid preparations are not preferred in pregnancy because fetal brain development depends mainly on maternal T4 supply.

---

## One-line intuition

Pregnancy increases thyroid hormone demand, and if mom cannot make enough T4, the fetus loses a key early brain-development signal.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In pregnancy, hypothyroid patients often need a higher levothyroxine dose because thyroid hormone requirement increases during pregnancy.
