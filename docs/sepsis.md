### Core idea

Sepsis is **life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection**.

Translation:

* infection happens somewhere
* immune system overreacts systemically
* blood vessels get leaky and dilated
* organs do not get enough effective blood flow
* organ failure can happen

So sepsis is not just “bacteria in the blood.”

It is the body’s inflammatory response becoming dangerous.

---

### Why the findings happen

Infection triggers inflammatory cytokines (immune signaling proteins), especially:

* TNF-alpha (tumor necrosis factor alpha)
* IL-1 (interleukin-1)
* IL-6 (interleukin-6)

These cause:

* **fever:** cytokines reset the hypothalamus (temperature control center)
* **tachycardia:** fast heart rate because the body is trying to maintain perfusion (blood delivery)
* **tachypnea:** fast breathing, often from metabolic acidosis (acid buildup in blood)
* **hypotension:** low blood pressure from vasodilation (blood vessel widening)
* **warm skin early:** dilated vessels send more blood to skin
* **cool/clammy skin later:** shock worsens and blood is shunted to vital organs
* **confusion:** brain is not getting enough oxygen/glucose delivery
* **high lactate:** tissues switch toward anaerobic metabolism (energy production without enough oxygen)

---

### Big mechanism

Sepsis causes:

* vasodilation
* capillary leak
* endothelial damage (damage to blood vessel lining)
* microthrombi (tiny clots)

That means organs can be injured from both:

* low perfusion
* tiny clots blocking small vessels

This is why severe sepsis can lead to DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation), where clotting gets activated everywhere and then clotting factors get consumed.

---

### Septic shock

Septic shock is sepsis plus persistent dangerous hypotension despite fluids, often with elevated lactate.

Intuition:

The vessels are so dilated and leaky that the circulatory system becomes like an over-expanded, leaky pipe system.

Even if the heart pumps, pressure and tissue perfusion stay poor.

---

## One-line intuition

Sepsis = infection makes the immune system overreact, causing leaky dilated vessels, poor organ perfusion, and possible shock.
