## Paraneoplastic cerebellar degeneration

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/Diagram_of_the_Microanatomy_of_Human_Cerebellar_Cortex.svg/1280px-Diagram_of_the_Microanatomy_of_Human_Cerebellar_Cortex.svg.png)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/287c/6238560/45512014de8c/AIAN-21-311-g001.jpg)

![Image](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/1476397/xml-images/fimmu-15-1476397-g001.webp)

Paraneoplastic cerebellar degeneration is when a cancer triggers the immune system to attack the cerebellum.

Paraneoplastic = cancer-associated but not from direct tumor invasion.

Cerebellar = involving the cerebellum, which coordinates movement.

Degeneration = neurons are being damaged/lost.

So the core idea is:

> cancer makes antigens (immune-recognized proteins) that look like cerebellar antigens, so antibodies (immune proteins that bind targets) and T cells (immune cells that kill or coordinate immune attacks) attack cerebellar neurons, especially Purkinje cells.

Purkinje cells are major inhibitory neurons in the cerebellar cortex. If they are damaged, the cerebellum loses its ability to smooth and coordinate movement.

---

### What symptoms happen?

Because the cerebellum controls coordination, patients get:

* ataxia (uncoordinated movement/walking)
* dysarthria (slurred speech)
* nystagmus (rhythmic eye movements)
* vertigo/dizziness
* dysmetria (overshooting/undershooting a target)
* intention tremor (tremor worse when reaching for something)

Intuition:

The muscles are not weak. The "movement calibration system" is broken.

---

### Why does cancer cause this?

Some tumors express proteins that are normally found in neurons.

The immune system sees the tumor protein and says:

> attack this foreign-looking thing.

But then it also attacks similar proteins in the cerebellum.

That is why the neurologic symptoms can appear before the cancer is even found.

---

### Classic cancer associations

Common associated malignancies:

* breast cancer
* ovarian cancer
* small cell lung carcinoma
* Hodgkin lymphoma

Important antibodies:

* anti-Yo: classically breast/ovarian cancer
* anti-Hu: classically small cell lung carcinoma
* anti-Tr: classically Hodgkin lymphoma

---

## One-line intuition

A hidden cancer accidentally trains the immune system to attack Purkinje cells, causing rapid cerebellar ataxia.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Paraneoplastic cerebellar degeneration often causes subacute ataxia, meaning symptoms worsen over days to weeks, and MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) can be normal early before later showing cerebellar atrophy.
