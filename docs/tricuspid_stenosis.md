# Tricuspid stenosis

![Image](https://www.secondscount.org/sites/default/files/2023-05/tricuspid-valve-stenosis.svg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499990/bin/MPG__TS.jpg)

### Core idea

Tricuspid stenosis is narrowing of the tricuspid valve, so blood has trouble moving from the right atrium (right upper heart chamber) into the right ventricle (right lower heart chamber).

Think:

* tight valve between right atrium and right ventricle
* blood backs up into systemic veins
* less blood reaches the lungs

---

### Why the findings happen

The right atrium has to push blood through a narrowed valve during diastole (heart relaxation/filling phase).

That causes:

* **Diastolic murmur:** blood is trying to flow through the tight tricuspid valve during ventricular filling
* **Louder with inspiration:** inspiration increases venous return (blood returning to the heart), so right-sided murmurs get louder
* **Jugular venous distension:** blood backs up into neck veins
* **Hepatomegaly/ascites/leg edema:** venous blood backs up into the liver, abdomen, and legs
* **Fatigue:** less blood passes through the right heart to the lungs, so less oxygenated blood eventually reaches the body

---

### Causes

Most classically:

* rheumatic heart disease

Other causes:

* carcinoid syndrome (serotonin-secreting neuroendocrine tumor syndrome)
* congenital valve abnormality
* infective endocarditis (infection of the heart valve/endocardium)

---

### Buzzwords

* diastolic rumble at the left lower sternal border
* increases with inspiration
* prominent a waves in JVP (jugular venous pulse)
* right atrial enlargement
* systemic venous congestion

---

## One-line intuition

Tricuspid stenosis is like a tight doorway from the right atrium to the right ventricle, so blood piles up behind it in the body veins.

---

## Pearl 🔥

On Step 1, tricuspid stenosis is usually rheumatic and often appears with mitral stenosis.

Easy distinction:

* mitral stenosis backs blood up into the lungs
* tricuspid stenosis backs blood up into the body veins
