Heroin use disorder is a type of **opioid use disorder** where a person compulsively uses heroin despite harm.

Heroin is an opioid (drug that activates opioid receptors), mainly acting at **mu opioid receptors** (opioid receptors that cause analgesia, euphoria, respiratory depression, and constipation).

---

### Core idea

Heroin strongly activates the brain reward pathway:

* mu opioid receptor activation
* increased dopamine (reward neurotransmitter) in the nucleus accumbens (reward center)
* euphoria
* repeated use → tolerance and dependence

Tolerance means needing more drug to get the same effect.

Dependence means the body adapts to the drug, so stopping causes withdrawal.

---

### Why the findings happen

Heroin is a **CNS depressant** (central nervous system depressant), so intoxication causes:

* euphoria
* sedation
* miosis (pinpoint pupils)
* respiratory depression
* constipation

Why respiratory depression?

Opioids suppress the brainstem respiratory centers, so the brain becomes less responsive to carbon dioxide (CO2), which normally tells you to breathe.

---

### Withdrawal

Opioid withdrawal is basically the opposite of opioid intoxication:

* mydriasis (dilated pupils)
* diarrhea
* sweating
* lacrimation (tearing)
* rhinorrhea (runny nose)
* yawning
* piloerection (goosebumps)
* muscle aches

Intuition:

Opioids slow everything down, especially the GI (gastrointestinal) tract and autonomic system. When the drug is removed, the body rebounds in the opposite direction.

---

### Treatment

Main treatments include:

* methadone: full opioid agonist (fully activates opioid receptors)
* buprenorphine: partial opioid agonist (activates opioid receptors but has a ceiling effect)
* naltrexone: opioid antagonist (blocks opioid receptors)
* naloxone: opioid antagonist used acutely for overdose

Naloxone reverses overdose because it kicks opioids off the mu opioid receptor and restores breathing.

---

## One-line intuition

Heroin use disorder is the brain learning to prioritize heroin reward, then the body becoming dependent on heroin to feel normal.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Opioid intoxication classically causes the triad:

* coma
* respiratory depression
* pinpoint pupils

Treat acute overdose with naloxone.
