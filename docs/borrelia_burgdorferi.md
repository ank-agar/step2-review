### Core idea

Borrelia burgdorferi is a spirochete (spiral-shaped bacteria) that causes Lyme disease.

It is transmitted by the Ixodes tick (deer tick/blacklegged tick).

---

### Why it causes Lyme findings

It enters through a tick bite, then can spread through blood to multiple tissues:

* skin
* joints
* heart
* nervous system

That is why Lyme disease can cause:

* erythema migrans (expanding red rash, classically bull's-eye)
* migratory arthritis (joint inflammation that moves around)
* facial nerve palsy (weakness of facial muscles)
* carditis (heart inflammation), especially AV block (atrioventricular conduction block)

---

### Why the rash expands

The bacteria move outward from the bite site through the skin.

So the immune reaction also spreads outward, making an enlarging ring-like rash.

That is erythema migrans:

* erythema = redness
* migrans = migrating/spreading

---

### Transmission clue

Ixodes ticks are tiny, especially the nymph stage (immature tick stage), so patients often do not remember a tick bite.

Classically associated with:

* Northeast United States
* upper Midwest United States
* wooded/grass areas
* deer and mice reservoirs

---

### Diagnosis/treatment idea

Early Lyme with classic erythema migrans is usually treated clinically.

Treatment:

* doxycycline for most patients
* amoxicillin in pregnancy or young children when doxycycline is avoided

Testing uses antibodies:

* ELISA (enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay) screening test
* Western blot confirmatory test

But early disease can be antibody-negative because the immune system has not made enough IgM (immunoglobulin M) or IgG (immunoglobulin G) yet.

---

## One-line intuition

Borrelia burgdorferi is a corkscrew-shaped tick-borne bacteria that spreads outward in skin and later can spread to joints, heart, and nerves.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Borrelia burgdorferi is one of the spirochetes. The big Step 1 spirochetes are:

* Borrelia burgdorferi = Lyme disease
* Treponema pallidum = syphilis
* Leptospira interrogans = leptospirosis

Memory hook: spirochetes "spiral" through the body and cause systemic disease.
