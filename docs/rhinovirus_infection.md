## Rhinovirus infection

Rhinovirus infection is basically the classic **common cold**.

Break the word apart:

* rhino- = nose
* virus = infectious particle that replicates inside host cells

So:

> rhinovirus = "nose virus"

It mainly infects the **upper respiratory tract** (nose, throat, sinuses, and larynx).

Common symptoms:

* runny nose
* nasal congestion
* sneezing
* sore throat
* cough
* mild headache/body aches
* sometimes low-grade fever

---

### Core idea

Rhinovirus infects the nasal mucosa (mucosa = moist epithelial lining), triggers local inflammation, and causes cold symptoms.

The symptoms are mostly from your immune response, not from massive tissue destruction.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Runny nose:** inflammation makes nasal blood vessels leaky, so fluid enters the nasal passages
* **Congestion:** swollen nasal mucosa narrows the airway
* **Sneezing:** irritation of nasal sensory nerves
* **Sore throat:** postnasal drip plus local pharyngeal irritation
* **Cough:** mucus dripping down + airway irritation

So the intuition is:

> virus irritates the upper airway lining, and your immune system creates most of the "cold" feeling.

---

### Virology details

Rhinovirus is a:

* **positive-sense single-stranded RNA virus** (RNA = ribonucleic acid; positive-sense means the viral genome can act like messenger RNA)
* **nonenveloped virus** (no lipid membrane envelope)
* member of the **Picornaviridae** family (pico = small, RNA = ribonucleic acid)

Nonenveloped viruses are generally tougher in the environment, which helps rhinovirus spread by hands, surfaces, and respiratory droplets.

---

### Important association

Rhinovirus is usually mild, but it can trigger:

* asthma exacerbations
* chronic obstructive pulmonary disease exacerbations (COPD = chronic obstructive pulmonary disease)
* otitis media (middle ear infection)
* sinusitis (sinus infection)

---

## One-line intuition

Rhinovirus is a small, tough, nonenveloped RNA virus that loves the nose and causes the common cold by inflaming the upper airway mucosa.

---

## HY pearl

Rhinovirus grows best at cooler temperatures around the nasal cavity, so it mainly causes **upper** respiratory symptoms rather than deep lung infection.
