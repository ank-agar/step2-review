### Core idea

AIDS dementia complex is severe HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder (HAND = HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder) from advanced HIV/AIDS (human immunodeficiency virus/acquired immunodeficiency syndrome) affecting the brain.

It causes a slow decline in:

* thinking/memory
* behavior/personality
* movement

Classic vibe:

> dementia + slowed movements in a patient with advanced HIV.

---

### Why it happens

HIV does not mainly infect neurons directly.

Instead, HIV gets into the CNS (central nervous system) through infected monocytes/macrophages (immune cells that can enter tissues), then activates/infects microglia (brain immune cells).

That leads to:

* chronic inflammation
* cytokine release (immune signaling chemicals)
* viral protein toxicity
* white matter and basal ganglia injury

So the neurons get damaged indirectly.

---

### Symptoms

Think slow brain + slow body:

* poor concentration
* memory problems
* apathy/depression-like behavior
* psychomotor slowing (slow thinking and slow movement)
* gait problems
* tremor or weakness

This is more subcortical dementia (basal ganglia/white matter pattern) than Alzheimer-type cortical dementia.

---

### Path findings

Histology can show:

* microglial nodules
* multinucleated giant cells
* perivascular macrophages/lymphocytes
* gliosis (scarring/reactive glial response)
* myelin pallor/loss

Key Step 1 phrase:

> multinucleated giant cells in the brain = HIV encephalitis clue.

---

## One-line intuition

HIV sneaks immune cells into the brain, activates microglia/macrophages, and the inflammatory damage causes slow thinking, behavioral change, and motor slowing.

---

## Pearl 🔥

AIDS dementia complex is usually associated with advanced HIV and low CD4 count (cluster of differentiation 4 T-cell count), but ART (antiretroviral therapy) greatly reduced the classic severe form.
