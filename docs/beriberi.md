Beriberi is disease from **thiamine deficiency**.

Thiamine = **vitamin B1**.

Core idea:

* low B1 means low ATP (cell energy)
* nerves and heart are very energy-dependent
* so B1 deficiency mainly hurts:
  * peripheral nerves
  * heart
  * brain, if severe

---

### Why thiamine deficiency causes problems

Thiamine is needed for carbohydrate metabolism.

Important enzymes that need thiamine:

* pyruvate dehydrogenase
* alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase
* branched-chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase
* transketolase

These enzymes help turn glucose into usable energy.

So if thiamine is low:

* pyruvate cannot efficiently enter the TCA cycle (tricarboxylic acid cycle)
* pyruvate gets shunted to lactate
* ATP production drops

That is why severe thiamine deficiency can cause:

* lactic acidosis
* neuropathy
* heart failure
* brain dysfunction

---

### Dry beriberi

Dry beriberi = mainly **neurologic**.

Findings:

* symmetric peripheral neuropathy
* weakness
* decreased reflexes
* muscle wasting
* foot drop/wrist drop sometimes

Intuition:

Nerves need lots of ATP to maintain ion gradients and axonal function. Without thiamine, nerves cannot make enough energy, so they malfunction and degenerate.

---

### Wet beriberi

Wet beriberi = mainly **cardiac**.

Findings:

* high-output heart failure
* tachycardia
* cardiomegaly
* edema
* dyspnea (shortness of breath)

Why "wet"?

Because heart failure causes fluid buildup, especially peripheral edema.

Intuition:

The heart is trying to pump harder, but it is energy-starved. Also, peripheral vasodilation lowers systemic vascular resistance, so the heart compensates with high output until it fails.

---

### Cerebral thiamine deficiency

In the brain, thiamine deficiency can cause **Wernicke encephalopathy**.

Classic triad:

* confusion
* ophthalmoplegia (eye movement weakness/paralysis)
* ataxia (uncoordinated walking)

If untreated, it can progress to **Korsakoff syndrome**:

* memory loss
* confabulation (making up stories to fill memory gaps)

---

## One-line intuition

Beriberi = low vitamin B1 makes cells unable to use glucose properly, so high-energy tissues like nerves and heart start failing.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In a malnourished or alcoholic patient, give **thiamine before glucose** because glucose metabolism uses thiamine and can acutely worsen Wernicke encephalopathy if thiamine is already depleted.
