# Hyper IgM syndrome

![Image](https://upload.medbullets.com/topic/105012/images/bcellclassswitching.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6e/Pneumocystis.jpg/500px-Pneumocystis.jpg)

Hyper IgM syndrome is an immunodeficiency where B cells cannot class switch.

Class switching means changing antibody type from:

* IgM (immunoglobulin M)

to:

* IgG (immunoglobulin G)
* IgA (immunoglobulin A)
* IgE (immunoglobulin E)

So the labs are:

* increased IgM
* decreased IgG, IgA, and IgE

---

### Core idea

B cells are stuck making the first antibody type: IgM.

They cannot upgrade to the more specialized antibody types.

---

### Why it happens

Most commonly, it is X-linked recessive due to defective CD40L (CD40 ligand) on helper T cells.

Normal process:

* helper T cell uses CD40L
* CD40L binds CD40 on the B cell
* this tells the B cell to class switch

In hyper IgM syndrome:

* CD40L is defective
* B cell never gets the class-switch signal
* B cell stays stuck making IgM

That is why IgM is high, but IgG, IgA, and IgE are low.

---

### Why infections happen

IgG (immunoglobulin G) helps opsonize bacteria, meaning it coats microbes so phagocytes can eat them.

Low IgG causes recurrent pyogenic infections, meaning pus-forming bacterial infections.

IgA (immunoglobulin A) protects mucosal surfaces like the gut and respiratory tract.

Low IgA causes sinopulmonary infections and diarrhea.

CD40L also helps activate macrophages, which are immune cells that kill intracellular organisms.

So patients can also get opportunistic infections like:

* Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia
* Cryptosporidium diarrhea

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* young boy
* recurrent bacterial infections
* opportunistic infections
* increased IgM
* decreased IgG, IgA, and IgE
* normal B-cell number

---

## One-line intuition

Hyper IgM syndrome = B cells can make the default antibody, IgM, but cannot switch to better antibody classes.

---

## HY pearl

Hyper IgM syndrome is a combined B-cell and T-cell problem because CD40L is on helper T cells, but the main antibody finding is failure of B-cell class switching.
