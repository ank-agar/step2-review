### Core idea

A hordeolum is a stye: an acute, painful infection/inflammation of an eyelid gland.

Usually caused by Staphylococcus aureus.

---

### Two types

* External hordeolum: infection of glands near the eyelash follicle
  * glands of Zeis = sebaceous oil glands near eyelashes
  * glands of Moll = sweat glands near eyelashes
* Internal hordeolum: infection of a meibomian gland
  * meibomian gland = oil gland inside the tarsal plate of the eyelid

So external is more at the lash line, internal is deeper in the eyelid.

---

### Why it hurts

The gland gets blocked/infected, then pus and inflammation build up in a tiny closed space.

That causes:

* red bump
* tenderness
* swelling
* sometimes a small yellow pustule

Think of it like a small abscess of an eyelid gland.

---

### Hordeolum vs chalazion

Hordeolum:

* acute
* painful
* infectious/inflammatory

Chalazion:

* chronic
* usually painless
* blocked meibomian gland with granulomatous inflammation

Granulomatous inflammation means macrophages wall off retained material, kind of like the body is reacting to trapped lipid/oil.

---

## One-line intuition

A hordeolum is a painful infected eyelid oil gland, while a chalazion is more like a painless leftover clogged gland.

---

## Pearl

Recurrent or persistent "chalazion" in an older adult should raise concern for sebaceous carcinoma, a malignant tumor of sebaceous oil glands.
