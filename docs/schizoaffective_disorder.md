### Core idea

Schizoaffective disorder = schizophrenia-type psychosis + a mood disorder.

So the patient has:

* psychosis (loss of contact with reality), like delusions (fixed false beliefs) or hallucinations (perceiving something that is not there)
* mood episodes, like mania (abnormally elevated or irritable mood with increased energy) or depression (low mood/loss of interest)

---

### The key diagnostic clue

There must be at least 2 weeks of psychosis without mood symptoms.

That means:

* if psychosis only happens during depression/mania -> mood disorder with psychotic features
* if psychosis happens even when mood is normal -> think schizoaffective disorder

---

### Why this matters

Schizoaffective disorder is not just "bipolar disorder plus schizophrenia symptoms."

The timing matters.

The brain has a schizophrenia-like psychotic process that can run independently, but the patient also has major mood episodes for a large part of the illness.

---

### Types

* Bipolar type: includes mania, with or without depression
* Depressive type: includes depression only

---

### One-line intuition

Schizoaffective disorder is psychosis that can stand on its own, plus major mood episodes riding along with it.

---

## HY pearl

High-yield (HY) test distinction:

* bipolar disorder with psychotic features = psychosis only during mood episodes
* schizoaffective disorder = at least 2 weeks of psychosis without mood symptoms
* schizophrenia = mood symptoms are absent or only a minor part of the illness
