## Adjustment disorder

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/ad79/3701359/57a72ebe9614/IJPsyM-35-4-g002.jpg)

Adjustment disorder is an emotional or behavioral reaction to an identifiable stressor that is stronger than expected and causes functional impairment.

In simple terms:

> a stressful event happens, and the patient's response is too intense or too impairing for what you would expect.

---

### Core idea

There must be a clear trigger, like:

* divorce
* job loss
* moving
* illness diagnosis
* school or family stress

Symptoms can look like:

* depressed mood
* anxiety
* irritability
* trouble sleeping
* poor concentration
* acting out or conduct problems

---

### Timing

Classically:

* symptoms begin within 3 months of the stressor
* symptoms do not persist more than 6 months after the stressor or its consequences end

That timing is a major clue.

---

### Why it happens

The brain is trying to adapt to a stressor.

Normally:

* stressor happens
* stress response activates
* coping mechanisms help the person return toward baseline

In adjustment disorder:

* the stress response is too intense
* coping does not fully keep up
* mood, anxiety, behavior, or function gets disrupted

So the problem is not “no stress allowed.”

The problem is:

> the reaction is disproportionate and impairing.

---

### How to distinguish it

Adjustment disorder vs major depressive disorder (MDD, major depressive disorder):

* adjustment disorder = clear stressor + does not meet full MDD criteria
* MDD = full depressive syndrome, like sleep/appetite change, guilt, low energy, poor concentration, suicidal ideation, etc.

Adjustment disorder vs PTSD (post-traumatic stress disorder):

* adjustment disorder = stressor can be any major life stress
* PTSD = exposure to actual/threatened death, serious injury, or sexual violence, plus intrusion/avoidance/hyperarousal symptoms

Adjustment disorder vs normal stress:

* normal stress = upset but still functioning reasonably
* adjustment disorder = distress is excessive or function is impaired

---

## One-line intuition

Adjustment disorder is when a real-life stressor causes a maladaptive emotional/behavioral response that is too intense and functionally impairing, but does not meet criteria for another specific disorder.

---

## Pearl 🔥

On exams, adjustment disorder usually has a clear recent stressor, symptoms within 3 months, and resolution within 6 months after the stressor ends.
