### Core idea

Endocervicitis means inflammation of the endocervix (the inner mucus-producing canal of the cervix).

Break it down:

* endo- = inside
* cervix = lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina
* -itis = inflammation

So:

> inflammation inside the cervical canal.

---

### What causes it

Most Step 1-relevant causes are STIs (sexually transmitted infections), especially:

* Chlamydia trachomatis
* Neisseria gonorrhoeae

Can also be from:

* Trichomonas vaginalis
* HSV (herpes simplex virus)
* irritation/trauma

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The endocervix is lined by columnar epithelium (tall mucus-secreting cells).

That matters because organisms like Chlamydia and Gonorrhea infect this mucosal surface, causing inflammation.

Inflammation causes:

* mucopurulent discharge = mucus + pus from neutrophils (acute inflammatory cells)
* cervical friability = cervix bleeds easily because inflamed tissue is fragile
* postcoital bleeding = bleeding after sex
* pelvic pain if infection ascends upward

---

### Big complication

If infection spreads upward from cervix to uterus/fallopian tubes, it can cause PID (pelvic inflammatory disease).

PID can lead to:

* infertility
* ectopic pregnancy
* chronic pelvic pain

Why? Inflamed fallopian tubes can scar, so the egg cannot move normally.

---

## One-line intuition

Endocervicitis is an infected/inflamed cervical canal, classically from Chlamydia or Gonorrhea, causing pus-like discharge and an easily bleeding cervix.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Chlamydia is the most common bacterial STI (sexually transmitted infection) in the United States and often causes cervicitis with a negative Gram stain because it is an obligate intracellular organism.
