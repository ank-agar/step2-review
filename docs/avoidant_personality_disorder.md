## Avoidant personality disorder

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/9761-avoidant-personality-disorder)

![Image](https://bellevuechristiancounseling.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/signs-and-symptoms-of-avoidance-behavior-4.jpg)

Avoidant personality disorder is a **Cluster C personality disorder** (an anxious/fearful personality disorder) where the person:

* wants relationships
* feels inferior or socially inadequate
* avoids people because rejection or criticism feels unbearable

So the key idea is:

> They are not uninterested in people; they are scared of being rejected by people.

---

### Why the avoidance happens

The internal loop is usually:

* “I am awkward / inferior”
* “They will criticize or reject me”
* “If I avoid the situation, I cannot be embarrassed”

Avoidance gives short-term relief, but long-term it reinforces the fear and causes isolation.

---

### Classic findings

* avoids jobs or activities with lots of interpersonal contact
* unwilling to get involved unless sure they will be liked
* restrained in close relationships due to fear of shame
* preoccupied with criticism or rejection
* sees self as socially inept or inferior
* avoids risks/new activities because of possible embarrassment

---

### Important distinction

**Schizoid personality disorder** = does **not desire** close relationships.

**Avoidant personality disorder** = **desires** close relationships but avoids them due to fear of rejection.

---

## One-line intuition

Avoidant personality disorder is “I want connection, but I am so afraid of rejection that I isolate myself.”

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember:

* **Avoidant** = wants friends but fears rejection
* **Schizoid** = does not care about having friends
* **Dependent** = needs others to make decisions and fears abandonment
