### Core idea

Eclampsia = seizures in a patient with preeclampsia.

Preeclampsia = new hypertension (high blood pressure) after 20 weeks of pregnancy plus proteinuria (protein in urine) or end-organ damage.

So:

> preeclampsia + seizure = eclampsia

---

### What is actually happening?

The placenta develops abnormally, causing widespread endothelial dysfunction.

Endothelium = inner lining of blood vessels.

That endothelial dysfunction causes:

* vasoconstriction (blood vessels squeeze down) → hypertension
* leaky vessels → edema (fluid swelling)
* kidney injury → proteinuria
* brain irritation/swelling → seizures

---

### Why seizures happen

High blood pressure and endothelial damage can disrupt brain blood flow.

This can cause cerebral edema (brain swelling), especially in the posterior brain, making neurons unstable and prone to firing abnormally.

That abnormal firing = seizure.

---

### Classic clues

Think eclampsia when a pregnant or recently postpartum patient has:

* seizure
* hypertension
* headache
* visual symptoms
* RUQ (right upper quadrant) or epigastric pain
* proteinuria

RUQ pain happens because preeclampsia can injure the liver and stretch the liver capsule.

---

## One-line intuition

Eclampsia is preeclampsia that has irritated the brain enough to cause seizures.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Treatment is magnesium sulfate, not a typical antiepileptic drug first.

Magnesium sulfate stabilizes the CNS (central nervous system) and prevents recurrent eclamptic seizures.
