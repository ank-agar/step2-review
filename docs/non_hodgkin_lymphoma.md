## Non-Hodgkin lymphoma

Non-Hodgkin lymphoma is a cancer of lymphocytes (B cells, T cells, or NK cells; immune cells that live in lymph nodes and lymph tissue).

Most cases are from B cells.

Core idea:

* lymphocytes become malignant
* they multiply too much
* they collect in lymph nodes, spleen, bone marrow, GI tract (gastrointestinal tract), or other lymph tissue

So the classic presentation is:

* painless lymphadenopathy (swollen lymph nodes)
* fever
* night sweats
* weight loss

The last 3 are called B symptoms (systemic lymphoma symptoms: fever, night sweats, weight loss).

---

### Why the lymph nodes get big

The cancer cells are lymphocytes.

Lymphocytes normally live in lymph nodes, so when they become malignant, they expand inside lymph nodes and make them enlarged.

That is why lymphoma often feels like firm, painless lymph node swelling in the:

* neck
* axilla (armpit)
* groin

---

### Non-Hodgkin vs Hodgkin

Big Step 1 distinction:

* Hodgkin lymphoma: Reed-Sternberg cells (large abnormal B cells with owl-eye nuclei)
* Non-Hodgkin lymphoma: no required Reed-Sternberg cells

Also:

* Hodgkin lymphoma usually spreads in a more orderly contiguous pattern
* Non-Hodgkin lymphoma is often more scattered and can involve extranodal sites (outside lymph nodes)

Extranodal is important because NHL (non-Hodgkin lymphoma) can show up in places like:

* GI tract
* skin
* brain
* bone marrow

---

### Common high-yield subtypes

DLBCL (diffuse large B-cell lymphoma):

* most common adult NHL
* aggressive
* large malignant B cells

Follicular lymphoma:

* BCL2 overexpression
* t(14;18) translocation (chromosome pieces switch places)
* indolent (slow-growing)
* apoptosis blocked

Why? BCL2 prevents apoptosis (programmed cell death), so lymphocytes survive when they should die.

Burkitt lymphoma:

* MYC activation
* t(8;14) translocation
* very aggressive
* starry-sky appearance on histology

Why starry-sky? The dark background is many rapidly dividing tumor cells, and the pale "stars" are macrophages (cleanup cells) eating dead tumor cells.

---

## One-line intuition

Non-Hodgkin lymphoma is a lymphocyte cancer where malignant immune cells expand in lymph nodes or lymph tissue, often causing painless nodes and sometimes systemic B symptoms.

---

## Pearl

Follicular lymphoma = t(14;18) causing BCL2 overexpression, which blocks apoptosis, so B cells live too long.
