# Impacted cerumen

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/10033.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557380/bin/Otoscopic-view-of-impacted-cerumen-mixed-with-hair-completely-obstructing-the-ear.jpg)

### Core idea

Impacted cerumen means earwax gets stuck and blocks the external auditory canal (ear canal).

Cerumen = earwax.

Impacted = packed in / stuck.

So literally:

> earwax is packed into the ear canal enough to cause symptoms or block the view of the tympanic membrane (eardrum).

---

### Why symptoms happen

Normally, earwax protects the ear and slowly migrates outward.

But if wax builds up, hardens, or gets pushed deeper, it can plug the canal.

That causes:

* decreased hearing
* ear fullness/pressure
* itching
* ear pain
* tinnitus (ringing in the ear)
* sometimes dizziness or cough

The hearing loss is usually conductive hearing loss, meaning sound cannot physically travel well through the outer/middle ear pathway.

---

### Why cotton swabs make it worse

Cotton swabs may remove a little wax near the opening, but they can push deeper wax inward.

That packs the wax closer to the tympanic membrane (eardrum), making a tighter plug.

Intuition:

* normal wax = slowly exits the canal
* swab use = shoves wax back in
* hardened plug = sound waves cannot reach the eardrum well

---

### Treatment idea

Common options:

* cerumenolytics (earwax-softening drops)
* irrigation (flushing the canal)
* manual removal by a clinician

Avoid irrigation if the tympanic membrane (eardrum) is perforated, because fluid can enter the middle ear and cause problems.

---

## One-line intuition

Impacted cerumen is basically a wax plug in the ear canal causing conductive hearing loss and ear fullness.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Impacted cerumen causes conductive hearing loss, so on tuning fork exam you can see:

* Weber test lateralizes to the affected ear
* Rinne test may show bone conduction > air conduction on the affected side

Conductive hearing loss = the inner ear works, but sound cannot get conducted to it properly.
