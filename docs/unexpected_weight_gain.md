### Core idea

Unexpected weight gain means gaining weight without intentionally eating more or decreasing activity.

Big buckets:

* more fat storage
* fluid retention
* hormonal problems
* medication side effect
* pregnancy/menstrual cycle changes

---

### Why it happens

Weight can go up from either:

* **fat gain:** stored energy increases
* **water weight:** extra fluid builds up in tissues

Fluid weight usually changes faster than fat weight.

So if someone gains several pounds in a few days, think:

* edema (fluid swelling)
* heart failure
* kidney disease
* liver disease
* pregnancy-related fluid retention
* medication effect

---

### Endocrine causes

Endocrine means hormone-related.

Classic endocrine causes:

* hypothyroidism = low thyroid hormone
* Cushing syndrome = too much cortisol (stress hormone)
* PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) = androgen excess + ovulatory dysfunction

Why hypothyroidism causes weight gain:

* low thyroid hormone slows metabolism
* less energy is burned
* also causes water/salt retention

Why Cushing causes weight gain:

* high cortisol increases appetite
* increases central fat deposition
* breaks down muscle, so arms/legs may look thin while trunk/face gain fat

---

### Medication causes

Common meds that can cause weight gain:

* corticosteroids like prednisone
* antipsychotics
* some antidepressants
* insulin
* some diabetes meds
* some birth control methods

Do not stop a medication suddenly without talking to a clinician.

---

## One-line intuition

Unexpected weight gain is either “more stored energy” or “more retained fluid” until proven otherwise.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Rapid weight gain + leg swelling or shortness of breath is more concerning for fluid retention than simple fat gain, so think heart/kidney/liver disease.

HY Step 1 pearl: hypothyroidism classically has weight gain, cold intolerance, constipation, fatigue, bradycardia (slow heart rate), and increased TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) in primary hypothyroidism.
