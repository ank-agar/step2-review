### Core idea

Annular pancreas = pancreatic tissue forms a ring around the duodenum (first part of the small intestine after the stomach).

Annular means ring-shaped.

So the pancreas basically wraps around and squeezes the duodenum.

---

### Why it happens

During embryology (development before birth), the ventral pancreatic bud (early pancreas tissue) is supposed to rotate around the duodenum and fuse normally.

If rotation is abnormal, pancreatic tissue can end up encircling the duodenum.

That ring can narrow the duodenal lumen (inside hollow space of the duodenum).

---

### What it causes

Duodenal obstruction, meaning food/milk cannot pass normally from:

* stomach
* into duodenum
* into the rest of the intestine

So babies can present with:

* vomiting
* feeding intolerance
* abdominal distension

Vomiting can be bilious (green bile-containing vomit) if the obstruction is distal to the ampulla of Vater (where bile enters the duodenum).

---

### Imaging clue

Can cause the **double bubble sign**:

* one bubble = stomach
* second bubble = proximal duodenum

The bubbles happen because air/fluid gets trapped before the obstruction.

---

## One-line intuition

Annular pancreas is a congenital pancreatic “ring” that clamps down on the duodenum and causes duodenal obstruction.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Annular pancreas is associated with Down syndrome (trisomy 21), and Step 1 may connect it with neonatal duodenal obstruction and the double bubble sign.
