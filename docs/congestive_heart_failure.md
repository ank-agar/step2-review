# Congestive heart failure

![Image](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/sites/default/files/inline-images/images_265_0.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fa/Diagram_of_the_human_heart.svg)

Congestive heart failure, or **CHF (congestive heart failure)**, means the heart cannot pump blood forward well enough for the body's needs.

"Congestive" means fluid backs up.

So the core idea is:

> weak or stiff pump -> blood backs up -> fluid leaks into tissues

---

### Left-sided heart failure

The left ventricle (main chamber that pumps blood to the body) fails.

Blood backs up into the lungs, causing:

* pulmonary edema (fluid in the lungs)
* shortness of breath
* orthopnea (shortness of breath when lying flat)
* paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, or PND (waking up at night short of breath)

Why?

Because the lungs drain blood into the left side of the heart. If the left heart cannot move blood forward, pressure backs up into pulmonary veins (lung veins), so fluid enters the alveoli (air sacs).

---

### Right-sided heart failure

The right ventricle (chamber that pumps blood to the lungs) fails.

Blood backs up into the body, causing:

* peripheral edema (leg swelling)
* jugular venous distension, or JVD (bulging neck veins)
* hepatomegaly (enlarged liver)
* ascites (fluid in the abdomen)

Why?

Because the body drains blood into the right side of the heart. If the right heart cannot move blood forward, venous pressure (pressure in veins) rises.

---

### Big causes

Common causes include:

* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* myocardial infarction, or MI (heart attack)
* coronary artery disease, or CAD (blocked heart arteries)
* valvular disease (bad heart valves)
* cardiomyopathy (disease of heart muscle)

---

## One-line intuition

CHF is basically a backed-up pump: left heart failure backs fluid into lungs, right heart failure backs fluid into the body.

---

## Pearl 🔥

BNP (B-type natriuretic peptide) increases in heart failure because stretched ventricles release it when they are overloaded with volume/pressure.
