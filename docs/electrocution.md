Electrocution means death caused by electrical current passing through the body.

Important wording:

* **electric shock** = electricity passes through the body
* **electrical injury** = shock causes tissue damage
* **electrocution** = shock causes death

So electrocution is basically the fatal version of electrical injury.

---

### Core idea

Electricity hurts you when your body becomes part of a circuit.

That means current enters at one point, travels through tissues, and exits somewhere else.

The most dangerous path is:

* hand to hand
* hand to foot

because the current can cross the chest and heart.

---

### Why it kills

Electricity can cause death mainly by:

* **ventricular fibrillation** = chaotic, ineffective ventricular beating
* **cardiac arrest** = the heart stops pumping effectively
* **respiratory muscle paralysis** = breathing muscles stop working
* severe internal burns and muscle damage

The heart is vulnerable because it already runs on tiny electrical signals.

So an outside current can scramble the heart's rhythm, especially if it passes through the chest.

---

### Why burns can look small but be dangerous

Electrical burns can have:

* an entry wound
* an exit wound
* deep tissue damage between them

The skin findings may look limited, but the current can cook deeper tissues like:

* muscle
* nerves
* blood vessels

That is why electrical injury can cause **rhabdomyolysis** (muscle breakdown releasing myoglobin into blood), which can lead to acute kidney injury.

---

## One-line intuition

Electrocution is when electricity turns the body into a wire and fatally disrupts the heart, breathing, or deep tissues.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: electrical injury can cause **rhabdomyolysis** (skeletal muscle breakdown), leading to high **creatine kinase** (muscle enzyme marker), myoglobinuria, and acute kidney injury.
