### Core idea

Carpal tunnel syndrome is compression of the median nerve as it passes through the carpal tunnel at the wrist.

Break it down:

* carpal = wrist
* tunnel = tight passageway
* syndrome = group of symptoms

So:

> the median nerve gets squeezed inside a tight wrist tunnel.

---

### What is inside the carpal tunnel?

The carpal tunnel contains:

* median nerve
* flexor tendons, which bend the fingers

The roof is the transverse carpal ligament, also called the flexor retinaculum.

If anything makes the tunnel tighter, the median nerve gets compressed.

---

### Symptoms

Median nerve compression causes:

* numbness/tingling in the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of ring finger
* symptoms worse at night
* pain that may radiate up the forearm
* weakness of thumb movements in severe cases
* thenar atrophy, meaning wasting of the thumb-base muscles

Thenar muscles = the muscles forming the fleshy mound at the base of the thumb.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The median nerve carries sensation from the lateral palm-side fingers.

So when it is squeezed:

* sensory fibers get irritated first → tingling/numbness
* prolonged compression damages motor fibers → thumb weakness
* chronic motor denervation → thenar muscle atrophy

Night symptoms happen because people often sleep with wrists flexed, which makes the tunnel narrower and increases pressure on the nerve.

---

### Key anatomy trick

The palm over the thenar eminence is usually spared.

Why?

The palmar cutaneous branch of the median nerve branches off before the carpal tunnel and travels outside the tunnel.

So carpal tunnel syndrome affects the fingers more than the central palm/thenar skin.

---

### Common associations

Carpal tunnel syndrome is associated with:

* pregnancy
* hypothyroidism
* rheumatoid arthritis
* diabetes mellitus
* obesity
* repetitive wrist use

Intuition:

Anything that causes swelling, inflammation, or less space in the tunnel can squeeze the median nerve.

---

## One-line intuition

Carpal tunnel syndrome is median nerve compression at the wrist, causing nighttime tingling in the thumb-side fingers and, if severe, thenar weakness/atrophy.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Carpal tunnel syndrome causes median nerve symptoms, but sensation over the thenar eminence is spared because the palmar cutaneous branch does not pass through the carpal tunnel.
