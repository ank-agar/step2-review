# Cervicitis

### Core idea

Cervicitis = inflammation of the cervix.

The cervix is the lower opening of the uterus into the vagina.

So cervicitis means the cervix becomes:

* red
* friable, meaning bleeds easily when touched
* irritated
* sometimes covered with mucopurulent discharge

Mucopurulent discharge = mucus + pus.

---

### Common causes

Most high-yield infectious causes:

* Chlamydia trachomatis
* Neisseria gonorrhoeae
* Trichomonas vaginalis
* HSV (herpes simplex virus)

Noninfectious causes can also happen:

* chemical irritation
* trauma
* foreign body irritation

But for Step 1, think STI (sexually transmitted infection), especially chlamydia and gonorrhea.

---

### Why the findings happen

Infection irritates the cervical epithelium, so immune cells rush in.

That causes:

* **pus-like discharge:** neutrophils are fighting infection
* **bleeding after sex or pelvic exam:** inflamed tissue is fragile/friable
* **pelvic discomfort:** inflammation can irritate nearby tissue
* **ascending infection risk:** organisms can move upward into uterus/fallopian tubes

If it ascends, it can cause PID (pelvic inflammatory disease), which can lead to infertility or ectopic pregnancy.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* abnormal vaginal discharge
* intermenstrual bleeding, meaning bleeding between periods
* postcoital bleeding, meaning bleeding after sex
* cervical motion tenderness if PID (pelvic inflammatory disease) is developing

On exam:

* mucopurulent endocervical discharge
* easily induced cervical bleeding

Endocervical = inside the cervical canal.

---

## One-line intuition

Cervicitis is basically an infected/irritated cervix that gets inflamed, leaks pus-like mucus, and bleeds easily.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Chlamydia trachomatis is often asymptomatic but can still ascend and cause PID (pelvic inflammatory disease), so untreated cervicitis can quietly lead to infertility or ectopic pregnancy.
