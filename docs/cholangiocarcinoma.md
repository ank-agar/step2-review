### Core idea

Cholangiocarcinoma is **cancer of the bile ducts**.

Break the word apart:

* cholangio- = bile duct
* carcinoma = malignant epithelial tumor (cancer from lining cells)

So:

> malignant cancer arising from the epithelial lining of bile ducts.

Bile ducts carry bile (fluid made by the liver that helps digest fat) from the liver/gallbladder into the small intestine.

---

### Why symptoms happen

The tumor grows inside or around bile ducts and blocks bile flow.

Blocked bile flow causes:

* **jaundice:** bilirubin backs up into blood
* **dark urine:** water-soluble conjugated bilirubin spills into urine
* **pale stools:** less bile reaches the gut, so stool loses brown pigment
* **pruritus:** itching from retained bile salts
* **RUQ pain:** right upper quadrant pain from biliary/liver irritation

One-line intuition:

**The bile drainage pipe gets cancer, so bile backs up.**

---

### Types by location

Cholangiocarcinoma can be:

* **intrahepatic:** inside the liver bile ducts
* **perihilar/hilar:** where right and left hepatic ducts meet
* **distal:** lower common bile duct near the pancreas/small intestine

Perihilar cholangiocarcinoma is also called a **Klatskin tumor**.

---

### Risk factors

Classic Step 1 associations:

* primary sclerosing cholangitis, or PSC (inflammatory scarring of bile ducts)
* ulcerative colitis, or UC (inflammatory bowel disease affecting the colon)
* liver flukes, especially Clonorchis sinensis and Opisthorchis viverrini
* choledochal cysts (congenital dilation of bile ducts)

Why chronic inflammation matters:

Repeated bile duct injury means repeated repair/proliferation, which increases the chance of mutations and cancer.

---

### Labs/tumor marker

You may see a cholestatic pattern:

* increased alkaline phosphatase, or ALP (bile duct enzyme)
* increased direct bilirubin

Tumor marker:

* CA 19-9 (carbohydrate antigen 19-9)

CA 19-9 can support the diagnosis, but it is not perfectly specific.

---

## One-line intuition

Cholangiocarcinoma is **adenocarcinoma (gland-forming cancer) of the bile ducts** that often presents like an obstructive jaundice problem.

---

## HY pearl

Primary sclerosing cholangitis, or PSC (inflammatory scarring of bile ducts), strongly increases the risk of cholangiocarcinoma, especially in patients with ulcerative colitis, or UC (inflammatory bowel disease affecting the colon).
