## Pyridoxine deficiency

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dc/Metabolic_pathway-_pyridoxal_5%27-phosphate_biosynthesis_I.svg/1280px-Metabolic_pathway-_pyridoxal_5%27-phosphate_biosynthesis_I.svg.png)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/51cd/11049216/da2643874cba/dentistry-12-00109-g006.jpg)

Pyridoxine deficiency = vitamin B6 deficiency.

Vitamin B6 gets converted into **PLP (pyridoxal phosphate)**, which is the active cofactor form.

Think of PLP as a helper for reactions involving:

* amino acids
* neurotransmitters
* heme synthesis
* sphingolipids/myelin

So if B6 is low, a bunch of “amino acid + nerve + blood” pathways get weak.

---

### Main findings

Classically causes:

* peripheral neuropathy (nerve damage causing numbness/tingling)
* seizures
* irritability/confusion
* sideroblastic anemia (iron is available but cannot be properly inserted into heme)
* glossitis (inflamed tongue)
* cheilosis/angular cheilitis (cracking at the lips/corners of mouth)
* seborrheic dermatitis-like rash (greasy/scaly rash, often on face/scalp)

---

### Why the findings happen

**Low PLP -> low GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid)**

GABA is the main inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brain.

So less GABA = less “brake pedal” in the brain -> seizures.

**Low PLP -> low heme synthesis**

PLP is needed for **ALA synthase (aminolevulinic acid synthase)**, the first step of heme synthesis.

So iron cannot be used well -> sideroblastic anemia.

**Low PLP -> impaired sphingolipid synthesis**

Sphingolipids help maintain myelin (nerve insulation).

So nerves malfunction -> peripheral neuropathy.

---

### Classic cause

Big Step 1 association:

* **isoniazid** = tuberculosis drug that can cause vitamin B6 deficiency

Isoniazid binds/inactivates pyridoxine, so patients can get:

* peripheral neuropathy
* sideroblastic anemia
* seizures, especially if severe

That is why patients on isoniazid are often given pyridoxine supplementation.

---

## One-line intuition

Vitamin B6 is the “PLP cofactor vitamin,” so deficiency mainly hurts neurotransmitter synthesis, heme synthesis, and nerve function.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1: **isoniazid + neuropathy = give vitamin B6/pyridoxine**.

Also remember: B6 deficiency can cause **sideroblastic anemia**, while B12 (cobalamin) or folate deficiency causes **megaloblastic anemia**.
