## Priapism

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK535381/bin/Gray1155.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/df/Ultrasonography_of_traumatic_arteriovenous_fistula_of_the_penis.jpg)

### Core idea

Priapism = prolonged erection, usually **>4 hours**, without sexual stimulation.

The main Step 1 idea:

* blood gets trapped in the **corpora cavernosa** (paired erectile tissue chambers)
* the penis stays rigid
* oxygen delivery drops
* tissue can become ischemic

So think:

> erection that cannot drain = compartment syndrome of the penis.

---

### Main types

**Ischemic priapism** = low-flow priapism.

This is the dangerous/common one.

* painful
* rigid shaft
* soft glans usually
* dark, hypoxic blood trapped in corpora cavernosa
* urologic emergency

**Nonischemic priapism** = high-flow priapism.

Usually from trauma causing an abnormal artery-to-corpus cavernosum connection.

* less painful
* less rigid
* oxygenated blood still flows in
* less emergent than ischemic priapism

---

### Why sickle cell disease causes it

In sickle cell disease, red blood cells sickle and block small vessels.

In the penis, that means:

* venous outflow gets obstructed
* blood pools in the corpora cavernosa
* pooled blood loses oxygen
* low oxygen causes more sickling
* more sickling causes even worse blockage

So it becomes a vicious cycle:

> sickling blocks drainage → low oxygen → more sickling → more trapped blood.

---

### Why it causes erectile dysfunction

If ischemia lasts too long, smooth muscle in the corpora cavernosa gets injured.

Damaged smooth muscle heals with fibrosis, meaning scar tissue.

Scarred erectile tissue cannot expand and trap blood normally later, causing **ED (erectile dysfunction)**.

---

### Treatment intuition

For ischemic priapism:

* aspirate trapped blood from the corpus cavernosum
* inject phenylephrine, an alpha-1 agonist

Phenylephrine causes vasoconstriction, which helps reduce inflow and restore venous drainage.

---

## One-line intuition

Priapism is when blood gets stuck in the erectile chambers, and the ischemic type is dangerous because trapped blood becomes deoxygenated and damages erectile tissue.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In sickle cell disease, priapism is a urologic emergency because prolonged low-flow ischemia can cause permanent erectile dysfunction from fibrosis of the corpora cavernosa.
