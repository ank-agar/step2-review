### Core idea

Threatened abortion means:

* vaginal bleeding before 20 weeks
* closed cervix
* no passage of products of conception
* fetus/embryo may still be viable

Abortion here means pregnancy loss, not elective termination.

So the key idea is:

> bleeding in early pregnancy, but the pregnancy has not been lost yet.

---

### Why it is “threatened”

It is called threatened because bleeding suggests a possible miscarriage, but the cervix is still closed.

Closed cervix = the uterus is not actively expelling pregnancy tissue.

That is what separates it from:

* inevitable abortion: bleeding + open cervix
* incomplete abortion: open cervix + some products of conception passed/retained
* complete abortion: all products of conception passed
* missed abortion: fetal demise, but products retained

---

### Classic presentation

* first-trimester bleeding
* mild cramping may be present
* cervical os is closed
* ultrasound may show viable intrauterine pregnancy

Cervical os means the opening of the cervix.

Intrauterine pregnancy means the pregnancy is inside the uterus.

---

### Why bleeding happens

Bleeding can happen from partial separation/irritation around the developing pregnancy.

One common cause is a subchorionic hematoma.

Subchorionic hematoma means blood collecting between the chorion and uterine wall.

Chorion = outer fetal membrane that helps form the placenta.

The pregnancy can still continue if the bleeding stops and the embryo/fetus remains viable.

---

## One-line intuition

Threatened abortion = the body is giving a “possible miscarriage” warning sign, but the cervix is closed and the pregnancy may still survive.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1/clinical vignettes, the most important discriminator is the cervix:

* closed cervix = threatened abortion
* open cervix = inevitable/incomplete abortion
