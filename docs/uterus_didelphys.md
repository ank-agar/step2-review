## Uterus didelphys

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/24346.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK570595/bin/didelphys.jpg)

### Core idea

Uterus didelphys means a “double uterus.”

It happens when the Mullerian ducts (embryologic tubes that form the uterus, fallopian tubes, cervix, and upper vagina) fail to fuse.

So instead of forming one uterus, they stay separate and form:

* 2 uterine cavities
* often 2 cervixes (cervix = lower opening of uterus)
* sometimes 2 vaginas, if there is a longitudinal vaginal septum (vertical wall dividing the vagina)

---

### Why it happens

Normally:

* left Mullerian duct + right Mullerian duct fuse in the midline
* the middle wall disappears
* one uterine cavity forms

In uterus didelphys:

* fusion fails almost completely
* each side develops separately

So the anatomy is duplicated.

---

### How to recognize it

Think:

* two separate uterine horns
* two separate cavities
* often two cervixes

It can be asymptomatic (no symptoms), but can cause:

* painful periods
* trouble with tampons if one vaginal canal is blocked or separate
* recurrent pregnancy loss
* preterm birth (delivery before 37 weeks)
* breech presentation (baby positioned buttocks/feet first)

---

### Important association

Uterus didelphys can be associated with renal agenesis (missing kidney), especially ipsilateral renal agenesis (missing kidney on the same side as the reproductive tract abnormality).

Why?

The reproductive tract and urinary tract develop close together embryologically, so Mullerian duct abnormalities can travel with kidney abnormalities.

---

## One-line intuition

The two embryologic uterus-forming tubes never fuse, so the patient ends up with two smaller, separate uteruses instead of one fused uterus.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: uterus didelphys + obstructed hemivagina (one blocked half of the vagina) + ipsilateral renal agenesis = OHVIRA syndrome (obstructed hemivagina and ipsilateral renal anomaly).
