# Alport syndrome

![Image](https://sites.manchester.ac.uk/alport-hub/wp-content/uploads/sites/416/2023/03/Image_4_Diseased_Glomerulus-1024px.png)

![Image](https://unckidneycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/413/2017/10/alport-syndrome.jpeg)

![Image](https://alportsyndrome.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/X-linked-through-mother-graphic-232x300.png)

Alport syndrome is an inherited defect in type IV collagen.

Type IV collagen is important in basement membranes, especially in the:

* kidney glomerular basement membrane, or GBM (glomerular basement membrane)
* inner ear
* eye

So the classic findings are:

* hematuria (blood in urine)
* progressive kidney disease
* sensorineural hearing loss (hearing loss from inner ear/nerve damage)
* eye problems, especially anterior lenticonus (cone-shaped bulging of the lens)

---

### Core idea

The GBM (glomerular basement membrane) is like the filter wall of the glomerulus.

In Alport syndrome, mutations in collagen genes make that wall structurally weak.

Important genes:

* COL4A5: most common, usually X-linked
* COL4A3 and COL4A4: autosomal recessive or autosomal dominant forms

X-linked means the mutation is on the X chromosome.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Hematuria:** the defective GBM lets red blood cells leak into urine
* **Proteinuria (protein in urine):** progressive GBM damage lets protein leak through too
* **Kidney failure:** chronic filter-wall injury causes scarring over time
* **Hearing loss:** type IV collagen is also needed in the inner ear
* **Eye abnormalities:** type IV collagen is also needed in ocular basement membranes

---

### Biopsy / EM finding

On EM (electron microscopy), the GBM can show:

* irregular thinning and thickening
* splitting/lamellation
* “basket-weave” appearance

Basket-weave basically means the basement membrane looks split up and layered instead of being one smooth clean line.

---

## One-line intuition

Alport syndrome = bad type IV collagen, so the kidney filter wall falls apart, causing blood in urine plus hearing and eye problems.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Alport syndrome is classically **X-linked** due to COL4A5 mutation, so males are usually more severely affected.
