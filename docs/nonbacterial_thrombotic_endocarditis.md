### Core idea

Nonbacterial thrombotic endocarditis (NBTE) is **sterile clot-like growths on heart valves**.

Break it down:

* nonbacterial = no bacteria/infection
* thrombotic = made of thrombus/clot material
* endocarditis = inflammation or lesion involving the endocardium (inner heart lining), especially valves

So NBTE means:

> sterile platelet-fibrin clots stuck to heart valves.

Platelet-fibrin means the vegetations are mostly **platelets + fibrin**, not pus or bacteria.

---

### Where it happens

Most commonly on left-sided valves:

* mitral valve
* aortic valve

These valves have high pressure/turbulence, so they are easier places for clots to form.

---

### Why it happens

NBTE is usually due to a **hypercoagulable state** (blood is more likely to clot).

Classic associations:

* advanced malignancy, especially adenocarcinoma
* systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE, autoimmune disease causing immune-complex inflammation)
* antiphospholipid syndrome (APS, autoimmune hypercoagulable state)
* disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC, widespread abnormal clotting and bleeding)

Why cancer causes it:

* some cancers release procoagulant factors
* blood becomes clot-prone
* sterile thrombi form on valve closure lines

---

### Main danger

The vegetations can break off and embolize.

Embolize means a piece travels through blood and blocks a vessel somewhere else.

Because NBTE is usually on the left-sided heart valves, emboli can go to:

* brain → stroke
* kidney → renal infarct
* spleen → splenic infarct
* limbs → acute ischemia

---

### How to distinguish from infective endocarditis

NBTE:

* sterile vegetations
* blood cultures negative
* little/no valve destruction
* little inflammation
* associated with hypercoagulability

Infective endocarditis:

* bacteria or fungi on valve
* positive blood cultures often
* more inflammation
* can destroy valve tissue

---

## One-line intuition

NBTE is when a clot-prone patient forms sterile “clot barnacles” on heart valves that can break off and cause embolic strokes/infarcts.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, think:

**NBTE = sterile platelet/fibrin vegetations + malignancy/APS/SLE + embolic stroke with negative blood cultures.**
