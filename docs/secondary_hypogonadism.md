## Secondary hypogonadism

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg/960px-Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279031/bin/endocrin-male-reprod-Image013.jpg)

Secondary hypogonadism means the **brain signal to the gonads is too low**.

Hypogonadism = low gonadal sex hormone function.

Gonads = testes or ovaries.

So:

* testes make low testosterone
* ovaries make low estrogen/progesterone

But the key is: the gonads are not the original problem.

The problem is in the:

* hypothalamus
* pituitary gland

---

### Core lab pattern

Secondary hypogonadism:

* low testosterone or estrogen
* low or inappropriately normal LH (luteinizing hormone)
* low or inappropriately normal FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)

This is also called **hypogonadotropic hypogonadism**.

Hypogonadotropic = low gonadotropins, meaning low LH and FSH.

---

### Why LH and FSH are low

Normally:

* hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* pituitary releases LH and FSH
* gonads make sex hormones

In secondary hypogonadism, the hypothalamus/pituitary does not send enough signal.

So the gonads are basically waiting for instructions that never come.

Result:

* low LH/FSH
* low stimulation of Leydig cells or ovarian follicles
* low sex hormones
* infertility or delayed puberty

---

### Primary vs secondary

Primary hypogonadism:

* problem = gonads
* LH/FSH = high
* sex hormones = low

Secondary hypogonadism:

* problem = hypothalamus or pituitary
* LH/FSH = low or inappropriately normal
* sex hormones = low

Intuition:

* primary = the gonads are broken, so the pituitary yells louder
* secondary = the pituitary/hypothalamus is not yelling enough

---

### Common causes

Think anything that lowers GnRH, LH, or FSH:

* pituitary adenoma
* hyperprolactinemia (high prolactin)
* Kallmann syndrome
* chronic opioid use
* severe stress, weight loss, or excessive exercise
* hemochromatosis (iron overload damaging pituitary cells)

Why does high prolactin cause this?

Prolactin suppresses GnRH release from the hypothalamus.

Less GnRH means:

* less LH
* less FSH
* less gonadal sex hormone production

---

### Symptoms

Before puberty:

* delayed puberty
* small testes or poor ovarian estrogen function
* absent or weak secondary sexual characteristics

After puberty:

* decreased libido
* erectile dysfunction in males
* amenorrhea (absence of menstrual periods) in females
* infertility
* low energy or low muscle mass in males

---

## One-line intuition

Secondary hypogonadism is **low sex hormones because the hypothalamus or pituitary is not sending enough LH/FSH signal to the gonads**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Kallmann syndrome = congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism with anosmia (loss of smell), due to failed GnRH neuron migration.
