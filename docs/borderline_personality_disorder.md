# Borderline personality disorder

![Image](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/sites/default/files/images/get-involved/social-media-graphics/2025-borderlinepd-x.jpg)

![Image](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/sites/default/files/images/get-involved/social-media-graphics/bpd-hero.png)

Borderline personality disorder (BPD) is a **Cluster B personality disorder** (dramatic/emotional/erratic personality disorder group) where the core problem is:

* unstable relationships
* unstable self-image
* unstable mood
* impulsive behavior

---

### Core idea

Think:

> “I feel emotions extremely intensely, especially around abandonment or rejection, and I may react impulsively to stop that pain.”

So the patient may have:

* fear of abandonment
* intense, unstable relationships
* splitting
* impulsivity
* self-harm or suicidal behavior
* mood swings
* chronic emptiness
* anger outbursts
* transient stress-related paranoia or dissociation

Splitting means seeing people as **all good or all bad**, with difficulty holding mixed views.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The big mechanism is **emotion dysregulation** (difficulty controlling emotional intensity).

So a small interpersonal trigger can feel huge:

* perceived rejection → panic/anger
* fear of abandonment → clingy or impulsive behavior
* unstable self-image → changing goals/values/identity
* intense emotions → self-harm can become a maladaptive coping strategy

Maladaptive means harmful or unhelpful.

---

### Classic test clue

A patient says:

* “You are the only doctor who understands me”

then later:

* “You are terrible and never cared about me”

That is **splitting**.

---

### Treatment

Best treatment is:

* dialectical behavior therapy (DBT)

DBT (dialectical behavior therapy) teaches:

* mindfulness
* distress tolerance
* emotion regulation
* interpersonal effectiveness

Basically: feel the emotion without letting it drive destructive behavior.

---

## One-line intuition

BPD is unstable mood + unstable relationships + unstable identity, driven by intense fear of abandonment and poor emotion regulation.

---

## Pearl 🔥

BPD has **recurrent self-harm/suicidal behavior**, but the defense mechanism most associated with it is **splitting**.
