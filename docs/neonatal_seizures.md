## Neonatal seizures

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557645/bin/Neo-seizure.jpg)

### Core idea

Neonatal seizures are seizures in a newborn, often caused by an acute brain or metabolic problem.

In neonates (newborns), seizures can look subtle:

* lip smacking
* eye deviation
* bicycling movements
* apnea (temporary stopping of breathing)
* jittery or tonic posturing

---

### Why they happen

The newborn brain is very excitable and vulnerable.

Common causes:

* **hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy:** brain injury from low oxygen/blood flow
* **hypoglycemia:** low blood glucose
* **hypocalcemia:** low calcium
* **intracranial hemorrhage:** bleeding inside the skull
* **infection:** meningitis (infection of meninges, the brain coverings) or sepsis (bloodstream infection)

Low glucose or calcium makes neurons unstable, so they fire abnormally.

---

## One-line intuition

Neonatal seizure = newborn brain is irritated, often from oxygen injury, bleeding, infection, or metabolic derangement.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY first labs for neonatal seizures include glucose, calcium, sodium, and infection workup, because metabolic causes are treatable and easy to miss.
