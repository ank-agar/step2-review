# Hodgkin lymphoma

![Image](https://images.ctfassets.net/g3mey2fqpv66/08-diagram-showing-the-lymph-nodes-lymphoma-most-c/6c058be13bab41817c295016cc6354d8/08-diagram-showing-the-lymph-nodes-lymphoma-most-c.svg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/21/Hodgkin%27s_lymphoma_with_RS_cell_10x.jpg)

Hodgkin lymphoma is a cancer of B cells (antibody-producing lymphocytes) in lymph nodes.

The key cell is the Reed-Sternberg cell, which is a large abnormal B cell with multiple nuclei.

Classic description:

* "owl-eye" nuclei
* CD15 positive
* CD30 positive
* weak PAX5 positive

PAX5 is a B-cell transcription factor (a protein that helps control B-cell gene expression).

---

### Core idea

A B cell becomes malignant, but instead of just looking like tons of normal lymphocytes, it forms big Reed-Sternberg cells surrounded by inflammatory cells.

That inflammatory background is why patients can get systemic symptoms even if the actual malignant Reed-Sternberg cells are not the majority of the tumor.

---

### Classic symptoms

* painless lymphadenopathy (swollen lymph nodes), often cervical nodes (neck lymph nodes)
* fever
* night sweats
* weight loss
* fatigue
* pruritus (itching)

Fever, night sweats, and weight loss are called B symptoms (systemic cancer symptoms used in lymphoma staging).

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Painless lymph nodes:** malignant cells and inflammatory cells expand the lymph node without necessarily causing infection-like pain
* **B symptoms:** cytokines (immune signaling proteins) drive fever, sweating, and weight loss
* **Pruritus:** cytokine-driven immune activation can irritate skin nerves
* **Mediastinal mass:** enlarged chest lymph nodes can compress airways or vessels

Mediastinum means the central chest area between the lungs.

---

### Spread pattern

Hodgkin lymphoma usually spreads in an orderly, contiguous way.

Contiguous means next-door spread.

So it often moves:

* one lymph node group
* then the nearby lymph node group
* then the next group

This is different from many non-Hodgkin lymphomas, which can spread more randomly.

---

## One-line intuition

Hodgkin lymphoma is a B-cell cancer where giant Reed-Sternberg cells sit in lymph nodes and trigger a big inflammatory reaction.

---

## HY pearl

Hodgkin lymphoma is classically associated with Reed-Sternberg cells that are CD15 positive and CD30 positive.
