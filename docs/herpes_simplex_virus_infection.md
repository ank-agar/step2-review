Herpes simplex virus infection is infection by **HSV (herpes simplex virus)**, an **enveloped double-stranded DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) virus**.

Main types:

* **HSV-1 (herpes simplex virus type 1):** classically oral herpes/cold sores
* **HSV-2 (herpes simplex virus type 2):** classically genital herpes

But both can infect either oral or genital sites.

---

### Core idea

HSV causes **painful grouped vesicles**.

Vesicles = small fluid-filled blisters.

Think:

> herpes = painful little blisters that recur in the same area.

---

### Why it recurs

HSV infects skin/mucosa first, then travels backward along sensory nerves to hide in sensory ganglia.

Ganglia = clusters of nerve cell bodies.

Where it hides:

* oral HSV: trigeminal ganglion
* genital HSV: sacral ganglia

Then stress, fever, sunlight, immunosuppression, or illness can reactivate it.

So the intuition is:

> HSV is not fully cleared; it goes dormant in nerves, then travels back out to skin when reactivated.

---

### What it looks like

Classically:

* painful grouped vesicles on an erythematous base
* erythematous = red/inflamed
* can ulcerate after vesicles rupture

Oral HSV:

* cold sores/fever blisters around lips
* gingivostomatitis in children

Gingivostomatitis = inflamed gums and mouth lining.

Genital HSV:

* painful genital ulcers/vesicles
* tender lymphadenopathy

Lymphadenopathy = enlarged lymph nodes.

---

### Diagnosis clue

Tzanck smear can show:

* multinucleated giant cells
* Cowdry type A inclusions

Multinucleated = multiple nuclei in one fused cell.

Why? HSV makes infected epithelial cells fuse together, so you get giant cells with multiple nuclei.

Best modern test is usually **PCR (polymerase chain reaction)** from a lesion swab.

---

### Treatment

Treat with nucleoside analogs:

* acyclovir
* valacyclovir
* famciclovir

These inhibit viral DNA replication.

Why they work: HSV is a DNA virus, so blocking viral DNA polymerase slows viral genome copying.

---

## One-line intuition

HSV makes painful recurrent vesicles because it hides latent in sensory ganglia and periodically reactivates back to skin/mucosa.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HSV-1 classically causes temporal lobe encephalitis.

Encephalitis = brain inflammation.

On Step 1, think:

* fever
* altered mental status
* seizures
* temporal lobe involvement
* treat with IV (intravenous) acyclovir
