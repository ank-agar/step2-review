# Candidiasis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/df/Candida_pseudohyphae%2C_chlamydospores%2C_blastospores.png)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/candidiasis/media/images/2024/04/candida_infection_illustrations.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a0/Human_tongue_infected_with_oral_candidiasis.jpg)

Candidiasis is a fungal infection caused by overgrowth of **Candida**, especially **Candida albicans**.

Candida normally lives on:

* skin
* mouth
* GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)
* vagina

So the key idea is:

> Candida is usually normal flora, but it becomes pathogenic when the local environment or immune system lets it overgrow.

### Common forms

* **Oral thrush:** white plaques in the mouth or throat
* **Vulvovaginal candidiasis:** itching, burning, thick white discharge
* **Cutaneous candidiasis:** red rash in warm/moist skin folds
* **Esophageal candidiasis:** painful swallowing, especially in immunocompromised patients
* **Invasive candidiasis:** Candida enters the bloodstream or organs

### Why it happens

Candida is kept under control by bacteria and immune defenses.

So candidiasis happens when that balance is disrupted:

* **Antibiotics:** kill normal bacteria, so Candida has less competition
* **Diabetes mellitus:** high glucose helps yeast grow
* **Immunosuppression:** weak T-cell immunity lets mucosal Candida invade
* **Pregnancy or estrogen exposure:** changes vaginal environment
* **Moist skin folds:** warm wet areas favor fungal growth

### Classic microscopy

Candida shows:

* budding yeast
* pseudohyphae

**Pseudohyphae** means elongated yeast cells stuck together, looking like hyphae (fungal filaments).

That is why the labeled image is high yield: Candida is not just round yeast; it can form pseudohyphae that help it stick to and invade mucosa.

### Treatment idea

Most superficial candidiasis is treated with antifungals like:

* nystatin
* topical azoles
* fluconazole

Severe invasive disease usually needs IV (intravenous) antifungals.

## One-line intuition

Candidiasis is Candida overgrowing when normal bacteria or immune control is weakened.

## Pearl 🔥

Candida is an opportunistic fungus, and **oral/esophageal candidiasis is a clue for impaired T-cell immunity**, especially HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) or other immunosuppression.
