## Secondary hyperthyroidism

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/08/Hypothalamus_and_Pituitary.svg/960px-Hypothalamus_and_Pituitary.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK278978/bin/thyrotropn-pit-adeno-Image002.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Adenome_hypophyse.JPG/960px-Adenome_hypophyse.JPG)

Secondary hyperthyroidism means hyperthyroidism caused by excess stimulation from the pituitary, not by a primary thyroid problem.

Most classic cause:

* TSH-secreting pituitary adenoma

TSH = thyroid-stimulating hormone.

So the sequence is:

* pituitary tumor makes too much TSH
* TSH overstimulates thyroid
* thyroid makes too much T3/T4
* patient becomes hyperthyroid

T3 = triiodothyronine.
T4 = thyroxine.

---

### Key lab pattern

Primary hyperthyroidism:

* high T3/T4
* low TSH

Secondary hyperthyroidism:

* high T3/T4
* high or inappropriately normal TSH

“Inappropriately normal” means TSH should be low, but it is not.

---

### Why TSH is not low

Normally:

* high T3/T4 gives negative feedback to the pituitary
* pituitary lowers TSH

But in secondary hyperthyroidism:

* pituitary adenoma is autonomous
* autonomous = acting on its own
* it keeps secreting TSH despite high thyroid hormone

So the thyroid is not the original problem.
The pituitary is pushing the thyroid too hard.

---

### Symptoms

Same hyperthyroid symptoms as usual:

* weight loss
* heat intolerance
* sweating
* tremor
* tachycardia, meaning fast heart rate
* anxiety
* diarrhea
* goiter, meaning enlarged thyroid

Goiter happens because TSH is a growth signal for the thyroid.

---

## One-line intuition

Primary hyperthyroidism = thyroid is overactive on its own.

Secondary hyperthyroidism = pituitary is yelling “make thyroid hormone” too loudly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

High T3/T4 with non-suppressed TSH should make you think:

* TSH-secreting pituitary adenoma
* thyroid hormone resistance

TSHoma usually has true hyperthyroid tissue effects; thyroid hormone resistance often has high T3/T4 but less severe hyperthyroid symptoms.
