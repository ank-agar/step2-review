### Core idea

Folic acid deficiency means low folate, aka vitamin B9 (a vitamin needed to make DNA).

Big picture:

* low folate
* decreased DNA synthesis
* cells cannot divide normally
* rapidly dividing cells get hit hardest

So you especially see problems in:

* bone marrow
* gastrointestinal mucosa
* developing fetus

---

### Why it causes megaloblastic anemia

Folate is needed to make thymidine (a DNA building block).

If folate is low:

* DNA synthesis slows down
* nucleus maturation is delayed
* cytoplasm keeps growing

So the cell becomes big because the cytoplasm keeps maturing, but the nucleus is stuck waiting on DNA replication.

That gives:

* macrocytic anemia = large RBCs (red blood cells)
* megaloblasts = big immature blood cell precursors
* hypersegmented neutrophils = neutrophils (white blood cells) with too many nuclear lobes

---

### Symptoms

Classic findings:

* fatigue and pallor from anemia
* glossitis (inflamed tongue)
* diarrhea from impaired gastrointestinal cell turnover
* no neurologic deficits

That last point helps separate it from vitamin B12 deficiency.

Vitamin B12 deficiency can cause neurologic symptoms because it affects myelin (nerve insulation).

Folate deficiency does **not** classically cause neurologic symptoms.

---

### Causes

Common causes:

* poor diet
* alcoholism
* pregnancy
* malabsorption
* methotrexate, trimethoprim, and phenytoin

Pregnancy increases folate demand because the fetus is rapidly making DNA.

That is why low folate in pregnancy increases risk of neural tube defects (failure of the embryonic brain/spinal cord tube to close), especially spina bifida and anencephaly.

---

### Labs

Folic acid deficiency causes:

* increased MCV (mean corpuscular volume; average RBC size)
* increased homocysteine
* normal methylmalonic acid

Vitamin B12 deficiency causes:

* increased homocysteine
* increased methylmalonic acid

So methylmalonic acid is the key lab divider.

---

## One-line intuition

Folate is needed to make DNA, so low folate makes fast-dividing cells grow big but divide poorly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Folate deficiency and vitamin B12 deficiency both cause megaloblastic anemia, but only vitamin B12 deficiency causes increased methylmalonic acid and neurologic symptoms.
