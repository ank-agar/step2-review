### Core idea

Oligohydramnios = too little amniotic fluid (fluid surrounding the fetus in the amniotic sac).

Break the word down:

* oligo- = low/few
* hydro- = water/fluid
* amnios = amniotic sac/fluid

So:

> low amniotic fluid around the fetus.

---

### Why it happens

In the second half of pregnancy, most amniotic fluid comes from fetal urine.

So if the fetus cannot make or release urine, amniotic fluid drops.

Classic fetal causes:

* bilateral renal agenesis (both kidneys fail to develop)
* renal dysplasia (abnormal kidney development)
* posterior urethral valves (obstruction of urine outflow in a male fetus)

Maternal/placental causes:

* placental insufficiency (placenta does not deliver enough blood/nutrients)
* preeclampsia (hypertension with end-organ signs in pregnancy)
* post-term pregnancy
* PROM (premature rupture of membranes, meaning the amniotic sac breaks before labor)
* PPROM (preterm premature rupture of membranes, meaning rupture before 37 weeks)

---

### Why low fluid is dangerous

Amniotic fluid is not just "cushion water."

It lets the fetus:

* move freely
* grow without being compressed
* develop lungs properly

So with too little fluid:

* fetus gets compressed
* limbs/face can deform
* umbilical cord can get compressed
* lungs may not develop well

The big Step 1 association is pulmonary hypoplasia (underdeveloped lungs).

Why?

The fetus needs fluid-filled space and fetal breathing movements to help lungs expand and develop. If there is not enough fluid, the chest/lungs stay compressed, so lung tissue develops poorly.

---

### Diagnosis

Usually found on ultrasound.

Important measurements:

* AFI (amniotic fluid index) < 5 cm suggests oligohydramnios
* MVP (maximum vertical pocket) < 2 cm suggests oligohydramnios

One-line intuition:

> If fetal urine is low or fluid is leaking out, the amniotic "swimming pool" dries up.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Oligohydramnios + bilateral renal agenesis can cause Potter sequence:

* pulmonary hypoplasia
* limb deformities
* flattened face
* low-set ears

Reason: no kidneys → no fetal urine → low amniotic fluid → fetal compression + poor lung development.
