### Core idea

Ectopic pregnancy = implantation of a fertilized egg outside the uterine cavity.

Most common location:

* fallopian tube, especially the ampulla

Ampulla = widest part of the fallopian tube where fertilization usually happens.

---

### Why it happens

Normally:

* egg is fertilized in the fallopian tube
* embryo travels to the uterus
* embryo implants in the endometrium

Endometrium = inner lining of the uterus.

If the tube is scarred or motility is impaired, the embryo gets stuck and implants too early in the tube.

Common risk factors:

* pelvic inflammatory disease, especially from Chlamydia trachomatis
* prior ectopic pregnancy
* tubal surgery
* endometriosis
* smoking
* intrauterine device, if pregnancy occurs

Pelvic inflammatory disease = infection/inflammation of the upper female reproductive tract.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* positive pregnancy test
* abdominal/pelvic pain
* vaginal bleeding

If rupture happens:

* severe pain
* peritoneal signs
* hypotension
* shock

Peritoneal signs = pain from irritation of the abdominal lining.

---

### Diagnosis intuition

Use:

* beta-hCG (beta-human chorionic gonadotropin)
* transvaginal ultrasound

High-yield pattern:

* positive beta-hCG
* no intrauterine pregnancy seen on ultrasound
* adnexal mass or free fluid

Adnexal = near the uterus, usually ovary/fallopian tube region.

Why beta-hCG matters:

* beta-hCG confirms pregnancy
* ultrasound tells you where the pregnancy is

So the danger pattern is:

> pregnant patient + empty uterus + pain/bleeding = think ectopic.

---

### Why it is dangerous

The fallopian tube is not built to expand like the uterus.

So as the pregnancy grows:

* tube stretches
* blood vessels tear
* tube can rupture
* intraperitoneal bleeding can occur

Intraperitoneal = inside the abdominal/peritoneal cavity.

---

## One-line intuition

Ectopic pregnancy is a pregnancy implanted in the wrong place, usually the fallopian tube, where growth can rupture the tube and cause life-threatening bleeding.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, the biggest risk factor association is prior salpingitis from Chlamydia trachomatis, because scarring of the fallopian tube traps the embryo before it reaches the uterus.

Salpingitis = inflammation of the fallopian tube.
