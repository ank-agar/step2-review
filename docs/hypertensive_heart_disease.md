## Hypertensive heart disease

Hypertensive heart disease means heart changes caused by long-standing hypertension (high blood pressure).

Core idea:

* high blood pressure = high afterload (pressure the heart must pump against)
* the left ventricle has to push harder
* the left ventricular muscle gets thick
* thick muscle becomes stiff
* stiff ventricle fills poorly

This is usually:

* left ventricular hypertrophy, or LVH (left ventricle muscle thickening)
* diastolic dysfunction (impaired filling during relaxation)
* left atrial enlargement (left atrium gets stretched from backup pressure)
* heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, or HFpEF (heart failure where squeezing is relatively preserved but filling is bad)

Why does hypertension cause LVH?

Think of the left ventricle like a person lifting heavier weights every day.

If the aorta/systemic arteries are under high pressure, the left ventricle must generate more force to eject blood.

So the myocardium (heart muscle) adapts by adding sarcomeres in parallel.

Sarcomeres (contractile units of muscle) in parallel make the wall thicker, not necessarily wider.

That is called concentric hypertrophy.

So:

> chronic pressure overload → concentric LVH → stiff ventricle → diastolic heart failure

Why does the patient get symptoms?

* stiff LV cannot relax/fill well → blood backs up into left atrium
* left atrial pressure rises → pulmonary congestion
* pulmonary congestion → dyspnea (shortness of breath), orthopnea (shortness of breath lying flat), crackles
* thick muscle needs more oxygen → angina (chest pain from myocardial ischemia) can happen
* enlarged/thick heart tissue disrupts conduction → arrhythmias (abnormal heart rhythms)

ECG (electrocardiogram) idea:

More left ventricular muscle mass creates bigger electrical forces.

So LVH can show:

* tall R waves in left-sided leads like V5/V6
* deep S waves in right-sided leads like V1/V2

## One-line intuition

Hypertension makes the left ventricle pump against a high-pressure wall, so it gets thick and strong at first, then stiff and bad at filling.

## Pearl 🔥

Hypertensive heart disease classically causes concentric LVH and HFpEF because pressure overload adds sarcomeres in parallel.
