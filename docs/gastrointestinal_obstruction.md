# Gastrointestinal obstruction

### Core idea

Gastrointestinal obstruction means a blockage somewhere in the GI tract (gastrointestinal tract = stomach + intestines).

So food, gas, and fluid cannot move forward normally.

Think:

> stuff goes in, but cannot pass the blocked point.

---

### What happens before and after the blockage

Before the obstruction:

* bowel dilates
* gas/fluid builds up
* peristalsis increases at first

After the obstruction:

* bowel is decompressed (empty/collapsed)
* stool/gas may stop passing

Peristalsis means the wave-like contractions that push contents through the gut.

---

### Symptoms

Classic findings:

* abdominal pain
* abdominal distension
* vomiting
* constipation or obstipation

Obstipation means inability to pass stool or gas.

Why vomiting happens:

* contents cannot move forward
* pressure builds up behind the blockage
* the gut backs up proximally

Proximal means closer to the mouth; distal means farther from the mouth.

---

### Common causes

Mechanical obstruction means there is a physical blockage.

Common Step 1 causes:

* adhesions after surgery
* hernia
* tumor
* volvulus
* intussusception
* gallstone ileus

Volvulus means twisting of bowel around itself.

Intussusception means one part of bowel telescopes into another.

Gallstone ileus means a gallstone enters the bowel through a fistula (abnormal connection) and blocks it.

---

### Key distinction

Mechanical obstruction:

* physical blockage
* bowel before blockage is dilated
* bowel after blockage is collapsed

Ileus:

* bowel stops moving properly
* no true physical blockage
* often after surgery, opioids, electrolyte problems

Opioids are pain medications like morphine that slow gut motility.

---

### Imaging intuition

On abdominal X-ray, obstruction can show:

* dilated bowel loops
* air-fluid levels
* little/no gas past the obstruction

Air-fluid levels happen because trapped gas rises above trapped fluid in bowel loops.

---

## One-line intuition

GI obstruction is a traffic jam in the gut: contents pile up before the blockage, while the bowel after it becomes empty.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Small bowel obstruction is most commonly caused by adhesions from prior abdominal surgery.
