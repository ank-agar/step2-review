## Neonatal thrush

![Image](https://assets.openstax.org/oscms-prodcms/media/original_images/c751c7ede952ee418ea3bc66673f81e97375f674.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a0/Human_tongue_infected_with_oral_candidiasis.jpg)

Neonatal thrush is **oral candidiasis** (Candida yeast infection in the mouth) in a newborn.

Most commonly caused by:

* **Candida albicans** (a yeast-like fungus)

It causes white, creamy plaques on:

* tongue
* inner cheeks
* lips
* palate (roof of mouth)

The classic look is:

> white patches that look like milk curds/cottage cheese.

---

### Why babies get it

Newborns are prone to thrush because:

* their immune system is immature
* their oral mucosa (mouth lining) is delicate
* frequent sucking causes minor irritation
* antibiotics can reduce normal bacteria, letting Candida overgrow
* Candida can pass between baby mouth and breastfeeding nipples

So the core idea is:

> Candida is normally present in small amounts, but in newborns it can overgrow on the mouth lining.

---

### Thrush vs milk residue

Milk residue:

* usually only on the tongue
* wipes away easily
* improves between feeds

Thrush:

* often involves cheeks/lips/palate too
* sticks more firmly
* if scraped hard, can leave a red or slightly bleeding base

That is why **white tongue alone** in a newborn is often just milk, not automatically thrush.

---

### Symptoms

Can be:

* asymptomatic
* fussiness with feeding
* poor latch or decreased sucking
* white plaques in mouth
* sometimes associated diaper rash

Diaper rash association happens because Candida can also overgrow in warm, moist skin folds.

---

### Treatment idea

Usually treated with:

* **nystatin** (topical antifungal medication) oral suspension

If recurrent or severe, clinicians may consider:

* treating breastfeeding parent nipple Candida too
* checking for antibiotic exposure
* considering immune problems if unusually severe/persistent

---

## One-line intuition

Neonatal thrush = **Candida overgrowth on a newborn's mouth lining**, making adherent white plaques that look like milk but do not wipe away easily.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Candida albicans is a **budding yeast with pseudohyphae** (elongated chained yeast forms), and it is **germ tube positive** on Step 1.
