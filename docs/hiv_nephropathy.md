### Core idea

HIV nephropathy, also called HIVAN (human immunodeficiency virus-associated nephropathy), is kidney damage from HIV (human immunodeficiency virus).

Classic pathology:

* collapsing FSGS (focal segmental glomerulosclerosis)
* microcystic tubular dilation
* interstitial inflammation

Break down FSGS:

* focal = some glomeruli
* segmental = part of each affected glomerulus
* glomerulosclerosis = scarring of the glomerulus

So in HIV nephropathy:

> HIV injures kidney epithelial cells, especially podocytes, causing the glomerular tuft to collapse and scar.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Heavy proteinuria:** podocytes are damaged, so the filtration barrier leaks protein
* **Nephrotic syndrome:** massive protein loss in urine causes hypoalbuminemia (low blood albumin) and edema
* **Rapid kidney failure:** collapsing FSGS is aggressive and scars glomeruli quickly
* **Enlarged echogenic kidneys on ultrasound:** tubular injury and inflammation make the kidneys look big and bright
* **Microcystic tubules:** damaged tubules dilate and fill with proteinaceous material

---

### Step 1 association

Think:

* HIV patient
* Black patient or African ancestry
* nephrotic-range proteinuria
* rapidly progressive renal failure
* collapsing FSGS on biopsy

This is HIVAN.

---

## One-line intuition

HIV makes podocytes lose their normal filtering structure, so the glomerulus collapses, leaks protein, and scars fast.

---

## HY pearl

HIVAN is strongly associated with APOL1 (apolipoprotein L1) risk variants, especially in patients of African ancestry.
