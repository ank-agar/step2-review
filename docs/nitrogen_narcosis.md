### Core idea

Nitrogen narcosis is a reversible altered mental state that happens when a diver breathes nitrogen under high pressure.

It feels like being drunk underwater:

* euphoria
* poor judgment
* confusion
* slowed thinking
* impaired coordination
* sometimes anxiety or panic

---

### Why it happens

Air is mostly nitrogen.

At the surface, that is fine.

But underwater, pressure rises as depth increases. So the diver breathes a higher partial pressure of nitrogen.

Partial pressure means the pressure contributed by one gas inside a gas mixture.

So:

* deeper dive
* higher nitrogen partial pressure
* more nitrogen dissolves into nervous system tissue
* brain function becomes impaired

The exact mechanism is not perfectly known, but the big idea is that nitrogen affects neuronal membranes, especially because the brain is lipid-rich.

Lipid-rich means high in fat-like molecules.

---

### Why the symptoms are dangerous

The scary part is not usually direct tissue damage.

The scary part is bad decisions underwater.

A narced diver may:

* ignore air supply
* keep swimming deeper
* forget the dive plan
* respond slowly to danger
* become overconfident

So the brain is impaired exactly when the diver needs good judgment.

---

### How to fix it

Ascend to a shallower depth.

As pressure decreases, nitrogen partial pressure decreases, and symptoms usually improve quickly.

Important:

* nitrogen narcosis improves with ascent
* decompression sickness may worsen if ascent is too fast

Decompression sickness means gas bubbles form in tissues/blood during ascent.

So the diver should ascend safely, not shoot straight to the surface.

---

## One-line intuition

Nitrogen narcosis = too much pressurized nitrogen makes the brain act drunk during deep diving.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Nitrogen narcosis classically starts becoming noticeable around 30 m / 100 ft, and it is relieved by ascending to a shallower depth.
