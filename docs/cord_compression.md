# Cord compression

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Diagram_showing_a_tumour_causing_spinal_cord_compression_CRUK_081.svg/3840px-Diagram_showing_a_tumour_causing_spinal_cord_compression_CRUK_081.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/52/Herniated_disk.svg/960px-Herniated_disk.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6b/Spinal_cord_compression.jpg)

Cord compression means something is physically pressing on the spinal cord.

The spinal cord is basically the main "wire" carrying signals between the brain and body.

So if it gets squeezed, signals cannot travel normally.

---

### Common causes

Anything that narrows the spinal canal can compress the cord:

* tumor
* herniated disc
* vertebral fracture
* abscess
* hematoma
* degenerative spine disease

Hematoma means a collection of blood.

Abscess means a collection of pus/infection.

---

### Why symptoms happen

Compression damages the long nerve tracts in the spinal cord.

That can cause:

* weakness below the lesion
* numbness/tingling
* hyperreflexia
* spasticity
* bowel/bladder dysfunction

Hyperreflexia means increased reflexes.

Spasticity means stiff, tight muscles from upper motor neuron damage.

Upper motor neuron means the nerve pathway from brain/spinal cord that controls movement before it reaches the peripheral nerve.

---

### Key intuition

Think of the spinal cord like a highway.

Compression is like a rockslide narrowing the highway:

* mild narrowing = traffic still moves, but slower
* severe narrowing = signals get blocked
* long compression = permanent damage

---

### Why this is urgent

The spinal cord does not tolerate prolonged pressure well.

If compression is not relieved, ischemia and edema can develop.

Ischemia means low blood flow.

Edema means swelling.

Swelling makes the cord even tighter inside the spinal canal, which worsens the problem.

---

## One-line intuition

Cord compression = something is squeezing the spinal cord, causing upper motor neuron signs and sensory/motor loss below the level of compression.

---

## Pearl

Metastatic spinal cord compression is a medical emergency: suspect it in a cancer patient with new back pain plus neurologic deficits or bowel/bladder dysfunction.
