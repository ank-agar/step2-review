## Right bundle branch block

![Image](https://litfl.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Sequence-of-conduction-in-RBBB.png)

![Image](https://litfl.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Right-Bundle-Branch-Block-RBBB-ECG-Strip-LITFL-1024x622.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/Right_bundle_branch_block_ECG_characteristics.png)

![Image](https://litfl.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/ECG-Right-Bundle-Branch-Block-RBBB-5-1024x377.jpg)

Right bundle branch block (RBBB) means the electrical signal cannot travel normally down the right bundle branch (the pathway that rapidly carries impulses to the right ventricle).

So the left ventricle activates first, then the signal spreads slowly from left ventricle to right ventricle.

That delayed right ventricular activation causes:

* wide QRS complex (ventricular depolarization takes longer)
* RSR' pattern in V1 (the classic "M-shaped" QRS)
* wide/slurred S wave in lateral leads like I, V5, and V6

Break it down:

* right = affects the right-sided conduction pathway
* bundle branch = His-Purkinje conduction pathway to the ventricles
* block = delayed or interrupted conduction

Why the ECG looks weird:

* The left ventricle depolarizes normally through the left bundle branch.
* The right ventricle depolarizes late by slower cell-to-cell spread.
* That late rightward electrical force points toward V1, creating the extra R' wave.

So V1 sees:

* first small r wave
* then S wave
* then late R' wave

That gives the classic rSR' or "rabbit ears" pattern.

Common associations:

* normal variant, especially incomplete RBBB (incomplete right bundle branch block)
* pulmonary embolism (clot in pulmonary artery)
* right ventricular hypertrophy (thickened right ventricle)
* atrial septal defect (hole between atria)
* ischemic heart disease (reduced blood flow to heart muscle)

## One-line intuition

The right ventricle gets the electrical signal late, so the QRS gets wide and V1 shows a late extra R' wave.

## Pearl 🔥

For USMLE, remember RBBB by **MaRRoW**:

* RBBB gives an M-shaped QRS in V1
* LBBB (left bundle branch block) gives a W-shaped QRS in V1
