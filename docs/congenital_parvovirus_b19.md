### Core idea

Congenital parvovirus B19 means the fetus gets infected with parvovirus B19 through the placenta during pregnancy.

Parvovirus B19 is a small single-stranded DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) virus that loves erythroid precursors (immature red blood cell-making cells).

So the big problem is:

* virus crosses placenta
* infects fetal red blood cell precursors
* fetal red blood cell production drops
* severe fetal anemia develops
* anemia can cause high-output heart failure
* heart failure leads to hydrops fetalis

Hydrops fetalis means abnormal fluid buildup in the fetus, like edema (swelling), ascites (fluid in the abdomen), pleural effusion (fluid around the lungs), or pericardial effusion (fluid around the heart).

---

### Why anemia causes hydrops

If the fetus has severe anemia, the blood carries less oxygen.

To compensate, the fetal heart pumps harder and faster.

At first:

* more cardiac output helps deliver oxygen

But if the anemia is severe:

* the heart cannot keep up
* venous pressure rises
* fluid leaks into tissues/body cavities
* hydrops fetalis happens

So the intuition is:

> Parvovirus B19 shuts down fetal RBC (red blood cell) production, and the fetus becomes so anemic that the heart fails and fluid backs up everywhere.

---

### Classic maternal clue

The mom may have fifth disease, also called erythema infectiosum (parvovirus B19 rash illness).

Classic finding:

* "slapped cheek" rash
* mild viral symptoms
* sometimes arthralgia (joint pain), especially in adults

But maternal infection can also be asymptomatic.

---

### Fetal findings

Think:

* nonimmune hydrops fetalis
* severe fetal anemia
* myocarditis (inflammation of heart muscle)
* miscarriage or stillbirth in severe cases

Nonimmune hydrops fetalis means hydrops not caused by Rh (Rhesus blood group) incompatibility.

---

## One-line intuition

Congenital parvovirus B19 is a placenta-crossing infection that destroys fetal RBC (red blood cell) precursors, causing severe anemia, high-output heart failure, and hydrops fetalis.

---

## Pearl

Parvovirus B19 causes aplastic crisis in sickle cell disease for the same reason it causes fetal hydrops: it attacks erythroid precursors (immature red blood cell precursors), so RBC (red blood cell) production suddenly crashes.
