### Core idea

Folate deficiency = low vitamin B9.

Folate is needed to make DNA (genetic material), especially thymidine, one of the DNA building blocks.

So the biggest problem is:

* poor DNA synthesis
* impaired cell division
* megaloblastic anemia

Megaloblastic anemia means anemia with large immature RBC (red blood cell) precursors because DNA synthesis is defective.

---

### Why the cells get big

RBC precursors need to divide several times before becoming mature RBCs.

If folate is low:

* DNA synthesis slows down
* nucleus cannot divide normally
* cytoplasm keeps growing
* cell becomes large

So:

> less DNA division = fewer cell divisions = bigger cells.

That gives macrocytosis, meaning large RBCs, with increased MCV (mean corpuscular volume).

---

### Classic findings

Folate deficiency causes:

* megaloblastic anemia
* macro-ovalocytes, meaning large oval RBCs
* hypersegmented neutrophils, meaning neutrophils with too many nuclear lobes
* glossitis, meaning inflamed tongue
* fatigue/pallor from anemia

Labs:

* increased MCV (mean corpuscular volume)
* increased homocysteine
* normal methylmalonic acid

---

### Folate vs B12

Both folate deficiency and vitamin B12 deficiency cause megaloblastic anemia.

Key difference:

* folate deficiency: no neurologic symptoms
* vitamin B12 deficiency: neurologic symptoms can happen

Also:

* folate deficiency: homocysteine high, methylmalonic acid normal
* B12 deficiency: homocysteine high, methylmalonic acid high

Methylmalonic acid is a metabolite that rises when vitamin B12-dependent metabolism is impaired.

---

### Causes

Think low intake or high demand:

* poor diet
* alcoholism
* pregnancy
* malabsorption, meaning poor intestinal absorption
* drugs that block folate metabolism, like methotrexate, trimethoprim, and phenytoin

Methotrexate inhibits DHFR (dihydrofolate reductase), the enzyme that regenerates THF (tetrahydrofolate), the active folate form.

---

### Pregnancy association

Folate deficiency in pregnancy increases risk of neural tube defects, meaning failure of the embryonic brain/spinal cord tube to close properly.

Classic examples:

* spina bifida
* anencephaly

This is why folic acid supplementation is recommended before conception and early pregnancy.

---

## One-line intuition

Folate is a DNA-building vitamin, so deficiency hurts rapidly dividing cells first, especially bone marrow RBC precursors.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Folate deficiency causes increased homocysteine but normal methylmalonic acid; increased methylmalonic acid points more toward vitamin B12 deficiency.
