## Lymphogranuloma venereum

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/Chlamydae_Life_Cycle.svg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cb/Lymph_node_regions.svg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0b/Lymphogranuloma_venerum_-_lymph_nodes.jpg)

Lymphogranuloma venereum, or **LGV (lymphogranuloma venereum)**, is an **STI (sexually transmitted infection)** caused by invasive strains of **Chlamydia trachomatis**.

The important strains are:

* **L1**
* **L2**
* **L3**

These are different from the more common chlamydia strains that cause urethritis/cervicitis.

---

### Core idea

Regular chlamydia usually stays more superficial in the mucosa.

LGV strains go deeper and spread to:

* lymphatic vessels
* regional lymph nodes

That is why the name has **lympho-** in it.

Break the word apart:

* lympho = lymph nodes/lymphatics
* granuloma = inflammatory immune mass
* venereum = sexually transmitted/venereal

So:

> sexually transmitted chlamydia that invades lymphatics and causes inflamed lymph nodes.

---

### Classic progression

1. Small painless genital ulcer or papule
2. Then tender inguinal lymphadenopathy
3. Buboes can form

Definitions:

* **inguinal** = groin area
* **lymphadenopathy** = enlarged lymph nodes
* **bubo** = painful swollen lymph node, classically in the groin

---

### Why the findings happen

The bacteria enter through genital/rectal mucosa, then travel through lymphatics.

Your immune system attacks infected lymph nodes, so they become:

* enlarged
* tender
* inflamed
* sometimes suppurative

**Suppurative** means pus-forming.

---

### Rectal disease

If acquired through anal exposure, LGV can cause **proctocolitis**.

**Proctocolitis** means inflammation of the rectum and colon.

Symptoms can include:

* rectal pain
* bleeding
* discharge
* tenesmus

**Tenesmus** means feeling like you still need to poop even after emptying.

---

## One-line intuition

LGV is the invasive chlamydia that does not just irritate mucosa; it spreads to lymph nodes and makes painful groin buboes.

---

## Pearl

High-yield strain map:

* **A-C:** trachoma
* **D-K:** urethritis/cervicitis and PID (pelvic inflammatory disease)
* **L1-L3:** lymphogranuloma venereum with painful lymph node involvement
