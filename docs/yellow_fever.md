### Core idea

Yellow fever is a viral hemorrhagic fever (viral infection that can cause bleeding and shock) spread by mosquitoes.

It is caused by yellow fever virus, which is a:

* Flavivirus
* positive-sense RNA virus (RNA genome that can act like messenger RNA)
* enveloped virus

The name comes from jaundice (yellow skin/eyes from bilirubin buildup), because the virus can damage the liver.

---

### Transmission

Main vector (organism that carries disease):

* Aedes aegypti mosquito

Cycles:

* jungle/sylvatic cycle: mosquito spreads virus between monkeys and humans
* urban cycle: mosquito spreads virus between humans

So the basic chain is:

> infected mosquito bite -> virus in blood -> liver injury -> fever + jaundice + bleeding risk

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Fever/myalgias (muscle aches):** systemic viral infection triggers inflammation
* **Jaundice:** liver cells are injured, so bilirubin handling gets impaired
* **Bleeding/black vomit:** severe liver injury reduces clotting factor production, and blood can enter the GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)
* **Shock/renal failure:** severe systemic illness causes poor perfusion (poor blood flow) to organs

The liver injury classically involves midzonal necrosis (death of hepatocytes in zone 2 of the liver lobule).

---

### Diagnosis clue

Think yellow fever when you see:

* travel to endemic region (area where disease is regularly present)
* mosquito exposure
* fever
* jaundice
* hemorrhage (bleeding)

---

## One-line intuition

Yellow fever is a mosquito-borne Flavivirus that hits the liver hard, so patients get fever + jaundice + bleeding.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Yellow fever is prevented with a live attenuated vaccine (weakened live virus vaccine), which is important for travel to endemic areas.
