### Core idea

A gastrointestinal carcinoid tumor is a slow-growing neuroendocrine tumor (tumor from hormone-secreting nerve/endocrine-type cells) in the GI tract (gastrointestinal tract).

Most Step 1-relevant sites:

* small intestine, especially ileum
* appendix
* rectum
* stomach

The classic cell of origin is the enterochromaffin cell, which can make serotonin.

Serotonin is also called 5-HT (5-hydroxytryptamine).

---

### Why symptoms happen

Many GI carcinoid tumors are silent at first because serotonin from the gut drains through the portal vein to the liver.

The liver breaks down serotonin before it reaches the rest of the body.

So:

* tumor in gut only -> serotonin gets metabolized by liver
* tumor spreads to liver -> serotonin enters systemic blood
* systemic serotonin/vasoactive substances -> carcinoid syndrome

Carcinoid syndrome means:

* flushing
* diarrhea
* wheezing/bronchospasm
* right-sided heart valve disease

The intuition:

> The liver normally acts like a filter. Once the tumor metastasizes to the liver, the hormone products bypass that filter and cause whole-body symptoms.

---

### Why right-sided heart disease happens

Serotonin reaches the right heart first through venous blood.

This can cause fibrous thickening of:

* tricuspid valve
* pulmonary valve

The left heart is usually spared because the lungs contain MAO (monoamine oxidase), an enzyme that breaks down serotonin before it reaches the left heart.

---

### Diagnosis clue

Serotonin is metabolized into 5-HIAA (5-hydroxyindoleacetic acid).

So the classic test is:

* increased urinary 5-HIAA

Histology often shows:

* nests/trabeculae of uniform neuroendocrine cells
* "salt and pepper" chromatin (finely speckled nuclear material)
* positive chromogranin and synaptophysin

---

## One-line intuition

GI carcinoid tumor is a slow neuroendocrine tumor that can secrete serotonin, but carcinoid syndrome usually appears only after liver metastasis lets serotonin escape liver breakdown.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Carcinoid syndrome can cause pellagra because tryptophan gets diverted toward serotonin production instead of niacin production.

Pellagra = diarrhea, dermatitis, dementia.
