# MALT lymphoma

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/71/MALT_and_mucosal_immune_response.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Gastric_MALT_lymphoma_2.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a0/Gastrointestinal_lymphoepithelial_lesion_-_high_mag.jpg)

MALT lymphoma = **mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue lymphoma**.

It is a low-grade **B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma** that forms in lymphoid tissue near mucosal surfaces, especially the stomach.

Break it down:

* **mucosa** = wet lining of organs, like stomach or airway lining
* **associated lymphoid tissue** = immune tissue sitting near that mucosa
* **lymphoma** = cancer of lymphocytes, usually B cells here

---

### Core idea

Chronic irritation keeps calling immune cells to a mucosal site.

Over time:

* chronic inflammation recruits lymphocytes
* B cells keep proliferating
* one B-cell clone can become malignant
* this becomes MALT lymphoma

So MALT lymphoma is basically:

> chronic immune stimulation causing a slow-growing B-cell cancer in mucosal tissue.

---

### Classic cause

The most tested association is:

* **H. pylori** = Helicobacter pylori, a spiral bacteria that infects the stomach
* chronic H. pylori gastritis can lead to gastric MALT lymphoma

Why?

H. pylori does not usually directly turn the B cell cancerous in one step.

Instead, it creates long-term inflammation:

* H. pylori antigen stimulates T cells
* T cells stimulate B cells
* B cells keep dividing
* repeated division increases risk of malignant transformation

---

### Why antibiotics can treat it

Early gastric MALT lymphoma can regress after H. pylori eradication therapy.

Reason:

If the lymphoma is still dependent on H. pylori-driven inflammation, removing H. pylori removes the growth signal.

So:

* remove bacteria
* remove chronic antigen stimulation
* B-cell clone may shrink

---

### Other associations

MALT lymphoma can also happen outside the stomach due to chronic inflammation/autoimmunity:

* salivary gland with Sjogren syndrome
* thyroid with Hashimoto thyroiditis
* lung, skin, ocular adnexa, intestine

**Sjogren syndrome** = autoimmune destruction of salivary/lacrimal glands causing dry mouth and dry eyes.

**Hashimoto thyroiditis** = autoimmune thyroid destruction causing hypothyroidism.

---

## One-line intuition

MALT lymphoma is a slow B-cell lymphoma that grows where chronic inflammation keeps making mucosal immune tissue.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: gastric MALT lymphoma is strongly associated with **H. pylori**, and early cases can be treated with H. pylori eradication antibiotics.
