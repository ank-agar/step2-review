## Mumps virus

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/24397.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/mumps/images/glands_image.gif)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/54/Mumps_virus.jpg)

Mumps virus is a **paramyxovirus** (enveloped, negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus) that classically infects the **parotid glands** (major salivary glands in front of the ears).

Main idea:

* virus enters through respiratory droplets/saliva
* replicates in the upper respiratory tract
* spreads through blood
* targets salivary glands, especially the parotid glands

This causes:

* fever
* headache/malaise
* painful parotid swelling
* puffy cheeks
* jaw pain, especially with chewing/sour foods

---

### Why the swelling happens

The virus infects and inflames salivary gland tissue.

Inflammation causes:

* fluid leakage into tissue
* immune cell infiltration
* gland enlargement
* pain/tenderness

So the classic look is:

> swollen parotid gland pushing the cheek/jaw area outward.

---

### Important complications

Mumps can spread beyond the parotid glands and cause:

* orchitis (testicular inflammation), especially after puberty
* aseptic meningitis (meningitis not caused by typical bacteria)
* pancreatitis (pancreas inflammation)
* sensorineural hearing loss (inner ear/nerve-related hearing loss)

Orchitis is the big Step 1 association.

---

### Prevention

Prevented by the **MMR vaccine** (measles, mumps, rubella vaccine), which is a live attenuated vaccine (weakened live virus vaccine).

---

## One-line intuition

Mumps virus is a respiratory-spread RNA virus that loves the parotid glands, causing painful cheek/jaw swelling.
