## HELLP syndrome

![Image](https://www.medcomic.com/images/comics/preeclampsia-pathophysiology.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK542208/bin/HEME010.jpg)

### Core idea

HELLP syndrome is a severe pregnancy complication, usually related to preeclampsia.

HELLP = 

* **H**emolysis (red blood cell destruction)
* **EL**evated liver enzymes (liver injury labs, like AST/ALT)
* **LP** = low platelets (thrombocytopenia, meaning decreased platelet count)

So the one-line idea is:

> pregnancy-related endothelial injury causes microthrombi, which shred RBCs, consume platelets, and injure the liver.

---

### Why the findings happen

In preeclampsia, abnormal placental blood flow can cause widespread endothelial dysfunction (blood vessel lining injury).

That causes small clots/microthrombi in tiny vessels.

Then:

* RBCs (red blood cells) get sliced as they pass through → **hemolysis**
* platelets get used up making microthrombi → **low platelets**
* liver blood flow gets disrupted → **elevated liver enzymes** and RUQ pain

RUQ pain = right upper quadrant pain, because the liver sits in the right upper abdomen.

---

### Classic clues

Think of HELLP in a pregnant patient, usually in the 3rd trimester, with:

* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* RUQ/epigastric pain
* nausea/vomiting
* headache or visual symptoms sometimes
* thrombocytopenia (low platelets)
* increased AST/ALT (liver enzymes)
* schistocytes (fragmented RBCs) on blood smear

Schistocytes are the "RBCs got mechanically chopped up" clue.

---

## One-line intuition

HELLP is basically severe preeclampsia where damaged vessels create tiny clots that chew up RBCs and platelets, while the liver gets injured.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: HELLP is a form of microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, meaning RBCs are destroyed mechanically in small vessels, so you see **schistocytes** and increased LDH (lactate dehydrogenase, a marker of cell breakdown).
