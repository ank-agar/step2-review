# Macular degeneration

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19532.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/04/Intermediate_age_related_macular_degeneration.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Macular_hard_drusen.png)

Macular degeneration is degeneration of the macula, which is the central part of the retina responsible for sharp central vision.

So the main problem is:

* central vision gets blurry or distorted
* peripheral vision is usually spared

Think:

> macula = middle/detail vision

So if the macula degenerates, the patient has trouble with things like reading, recognizing faces, or seeing fine detail.

---

### Main types

**Dry macular degeneration** is more common.

It is due to:

* retinal pigment epithelium damage
* photoreceptor loss
* drusen buildup

Drusen are yellow extracellular deposits under the retina.

Intuition:

> trash builds up under the macula, and the support layer for photoreceptors slowly wears down.

---

**Wet macular degeneration** is less common but more severe.

It is due to:

* abnormal new blood vessel growth under the retina
* leakage
* hemorrhage
* scarring

This abnormal vessel growth is called choroidal neovascularization, meaning new blood vessels growing from the choroid, which is the vascular layer under the retina.

Why it is worse:

* new vessels are fragile
* fragile vessels leak blood/fluid
* blood/fluid damages the macula quickly

---

### Symptoms

Classic findings:

* painless central vision loss
* blurry central vision
* distorted straight lines
* difficulty reading

The distortion of straight lines is called metamorphopsia, meaning objects look warped or bent.

---

## One-line intuition

Macular degeneration damages the retina's central high-resolution zone, so the patient loses detailed central vision while peripheral vision is relatively preserved.

---

## HY pearl

Wet age-related macular degeneration is treated with anti-VEGF therapy, meaning anti-vascular endothelial growth factor therapy, because VEGF drives abnormal leaky blood vessel growth.
