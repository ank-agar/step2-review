# Chediak-Higashi disease

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK5188/bin/chediak-higashi-Image001.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Autosomal_recessive_-_en.svg/960px-Autosomal_recessive_-_en.svg.png)

Chediak-Higashi disease is an **autosomal recessive** disorder caused by mutation in **LYST** (lysosomal trafficking regulator).

Core problem:

* defective lysosomal trafficking
* giant granules in neutrophils
* impaired killing of bacteria

Break it down:

* lysosome = cell organelle that digests/kills things
* trafficking = moving vesicles/granules to the right place
* neutrophil = innate immune cell that kills bacteria

So the intuition is:

> The neutrophil has the weapons, but it cannot package and deliver them correctly.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Recurrent pyogenic infections:** neutrophils cannot kill bacteria well
* **Giant granules in neutrophils:** lysosomal granules abnormally fuse together
* **Partial albinism:** melanosomes (pigment-containing organelles) also have trafficking problems
* **Bleeding tendency:** platelet dense granules do not work normally
* **Neurologic problems:** abnormal lysosome-related organelles can affect neurons too

---

### Classic buzzwords

Think:

* recurrent infections
* partial albinism / silvery hair
* peripheral neuropathy
* giant granules in neutrophils

---

## One-line intuition

Chediak-Higashi = **bad lysosomal trafficking**, so neutrophils form **giant granules** and cannot kill bacteria properly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Differentiate from **chronic granulomatous disease**:

* Chediak-Higashi = defective lysosomal trafficking, giant granules
* Chronic granulomatous disease = defective NADPH oxidase (enzyme that makes reactive oxygen species), abnormal oxidative burst
