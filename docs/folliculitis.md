## Folliculitis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2a/Pityrosporum_folliculitis.jpg/960px-Pityrosporum_folliculitis.jpg)

Folliculitis = inflammation of the hair follicle.

A hair follicle is the little skin pocket that a hair grows out of.

So the core idea is:

> inflamed/infected hair pore = small red bump or pustule centered around a hair.

Pustule means a small pus-filled bump.

---

### What causes it?

Common causes:

* **Staphylococcus aureus** bacteria
* shaving/skin trauma
* friction from tight clothing
* hot tubs, classically **Pseudomonas aeruginosa** bacteria
* **Malassezia** yeast, also called pityrosporum folliculitis

Reasoning:

The follicle is like a tiny tunnel into the skin. If it gets irritated or blocked, bacteria/yeast can overgrow there, so immune cells rush in and create redness, tenderness, and pus.

---

### What does it look like?

Usually:

* small red bumps
* white-headed pustules
* centered around hairs
* itchy or tender
* common on beard area, scalp, chest, back, buttocks, thighs

Key visual clue:

> bumps are follicle-centered, meaning each bump is around a hair follicle.

---

### Superficial vs deep

**Superficial folliculitis** = upper part of the follicle is inflamed.

Usually mild, small pustules.

**Deep folliculitis** = deeper follicle is involved.

Can become a furuncle, meaning a boil, which is a deeper painful pus-filled infection.

---

### One-line intuition

Folliculitis is basically acne-like inflammation, but the problem is specifically centered on hair follicles.

---

## Pearl

Hot tub folliculitis is classically caused by **Pseudomonas aeruginosa** after exposure to contaminated warm water, and it often shows up under swimsuit-covered areas.
