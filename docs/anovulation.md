# Anovulation

### Core idea

Anovulation = no ovulation.

Ovulation means release of an oocyte (egg) from the ovary.

So anovulation means:

* the follicle does not successfully release an egg
* no corpus luteum forms
* progesterone stays low

---

### Why bleeding can still happen

Even if there is no ovulation, the patient can still have uterine bleeding.

Reason:

* estrogen can still build up the endometrium (uterine lining)
* but without ovulation, there is no corpus luteum
* no corpus luteum = low progesterone
* low progesterone = the lining is not stabilized

So the endometrium grows in an irregular, unstable way and can shed unpredictably.

That is why anovulation often causes:

* irregular periods
* heavy bleeding
* skipped periods
* infertility

---

### Big causes

Common causes include:

* PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)
* hypothalamic dysfunction from stress, low weight, or excessive exercise
* hyperprolactinemia (high prolactin)
* thyroid disease
* perimenopause (transition before menopause)

PCOS is very high-yield because increased androgens (male-type sex hormones) and abnormal LH (luteinizing hormone) signaling prevent normal follicle maturation.

---

## One-line intuition

Anovulation is a cycle where estrogen may still grow the uterine lining, but no egg is released, so progesterone never shows up to organize and stabilize the lining.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Chronic anovulation causes unopposed estrogen, which increases risk of endometrial hyperplasia (overgrowth of the uterine lining) and endometrial cancer.
