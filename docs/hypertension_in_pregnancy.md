Hypertension in pregnancy = high blood pressure during pregnancy.

Usually defined as:

* systolic blood pressure (SBP; top number) >= 140 mmHg
* or diastolic blood pressure (DBP; bottom number) >= 90 mmHg

The big Step 1 distinction is timing:

* **Before 20 weeks:** think chronic hypertension
* **After 20 weeks:** think gestational hypertension or preeclampsia

---

### Main categories

* **Chronic hypertension:** hypertension that existed before pregnancy or is found before 20 weeks
* **Gestational hypertension:** new hypertension after 20 weeks, but no proteinuria (protein in urine) or end-organ damage
* **Preeclampsia:** new hypertension after 20 weeks plus proteinuria or end-organ damage
* **Eclampsia:** preeclampsia plus seizures
* **HELLP syndrome:** hemolysis (red blood cell breakdown), elevated liver enzymes, and low platelets

---

### Why preeclampsia happens

Preeclampsia is mainly a placenta problem.

Normal pregnancy:

* trophoblasts (placental cells) remodel maternal spiral arteries
* arteries become wide, low-resistance vessels
* placenta gets good blood flow

Preeclampsia:

* poor trophoblast invasion
* spiral arteries stay narrow/high-resistance
* placenta becomes relatively ischemic (low oxygen/blood flow)
* placenta releases antiangiogenic factors (factors that block blood vessel growth)
* maternal endothelial dysfunction occurs

Endothelial dysfunction = injured blood vessel lining.

That explains the findings:

* vasoconstriction -> hypertension
* glomerular injury -> proteinuria
* liver irritation -> RUQ (right upper quadrant) pain and elevated liver enzymes
* brain irritation -> headache, visual symptoms, seizures
* platelet activation/consumption -> thrombocytopenia (low platelets)

---

## One-line intuition

Hypertension in pregnancy is dangerous when the placenta triggers whole-body maternal endothelial dysfunction, causing high BP plus kidney, liver, brain, or platelet problems.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Preeclampsia after 20 weeks + seizure = **eclampsia**. Treat/prevent seizures with magnesium sulfate, and definitive treatment is delivery of the placenta.
