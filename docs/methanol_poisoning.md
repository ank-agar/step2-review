## Methanol poisoning

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/5dd2/3060395/a534d1633e3f/kjo-25-146-g002.jpg)

Methanol poisoning = toxic alcohol ingestion where methanol gets metabolized into **formic acid**, which causes:

* high anion gap metabolic acidosis (too many unmeasured acids in blood)
* visual problems/blindness
* CNS (central nervous system) depression

---

### Core pathway

Methanol itself is not the main problem.

The problem is what the liver turns it into:

* methanol
* formaldehyde
* formic acid/formate

Enzymes:

* ADH (alcohol dehydrogenase): methanol to formaldehyde
* ALDH (aldehyde dehydrogenase): formaldehyde to formic acid

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Metabolic acidosis:** formic acid adds acid to the blood
* **Visual symptoms:** formic acid damages retina/optic nerve mitochondria (cell energy factories)
* **CNS depression:** methanol acts like other alcohols and slows brain activity
* **Delayed symptoms:** it takes time for methanol to be converted into toxic formic acid

Classic visual complaint:

* blurry vision
* "snowfield" vision
* blindness

---

### Treatment intuition

Give **fomepizole**.

Fomepizole inhibits ADH (alcohol dehydrogenase), so methanol cannot become formic acid.

Ethanol can also work because ADH prefers ethanol over methanol, so ethanol competitively blocks methanol metabolism.

Also consider:

* bicarbonate for severe acidosis
* hemodialysis (blood filtering) for severe poisoning
* folinic acid/folic acid to help clear formate

---

## One-line intuition

Methanol poisoning is dangerous because the body turns methanol into formic acid, which acidifies blood and selectively injures the optic nerve.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Toxic alcohol pattern:

* methanol = blindness
* ethylene glycol = kidney failure with calcium oxalate crystals

Both cause high anion gap metabolic acidosis.
