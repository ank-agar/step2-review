## Hypovolemic shock

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/170225_CVS_Hypovolemic_Shock_Pathophysiology_of_Disease.png?utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original&utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org)

![Image](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f%3Dwebp/apps/archive/20260407.195030/resources/e08b3fa56ffe450c120fde3e7cbfe301d2fdc45b)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17231.jpg)

Hypovolemic shock = shock from low intravascular volume (low fluid/blood inside the blood vessels).

Most common causes:

* hemorrhage (bleeding)
* severe vomiting/diarrhea
* burns
* third spacing (fluid leaves blood vessels and gets trapped in tissues/body spaces)

---

### Core idea

The body does not have enough circulating volume to fill the heart.

So:

* low volume → low venous return (less blood returning to heart)
* low venous return → low preload (ventricular filling before contraction)
* low preload → low stroke volume (blood pumped per beat)
* low stroke volume → low cardiac output (blood pumped per minute)
* low cardiac output → poor tissue perfusion (poor blood delivery to tissues)

That is shock.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Tachycardia (fast heart rate):** baroreceptors (pressure sensors) sense low pressure → sympathetic nervous system turns on
* **Cool, clammy skin:** vasoconstriction (blood vessel tightening) shunts blood to brain/heart
* **Narrow pulse pressure:** systolic pressure drops more because stroke volume is low
* **Low urine output:** kidneys sense low perfusion and try to conserve water
* **Lactic acidosis:** tissues do anaerobic metabolism (energy production without enough oxygen), making lactate

---

### Step 1 hemodynamics

Hypovolemic shock has:

* decreased preload
* decreased cardiac output
* increased systemic vascular resistance (blood vessel constriction)
* decreased pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (left atrial filling pressure estimate)

Why increased systemic vascular resistance?

Because the body is trying to squeeze the blood vessels to maintain blood pressure despite low volume.

---

## One-line intuition

Hypovolemic shock is a volume problem: the pump may work, but there is not enough fluid in the tank.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

In early hypovolemic shock, blood pressure can still look normal because sympathetic compensation keeps vessels tight; tachycardia is often the earlier clue.
