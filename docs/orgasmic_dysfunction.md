## Orgasmic dysfunction

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Phases_of_sexual_arousal_and_female_orgasm.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Human_male_reproductive_system_en.svg)

Orgasmic dysfunction means difficulty, delay, or inability to reach orgasm (sexual climax) despite adequate sexual stimulation and arousal.

Core idea:

* arousal can be present
* desire can be present
* but the orgasm/reflex climax phase does not happen normally

---

### What orgasm normally is

Orgasm is the peak of the sexual response cycle:

* desire
* arousal
* plateau
* orgasm
* resolution

During orgasm, there are rhythmic pelvic muscle contractions and a burst of autonomic nervous system activity.

Autonomic nervous system = automatic nerves controlling things like heart rate, sweating, erection, ejaculation, and genital blood flow.

---

### Why dysfunction happens

Think of orgasm as needing:

* enough stimulation
* intact nerves
* enough blood flow
* appropriate hormones
* low enough psychological inhibition
* no medication blocking the pathway

If any part is impaired, orgasm can be delayed or absent.

Common causes:

* SSRIs (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors), like fluoxetine or sertraline
* diabetic neuropathy (nerve damage from diabetes mellitus)
* spinal cord injury
* low testosterone
* depression/anxiety
* relationship or performance stress
* pelvic surgery or nerve injury

---

### Why SSRIs cause it

Serotonin generally inhibits sexual function.

So SSRIs increase serotonin signaling, which can blunt:

* libido (sexual desire)
* arousal
* orgasm
* ejaculation

One-line intuition:

> Too much serotonin puts a brake on the orgasm pathway.

---

### Related male term

Delayed ejaculation means orgasm/ejaculation takes much longer than desired or cannot happen.

Ejaculation = semen leaving the urethra (tube that carries urine/semen out of the body).

Orgasm and ejaculation often happen together, but they are not exactly the same thing.

---

## One-line intuition

Orgasmic dysfunction is when the body can start the sexual response, but cannot reliably complete the climax/reflex phase.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 pearl: SSRIs commonly cause sexual dysfunction because increased serotonin inhibits orgasm and ejaculation; bupropion is less likely to cause this because it mainly increases dopamine/norepinephrine instead of serotonin.
