### Core idea

Kwashiorkor = severe protein deficiency, usually with enough calories but not enough protein.

Classic picture:

* edema (swelling from fluid)
* big belly
* fatty liver
* skin/hair changes
* growth failure

---

### Why the edema happens

Protein deficiency → low albumin (main blood protein that keeps fluid inside vessels)

Low albumin → low oncotic pressure (protein-pulling force that keeps water in blood vessels)

So fluid leaves the blood and goes into tissues:

* pitting edema
* swollen feet/legs
* ascites (fluid in the abdomen)
* protuberant belly

Intuition:

Albumin acts like a sponge inside blood vessels. If albumin is low, the blood loses its “water-holding” power, so water leaks out into tissues.

---

### Why fatty liver happens

The liver needs protein to make apolipoproteins (protein carriers that export fat from the liver).

Low protein → low apolipoproteins → fat cannot leave the liver well → fatty liver.

So the belly can look big from:

* edema/ascites (fluid in abdomen)
* hepatomegaly (enlarged liver)
* weak abdominal muscles

---

### Skin and hair findings

Can see:

* “flaky paint” dermatosis (peeling, patchy skin rash)
* sparse/light-colored hair
* flag sign = alternating pale and dark hair bands from periods of poor and better nutrition

---

## One-line intuition

Kwashiorkor is protein starvation: low albumin causes edema, and low apolipoproteins cause fatty liver.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency with edema.

Marasmus = total calorie deficiency with severe wasting but usually no edema.
