Erectile dysfunction, or **ED** (erectile dysfunction), means inability to get or maintain an erection firm enough for sexual activity.

### Core idea

An erection is basically a **blood-flow + blood-trapping event**.

For a normal erection:

* parasympathetic nerves release **NO** (nitric oxide)
* NO increases **cGMP** (cyclic guanosine monophosphate)
* cGMP relaxes smooth muscle in the corpora cavernosa
* arteries dilate, so more blood enters
* veins get compressed, so blood is trapped inside

So ED happens when this system fails.

---

### Why ED happens

Common causes:

* **vascular disease:** not enough arterial blood gets in
* **venous leak:** blood enters but leaks out too quickly
* **diabetes mellitus:** damages vessels and nerves
* **neurologic disease:** impaired nerve signaling
* **low testosterone:** decreased libido and erectile support
* **psychogenic causes:** anxiety, depression, stress
* **medications:** especially some antihypertensives and antidepressants

The biggest Step 1 intuition:

> ED is often a vascular problem until proven otherwise.

Penile arteries are small, so atherosclerosis can show up as ED before obvious coronary artery disease.

---

### Why PDE5 inhibitors help

**PDE5** (phosphodiesterase type 5) normally breaks down cGMP (cyclic guanosine monophosphate).

Drugs like sildenafil inhibit PDE5, so:

* cGMP stays around longer
* smooth muscle stays relaxed
* blood flow into the corpora cavernosa improves

Important: these drugs still need sexual stimulation because they amplify the NO-cGMP pathway; they do not directly create NO.

---

## One-line intuition

ED means the penis cannot properly relax smooth muscle, fill with blood, and trap that blood long enough to maintain rigidity.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Never give PDE5 inhibitors with **nitrates** because both increase the NO-cGMP pathway, which can cause severe hypotension (dangerously low blood pressure).
