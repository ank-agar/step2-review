### Core idea

Hematochezia means **bright red blood in the stool or from the rectum**.

Usually, this points to bleeding from the **lower GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)**:

* colon
* rectum
* anus

---

### Why it looks bright red

Blood stays bright red when it has not had much time to be digested.

So if bleeding happens near the exit, like the rectum or anus, the blood comes out quickly and stays red.

That is why hematochezia usually means:

* lower GI bleeding
* fresh blood
* faster transit through the gut

---

### Common causes

Common causes include:

* hemorrhoids
* anal fissure
* diverticulosis
* colorectal cancer
* inflammatory bowel disease, or IBD (inflammatory bowel disease)
* infectious colitis (infection/inflammation of the colon)

Hemorrhoids and anal fissures often cause **bright red blood on toilet paper or coating the stool**.

Bleeding mixed into stool can suggest a source higher in the colon.

---

### Hematochezia vs melena

* **Hematochezia:** bright red blood, usually lower GI bleed
* **Melena:** black, tarry stool, usually upper GI bleed

Melena is black because blood from an upper GI bleed gets digested as it moves through the gut.

---

## One-line intuition

Hematochezia = fresh red blood coming out with stool, so think bleeding close to the rectum/anus or a very fast bleed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

A massive upper GI bleed can also cause hematochezia if blood moves through the intestines very quickly, but the classic association is **lower GI bleeding**.
