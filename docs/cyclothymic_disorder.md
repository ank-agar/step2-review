# Cyclothymic disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/19/Bipolar_mood_shifts.png)

![Image](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/sites/default/files/images/Bipolar-Disorder-Infographic-SMI.jpg)

Cyclothymic disorder is a chronic mood disorder with repeated swings between:

* hypomanic symptoms
* depressive symptoms

but the symptoms are **not severe enough** to meet criteria for:

* a full manic episode
* a full hypomanic episode
* a major depressive episode

So think:

> chronic mild bipolar-type cycling.

---

### Key definitions

**Hypomanic symptoms** = elevated/irritable mood + increased energy, but less severe than mania.

Symptoms can include:

* decreased need for sleep
* increased talkativeness
* racing thoughts
* distractibility
* increased goal-directed activity
* risky behavior

**Depressive symptoms** = low mood-type symptoms, but not enough for MDD (major depressive disorder).

Symptoms can include:

* low mood
* low energy
* poor sleep
* low self-esteem
* poor concentration
* anhedonia (loss of interest or pleasure)

---

### Diagnosis idea

Cyclothymic disorder requires chronic symptoms for:

* at least **2 years** in adults
* at least **1 year** in children/adolescents

During that time:

* symptoms are present at least half the time
* the person is not symptom-free for more than 2 months
* symptoms cause distress or impairment

---

### Why it happens

The mood-regulation system is unstable.

Instead of staying near baseline mood, the person keeps drifting upward and downward:

* upward = hypomanic symptoms
* downward = depressive symptoms

But the swings do not cross the threshold into full mania or major depression.

---

### Cyclothymia vs bipolar disorders

**Bipolar I:**

* at least 1 manic episode

**Bipolar II:**

* hypomanic episode + major depressive episode

**Cyclothymic disorder:**

* hypomanic symptoms + depressive symptoms
* chronic for years
* never reaches full episode criteria

So:

> Cyclothymia is to bipolar disorder what persistent depressive disorder is to major depression: more chronic, less severe per episode.

---

### Treatment idea

Use mood-stabilizing treatment, often with:

* psychotherapy (talk therapy)
* mood stabilizers such as lithium, valproate, or lamotrigine

Be careful with antidepressants alone because they can push the patient upward into hypomania or mania.

---

## One-line intuition

Cyclothymic disorder is long-term mood cycling where the highs and lows are real and impairing, but not high enough for bipolar I/II or low enough for MDD.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cyclothymic disorder increases risk of later developing bipolar I or bipolar II disorder, so Step 1 wants you to recognize it as part of the bipolar spectrum.
