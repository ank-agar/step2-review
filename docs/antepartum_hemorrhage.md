## Antepartum hemorrhage

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17122.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK482335/bin/Placental__Abruption.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ca/Placenta_previa.svg/1280px-Placenta_previa.svg.png)

Antepartum hemorrhage means vaginal bleeding before delivery, usually after 20 weeks of gestation.

Break it down:

* antepartum = before childbirth
* hemorrhage = bleeding

Big Step 1 causes:

* placenta previa
* placental abruption
* vasa previa

---

### Placenta previa

Placenta previa means the placenta implants low in the uterus and covers/approaches the internal cervical os (opening of the cervix into the uterus).

Classic presentation:

* painless bright red vaginal bleeding
* soft, nontender uterus
* abnormal fetal lie sometimes

Why painless?

The placenta is just sitting over the cervical opening. As the lower uterine segment stretches, placental vessels tear, but the uterus is not contracting hard or inflamed.

Think:

> previa = placental position problem

---

### Placental abruption

Placental abruption means premature separation of a normally implanted placenta from the uterine wall.

Classic presentation:

* painful vaginal bleeding
* tender, rigid uterus
* fetal distress
* possible DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation)

Why painful?

Blood collects behind the placenta, irritating the uterus and causing strong contractions/tenderness.

Think:

> abruption = placenta rips away from uterus

---

### Vasa previa

Vasa previa means fetal blood vessels run across/near the cervical os without protection from placenta or umbilical cord.

Classic presentation:

* painless bleeding after rupture of membranes
* fetal bradycardia (slow fetal heart rate)

Why dangerous?

The bleeding is fetal blood, so even a small amount can be catastrophic for the fetus.

---

## One-line intuition

Antepartum hemorrhage is late-pregnancy bleeding; the key is deciding whether it is painless placenta previa, painful placental abruption, or fetal-vessel bleeding in vasa previa.

---

## Pearl

Do **not** do a digital vaginal exam in suspected placenta previa until ultrasound rules it out, because touching the placenta can trigger severe bleeding.
