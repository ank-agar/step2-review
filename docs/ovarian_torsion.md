### Core idea

Ovarian torsion is when the ovary twists around its vascular pedicle (the stalk containing the blood vessels).

This cuts off blood flow, so it is a surgical emergency.

Think:

> ovary twists = blood vessels kink = ischemia

Ischemia means decreased blood supply causing tissue injury.

---

### Why it happens

Usually there is something making the ovary heavier or more mobile, like:

* ovarian cyst
* ovarian mass
* pregnancy-related corpus luteum cyst
* enlarged ovary from fertility treatment

The extra weight lets the ovary swing and twist on its supporting ligaments.

---

### Symptoms

Classic presentation:

* sudden unilateral pelvic pain
* nausea/vomiting
* adnexal tenderness

Unilateral means one-sided.

Adnexa means the structures next to the uterus, mainly the ovaries and fallopian tubes.

Pain can be intermittent because the ovary may twist, untwist, then twist again.

---

### Why the ovary gets big

First, venous outflow is blocked.

Venous outflow means blood leaving through veins.

So blood gets trapped in the ovary:

* congestion
* edema
* enlarged ovary
* peripheral follicles on ultrasound

Then later, arterial inflow can also get blocked.

Arterial inflow means oxygenated blood entering through arteries.

Once arterial inflow is blocked, the ovary can become ischemic/necrotic.

Necrosis means tissue death.

---

### Diagnosis

Ultrasound with Doppler is the first-line imaging test.

Doppler means ultrasound that looks at blood flow.

Findings can include:

* enlarged ovary
* peripheral follicles
* decreased or absent blood flow
* twisted vascular pedicle
* whirlpool sign

Whirlpool sign means the twisted ovarian vessels look like a spiral.

Important: normal Doppler flow does **not** fully rule out torsion because the ovary has dual blood supply and torsion can be intermittent.

---

## One-line intuition

Ovarian torsion is basically the ovary wringing its own blood supply shut.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Ovarian torsion is the female analog of testicular torsion: sudden severe pain + threatened gonad = urgent surgery.
