### Core idea

Amblyopia = decreased vision from abnormal visual development in childhood.

It is often called lazy eye, but the problem is really:

* the brain learns to ignore input from one eye
* so the visual cortex (vision-processing part of the brain) for that eye does not develop normally

The eye itself can look structurally normal.

---

### Why it happens

During childhood, the brain needs clear, equal images from both eyes.

If one eye sends a bad or conflicting image, the brain suppresses it.

Common causes:

* strabismus (eye misalignment)
* anisometropia (unequal refractive error between the two eyes)
* congenital cataract (lens opacity present at birth)
* ptosis (droopy eyelid) blocking vision

---

### Why patching helps

Patching the good eye forces the brain to use the weaker eye.

So the goal is:

* strengthen the visual pathway from the amblyopic eye
* prevent permanent cortical underdevelopment

This works best when started early because the visual cortex is still plastic.

---

## One-line intuition

Amblyopia is when the brain "turns down" one eye during childhood, so that eye never learns to see sharply.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 pearl: congenital cataracts must be treated early because visual deprivation during the critical period can cause permanent amblyopia, even if the cataract is removed later.
