## Measles

Measles, also called rubeola, is a very contagious viral infection caused by measles virus.

It is a:

* enveloped
* negative-sense ssRNA virus (single-stranded ribonucleic acid virus)
* in the Paramyxovirus family

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* high fever
* cough
* coryza (runny nose)
* conjunctivitis (inflamed/red eyes)
* Koplik spots
* maculopapular rash

Koplik spots are tiny white/blue-white spots on the buccal mucosa (inner cheek lining).

They are high-yield because they show up before the skin rash.

---

### Rash pattern

The rash usually starts on the face/hairline, then spreads downward:

* face
* neck
* trunk
* arms/legs

That head-to-toe spread is called cephalocaudal spread.

cephalo- = head  
caudal = toward the tail/lower body

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Measles enters through the respiratory tract, then spreads through lymphoid tissue and blood.

So you get:

* **cough/coryza/conjunctivitis:** virus infects respiratory and mucosal surfaces
* **fever:** systemic immune response
* **Koplik spots:** viral/immune injury in oral mucosa
* **rash:** T-cell immune response against virus-infected endothelial/skin cells

So the rash is not just “virus sitting on the skin” - it is largely your immune system reacting to infected cells.

---

### Complications

Important complications:

* pneumonia
* otitis media (middle ear infection)
* diarrhea
* encephalitis (brain inflammation)
* SSPE (subacute sclerosing panencephalitis)

SSPE is a rare, delayed, progressive brain disease that can happen years after measles infection.

---

### Prevention

Prevention is with the MMR vaccine.

MMR = measles, mumps, rubella vaccine.

It is a live attenuated vaccine, meaning it uses a weakened living virus to train the immune system.

---

## One-line intuition

Measles is an airborne Paramyxovirus that causes fever + 3 C's + Koplik spots, then a face-to-body maculopapular rash.

3 C's = cough, coryza, conjunctivitis.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Measles histology can show Warthin-Finkeldey giant cells: multinucleated giant cells in lymphoid tissue.

Also HY: vitamin A deficiency increases risk of severe measles complications.
