## Primary hypogonadism

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e5/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis_in_males.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1f/Male_reproductive_hormone_chart.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a5/Human_karyotype_with_extra_X_chromosome.png)

Primary hypogonadism means the **gonads are failing**.

Gonads = testes or ovaries.

Hypogonadism = low gonadal sex hormone function.

So:

* testes fail -> low testosterone
* ovaries fail -> low estrogen

The key Step 1 lab pattern is:

* low sex hormones
* high LH (luteinizing hormone)
* high FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)

This is also called **hypergonadotropic hypogonadism**.

Hypergonadotropic = high gonadotropins, meaning high LH and FSH.

---

### Why LH and FSH are high

Think of the pituitary as yelling at the gonads.

Normally:

* hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* pituitary releases LH and FSH
* gonads make sex hormones
* sex hormones give negative feedback to the brain

In primary hypogonadism, the gonads are damaged and do not respond well.

So testosterone/estrogen stays low.

The brain senses low sex hormone feedback and says:

> "The gonads are not working, send more signal."

So LH and FSH go up.

---

### Common causes

In males:

* Klinefelter syndrome
* mumps orchitis (testicular inflammation after mumps infection)
* testicular radiation/chemotherapy
* trauma or torsion damaging the testes

In females:

* Turner syndrome
* premature ovarian failure
* ovarian radiation/chemotherapy
* autoimmune ovarian damage

---

### Symptoms

Before puberty:

* delayed puberty
* absent or poor secondary sexual characteristics
* infertility later

After puberty:

* decreased libido
* infertility
* low muscle mass or fatigue in males
* amenorrhea (absence of menstrual periods) in females
* hot flashes or vaginal dryness in females

Why infertility?

The gonads are not making enough sex hormones and/or gametes.

Gametes = sperm or eggs.

---

### Primary vs secondary

Primary hypogonadism:

* problem = gonads
* LH/FSH = high
* sex hormones = low

Secondary hypogonadism:

* problem = hypothalamus or pituitary
* LH/FSH = low or inappropriately normal
* sex hormones = low

Intuition:

* primary = the worker is broken, so the boss yells louder
* secondary = the boss is not yelling enough

---

## One-line intuition

Primary hypogonadism is **gonadal failure**, so testosterone/estrogen is low and the pituitary compensates by raising LH and FSH.

---

## Pearl

Klinefelter syndrome is the classic male Step 1 cause of primary hypogonadism: **47,XXY male + small firm testes + gynecomastia + infertility + high LH/FSH**.
