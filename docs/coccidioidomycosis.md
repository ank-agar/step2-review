## Coccidioidomycosis

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/phil_images/20030610/12/PHIL_480_lores.jpg)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/1600.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/valley-fever/media/images/usavalleyfevermap.jpg)

Coccidioidomycosis = Valley fever.

It is a fungal lung infection caused by **Coccidioides** species, usually:

* Coccidioides immitis
* Coccidioides posadasii

You get it by inhaling fungal spores from soil, especially in the southwestern United States.

---

### Core idea

Think:

> desert dust spores get inhaled into the lungs.

The fungus lives in soil as mold-like hyphae (branching fungal filaments).

When soil is disturbed:

* spores become airborne
* you inhale them
* they enter the lungs
* they become spherules

A **spherule** is a round fungal structure packed with endospores.

An **endospore** is a small internal reproductive form inside the spherule.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

In the lungs, spherules rupture and release endospores.

That causes inflammation, so it can look like pneumonia:

* fever
* cough
* chest pain
* fatigue
* shortness of breath

It can also cause:

* erythema nodosum (tender red bumps, often on the shins)
* joint pain

That is why an old nickname is:

> desert rheumatism

because it can cause desert exposure + joint pain.

---

### What Step 1 wants you to recognize

Classic clues:

* pneumonia after travel/living in Arizona, California, New Mexico, Texas, or desert Southwest
* fungal infection from soil
* spherules filled with endospores
* can disseminate (spread beyond the lungs)

Dissemination can involve:

* skin
* bones
* joints
* meninges

Meninges = membranes covering the brain and spinal cord.

---

### Histology clue

The buzzword is:

> spherules with endospores

That is very high yield.

Do not confuse it with Histoplasma, which is small intracellular yeast inside macrophages.

Macrophages = immune cells that eat microbes.

---

## One-line intuition

Coccidioidomycosis is a desert soil fungus that is inhaled into the lungs and forms big spherules full of endospores.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Coccidioides is the only major dimorphic fungus classically described as **spherules with endospores** in tissue.

Dimorphic fungus = fungus that changes form depending on temperature/environment.
