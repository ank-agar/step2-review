### Core idea

Pancreatic insufficiency means the pancreas is not making enough of what the body needs.

Usually on exams, this means **exocrine pancreatic insufficiency**, where the pancreas does not release enough digestive enzymes into the duodenum (first part of the small intestine).

Key enzymes:

* lipase = digests fat
* amylase = digests carbohydrates
* proteases = digest proteins

The biggest clinical problem is low **lipase**, because fat digestion becomes poor.

---

### What happens

If pancreatic enzymes are low:

* food is not broken down well
* nutrients are not absorbed well
* fat stays in the stool

That causes **steatorrhea**, meaning fatty, bulky, foul-smelling stool that may float.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Steatorrhea:** low pancreatic lipase means fat cannot be digested into absorbable pieces
* **Weight loss:** malabsorption, meaning poor nutrient absorption
* **Bloating/diarrhea:** undigested nutrients stay in the gut and pull water in
* **Fat-soluble vitamin deficiency:** vitamins A, D, E, and K need fat absorption

So if fat is not absorbed, these vitamins are not absorbed either.

---

### Causes to know

Common causes:

* chronic pancreatitis
* cystic fibrosis
* pancreatic duct obstruction
* pancreatic cancer
* pancreatic surgery

Reasoning:

Anything that destroys the exocrine pancreas or blocks pancreatic enzymes from reaching the intestine can cause pancreatic insufficiency.

---

### Diagnosis and treatment

Important test:

* low fecal elastase

Elastase is a pancreatic enzyme measured in stool. Low stool elastase suggests the pancreas is not secreting enough enzymes.

Treatment:

* pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy

That means giving digestive enzymes by mouth with meals.

---

## One-line intuition

Pancreatic insufficiency = not enough pancreatic digestive enzymes, so fat is not digested and comes out as greasy stool.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In chronic pancreatitis, steatorrhea usually appears only after severe exocrine damage because the pancreas has a large functional reserve.
