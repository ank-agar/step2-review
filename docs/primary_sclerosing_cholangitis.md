### Core idea

Primary sclerosing cholangitis, or PSC (primary sclerosing cholangitis), is chronic inflammation and fibrosis (scarring) of the bile ducts.

It affects:

* intrahepatic bile ducts = bile ducts inside the liver
* extrahepatic bile ducts = bile ducts outside the liver

Break the name apart:

* primary = not caused by a known outside obstruction
* sclerosing = scarring/hardening
* cholangitis = inflammation of bile ducts

So PSC basically means:

> bile ducts get inflamed, scar down, and become narrowed.

---

### Why the findings happen

Bile normally flows:

* liver → bile ducts → gallbladder/small intestine

In PSC, scarred bile ducts become narrowed, so bile backs up.

That causes cholestasis (impaired bile flow), which gives:

* increased alkaline phosphatase, or ALP (alkaline phosphatase)
* jaundice from increased conjugated bilirubin (water-soluble processed bilirubin)
* pruritus (itching), because bile salts build up
* dark urine, because conjugated bilirubin is water-soluble and enters urine
* pale stools, because less bile pigment reaches the gut

The classic imaging finding is:

* beading of bile ducts

Why beading?

* strictures (narrow scarred segments) alternate with dilated segments
* narrow-wide-narrow-wide looks like beads on a string

---

### Classic association

PSC is strongly associated with ulcerative colitis, or UC (ulcerative colitis).

UC is a type of IBD (inflammatory bowel disease) affecting the colon.

So if you see:

* young/middle-aged man
* history of UC
* cholestatic labs, especially high ALP
* beaded bile ducts

Think:

> primary sclerosing cholangitis.

---

### Histology

Classic biopsy finding:

* onion-skin fibrosis

This means concentric rings of fibrosis around bile ducts.

Intuition:

The immune/inflammatory injury keeps wrapping scar tissue around the duct, like layers around an onion, eventually squeezing the duct shut.

---

### Complications

PSC can progress to:

* biliary cirrhosis (liver scarring caused by chronic bile duct disease)
* portal hypertension (high pressure in the portal venous system)
* cholangiocarcinoma (bile duct cancer)

Important: PSC increases cholangiocarcinoma risk.

---

## One-line intuition

PSC is a chronic bile duct scarring disease, classically in UC, where alternating strictures and dilation create beaded ducts and cholestasis.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PSC is associated with p-ANCA (perinuclear anti-neutrophil cytoplasmic antibody) and ulcerative colitis, while primary biliary cholangitis, or PBC (primary biliary cholangitis), is associated with anti-mitochondrial antibodies and destruction of small intrahepatic bile ducts.
