### Core idea

Hypophosphatemia = low phosphate in the blood.

Phosphate is needed for:

* ATP (adenosine triphosphate), aka cellular energy
* 2,3-BPG (2,3-bisphosphoglycerate), which helps red blood cells release oxygen
* bone mineralization
* muscle function
* cell membranes and signaling

So low phosphate basically means:

> low energy + weak muscles + poor bone mineralization.

---

### Why symptoms happen

* **Muscle weakness:** low phosphate means low ATP, so muscles cannot contract well
* **Rhabdomyolysis:** severe ATP depletion injures skeletal muscle cells
* **Hemolysis:** red blood cells become fragile when ATP is low
* **Respiratory failure:** diaphragm weakness makes breathing harder
* **Bone pain/rickets/osteomalacia:** phosphate is needed to mineralize osteoid (new bone matrix)
* **Confusion/seizures:** neurons are energy-hungry, so severe deficiency can affect the brain

Breakdown:

* rickets = poor mineralization of growing bone in children
* osteomalacia = poor mineralization of adult bone
* rhabdomyolysis = skeletal muscle breakdown
* hemolysis = red blood cell breakdown

---

### Big causes

Think 3 buckets:

* **Not enough intake/absorption:** malnutrition, alcoholism, antacids that bind phosphate
* **Shift into cells:** refeeding syndrome, insulin, respiratory alkalosis
* **Kidney wasting:** hyperparathyroidism, Fanconi syndrome, FGF23-mediated disease

FGF23 (fibroblast growth factor 23) lowers phosphate by making the kidney dump phosphate into urine.

PTH (parathyroid hormone) also lowers phosphate by decreasing phosphate reabsorption in the proximal tubule (early kidney tubule).

---

### Refeeding syndrome intuition

In starvation, the body is phosphate-depleted.

Then you give carbs:

* insulin rises
* glucose enters cells
* cells start making ATP again
* phosphate gets pulled into cells

So serum phosphate can crash.

This is why refeeding syndrome causes:

* hypophosphatemia
* hypokalemia
* hypomagnesemia

Hypokalemia = low potassium.  
Hypomagnesemia = low magnesium.

---

## One-line intuition

Hypophosphatemia is low blood phosphate, so cells cannot make enough ATP and bones cannot mineralize properly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Severe hypophosphatemia after starting nutrition in a malnourished patient = think **refeeding syndrome**, especially if there is weakness, arrhythmia, or respiratory failure.
