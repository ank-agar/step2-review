### Core idea

Thiamine deficiency = low vitamin B1.

Thiamine is needed to make **TPP (thiamine pyrophosphate)**, a cofactor (helper molecule for enzymes) used in energy metabolism.

Big picture:

* low thiamine
* low ATP (cell energy)
* high-energy tissues get injured first:
  * brain
  * nerves
  * heart

---

### Why the findings happen

Thiamine helps enzymes that process glucose, especially:

* pyruvate dehydrogenase
* alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase
* branched-chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase
* transketolase

If thiamine is low, cells cannot use glucose efficiently.

So pyruvate gets pushed toward lactate:

* increased lactic acid
* decreased ATP (adenosine triphosphate, the cell's energy currency)

That is why thiamine deficiency especially hurts the brain: the brain is very glucose-dependent.

---

### Major presentations

**Wernicke encephalopathy** = acute brain dysfunction from thiamine deficiency.

Classic triad:

* confusion
* ataxia (unsteady walking)
* ophthalmoplegia (eye movement weakness)

**Korsakoff syndrome** = chronic memory problem after untreated Wernicke.

Classic finding:

* confabulation (making up stories without realizing it) due to memory gaps

**Dry beriberi** = peripheral neuropathy (nerve damage outside the brain/spinal cord).

Findings:

* weakness
* numbness/tingling
* decreased reflexes

**Wet beriberi** = high-output heart failure.

Findings:

* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* edema (swelling)
* shortness of breath

---

### Alcohol association

Chronic alcohol use causes thiamine deficiency because alcohol:

* decreases thiamine absorption
* decreases liver storage
* worsens nutrition

This is why an alcoholic patient with confusion + gait problems + eye findings is Wernicke until proven otherwise.

---

## One-line intuition

Thiamine deficiency means cells cannot efficiently turn glucose into ATP, so the brain, nerves, and heart start failing.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Give thiamine before glucose in suspected Wernicke encephalopathy, because glucose metabolism uses thiamine and can worsen the deficiency.
