## Laryngomalacia

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/LaryngomalaciaDrawing.jpg)

### Core idea

Laryngomalacia is when the supraglottic larynx (upper larynx above the vocal cords) is floppy and collapses inward during inspiration (breathing in).

Basically:

* infant laryngeal cartilage is too soft
* negative pressure while breathing in pulls tissue inward
* the upper airway partially obstructs

---

### Why symptoms happen

Because the collapse happens above the vocal cords, it causes inspiratory stridor (high-pitched noisy breathing when breathing in).

Symptoms often:

* start in early infancy
* get worse with feeding, crying, or lying supine (on the back)
* improve when prone (on the stomach) or as cartilage stiffens with age

Severe cases can cause:

* feeding difficulty
* poor weight gain
* cyanosis (bluish skin from low oxygen)

---

## One-line intuition

Laryngomalacia = floppy upper airway tissue gets sucked inward during inspiration.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Laryngomalacia is the most common cause of chronic inspiratory stridor in infants and usually resolves spontaneously by 12-24 months.
