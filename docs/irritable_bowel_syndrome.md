# Irritable bowel syndrome

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7c/Diagram_showing_the_parts_of_the_large_bowel_CRUK_329.svg/1280px-Diagram_showing_the_parts_of_the_large_bowel_CRUK_329.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/01/Irritable_bowel_syndrome_factors.jpg)

Irritable bowel syndrome, or IBS (irritable bowel syndrome), is a functional GI (gastrointestinal) disorder where the bowel acts abnormal, but there is no visible structural damage.

Functional = the organ looks okay, but the function is off.

Classic symptoms:

* abdominal pain
* bloating
* diarrhea
* constipation
* diarrhea + constipation alternating
* pain related to bowel movements

---

### Core idea

IBS is mainly a problem of the gut-brain axis.

Gut-brain axis = two-way communication between:

* CNS (central nervous system)
* ENS (enteric nervous system, the nervous system inside the gut)

So the intestine becomes extra sensitive and/or moves stool too fast or too slow.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Pain/cramping:** visceral hypersensitivity, meaning the gut nerves overreact to normal stretching
* **Diarrhea:** increased motility, meaning stool moves too quickly through the colon
* **Constipation:** decreased motility, meaning stool moves too slowly and dries out
* **Bloating:** abnormal gas handling + sensitive gut nerves
* **Stress worsening symptoms:** stress affects CNS (central nervous system) signals to the gut

---

### Key distinction

IBS (irritable bowel syndrome) does **not** cause:

* bloody stool
* weight loss
* fever
* anemia
* colon cancer risk
* visible bowel inflammation

Those are red flags for other diseases like IBD (inflammatory bowel disease), cancer, infection, or celiac disease.

---

### IBS vs IBD

IBS (irritable bowel syndrome):

* functional problem
* no bowel wall damage
* no blood usually
* normal colonoscopy

IBD (inflammatory bowel disease):

* inflammatory problem
* bowel wall damage
* can have blood, fever, weight loss
* abnormal colonoscopy/biopsy

---

## One-line intuition

IBS is a hypersensitive, dysregulated colon where nerves and motility are abnormal, but the bowel structure is not destroyed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

IBS classically improves with defecation and is associated with change in stool frequency or stool form.

That is very Step 1/clinical pattern-recognition: abdominal pain + altered bowel habits + no alarm signs.
