## Miosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/80/Iris-muscles.svg/3840px-Iris-muscles.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Mydriasis_and_miosis.png)

Miosis = constriction of the pupil.

Basically:

* smaller pupil = miosis
* bigger pupil = mydriasis

The pupil is the black opening in the middle of the iris (colored part of the eye). The iris controls how big that opening is.

---

### Core idea

Miosis happens when the iris sphincter muscle contracts.

Think:

* sphincter = circular muscle that closes an opening
* iris sphincter contracts → pupil gets smaller
* less light enters the eye

So in bright light, your pupils constrict because your eye is trying to limit incoming light.

---

### Parasympathetic connection

Miosis is a parasympathetic nervous system effect.

Pathway:

* CN III (cranial nerve 3, oculomotor nerve)
* Edinger-Westphal nucleus (parasympathetic nucleus for eye reflexes)
* ciliary ganglion (parasympathetic relay near the eye)
* short ciliary nerves
* sphincter pupillae muscle

So:

> Parasympathetics constrict the pupil.

---

### Common causes

Miosis can be normal or pathologic.

Normal:

* bright light
* focusing on a near object

Drugs/toxins:

* opioids
* organophosphates
* cholinergic drugs

Neuro:

* Horner syndrome

---

### Why opioids cause miosis

Opioids increase parasympathetic outflow to the pupil.

More parasympathetic tone → sphincter pupillae contracts → pinpoint pupils.

That is why opioid intoxication classically causes:

* CNS (central nervous system) depression
* respiratory depression
* miosis

---

## One-line intuition

Miosis = the eye's "close the aperture" response: parasympathetic input squeezes the iris sphincter, making the pupil small.

---

## HY pearl

Horner syndrome causes the classic triad:

* ptosis (droopy eyelid)
* miosis (small pupil)
* anhidrosis (decreased sweating)

Why miosis? Loss of sympathetic input means the dilator pupillae muscle cannot dilate the pupil, so parasympathetic constriction is unopposed.
