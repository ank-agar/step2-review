### Core idea

Panhypopituitarism = low or absent secretion of most/all pituitary hormones.

Break it down:

* pan- = all
* hypo- = low
* pituitarism = pituitary gland function

So it means:

> the pituitary gland is globally underfunctioning.

The pituitary gland is the “master gland” because it sends hormone signals to other endocrine glands.

Endocrine glands = glands that release hormones into blood.

---

### What hormones are low?

Usually the big anterior pituitary hormones are low:

* ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone)
* TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
* LH (luteinizing hormone)
* FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* GH (growth hormone)
* prolactin

Sometimes posterior pituitary hormones are affected too:

* ADH (antidiuretic hormone)
* oxytocin

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Low ACTH means low cortisol from adrenal cortex:

* fatigue
* weakness
* hypotension
* hypoglycemia

Cortisol = stress hormone.

Low TSH means low thyroid hormone:

* cold intolerance
* constipation
* weight gain
* slow heart rate

Low LH/FSH means low sex hormones:

* amenorrhea (absence of menstrual periods)
* infertility
* low libido
* delayed puberty

Low GH causes:

* poor growth in kids
* decreased muscle/bone mass in adults

Low prolactin can cause:

* failure to lactate after delivery

Low ADH causes diabetes insipidus:

* polyuria (excess urination)
* polydipsia (excess thirst)

Diabetes insipidus = inability to concentrate urine because ADH signaling is low.

---

### Common causes

Think anything that damages/compresses the pituitary:

* pituitary adenoma (benign pituitary tumor)
* pituitary surgery
* radiation
* trauma
* infiltrative disease
* Sheehan syndrome

Sheehan syndrome = pituitary infarction after severe postpartum hemorrhage.

Postpartum = after delivery.

---

## One-line intuition

The pituitary is the remote control for multiple hormone axes, so panhypopituitarism is like the remote control breaking: thyroid, adrenal, gonadal, growth, and sometimes water-balance signals all drop.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: In suspected panhypopituitarism, replace glucocorticoids before thyroid hormone, because giving thyroid hormone first can increase cortisol demand and trigger adrenal crisis.
