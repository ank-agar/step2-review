# Induced abortion

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/15/Vacuum-aspiration.svg)

![Image](https://www.ipas.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/6937-MifeMiso_Cena_09.png)

![Image](https://www.ipas.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/ASC-BlisterCombi-and-Pills.png)

Induced abortion = intentional termination of a pregnancy before viability.

Key word:

* **induced** = intentionally caused
* **abortion** = pregnancy ending before viability

So this is different from **spontaneous abortion** (miscarriage), where the pregnancy ends naturally without intentional intervention.

---

### Main types

**Medication abortion** = uses drugs to end an early pregnancy.

Common mechanism:

* **mifepristone** blocks progesterone
* **progesterone** = hormone that maintains the endometrium (uterine lining) during pregnancy
* without progesterone support, the pregnancy detaches from the uterine lining
* **misoprostol** is a prostaglandin analog (drug that acts like prostaglandins)
* prostaglandins cause uterine contractions and cervical softening

So the intuition is:

> mifepristone removes hormonal support, and misoprostol helps the uterus expel the pregnancy tissue.

**Procedural abortion** = physically removes pregnancy tissue from the uterus.

Examples:

* vacuum aspiration = suction removes uterine contents
* D&C (dilation and curettage) = cervix is dilated, then uterine lining/tissue is removed
* D&E (dilation and evacuation) = procedure used later in pregnancy

---

### Why bleeding and cramping happen

Bleeding happens because the pregnancy separates from the endometrium (uterine lining).

Cramping happens because the myometrium (uterine smooth muscle) contracts to empty the uterus.

This is the same basic reason miscarriages can cause bleeding/cramping too: the uterus is trying to expel intrauterine tissue.

---

### Complications to know

Possible complications include:

* hemorrhage (heavy bleeding)
* infection
* retained products of conception (pregnancy tissue left in uterus)
* uterine perforation, especially with procedures

Retained products can cause continued bleeding because the uterus cannot fully clamp down and return to baseline.

---

## One-line intuition

Induced abortion is intentionally ending a pregnancy, either by removing progesterone support + causing uterine contractions, or by mechanically evacuating the uterus.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Septic abortion = infected abortion tissue, classically causing fever, uterine tenderness, pelvic pain, and foul-smelling discharge after abortion or miscarriage.
