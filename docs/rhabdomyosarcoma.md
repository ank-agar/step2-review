# Rhabdomyosarcoma

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Skeletal_muscle.png)

![Image](https://nci-media.cancer.gov/pdq/media/images/415526.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.orthobullets.com/topic/8059/images/51d_moved.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.orthobullets.com/topic/8059/images/51a_moved.jpg)

Rhabdomyosarcoma is a malignant tumor (cancerous tumor) that tries to look like developing skeletal muscle.

Break the word apart:

* rhabdo- = striated/skeletal muscle
* myo- = muscle
* sarcoma = malignant tumor of mesenchymal tissue (connective tissue, muscle, bone, fat, vessels)

So:

> rhabdomyosarcoma = cancer of immature skeletal muscle-type cells.

Important: it does **not** have to arise inside an obvious skeletal muscle. It can show up in places like:

* head and neck
* orbit (eye socket)
* genitourinary tract (urinary/reproductive tract)
* extremities (arms/legs)

because the tumor comes from primitive mesenchymal cells (early connective-tissue-type cells) that can differentiate toward skeletal muscle.

---

### Core idea

Think:

* child
* soft tissue mass
* skeletal muscle differentiation
* small round blue cell tumor

"Small round blue cell tumor" means the biopsy has many primitive-looking cells with dark blue nuclei on H&E stain (hematoxylin and eosin stain).

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Symptoms depend on where the mass grows:

* orbit: proptosis (eye bulging forward)
* bladder/prostate/vagina: urinary obstruction or bleeding
* nasal/pharyngeal area: congestion, bleeding, or obstruction
* extremity: enlarging soft tissue mass

The tumor acts like a space-occupying mass, so it compresses nearby structures.

---

### Main subtypes

* Embryonal: most common overall, usually younger kids, often head/neck or genitourinary tract
* Alveolar: more aggressive, often extremities, associated with PAX3/7-FOXO1 fusion (gene fusion involving PAX3 or PAX7 with FOXO1)
* Botryoid: grape-like subtype of embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma, classically in hollow mucosal organs like vagina or bladder

Botryoid means "grape-like."

---

### Diagnosis clues

Tumor cells often stain positive for muscle markers:

* desmin (muscle intermediate filament marker)
* myogenin (skeletal muscle differentiation marker)
* MyoD1 (skeletal muscle differentiation marker)

These markers tell you:

> this primitive tumor is trying to become skeletal muscle.

---

## One-line intuition

Rhabdomyosarcoma is a pediatric soft tissue cancer where primitive cells are stuck trying to become skeletal muscle.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma is classically associated with t(2;13) causing PAX3-FOXO1 fusion, and it is more aggressive than embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma.
