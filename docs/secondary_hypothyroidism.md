### Core idea

Secondary hypothyroidism = low thyroid hormone because the **pituitary gland** is not making enough **TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)**.

Normal pathway:

* Hypothalamus releases **TRH (thyrotropin-releasing hormone)**
* Pituitary releases **TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)**
* Thyroid releases **T3/T4 (thyroid hormones)**

In secondary hypothyroidism:

* thyroid gland itself may be fine
* pituitary fails to stimulate it
* so T3/T4 become low

---

### Lab pattern

Classic labs:

* low free T4 (thyroxine)
* low or inappropriately normal TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)

Important intuition:

If thyroid hormone is low, the pituitary should respond by making lots of TSH.

So if T4 is low but TSH is not high, the pituitary is the problem.

---

### Primary vs secondary

Primary hypothyroidism = thyroid problem.

* low T4
* high TSH

Why high TSH?

Because the pituitary is yelling at the thyroid, but the thyroid cannot respond.

Secondary hypothyroidism = pituitary problem.

* low T4
* low/inappropriately normal TSH

Why low TSH?

Because the pituitary is not yelling loudly enough, so the thyroid never gets stimulated.

---

### Causes

Think pituitary damage:

* pituitary tumor
* pituitary surgery/radiation
* Sheehan syndrome (postpartum pituitary infarction)
* hypopituitarism (low pituitary hormone output)

---

## One-line intuition

The thyroid is not lazy because it is broken; it is lazy because the pituitary is not telling it to work.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Secondary hypothyroidism is a form of **central hypothyroidism**.

Central = problem is in the brain control system:

* secondary = pituitary problem
* tertiary = hypothalamus problem
