## Trichotillomania

### Core idea

Trichotillomania is **recurrent pulling out of one’s own hair**, causing visible hair loss.

Break the word apart:

* tricho- = hair
* tillo- = pulling/plucking
* mania = repetitive urge/behavior

So the literal idea is:

> compulsive hair-pulling.

---

### What it looks like

Hair loss is usually:

* patchy
* irregularly shaped
* with hairs of different lengths
* often on scalp, eyebrows, or eyelashes

The key clue is **broken hairs at variable lengths**, because the patient is pulling hairs out at different times.

---

### Why the findings happen

Pulling the hair mechanically traumatizes the hair shaft and follicle (the skin structure that produces hair).

So you get:

* **short broken hairs:** repeated pulling snaps hairs
* **patchy alopecia (hair loss):** certain areas are repeatedly targeted
* **normal-looking scalp skin:** this is usually nonscarring alopecia (hair loss without permanent follicle destruction), especially early
* **possible regrowth:** if the follicle is not permanently damaged, hair can grow back

---

### Psych association

Trichotillomania is a **body-focused repetitive behavior** and is related to OCD (obsessive-compulsive disorder), but it is not exactly the same thing.

The behavior often reduces tension briefly, which reinforces the cycle:

* urge/tension
* pulling
* temporary relief
* shame/distress
* urge returns

---

## One-line intuition

Trichotillomania is self-induced traumatic hair loss from repeated hair pulling, classically causing patchy hair loss with hairs broken at different lengths.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: trichotillomania can be confused with alopecia areata (autoimmune patchy hair loss), but trichotillomania has **irregular patches with broken hairs of different lengths**, while alopecia areata is classically smoother and may show **exclamation point hairs**.
