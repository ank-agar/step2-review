# Toxic shock syndrome

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK587120/bin/superantigens-Image001.jpg)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil///PHIL_Images/20040116/4cb40f323d524c539b29a1f91bdb0a20/5116_lores.jpg)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil///PHIL_Images/20031110/5e70c59bfe2e4c6bb34a024672237092/5113_lores.jpg)

Toxic shock syndrome is a life-threatening toxin-mediated illness causing:

* fever
* hypotension (low blood pressure)
* diffuse rash
* multiorgan dysfunction

It is usually caused by toxin-producing:

* Staphylococcus aureus
* Streptococcus pyogenes, also called group A strep

---

### Core mechanism

The key idea is:

> superantigen causes massive T-cell activation.

A superantigen is a toxin that nonspecifically links:

* MHC II (major histocompatibility complex class II) on an antigen-presenting cell
* TCR (T-cell receptor) on a T cell

Normally, only a tiny number of T cells respond to a specific antigen.

But superantigens bypass specificity and activate tons of T cells at once.

That causes a cytokine storm (massive inflammatory cytokine release).

Important cytokines:

* IL-1 (interleukin-1) -> fever
* IL-2 (interleukin-2) -> T-cell proliferation
* TNF-alpha (tumor necrosis factor-alpha) -> hypotension/shock

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Fever:** IL-1 resets the hypothalamic temperature set point
* **Hypotension:** TNF-alpha causes vasodilation and capillary leak
* **Rash:** widespread inflammation affects skin vessels
* **Vomiting/diarrhea:** cytokines irritate the GI (gastrointestinal) system
* **Kidney/liver dysfunction:** poor perfusion + systemic inflammation injure organs
* **Desquamation:** peeling skin, classically palms/soles, happens later

---

### Classic associations

Staph aureus toxic shock syndrome is classically associated with:

* tampon use
* nasal packing
* surgical wounds
* skin infections

Strep pyogenes toxic shock syndrome is often associated with:

* necrotizing fasciitis
* cellulitis
* invasive soft tissue infection

---

## One-line intuition

Toxic shock syndrome is bacteria making a toxin that slams the immune system's gas pedal, causing cytokine storm, vasodilation, shock, rash, and organ failure.

---

## Pearl

Staph aureus toxic shock syndrome is usually due to TSST-1 (toxic shock syndrome toxin-1), a superantigen; treatment includes removing the source plus antibiotics, and clindamycin is useful because it inhibits toxin production.
