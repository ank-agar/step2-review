# Premature separation of placenta

Premature separation of placenta = **placental abruption**.

It means the placenta separates from the uterine wall **before delivery**.

Break it down:

* placenta = organ that gives fetus oxygen/nutrients
* abruption = sudden tearing away/separation
* premature = before the baby is delivered

So:

> the placenta peels off too early.

---

### Core idea

The placenta is supposed to stay attached until after birth.

If it detaches early:

* maternal blood vessels tear
* bleeding occurs behind the placenta
* fetal oxygen delivery drops

This can cause:

* painful vaginal bleeding
* uterine tenderness
* fetal distress
* shock in severe cases

---

### Why it causes fetal distress

The fetus gets oxygen through the placenta.

If the placenta separates from the uterus, that area can no longer exchange oxygen well.

So the fetus becomes hypoxic (low oxygen).

More separation = less working placenta = more fetal compromise.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* third-trimester bleeding
* painful bleeding
* tender, rigid uterus
* fetal distress

This contrasts with placenta previa (placenta covering the cervical opening), which classically causes **painless** third-trimester bleeding.

---

### Risk factors

High-yield risks:

* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* preeclampsia (hypertension plus end-organ signs in pregnancy)
* cocaine use
* smoking
* abdominal trauma
* prior placental abruption

Why cocaine?

Cocaine causes vasoconstriction (blood vessel narrowing), which can damage placental blood flow and trigger separation.

---

## One-line intuition

Placental abruption is when the placenta rips off the uterine wall too early, causing painful bleeding and cutting off fetal oxygen.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Placental abruption is associated with **DIC** (disseminated intravascular coagulation), because damaged placenta releases tissue factor, which overactivates clotting and consumes clotting factors.
