# Nongonococcal urethritis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/38/Male_and_female_urethra.svg/1280px-Male_and_female_urethra.svg.png)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil///PHIL_Images/18954/18954_lores.jpg)

Nongonococcal urethritis means:

* inflammation of the urethra
* **not caused by gonorrhea**

Break it down:

* non = not
* gonococcal = from *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (gonorrhea bacteria)
* urethritis = inflammation of the urethra (tube that carries urine out of the body)

So **NGU (nongonococcal urethritis)** is basically:

> urethral inflammation from something other than gonorrhea.

---

### Most common cause

The classic cause is:

* **Chlamydia trachomatis**

Other causes can include:

* *Mycoplasma genitalium*
* *Ureaplasma urealyticum*
* *Trichomonas vaginalis*

---

### Symptoms

Usually presents with:

* dysuria (pain/burning with urination)
* urethral discharge
* urethral itching/irritation

The discharge is often more **watery or mucoid** than the thick purulent discharge classically associated with gonorrhea.

---

### Why it happens

The pathogen infects the urethral epithelium (lining cells of the urethra).

That causes inflammation, so white blood cells come in.

Inflammation of the urethral lining causes:

* burning when urine passes over irritated tissue
* discharge from inflammatory fluid/cells
* positive leukocyte esterase sometimes, because leukocytes (white blood cells) are present

---

### Diagnosis idea

You suspect urethritis, but gonorrhea testing/Gram stain does **not** show gram-negative intracellular diplococci.

Then think:

* not gonorrhea
* likely chlamydia
* test with NAAT (nucleic acid amplification test), which detects organism genetic material

---

## One-line intuition

NGU is “urethritis symptoms, but no gonorrhea,” with **Chlamydia trachomatis** as the Step 1 favorite cause.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Chlamydia is an obligate intracellular organism, so it lives inside host cells and is treated with intracellular-penetrating antibiotics like doxycycline.
