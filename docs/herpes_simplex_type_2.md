Herpes simplex type 2, or HSV-2 (herpes simplex virus type 2), is an enveloped double-stranded DNA virus that classically causes genital herpes.

Core idea:

* HSV-1 (herpes simplex virus type 1) usually causes oral cold sores
* HSV-2 (herpes simplex virus type 2) usually causes genital herpes
* either type can infect either location, but HSV-2 is more associated with recurrent genital disease

---

### What it causes

HSV-2 infects skin/mucosa (moist epithelial surfaces), especially genital or anal mucosa.

Classic lesion:

* painful grouped vesicles
* on an erythematous base
* that rupture into shallow ulcers

Vesicle = small fluid-filled blister.

Erythematous = red from inflammation.

---

### Why it comes back

HSV-2 does not just stay on the skin.

After initial infection:

* it replicates in epithelial cells
* enters sensory nerves
* travels back to the dorsal root ganglia
* becomes latent there

Latency = the virus is hiding quietly, not actively making lots of virus.

For genital herpes, the key ganglia are usually the sacral dorsal root ganglia.

Then later, stress/illness/immunosuppression/other triggers can reactivate it:

* virus travels back down the nerve
* reaches the skin/mucosa again
* causes recurrent painful vesicles/ulcers

---

### Why the lesions hurt

HSV damages epithelial cells and causes local inflammation.

Also, because it lives in sensory nerves, outbreaks can have:

* burning
* tingling
* pain before lesions appear

That tingling/pain before the rash is called a prodrome (early warning symptoms before the main disease appears).

---

### Diagnosis

Best test from an active lesion:

* PCR (polymerase chain reaction) swab

PCR detects viral DNA, so it is more sensitive than culture.

Tzanck smear can show multinucleated giant cells, but it does not distinguish HSV-1 from HSV-2.

Multinucleated giant cells = infected cells fused together with multiple nuclei.

---

### Treatment

Treat with nucleoside analogs:

* acyclovir
* valacyclovir
* famciclovir

They inhibit viral DNA polymerase (the viral enzyme that copies viral DNA).

Important mechanism:

* acyclovir needs viral thymidine kinase for activation

Thymidine kinase = viral enzyme that phosphorylates/activates the drug.

This is why acyclovir selectively hits infected cells more than normal cells.

---

## One-line intuition

HSV-2 is a genital-loving herpes DNA virus that makes painful vesicles, hides in sacral sensory ganglia, and reactivates later.

---

## Pearl

HSV-2 classically causes genital herpes and establishes latency in sacral dorsal root ganglia; HSV-1 classically causes temporal lobe encephalitis.
