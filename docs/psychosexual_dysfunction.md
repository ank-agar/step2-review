# Psychosexual dysfunction

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Phases_of_sexual_arousal_and_female_orgasm.png)

![Image](https://upload.medbullets.com/topic/116004/images/traditional%20sexual%20response%20cycle.jpg)

### Core idea

Psychosexual dysfunction means sexual function is impaired mainly because of psychologic or relationship factors, not because of a primary structural/anatomic disease.

It can affect any part of the sexual response cycle:

* desire
* arousal
* orgasm
* pain/avoidance

Examples:

* low libido
* erectile dysfunction
* premature ejaculation
* delayed orgasm
* vaginismus, meaning involuntary pelvic floor muscle spasm
* dyspareunia, meaning pain with sex

---

### Why it happens

Sexual function needs both:

* body physiology
* brain/psychologic comfort

So even if the anatomy is normal, anxiety can block the response.

Why anxiety causes dysfunction:

* anxiety increases sympathetic tone
* sympathetic tone is the "fight-or-flight" state
* fight-or-flight makes the body less focused on arousal
* performance pressure creates a feedback loop

Basically:

> fear of sexual failure can itself cause sexual failure.

---

### Psychogenic vs organic clue

Psychogenic dysfunction often has:

* sudden onset
* situational pattern
* normal morning/nocturnal erections
* normal function with masturbation sometimes
* major stress, anxiety, depression, trauma, or relationship conflict

Organic dysfunction is more likely with:

* gradual onset
* consistent dysfunction in all settings
* diabetes mellitus
* vascular disease
* neurologic disease
* medications
* low testosterone

---

### Treatment idea

Treat the driver:

* psychotherapy or sex therapy for anxiety/trauma/relationship factors
* treat depression or anxiety
* review meds, especially SSRIs (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors)
* PDE5 inhibitors (phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors) for erectile dysfunction when appropriate

Why PDE5 inhibitors help erectile dysfunction:

* nitric oxide increases cGMP (cyclic guanosine monophosphate)
* cGMP relaxes penile smooth muscle
* relaxed smooth muscle lets blood fill the corpora cavernosa
* PDE5 inhibitors prevent cGMP breakdown

---

## One-line intuition

Psychosexual dysfunction is when the sexual response cycle is blocked by the brain/stress/relationship side of the system, even if the anatomy may be normal.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 HY: normal nocturnal or morning erections suggest psychogenic erectile dysfunction rather than organic vascular/neurologic erectile dysfunction.
