# Chondritis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/73/Costal_cartilages_frontal.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/623158.fig.001.jpg)

### Core idea

Chondritis = inflammation of cartilage.

Break the word apart:

* chondro- = cartilage
* -itis = inflammation

So chondritis literally means:

> inflamed cartilage.

Cartilage is the firm, flexible connective tissue found in places like:

* ear
* nose
* rib cage
* joints
* airway cartilage

---

### Why it causes pain/swelling

Cartilage itself has poor blood supply, but the tissue around it can get very inflamed.

That causes:

* pain
* tenderness
* redness
* swelling
* sometimes cartilage damage if inflammation keeps recurring

Think:

> cartilage is normally smooth + structural; inflammation makes it painful, swollen, and sometimes weak.

---

### Common examples

* **Costochondritis** = inflammation of costal cartilage (rib cartilage near the sternum/breastbone)
* **Auricular chondritis** = inflammation of ear cartilage
* **Relapsing polychondritis** = recurrent inflammation of multiple cartilage sites

In the ear, a classic clue is:

* red, painful upper ear
* relative sparing of the earlobe

Why?

The upper ear has cartilage, but the earlobe does not.

---

## One-line intuition

Chondritis is when the body attacks or irritates cartilage, so cartilage-heavy structures become painful, red, swollen, and sometimes deformed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Relapsing polychondritis can affect airway cartilage, so hoarseness, stridor (high-pitched breathing sound), or breathing trouble is high-risk because the trachea (windpipe) can become inflamed or collapse.
