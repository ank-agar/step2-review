# Genu valgum

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e7/Genu_valgum_and_Genu_Varum.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6b/Genu-Valgum.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0a/Valgus.jpg)

Genu valgum means **knock-knees**.

Break it down:

* **genu** = knee
* **valgum/valgus** = distal part angles away from the midline

So in genu valgum:

* knees move inward toward each other
* ankles stay farther apart
* the lower leg angles outward relative to the femur (thigh bone)

---

### Core idea

The leg alignment makes an **X-shape**.

Think:

> knees together, feet apart.

That is opposite of **genu varum** (bowlegs), where the knees stay apart and the legs look more like parentheses.

---

### Why it happens

In many young kids, mild genu valgum is physiologic (normal developmental variant) because leg alignment naturally changes as they grow.

But pathologic (abnormal) genu valgum can happen when bone growth or mineralization is abnormal, like in:

* rickets (defective bone mineralization, classically from vitamin D deficiency)
* skeletal dysplasias (genetic disorders of bone/cartilage development)
* trauma to the growth plate
* obesity, which can worsen mechanical stress on the knees

---

### Why the findings happen

The mechanical axis (weight-bearing line from hip to ankle) shifts laterally (toward the outside of the knee).

That can cause:

* lateral knee compartment stress
* patellar maltracking (kneecap does not glide normally)
* knee pain if severe
* abnormal gait if significant

Intuition:

The knee is being loaded from the wrong angle, so the joint and kneecap get pulled/stressed in a less efficient direction.

---

## One-line intuition

Genu valgum is **knees collapsing inward**, making the legs look like an X.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember: **rickets can cause genu valgum or genu varum** because poorly mineralized growing bone bends under body weight.
