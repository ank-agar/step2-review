### Core idea

C1q deficiency means low/absent **C1q**, the recognition protein that starts the **classical complement pathway** (antibody-triggered complement pathway).

Big association:

* **SLE** (systemic lupus erythematosus)
* recurrent infections, especially with encapsulated bacteria

---

### Why it causes lupus-like disease

C1q normally helps tag and clear:

* immune complexes (antibody-antigen clumps)
* apoptotic cells (programmed dying cells)
* nuclear debris from dead cells

If C1q is missing, that cellular trash stays around.

Then the immune system keeps seeing self nuclear material, like DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid), and may start making autoantibodies (antibodies against self).

That is why C1q deficiency has such a strong association with SLE.

---

### What to recognize

Think:

* early-onset lupus-like disease
* malar rash/discoid rash
* oral ulcers
* kidney disease/nephritis
* recurrent bacterial infections

Nephritis means kidney inflammation.

---

## One-line intuition

C1q is the immune system's cleanup starter for the classical complement pathway; without it, dead-cell debris piles up and can trigger lupus.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Early classical complement deficiencies, especially **C1q, C2, and C4**, predispose to **SLE** because they impair clearance of immune complexes and apoptotic debris.
