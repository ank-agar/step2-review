### Core idea

Parvovirus B19 is a small nonenveloped **ssDNA virus** (single-stranded DNA virus) that infects **erythroid precursors** (immature red blood cell-making cells) in bone marrow.

So the big board idea is:

* kids: **erythema infectiosum** (fifth disease) with slapped-cheek rash
* adults: symmetric joint pain, especially hands/wrists/knees
* sickle cell disease or other hemolytic anemia: transient aplastic crisis
* pregnancy: fetal anemia → hydrops fetalis

---

### Why it causes anemia

Parvovirus B19 likes cells with **P antigen** (globoside; a cell-surface receptor), especially erythroid precursors.

It infects those red cell precursors and temporarily shuts down **erythropoiesis** (red blood cell production).

In a normal person, this usually is not a big deal because existing **RBCs** (red blood cells) live around 120 days.

But in hemolytic diseases like sickle cell disease, RBCs are already being destroyed quickly, so the patient depends on constant new RBC production.

So:

> Parvovirus B19 pauses RBC production + sickle cell patients need constant RBC production = aplastic crisis.

Labs classically show:

* severe anemia
* very low reticulocytes

**Reticulocytes** are young RBCs, so low reticulocytes means the bone marrow is not making new RBCs.

---

### Why the rash happens

The slapped-cheek rash usually appears after the early fever/cold-like symptoms.

Important intuition:

* early phase = viral replication and contagiousness
* later rash phase = immune complex reaction

So by the time the classic rash appears, the patient is often less contagious.

---

### Pregnancy complication

If maternal infection spreads to the fetus, the virus can infect fetal erythroid precursors.

The fetus is very dependent on making new RBCs, so infection can cause severe fetal anemia.

Severe fetal anemia can cause **hydrops fetalis** (abnormal fluid accumulation in fetal compartments).

---

## One-line intuition

Parvovirus B19 is the virus that “slaps the cheeks” in kids and “slaps the bone marrow” in patients who desperately need new RBCs.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Parvovirus B19 causes **transient aplastic crisis** in sickle cell disease: severe anemia with **low reticulocytes**, because the marrow temporarily stops making RBCs.
