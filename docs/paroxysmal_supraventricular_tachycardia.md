### Core idea

Paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia is a sudden-on/sudden-off fast heart rhythm that starts above the ventricles.

Break it down:

* paroxysmal = comes in sudden episodes
* supra = above
* ventricular = ventricles
* tachycardia = fast heart rate

So:

> sudden episodes of fast heart rate coming from above the ventricles.

Commonly abbreviated **PSVT (paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia)**.

---

### What is actually happening

Most classic PSVT is due to **AVNRT (atrioventricular nodal reentrant tachycardia)**.

That means there is a tiny electrical loop near the **AV node (atrioventricular node)**.

Instead of the signal going:

* atria -> AV node -> ventricles -> stop

it loops back around and keeps firing:

* atria -> AV node -> ventricles -> back up -> repeat

That loop makes the heart beat very fast and very regularly.

---

### Why it starts and stops suddenly

The rhythm depends on a reentry loop.

Once the loop catches, the heart rate jumps up suddenly.

Once the loop is interrupted, the rhythm can stop suddenly and return to normal.

That is why PSVT feels like:

* sudden racing heartbeat
* palpitations
* lightheadedness
* shortness of breath sometimes
* chest discomfort sometimes

---

### ECG idea

ECG (electrocardiogram) classically shows:

* regular rhythm
* narrow QRS complexes
* very fast rate, often around 150-250 bpm (beats per minute)
* P waves may be hidden inside the QRS complexes

Why narrow QRS?

Because the ventricles are still being activated through the normal His-Purkinje system, so ventricular conduction is normal and fast.

---

### Treatment intuition

The AV node is usually part of the loop, so treatment tries to slow or block the AV node.

Common acute options:

* vagal maneuvers = increase vagus nerve activity to slow the AV node
* adenosine = briefly blocks AV nodal conduction

If the AV node is the "gate" of the loop, blocking the gate breaks the loop.

---

## One-line intuition

PSVT is a sudden electrical short-circuit above the ventricles, usually looping through/near the AV node, causing a fast regular narrow-complex tachycardia.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Adenosine works for many PSVT episodes because it transiently blocks the AV node, which breaks AV node-dependent reentry like AVNRT.
