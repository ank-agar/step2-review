### Core idea

A hydatidiform mole is an abnormal pregnancy where placental tissue grows weirdly into swollen, cystic chorionic villi (placental projections that normally help exchange nutrients).

Think:

* placenta goes crazy
* villi swell with fluid
* uterus may fill with “grape-like” cysts
* beta-hCG (beta-human chorionic gonadotropin, the pregnancy hormone) is very high

It is part of GTD (gestational trophoblastic disease, abnormal growth of pregnancy-related placental tissue).

---

### Why beta-hCG gets high

The proliferating trophoblasts (placental cells that normally make beta-hCG) overgrow.

So:

* more trophoblast tissue = more beta-hCG
* very high beta-hCG can cause hyperemesis gravidarum (severe vomiting in pregnancy)
* very high beta-hCG can also cause hyperthyroidism because hCG can weakly stimulate the TSH receptor (thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor)

---

### Complete vs partial mole

**Complete mole:**

* usually 46,XX
* all paternal DNA
* no fetal tissue
* diffuse villous swelling
* higher risk of choriocarcinoma (malignant trophoblastic tumor)

Mechanism:

An empty egg gets fertilized, then paternal DNA duplicates.

Intuition:

No maternal DNA means no real embryo formation, but placental/trophoblast tissue can still grow like crazy.

**Partial mole:**

* usually 69,XXY
* maternal DNA + extra paternal DNA
* fetal tissue may be present
* focal villous swelling
* lower risk of choriocarcinoma

Mechanism:

A normal egg gets fertilized by 2 sperm.

Intuition:

There is some maternal DNA, so some fetal development can start, but the extra paternal DNA pushes abnormal placental overgrowth.

---

### Classic findings

* vaginal bleeding in early pregnancy
* uterus larger than expected for gestational age
* very high beta-hCG
* hyperemesis gravidarum
* hyperthyroid symptoms
* “snowstorm” ultrasound pattern from many tiny cystic spaces
* grape-like vesicles grossly

---

## One-line intuition

A hydatidiform mole is a pregnancy where abnormal fertilization makes the placenta overgrow into fluid-filled “grapes,” causing very high beta-hCG.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Complete mole = “completely paternal” DNA and no fetus.

Partial mole = “part mom, too” and may have fetal tissue.
