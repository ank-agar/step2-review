### Core idea

Autism spectrum disorder, or **ASD (autism spectrum disorder)**, is a neurodevelopmental disorder, meaning brain development affects behavior and communication.

It is mainly defined by:

* impaired social communication/social interaction
* restricted, repetitive behaviors or interests

---

### What "spectrum" means

Spectrum does **not** mean mild to severe in one straight line.

It means different people can have different mixes of:

* language ability
* social communication difficulty
* sensory sensitivity
* repetitive behaviors
* intellectual ability
* need for support

So two patients with ASD can look very different.

---

### Why the findings happen

The brain processes social cues, language, sensory input, and routines differently.

So patients may have:

* reduced eye contact or difficulty reading facial expressions
* delayed speech or unusual speech patterns
* repetitive movements like hand flapping
* intense focus on specific interests
* distress with changes in routine
* hyperreactivity or hyporeactivity to sensory input

Hyperreactivity means too strong of a response.

Hyporeactivity means too weak of a response.

Example: a child may be very bothered by loud sounds, textures, or bright lights because sensory input feels amplified.

---

### Associations

ASD is associated with:

* intellectual disability
* epilepsy
* fragile X syndrome
* tuberous sclerosis

Fragile X syndrome is a genetic cause of intellectual disability due to **FMR1 (fragile X messenger ribonucleoprotein 1)** gene mutation.

Tuberous sclerosis is a genetic disorder causing benign tumors called hamartomas in the brain, skin, kidneys, and heart.

---

## One-line intuition

ASD is a brain development difference where social communication and behavioral flexibility are the main affected areas.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For **USMLE Step 1 (United States Medical Licensing Examination Step 1)**, remember:

ASD is strongly associated with **fragile X syndrome**, especially in a boy with intellectual disability, long face, large ears, macroorchidism, and autism-like behaviors.

Macroorchidism means enlarged testes.
