Rumination can mean 2 things:

* in psychiatry/psychology: repetitive, stuck-on-loop thinking
* in GI (gastrointestinal) medicine: repeated regurgitation of recently eaten food

For Step-style medicine, **rumination disorder/syndrome** usually means:

> Food comes back up into the mouth after eating, then the person may re-chew, re-swallow, or spit it out.

Key point:

* it is **effortless**
* it is **not intentional vomiting**
* it usually happens soon after meals
* the food is often undigested and not acidic at first

---

### Core idea

Think:

**eat → abdominal wall contracts → stomach pressure rises → food comes back up**

It is like the body accidentally learns a reflex after eating.

The person is not usually trying to vomit. The movement becomes a habit/reflex loop.

---

### Why it is not vomiting

Vomiting usually has:

* nausea
* retching
* forceful expulsion
* acidic/bitter taste

Rumination usually has:

* no nausea
* no retching
* effortless regurgitation
* recently eaten food that may taste normal

So if a vignette says **effortless regurgitation after meals**, think rumination.

---

### Why diaphragmatic breathing helps

Diaphragmatic breathing means breathing using the diaphragm (main breathing muscle under the lungs).

It helps because it competes with the abdominal wall contraction.

Basically:

* rumination needs abdominal pressure to push food upward
* diaphragmatic breathing relaxes/changes that pressure pattern
* so stomach contents are less likely to come back up

---

### Do not confuse with GERD

GERD (gastroesophageal reflux disease) is acid reflux from stomach acid moving into the esophagus (food pipe).

GERD is more associated with:

* heartburn
* sour/acid taste
* worse lying down
* response to PPIs (proton pump inhibitors, acid-lowering drugs)

Rumination is more:

* behavioral/reflexive
* soon after meals
* undigested food
* treated mainly with behavioral therapy/diaphragmatic breathing

---

## One-line intuition

Rumination disorder is **a learned post-meal reflex where food comes back up effortlessly, without nausea or retching**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY (high-yield): **Rumination disorder is treated first-line with diaphragmatic breathing**, not PPIs (proton pump inhibitors), because the problem is mainly a reflex/pressure pattern rather than excess stomach acid.
