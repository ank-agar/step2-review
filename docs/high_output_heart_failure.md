# High-output heart failure

![Image](https://www.ccjm.org/content/ccjom/92/6/362/F1.large.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK559213/bin/Arteriovenous__Fistula.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ccjm.org/content/ccjom/92/6/362/F2.large.jpg)

![Image](https://www.mayoclinic.org/-/media/kcms/gbs/patient-consumer/images/2015/04/02/16/04/mcdc7_arteriovenous_fistula_6col.jpg)

High-output heart failure is heart failure where cardiac output (CO = amount of blood the heart pumps per minute) is high, but still not enough for the body’s demand.

So the problem is not “weak pump at first.”

The problem is:

* body demand is too high, or
* systemic vascular resistance (SVR = resistance in the body’s blood vessels) is too low

Then the heart keeps pumping more and more until it eventually cannot keep up.

---

### Core idea

Normal heart failure:

* low cardiac output
* cold, poor perfusion

High-output heart failure:

* high cardiac output
* warm extremities often
* bounding pulses sometimes
* still has congestion/edema because the heart is overworked

---

### Why it happens

If SVR (systemic vascular resistance) drops, blood “runs off” into the low-resistance circulation.

Examples:

* AV fistula (arteriovenous fistula = abnormal artery-to-vein connection)
* sepsis
* cirrhosis
* beriberi from thiamine/vitamin B1 deficiency

The body senses low effective arterial volume, so it activates:

* RAAS (renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system)
* sympathetic nervous system

That causes salt/water retention, so preload (blood returning to the heart) goes up.

More preload = more volume the heart must pump.

Eventually:

* dilated chambers
* pulmonary edema
* peripheral edema
* heart failure symptoms

---

### Classic causes to know

Think:

* severe anemia
* AV fistula
* hyperthyroidism
* beriberi
* Paget disease of bone
* pregnancy
* cirrhosis
* sepsis

Why anemia causes it:

Low hemoglobin (oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells) means each liter of blood carries less oxygen, so the heart pumps more liters per minute to deliver enough oxygen.

Why AV fistula causes it:

Blood skips arterioles/capillaries and goes straight from artery to vein, so resistance drops and venous return increases. The heart gets flooded with extra returning blood.

---

## One-line intuition

High-output heart failure = the heart is pumping a lot, but the body is demanding even more, so the heart eventually gets volume-overloaded and fails.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Thiamine deficiency causes wet beriberi, which is a classic high-output heart failure cause because thiamine deficiency impairs ATP (adenosine triphosphate = cellular energy) production, leading to peripheral vasodilation and low SVR (systemic vascular resistance).
