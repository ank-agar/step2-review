# Metabolic encephalopathy

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/76/Symptoms_of_hyperammonemia.svg/960px-Symptoms_of_hyperammonemia.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/Urea_cycle.svg/500px-Urea_cycle.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.siumed.edu/sites/default/files/inline-images/EEG5.gif)

Metabolic encephalopathy means brain dysfunction caused by a body chemistry problem.

Break it down:

* metabolic = related to body chemistry/energy/toxins
* encephalo- = brain
* -pathy = disease/problem

So:

> the brain is globally not working well because the internal environment is abnormal.

---

### Core idea

The neurons are usually not destroyed at first.

They are “stunned” because they are getting the wrong chemical environment, like:

* low glucose
* low oxygen
* high ammonia
* uremia (buildup of kidney-failure toxins)
* severe sodium abnormality
* severe calcium abnormality
* liver failure
* kidney failure
* sepsis (whole-body inflammatory response to infection)
* drugs/toxins

That is why it often causes diffuse symptoms instead of one clean focal neurologic deficit.

---

### What it looks like clinically

Usually:

* confusion
* altered mental status
* sleepiness
* agitation
* delirium
* coma if severe

Because the whole brain is being affected, you usually do **not** expect one isolated finding like only right arm weakness.

That pattern would make you think more about stroke or a focal brain lesion.

---

### Why the brain gets confused

The brain is very sensitive to chemistry because it needs constant:

* glucose for energy
* oxygen for ATP (adenosine triphosphate, the cell’s energy molecule)
* normal electrolytes for action potentials
* toxin clearance

If these are off, neurons cannot fire normally.

So the patient becomes globally “foggy” rather than having one specific tract knocked out.

---

### Classic example: hepatic encephalopathy

Hepatic encephalopathy = brain dysfunction from liver failure.

The liver normally converts ammonia into urea through the urea cycle.

If the liver fails:

* ammonia builds up
* ammonia reaches the brain
* astrocytes (support cells in the brain) swell
* brain function slows down

That gives confusion, asterixis, and coma in severe cases.

Asterixis = “flapping tremor” when the patient holds their hands out.

---

### EEG clue

EEG (electroencephalogram, brain-wave test) may show:

* diffuse slowing
* triphasic waves

Triphasic waves are classically associated with metabolic encephalopathy, especially hepatic encephalopathy, but they are not specific.

---

## One-line intuition

Metabolic encephalopathy is when bad blood chemistry makes the whole brain run slowly and diffusely.

---

## Pearl 🔥

On Step 1, **altered mental status + asterixis + liver disease** should make you think hepatic encephalopathy from increased ammonia.
