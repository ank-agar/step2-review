# Precocious puberty

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499898/bin/HPG__axis.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg)

### Core idea

Precocious puberty = puberty happening too early:

* before age 8 in girls
* before age 9 in boys

Puberty means activation of sex-hormone effects, causing things like:

* breast development
* testicular enlargement
* pubic hair
* growth spurt
* early menses

---

### Main mechanism

The big pathway is the HPG axis (hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis):

* hypothalamus releases GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* pituitary releases LH (luteinizing hormone) and FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* gonads make sex hormones
  * ovaries make estrogen
  * testes make testosterone

Those sex hormones cause secondary sexual characteristics.

---

### Central vs peripheral

**Central precocious puberty** = the brain turns on the normal puberty pathway too early.

So:

* GnRH is high
* LH/FSH are high
* puberty is usually in the normal sequence, just early

Think:

> normal puberty button pressed too soon.

Causes include:

* idiopathic (unknown cause), especially in girls
* CNS (central nervous system) lesions
* hypothalamic hamartoma

**Peripheral precocious puberty** = sex hormones are high without the brain starting puberty.

So:

* estrogen/testosterone is high
* LH/FSH are low due to negative feedback

Think:

> hormone is coming from outside the normal puberty command center.

Causes include:

* ovarian/testicular tumors
* adrenal tumors
* congenital adrenal hyperplasia
* exogenous sex steroids

---

### Why short adult height can happen

Sex hormones initially make the child grow faster.

But they also close the epiphyseal growth plates (growth plates at the ends of long bones) earlier.

So the child may be tall early, but short later because the growth plates fuse too soon.

---

### How it is evaluated

Common workup:

* bone age X-ray to see if skeletal maturation is advanced
* LH/FSH levels
* GnRH stimulation test
* estradiol in girls or testosterone in boys
* brain MRI if central precocious puberty is suspected, especially in boys or very young children

---

## One-line intuition

Precocious puberty is early sex-hormone exposure: either the brain starts the real puberty pathway too soon, or hormones are being produced independently.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Central precocious puberty is treated with continuous GnRH agonists (gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonists), which downregulate pituitary GnRH receptors and decrease LH/FSH release.
