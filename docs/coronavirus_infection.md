### Core idea

Coronavirus infection means infection by an enveloped, positive-sense single-stranded RNA virus (RNA virus whose genome can be directly translated like messenger RNA).

Most human coronaviruses cause mild upper respiratory infection, like:

* cough
* fever
* sore throat
* runny nose

More severe coronaviruses can cause pneumonia (infection/inflammation of lung air spaces), especially:

* SARS-CoV-1 (severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 1)
* MERS-CoV (Middle East respiratory syndrome coronavirus)
* SARS-CoV-2 (severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2), the virus that causes COVID-19 (coronavirus disease 2019)

---

### Why the findings happen

The virus spreads mainly by respiratory droplets/aerosols, then infects respiratory epithelial cells (lining cells of the airway).

For SARS-CoV-2:

* spike protein binds ACE2 (angiotensin-converting enzyme 2) on host cells
* viral RNA enters the cell
* host ribosomes translate viral proteins
* new virions (complete virus particles) assemble and bud out

So the symptoms come from two things:

* **Direct epithelial cell injury:** cough, sore throat, loss of smell/taste sometimes
* **Immune/inflammatory response:** fever, myalgias (muscle aches), fatigue

If infection reaches the alveoli (tiny air sacs where gas exchange happens), inflammation fills the air spaces with fluid/cells:

* less oxygen crosses into blood
* chest imaging can show bilateral patchy opacities
* severe cases can progress to ARDS (acute respiratory distress syndrome)

---

### High-yield structure

Coronaviruses are:

* **enveloped:** lipid membrane around the virus
* **positive-sense single-stranded RNA:** genome acts like messenger RNA
* **helical nucleocapsid:** RNA wrapped with nucleocapsid protein
* **spike proteins:** surface proteins that help attach to host cells

The “corona” name comes from the crown-like spike appearance.

---

### Diagnosis

Usually diagnosed with:

* RT-PCR (reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction), which detects viral RNA
* antigen test, which detects viral proteins

Serology (blood antibody testing) is more about prior exposure than acute diagnosis.

---

## One-line intuition

Coronavirus infection is a respiratory RNA virus infection where spike-mediated entry injures airway/lung cells and the immune response creates the fever, cough, and pneumonia.

---

## HY pearl

SARS-CoV-2 uses ACE2 (angiotensin-converting enzyme 2) to enter cells; MERS-CoV uses DPP-4 (dipeptidyl peptidase-4).
