## Viral cardiomyopathy

![Image](https://www.wikidoc.org/images/5/5c/Pathophys_of_Viral_Myocarditis.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/22714.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6c/Viral_myocarditis_%281%29.JPG/250px-Viral_myocarditis_%281%29.JPG)

Viral cardiomyopathy means a virus damages the heart muscle enough to weaken heart pumping.

Usually the sequence is:

* viral infection
* myocarditis (inflammation of the heart muscle)
* myocyte injury/death
* ventricular dilation
* systolic heart failure

Systolic heart failure means the heart cannot squeeze blood forward well.

---

### Core idea

Think:

> virus inflames myocardium → myocardium gets weak → heart dilates to compensate → poor contraction

The heart becomes a stretched-out, weak pump.

---

### Why viruses cause this

Viruses can injure the heart in 2 big ways:

* **Direct viral damage:** virus infects myocytes (heart muscle cells) and causes cell injury/death
* **Immune damage:** the immune system attacks infected tissue, but the inflammation also damages myocardium

So even after the initial infection, inflammation can keep hurting the heart.

---

### Common viruses

Classic Step 1 association:

* Coxsackie B virus

Others can include:

* adenovirus
* parvovirus B19
* HIV (human immunodeficiency virus)
* SARS-CoV-2 (severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2)

---

### What it can look like clinically

Because the myocardium is weak, patients can have heart failure symptoms:

* dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* fatigue
* edema (swelling from fluid buildup)
* chest pain sometimes
* arrhythmias (abnormal heart rhythms)

Labs may show increased troponin because myocytes are injured.

Troponin = marker of heart muscle damage.

---

## One-line intuition

Viral cardiomyopathy is basically myocarditis that leaves the heart muscle weak and dilated.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Coxsackie B virus is the classic viral cause of myocarditis, which can progress to dilated cardiomyopathy.
