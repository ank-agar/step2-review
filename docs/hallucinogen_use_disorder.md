## Hallucinogen use disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/76/Biology-12-01380-g003.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/LSD_blotter.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/Psilocybe_semilanceata_6514.jpg)

Hallucinogen use disorder is a substance use disorder where a person keeps using hallucinogens despite harm.

Hallucinogens are drugs that distort:

* perception
* mood
* thought
* sensory experience

Examples:

* LSD (lysergic acid diethylamide)
* psilocybin
* mescaline
* MDMA (3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine)
* PCP (phencyclidine), though PCP has its own category clinically

---

### Core idea

The issue is not just “this person used a hallucinogen once.”

The disorder means:

* repeated use
* impaired control
* social/functional problems
* risky use
* continued use despite consequences

So the key word is **maladaptive pattern**.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Classic psychedelics mainly stimulate **5-HT2A receptors**.

5-HT means serotonin, and 5-HT2A is a serotonin receptor subtype.

So:

> hallucinogen = abnormal serotonin signaling in cortex → distorted perception/thinking.

That is why patients can have:

* visual hallucinations
* altered sense of time
* depersonalization
* derealization
* panic or paranoia

Depersonalization means feeling detached from yourself.

Derealization means feeling like the world is unreal.

---

### Diagnosis intuition

Think:

> “I keep using it even though it is messing up my life.”

DSM-style clues include:

* craving
* using more than intended
* failed attempts to cut down
* role impairment
* interpersonal problems
* hazardous use
* tolerance

Withdrawal is not usually a major feature for classic hallucinogens.

---

### Important distinction

Hallucinogen intoxication is the acute trip.

Hallucinogen use disorder is the ongoing problematic pattern.

So:

* intoxication = what happens right after using
* use disorder = repeated use despite harm

---

## One-line intuition

Hallucinogen use disorder is when serotonin-disrupting perception drugs become a repeated behavior pattern that causes real-life impairment.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Classic hallucinogens usually cause **tolerance** but not a prominent withdrawal syndrome, which helps separate them from alcohol, opioids, and sedative-hypnotics.
