# Breast cyst

![Image](https://www.mayoclinic.org/-/media/kcms/gbs/patient-consumer/images/2013/08/26/10/50/ds01071_hb7_breastcystthu_jpg.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/Ultrasound_Scan_ND_0112144335_1445010.png/250px-Ultrasound_Scan_ND_0112144335_1445010.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/13/Zyste.jpg/250px-Zyste.jpg)

A breast cyst is a benign (non-cancerous), fluid-filled sac inside breast tissue.

Think:

* cyst = closed sac
* breast cyst = sac filled with fluid in the breast

---

### Core idea

Breast ducts/lobules can dilate and fill with fluid, especially with hormonal changes.

So the patient may feel a lump that is:

* round or oval
* smooth
* mobile
* sometimes tender
* often changes with the menstrual cycle

---

### Why it feels like a lump

Fluid collects inside a walled-off space.

That makes it feel like a soft grape or water balloon under the skin.

This is different from a solid tumor because the inside is fluid, not proliferating tumor cells.

---

### Diagnosis

Ultrasound is useful because it can tell:

* fluid-filled cyst
* vs solid mass

A simple cyst on ultrasound is usually:

* anechoic (black because fluid does not reflect much sound)
* well-circumscribed (clear smooth border)
* posterior acoustic enhancement (brighter area behind it because sound passes through fluid easily)

---

### Treatment

Usually no treatment is needed if it is a simple cyst.

If painful or large, it can be drained with FNA (fine-needle aspiration), meaning a thin needle removes the fluid.

If the fluid is bloody or the mass does not go away after aspiration, evaluate further because that is less reassuring.

---

## One-line intuition

A breast cyst is basically a small fluid balloon in breast tissue, usually benign and often hormone-related.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Breast cysts are part of nonproliferative fibrocystic change, so simple cysts do **not** increase breast cancer risk.
