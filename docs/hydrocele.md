## Hydrocele

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19667.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Hydrocele_in_Pediatric_Population._Processus_vaginalis.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Ultrasonography_of_hydrocele.jpg)

A **hydrocele** is a collection of fluid around the testicle, usually in the **tunica vaginalis** (thin serous sac surrounding the testis).

Break the word apart:

* hydro- = water/fluid
* -cele = swelling or sac

So:

> fluid-filled scrotal swelling around the testicle.

---

### Core idea

Hydrocele = **painless scrotal enlargement from fluid**, not a solid mass.

Classic findings:

* painless swollen scrotum
* feels like a water balloon
* transilluminates with light
* testis may be hard to feel if the hydrocele is large

**Transillumination** means light passes through it because the swelling is fluid-filled.

---

### Why it happens

Normally, the testis descends from the abdomen into the scrotum, dragging down a peritoneal outpouching called the **processus vaginalis** (embryologic peritoneal tube).

Then the processus vaginalis should close.

If it does not close:

* peritoneal fluid can track down into the scrotum
* fluid collects around the testis
* this creates a **communicating hydrocele** (hydrocele connected to the peritoneal cavity)

If the connection closes but fluid remains trapped:

* you get a **noncommunicating hydrocele** (isolated fluid collection)

---

### Key association

A patent processus vaginalis also predisposes to an **indirect inguinal hernia** (bowel entering the inguinal canal through a congenital opening).

So the connection is:

* patent processus vaginalis → communicating hydrocele
* patent processus vaginalis → indirect inguinal hernia

Same embryologic problem, different contents:

* hydrocele = fluid
* hernia = bowel/abdominal contents

---

### One-line intuition

A hydrocele is basically a **water-filled sac around the testicle**, often because the fetal tunnel from abdomen to scrotum did not close correctly.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Hydrocele usually **transilluminates**, while testicular cancer usually presents as a **solid, non-transilluminating testicular mass**.
