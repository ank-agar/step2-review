### Core idea

Acromegaly is excess **GH (growth hormone)** in an adult, usually from a **pituitary adenoma (benign tumor of the pituitary gland)**.

Because adults already have closed **epiphyseal plates (growth plates)**, they cannot grow taller.

Instead, GH causes increased **IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1)**, which makes soft tissue and bones get thicker/wider:

* enlarged hands and feet
* coarse facial features
* enlarged jaw
* enlarged tongue
* thick skin
* organ enlargement

---

### Why the findings happen

GH stimulates the liver and other tissues to make IGF-1.

IGF-1 is the main "growth signal," so it causes:

* **hands/feet enlargement:** periosteal bone growth + soft tissue growth
* **jaw protrusion:** mandibular bone growth
* **deep voice/sleep apnea:** soft tissue and tongue enlargement
* **headache/vision problems:** pituitary tumor compresses nearby structures
* **hyperglycemia (high blood glucose):** GH has anti-insulin effects

---

### Diagnosis idea

Screen with:

* increased **IGF-1 (insulin-like growth factor 1)**

Confirm with:

* oral glucose test: GH should normally go down after glucose
* in acromegaly, GH fails to suppress

Why?

Glucose normally tells the body, "we have enough fuel, stop growth hormone."

But a GH-secreting pituitary adenoma is autonomous, meaning it keeps secreting GH anyway.

---

## One-line intuition

Acromegaly = adult GH excess, so the person grows outward/thicker instead of taller.

---

## Pearl 🔥

GH excess before epiphyseal plate closure causes **gigantism (increased height)**; GH excess after epiphyseal plate closure causes **acromegaly (enlarged hands, feet, jaw, and soft tissues)**.
