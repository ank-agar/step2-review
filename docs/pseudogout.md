Pseudogout is acute arthritis caused by calcium pyrophosphate crystals depositing in joints.

CPPD (calcium pyrophosphate deposition disease) is the underlying crystal disease.

It is called **pseudo-gout** because it can look like gout:

* sudden painful swollen joint
* red/warm joint
* often the knee

But the crystal is different:

* gout = uric acid crystals
* pseudogout = calcium pyrophosphate crystals

---

### Core idea

Calcium pyrophosphate crystals sit in cartilage/joint space and irritate the joint.

The immune system reacts to the crystals, especially neutrophils (acute inflammatory white blood cells), causing:

* pain
* swelling
* warmth
* stiffness

So the joint hurts because the body is treating the crystals like inflammatory debris.

---

### Classic findings

Synovial fluid (joint fluid) shows:

* rhomboid-shaped crystals
* weakly positive birefringence

Birefringence means the crystal changes color under polarized light microscopy (microscope using polarized light).

Step 1 contrast:

* gout = needle-shaped, negatively birefringent crystals
* pseudogout = rhomboid-shaped, positively birefringent crystals

---

### Chondrocalcinosis

Chondrocalcinosis means calcium deposits in cartilage.

Break it down:

* chondro- = cartilage
* calcinosis = calcium deposition

On X-ray, this can show up as thin white calcified lines in cartilage, especially around the knee meniscus (fibrocartilage cushion in the knee).

---

## One-line intuition

Pseudogout is gout’s look-alike, but instead of uric acid needles, it is calcium pyrophosphate rhomboids irritating the joint.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Pseudogout is associated with hemochromatosis (iron overload disease), hyperparathyroidism (too much parathyroid hormone), and older age.
