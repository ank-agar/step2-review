# Placenta accreta

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/54/Placenta_accreta.png)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/94dc/5424888/4134aac5a45a/nihms803178f1.jpg)

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/94dc/5424888/ac8c85e4d846/nihms803178f4.jpg)

Placenta accreta is when the placenta attaches too deeply into the uterine wall.

Normally, the placenta separates after delivery because there is a clean boundary layer called the decidua basalis (maternal endometrial lining under the placenta).

In placenta accreta, that boundary is defective, so placental villi (fingerlike placental tissue) stick directly onto or into the myometrium (uterine smooth muscle).

---

### Spectrum

Think: increasing depth of invasion.

* **Accreta:** placenta attaches to myometrium
* **Increta:** placenta invades into myometrium
* **Percreta:** placenta goes through myometrium, sometimes into bladder

So:

> accreta = stuck on  
> increta = grows in  
> percreta = penetrates through

---

### Why it is dangerous

The placenta is supposed to peel off after delivery.

But if it is abnormally stuck to the uterus, trying to remove it can tear open large maternal blood vessels.

That causes:

* severe postpartum hemorrhage (heavy bleeding after delivery)
* retained placenta
* possible need for hysterectomy (surgical removal of uterus)

---

### Biggest risk factors

Classic setup:

* prior C-section (cesarean delivery)
* placenta previa (placenta covering or near the cervical opening)

Why?

A C-section scar can damage the decidua basalis, and if the placenta implants over that scar, it can invade too deeply.

---

### Diagnosis

Usually suspected on prenatal ultrasound.

Findings can include:

* placental lacunae (irregular vascular spaces in the placenta)
* loss of normal clear zone between placenta and uterus
* very thin myometrium
* increased blood flow on Doppler ultrasound

MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) can help if ultrasound is unclear or if percreta is suspected.

---

## One-line intuition

The placenta is abnormally glued into the uterine wall, so delivery risks massive bleeding when the placenta cannot detach normally.

---

## HY pearl

Placenta accreta spectrum is strongly associated with **placenta previa + prior C-section**.
