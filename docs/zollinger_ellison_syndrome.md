# Zollinger-Ellison syndrome

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/-/media/Images/Health-Information/Digestive-Diseases/pancreas-anatomy_1586x1534.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/54/ZES_endo.jpg)

### Core idea

Zollinger-Ellison syndrome is when a **gastrinoma** (gastrin-secreting tumor) makes too much **gastrin** (hormone that stimulates stomach acid).

Most gastrinomas are in the:

* pancreas
* duodenum (first part of the small intestine)

So the chain is:

> gastrinoma -> high gastrin -> high stomach acid -> recurrent peptic ulcers + diarrhea

---

### Why ulcers happen

Gastrin normally tells parietal cells (acid-secreting stomach cells) to make hydrochloric acid.

In Zollinger-Ellison syndrome:

* gastrinoma keeps releasing gastrin
* gastrin keeps stimulating parietal cells
* stomach acid stays very high
* acid injures the stomach/duodenum/jejunum lining

That causes severe or recurrent **peptic ulcers** (acid-related ulcers in the GI tract).

---

### Why diarrhea happens

Too much acid spills into the small intestine.

That causes diarrhea because acid:

* damages intestinal mucosa
* inactivates pancreatic enzymes
* prevents normal fat digestion

So patients can get watery diarrhea and sometimes steatorrhea (fatty, foul-smelling stool).

---

### Classic clue

Think Zollinger-Ellison syndrome when ulcers are:

* multiple
* recurrent despite treatment
* distal to the duodenal bulb
* associated with diarrhea

Also suspect it if there is very high fasting gastrin with low gastric pH (very acidic stomach).

---

### Important association

Associated with **MEN1** (multiple endocrine neoplasia type 1).

MEN1 classically causes tumors in:

* parathyroid
* pituitary
* pancreas

Gastrinoma is the pancreatic endocrine tumor to remember here.

---

## One-line intuition

A gastrin-secreting tumor turns the stomach acid pump way up, so the patient gets stubborn ulcers and diarrhea.

---

## Pearl

Secretin normally decreases gastrin release, but in Zollinger-Ellison syndrome, secretin paradoxically increases gastrin from the gastrinoma.
