## Bile duct obstruction

### Core idea

Bile duct obstruction means something is blocking bile from flowing from the liver/gallbladder into the duodenum (first part of the small intestine).

Bile = fluid made by the liver that helps digest fat and carries bilirubin (yellow breakdown product from heme) out of the body.

So if bile cannot drain:

* bile backs up into the liver/blood
* bilirubin rises
* fat digestion gets worse

---

### Most common cause

Usually:

* gallstone stuck in the common bile duct

This is called choledocholithiasis:

* choledocho- = common bile duct
* lithiasis = stones

Other causes:

* pancreatic cancer compressing the common bile duct
* cholangiocarcinoma (bile duct cancer)
* stricture (scar-like narrowing)
* primary sclerosing cholangitis, or PSC (chronic inflammation/scarring of bile ducts)

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Jaundice:** conjugated bilirubin backs up into blood, making skin/eyes yellow
* **Dark urine:** conjugated bilirubin is water-soluble, so kidneys pee it out
* **Pale stools:** bilirubin cannot reach gut, so stool loses brown pigment
* **Pruritus:** itching from retained bile salts in skin
* **Steatorrhea:** fatty stools because bile cannot enter gut to absorb fats
* **RUQ pain:** right upper quadrant pain from gallbladder/biliary tract irritation

---

### Labs

Classic obstructive/cholestatic pattern:

* increased direct/conjugated bilirubin
* increased ALP (alkaline phosphatase)
* increased GGT (gamma-glutamyl transferase)

Why ALP/GGT go up:

The bile duct cells are irritated/stretched by backed-up bile, so they release cholestatic enzymes.

---

### Dangerous complication

Obstruction + infection = ascending cholangitis.

Classic Charcot triad:

* fever
* jaundice
* RUQ pain

This is an emergency because infected bile is trapped under pressure.

---

## One-line intuition

Bile duct obstruction is like clogging the liver's drainage pipe: bilirubin and bile salts back up into blood, while bile cannot reach the intestine.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Painless jaundice + palpable gallbladder suggests pancreatic cancer compressing the common bile duct.

This is Courvoisier sign: painless enlarged gallbladder is unlikely to be from gallstones, because chronic gallstones usually make the gallbladder fibrotic and shrunken.
