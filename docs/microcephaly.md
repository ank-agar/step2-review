# Microcephaly

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/birth-defects/media/images/severity_of_microcephaly.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ee/Microcephaly-comparison-500px.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/birth-defects/media/images/Untitleddesign8.png)

Microcephaly means:

* micro- = small
* -cephaly = head

So it literally means:

> abnormally small head.

More specifically, the baby's **head circumference** (distance around the head) is much smaller than expected for age and sex.

---

### Core idea

The skull is small because the **brain is small**.

The skull grows because the brain pushes it outward as the brain develops.

So if the brain does not grow normally:

* less brain growth
* less skull expansion
* small head circumference

---

### Why it happens

Microcephaly can happen from anything that injures or limits fetal brain growth, especially:

* genetic problems
* congenital infections (infections present before birth)
* Zika virus
* cytomegalovirus, or CMV (a congenital viral infection)
* toxoplasmosis (a parasitic infection)
* fetal alcohol exposure
* poor blood supply to the developing brain

---

### What problems can come with it

Because the brain itself may be underdeveloped, babies can have:

* seizures
* developmental delay
* intellectual disability
* feeding difficulty
* hearing or vision problems
* motor problems (movement problems)

Severity varies a lot.

Some mild cases have few issues, but severe microcephaly usually means more neurologic problems.

---

## One-line intuition

Microcephaly is small head size because the brain did not grow enough, and the skull follows the brain's growth.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, think **congenital Zika infection** when you see:

* microcephaly
* intracranial calcifications (calcium deposits inside the skull/brain)
* eye abnormalities
* maternal mosquito exposure
