### Core idea

An anal fissure is a small tear in the lining of the anal canal.

Anal canal = the short tube at the end of the rectum where stool exits.

Think:

> paper cut in a very sensitive area.

That is why it can cause sharp pain even though the tear is small.

---

### Why it happens

Most commonly from trauma to the anal lining, especially:

* hard stool
* constipation
* straining
* chronic diarrhea
* childbirth

Hard stool stretches the anal canal too much, so the delicate mucosa (thin moist lining) splits.

---

### Why it hurts so much

The lower anal canal is somatically innervated.

Somatic innervation = nerves that carry sharp, well-localized pain from body-wall type tissue.

So a fissure causes:

* sharp pain with bowel movements
* burning pain after bowel movements
* bright red blood on toilet paper or stool

Bright red blood means the bleeding is distal/lower GI (gastrointestinal), close to the anus.

---

### Why chronic fissures do not heal

Pain makes the internal anal sphincter spasm.

Internal anal sphincter = smooth muscle ring that helps keep the anus closed.

Spasm causes:

* more tearing/tension
* less blood flow
* slower healing
* more pain

So the cycle is:

> tear → pain → sphincter spasm → decreased blood flow → poor healing → persistent tear

---

### Classic location

Most anal fissures are in the posterior midline.

Posterior midline = back side of the anal canal, around the 6 o'clock position if the patient is lying on their back.

Why there?

That area has relatively poorer blood supply, so it is more vulnerable to tearing and poor healing.

---

### Treatment intuition

Treatment is mainly about making stool soft and relaxing the sphincter.

Common approaches:

* fiber and fluids
* stool softeners
* sitz baths (warm shallow baths for the perineal/anal area)
* topical nitroglycerin or calcium channel blockers
* botulinum toxin in some chronic cases
* lateral internal sphincterotomy if refractory

Lateral internal sphincterotomy = cutting part of the internal anal sphincter to reduce spasm and improve blood flow.

---

## One-line intuition

Anal fissure = a painful tear in the anal lining, usually from hard stool, kept open by sphincter spasm and poor blood flow.

---

## HY Step 1 pearl

Anal fissure causes painful bright red rectal bleeding, while internal hemorrhoids usually cause painless bright red bleeding.
