### Core idea

Varicella zoster virus (VZV) is a herpesvirus (DNA virus family that can stay latent, meaning hidden/inactive, in nerves).

It causes 2 big diseases:

* **chickenpox** = primary infection
* **shingles** = reactivation later

So the same virus gives:

> first exposure = diffuse itchy vesicles  
> later reactivation = painful vesicles in one dermatome

A dermatome is a strip of skin supplied by one sensory nerve.

---

### Why it causes chickenpox first

VZV enters through the respiratory tract, spreads through blood, then reaches the skin.

That is why chickenpox is:

* diffuse
* itchy
* vesicular
* lesions in different stages

"Lesions in different stages" means you can see macules (flat red spots), papules (raised bumps), vesicles (fluid-filled blisters), and crusts at the same time.

---

### Why shingles happens later

After chickenpox, VZV does not fully leave.

It hides in sensory ganglia (clusters of nerve cell bodies), especially:

* dorsal root ganglia (sensory nerve clusters near the spinal cord)
* trigeminal ganglion (sensory nerve cluster for the face)

Years later, if cell-mediated immunity (T-cell immune control) drops, the virus can reactivate.

Then it travels down that nerve to the skin.

That is why shingles is:

* painful before the rash
* unilateral
* dermatomal
* vesicles on an erythematous base (blisters on a red base)

Unilateral means one-sided.

---

### Big complications

Important VZV complications:

* **postherpetic neuralgia** = chronic nerve pain after shingles
* **pneumonia** = lung infection, especially in adults or immunocompromised patients
* **encephalitis** = brain inflammation
* **herpes zoster ophthalmicus** = shingles involving the eye region, usually V1 (ophthalmic branch of trigeminal nerve)

Postherpetic neuralgia makes sense because shingles is nerve reactivation, so the nerve itself can stay damaged/irritated after the rash is gone.

---

## One-line intuition

Varicella zoster virus is the herpes DNA virus that causes chickenpox first, then hides in nerves and can come back years later as painful dermatomal shingles.

---

## Pearl 🔥

VZV is treated with acyclovir (nucleoside analog antiviral), and acyclovir is activated by viral thymidine kinase (viral enzyme that phosphorylates the drug).
