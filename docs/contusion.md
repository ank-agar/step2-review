## Contusion

![Image](https://content.healthwise.net/resources/14.8/en-us/media/medical/hw/h9991363_006.jpg)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19690.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ef/Blood_donation_bruise.jpg/960px-Blood_donation_bruise.jpg)

A contusion is basically a bruise.

It is a closed injury where blunt force damages small blood vessels, but the skin stays intact.

So:

* skin not broken
* capillaries (tiny blood vessels) rupture
* blood leaks into surrounding tissue
* trapped blood causes discoloration, swelling, and tenderness

---

### Core idea

Think:

> blunt trauma + intact skin = blood leaks underneath = bruise/contusion

It is different from a cut/laceration because blood is not escaping out of the body.

Instead, it is trapped under the skin or inside deeper tissue.

---

### Why it changes color

The color changes because RBCs (red blood cells) leak out and then get broken down.

Hemoglobin (oxygen-carrying protein in RBCs) gets degraded into pigments:

* red/purple/blue early = fresh leaked blood
* green = biliverdin
* yellow = bilirubin

That is why old bruises often look yellow-green before fading.

---

### Types

Contusions can happen in different tissues:

* skin contusion = classic bruise
* muscle contusion = deeper painful swelling after a direct blow
* bone contusion = bone bruise
* lung contusion = bruised lung tissue after chest trauma
* brain contusion = bruised brain tissue after head trauma

Same concept:

> tissue gets hit, small vessels rupture, blood leaks locally.

---

## One-line intuition

A contusion is internal bleeding from blunt trauma where the skin stays closed.
