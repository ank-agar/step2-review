### Core idea

Rickets is defective mineralization of growing bone in children.

Mineralization means hardening bone with calcium and phosphate.

So the simple idea:

> bone is being made, but it is too soft because it is not getting hardened correctly.

---

### Most common cause

Usually due to vitamin D deficiency.

Vitamin D helps the gut absorb:

* calcium
* phosphate

These minerals are needed to harden osteoid (new unmineralized bone matrix).

---

### Why the bones bow

In kids, bones are still growing at growth plates (cartilage areas near the ends of long bones where bone length increases).

If the growth plate does not mineralize well:

* it becomes widened and weak
* weight-bearing bones bend under body weight
* legs can become bowed

That is why rickets causes:

* bow legs
* delayed growth
* bone pain
* fractures
* rachitic rosary

Rachitic rosary = bead-like bumps at the costochondral junctions (where ribs meet cartilage).

---

### Labs intuition

Classically:

* low vitamin D
* low/normal calcium
* low phosphate
* high ALP (alkaline phosphatase, enzyme increased with osteoblast/bone-forming activity)
* high PTH (parathyroid hormone, hormone that raises blood calcium)

Why high PTH?

Low calcium makes the parathyroid glands panic, so they release PTH to bring calcium back up.

PTH also makes the kidney waste phosphate, so phosphate drops too.

---

## One-line intuition

Rickets is soft, poorly mineralized growing bone, so the growth plates widen and weight-bearing bones bend.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Rickets is in children because growth plates are still open; the adult version is osteomalacia (soft, poorly mineralized bone after growth plates are closed).
