## Metabolic bone disease

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/Child_with_rickets.svg/3840px-Child_with_rickets.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/99/Bone_remodeling_01.svg/3840px-Bone_remodeling_01.svg.png)

Metabolic bone disease means abnormal bone strength/structure because the body has a problem with:

* minerals
* vitamins
* hormones
* kidney handling of calcium/phosphate
* bone remodeling

So the bone itself is not primarily infected or cancerous.

The problem is the body is giving bone the wrong chemical environment.

---

### Core idea

Bone needs:

* calcium
* phosphate
* vitamin D
* PTH (parathyroid hormone)
* normal osteoblasts (bone-building cells)
* normal osteoclasts (bone-resorbing cells)

If these are off, bone becomes:

* weak
* poorly mineralized
* too resorbed
* fragile
* painful

---

### Big examples

* **Osteoporosis:** low bone mass, but mineralization is normal
* **Osteomalacia:** poorly mineralized bone in adults
* **Rickets:** poorly mineralized growing bone in children
* **Renal osteodystrophy:** bone disease from CKD (chronic kidney disease)
* **Hyperparathyroid bone disease:** too much PTH (parathyroid hormone) increases bone resorption

---

### Why the findings happen

If vitamin D is low:

* less calcium/phosphate absorption from gut
* less mineral gets deposited into osteoid
* osteoid = unmineralized bone matrix

So the skeleton lays down "soft scaffolding," but cannot harden it well.

That gives:

* bone pain
* fractures/pseudofractures
* bowing deformities in children
* muscle weakness

---

### Rickets vs osteomalacia

Same basic problem:

> poor mineralization.

But age changes the appearance.

* **Kids:** growth plates are still open, so poor mineralization causes widened growth plates and bowed legs
* **Adults:** growth plates are closed, so poor mineralization causes bone pain and pseudofractures

Pseudofractures = Looser zones, which are incomplete stress-fracture-like lines seen in osteomalacia.

---

## One-line intuition

Metabolic bone disease is when bones are weak because the body's mineral/hormone system is wrong, not because the bone is primarily infected or malignant.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember:

* **Osteoporosis:** normal calcium, phosphate, alkaline phosphatase
* **Osteomalacia/rickets:** often increased alkaline phosphatase because osteoblasts are trying to build bone, but mineralization is defective

Alkaline phosphatase = enzyme marker of osteoblast activity.
