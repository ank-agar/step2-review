# McArdle disease

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560785/bin/McArdle.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560785/bin/Glycogen__Storage__Disease__V__and__VII__v2.gif)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560785/bin/Vacuolar_myopathy_mcArdle_type_V_glcogenosis.gif)

McArdle disease = glycogen storage disease type V (GSD V), caused by deficiency of **muscle glycogen phosphorylase**.

Muscle glycogen phosphorylase = **myophosphorylase**, the enzyme that breaks down muscle glycogen into glucose-1-phosphate.

So the problem is:

* muscle has stored glycogen
* but cannot break it down well
* especially during exercise

---

### Core idea

Muscle cannot use its own glycogen for quick energy.

Normally:

* glycogen gets broken down
* glucose-1-phosphate enters glycolysis (glucose breakdown pathway)
* muscle makes ATP (adenosine triphosphate, the cell's energy currency)

In McArdle disease:

* glycogen phosphorylase is missing
* glycogen stays trapped in muscle
* ATP production is poor during exertion

So patients get:

* exercise intolerance
* muscle cramps
* fatigue
* myoglobinuria (myoglobin in urine, causing dark urine) after intense exercise

---

### Why lactate does not rise

During exercise, normal muscle breaks glycogen down into glucose, then runs glycolysis.

Glycolysis makes pyruvate, which can become lactate.

But in McArdle disease:

* glycogen cannot enter glycolysis
* less pyruvate is made
* less lactate is made

So after forearm exercise testing:

* **lactate fails to rise**
* ammonia may still rise

This is very testable.

---

### Second wind phenomenon

After a few minutes of exercise, symptoms can improve.

Why?

At first, the muscle wants to use its own stored glycogen, but it cannot.

After continued gentle exercise, blood flow increases and the muscle starts using fuels from the blood, like:

* glucose
* free fatty acids

So the patient gets a "second wind."

---

### Inheritance

McArdle disease is autosomal recessive (both copies of the gene must be abnormal).

The gene is **PYGM** (the gene for muscle glycogen phosphorylase).

---

## One-line intuition

McArdle disease is like having a full gas tank in skeletal muscle, but the muscle cannot open the tank during exercise.

---

## Pearl 🔥

McArdle disease = **muscle cramps + myoglobinuria + no lactate rise with exercise + second wind phenomenon**.
