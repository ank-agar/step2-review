Perniosis, also called **chilblains** or **pernio**, is a cold-induced inflammation of small skin blood vessels.

It usually happens after exposure to:

* cold
* dampness
* non-freezing temperatures

Most common sites:

* fingers
* toes
* ears
* nose

---

### Core idea

Cold makes small blood vessels constrict.

Then when the area warms back up, blood flow returns unevenly and irritates the vessel walls.

That causes:

* inflammation
* swelling
* red-purple bumps
* itching or burning pain

So the intuition is:

> cold exposure injures tiny skin vessels, especially at the tips of the body.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Red/purple lesions:** inflamed superficial blood vessels leak and dilate
* **Itching/burning:** local inflammation irritates skin nerves
* **Swelling:** fluid leaks into surrounding tissue
* **Acral location:** acral means far from the center of the body, like fingers and toes; these areas get cold easily
* **Improves with warmth:** removing the cold trigger lets the vascular inflammation calm down

---

### Important associations

Perniosis can be idiopathic, meaning no clear underlying cause.

But it can also be associated with:

* Raynaud phenomenon (cold/stress-induced color change of fingers or toes from blood vessel spasm)
* systemic lupus erythematosus, or SLE (autoimmune disease that can affect skin, joints, kidneys, and blood)
* low body weight or poor nutrition
* smoking

If someone has recurrent or severe perniosis, think about secondary causes like autoimmune disease.

---

## One-line intuition

Perniosis is basically **cold-triggered small-vessel inflammation in fingers/toes causing itchy, tender red-purple bumps**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do **not** confuse **perniosis/chilblains** with **lupus pernio**.

* Perniosis = cold-induced lesions on acral sites
* Lupus pernio = chronic violaceous facial lesions seen in sarcoidosis
