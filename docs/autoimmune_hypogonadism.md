# Autoimmune hypogonadism

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg/960px-Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93gonadal_axis.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/de/Schematic_figure_of_a_transverse_section_of_the_seminiferous_tubule.jpg)

![Image](https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/583135/xml-images/fimmu-11-583135-g0001.webp)

Autoimmune hypogonadism means the immune system attacks the gonads:

* testes in males
* ovaries in females

Hypogonadism = low gonad function, meaning low sex hormone production and/or impaired gamete production.

Sex hormones:

* testosterone in males
* estrogen/progesterone in females

Gametes = sperm or eggs.

---

### Core idea

The gonad is the problem.

So this is usually **primary hypogonadism**:

* low testosterone or estrogen
* high LH (luteinizing hormone)
* high FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)

Why high LH/FSH?

Because the brain is yelling at a failing gonad.

Normally, sex hormones give negative feedback to the hypothalamus and pituitary gland. If the gonad is damaged, sex hormone levels fall, so that negative feedback is removed.

Result:

* pituitary releases more LH
* pituitary releases more FSH

But the damaged gonad cannot respond well.

---

### What autoimmune attack can cause

In ovaries:

* autoimmune oophoritis = autoimmune inflammation of the ovary
* amenorrhea = no menstrual periods
* infertility
* premature ovarian insufficiency

In testes:

* autoimmune orchitis = autoimmune inflammation of the testis
* low testosterone
* impaired sperm production
* infertility

---

### Important association

Think autoimmune polyendocrine syndromes.

Autoimmune polyendocrine syndrome = multiple endocrine glands being attacked by autoimmunity.

Classically associated with:

* Addison disease = primary adrenal insufficiency
* autoimmune thyroid disease
* type 1 diabetes mellitus
* autoimmune gonadal failure

---

## One-line intuition

Autoimmune hypogonadism is when the immune system damages the ovaries or testes, so sex hormones drop and LH/FSH rise because the pituitary keeps trying to stimulate the failing gonads.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Primary hypogonadism = problem in the gonad, so LH/FSH are high.

Secondary hypogonadism = problem in the hypothalamus or pituitary, so LH/FSH are low or inappropriately normal.
