# Gallbladder cancer

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17493/N00043-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/Diagram_showing_stage_T3_gallbladder_cancer_CRUK_436.svg/250px-Diagram_showing_stage_T3_gallbladder_cancer_CRUK_436.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/19/Gallbladder_adenocarcinoma_%282%29_histopathology.jpg)

Gallbladder cancer is a malignant tumor starting in the gallbladder, which is the small bile-storing sac under the liver.

Most gallbladder cancers are:

* adenocarcinomas (cancers from gland-forming epithelial cells)

Break it down:

* adeno- = gland
* carcinoma = cancer from epithelial cells

So gallbladder adenocarcinoma means:

> gland-forming epithelial cancer of the gallbladder lining.

---

### Core idea

Chronic irritation of the gallbladder lining can cause dysplasia (abnormal pre-cancer cell growth), then carcinoma (invasive epithelial cancer).

Common risk factors:

* gallstones
* chronic cholecystitis (long-term gallbladder inflammation)
* porcelain gallbladder (calcified gallbladder wall)
* primary sclerosing cholangitis, PSC (autoimmune scarring/inflammation of bile ducts)
* older age, female sex

---

### Why symptoms happen

The gallbladder sits right under the liver and drains bile into the biliary tree (duct system carrying bile).

So a tumor can cause:

* RUQ pain (right upper quadrant pain): local gallbladder/liver capsule irritation
* jaundice (yellow skin/eyes): tumor blocks bile drainage
* weight loss: cancer-related inflammation and poor intake
* nausea/vomiting: biliary obstruction and digestive disruption

It is often found late because early gallbladder cancer can look like regular gallstone disease.

---

### Important anatomy intuition

The gallbladder wall has no true submucosa (supportive layer under mucosa).

That matters because cancer has less of a barrier before it reaches deeper layers and the liver.

So gallbladder cancer can spread directly into:

* liver
* bile ducts
* regional lymph nodes (nearby immune drainage stations)

---

## One-line intuition

Gallbladder cancer is usually adenocarcinoma from chronically irritated gallbladder lining, often linked to gallstones and dangerous because it hides until it invades nearby liver/bile ducts.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Porcelain gallbladder = calcified gallbladder wall from chronic inflammation, classically associated with increased risk of gallbladder carcinoma.
