### Core idea

Thyroid storm is severe, life-threatening hyperthyroidism.

Hyperthyroidism means too much thyroid hormone, mainly:

* T3 (triiodothyronine)
* T4 (thyroxine)

Think:

> regular hyperthyroidism = metabolism is too high  
> thyroid storm = metabolism is dangerously out of control

---

### What causes it

Usually someone already has hyperthyroidism, often from Graves disease, then a stressor pushes them over the edge.

Common triggers:

* infection
* surgery
* trauma
* childbirth
* stopping antithyroid drugs
* iodine load, like contrast dye or amiodarone

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Too much T3 (triiodothyronine) and T4 (thyroxine) makes the body hypermetabolic.

That causes:

* fever because cells are burning energy and making heat
* tachycardia (fast heart rate) because thyroid hormone increases beta-adrenergic sensitivity
* sweating because the body is trying to dump heat
* diarrhea because gut motility increases
* agitation, delirium, or coma because the central nervous system is overstimulated
* heart failure or atrial fibrillation (irregularly irregular heart rhythm) because the heart is being overdriven

---

### Labs

Typical thyroid labs:

* low TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
* high free T4 (thyroxine)
* high T3 (triiodothyronine)

TSH is low because the pituitary senses high thyroid hormone and tries to shut the thyroid down.

---

### Treatment logic

Treatment is in order:

* beta-blocker first, like propranolol, to block adrenergic symptoms
* PTU (propylthiouracil) or methimazole to block new thyroid hormone synthesis
* iodine after PTU/methimazole to block thyroid hormone release
* steroids to decrease T4 to T3 conversion and cover possible adrenal insufficiency
* cooling and supportive care

Important: give iodine after PTU (propylthiouracil) or methimazole.

Why?

If you give iodine first, the thyroid can use it as raw material to make more hormone.

---

## One-line intuition

Thyroid storm is when excess thyroid hormone turns the body into an overheated, overstimulated, high-output emergency.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PTU (propylthiouracil) blocks thyroid peroxidase and also blocks peripheral conversion of T4 (thyroxine) to T3 (triiodothyronine), which is why it is classically preferred in thyroid storm.
