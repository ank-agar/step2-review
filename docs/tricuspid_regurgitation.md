## Tricuspid regurgitation

![Image](https://www.mayoclinic.org/-/media/kcms/gbs/patient-consumer/images/2015/01/21/09/05/mcdc7_tricuspid_valve_regurgitation-8col.jpg)

![Image](https://johnsonfrancis.org/professional/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/Colour-Doppler-in-tricuspid-regurgitation.png)

![Image](https://modules.oc4d.org/en-medline_plus/ency/images/ency/fullsize/18091.jpg)

Tricuspid regurgitation means the tricuspid valve is leaky.

The tricuspid valve sits between the:

* right atrium
* right ventricle

Normally:

* blood flows right atrium → right ventricle
* then the right ventricle pumps blood to the lungs

In tricuspid regurgitation:

* the right ventricle contracts
* but the tricuspid valve does not close tightly
* so blood leaks backward into the right atrium

Regurgitation = backward leakage of blood.

---

### Why the findings happen

Backward flow into the right atrium causes pressure to back up into the venous system.

So you can see right-sided congestion:

* jugular venous distension (JVD; bulging neck veins)
* pulsatile liver
* hepatomegaly (enlarged liver)
* peripheral edema (leg swelling)
* ascites (fluid in the abdomen)

It can also cause a systolic murmur because the leak happens during systole, when the ventricle is squeezing.

Systole = the part of the heartbeat when ventricles contract.

---

### Murmur clue

Tricuspid regurgitation causes a:

* holosystolic murmur
* best heard at the left lower sternal border
* louder with inspiration

Holosystolic = heard throughout all of systole.

Why louder with inspiration?

Inspiration increases venous return to the right heart, so more blood enters the right ventricle, causing more regurgitant flow across the tricuspid valve.

---

### Common causes

Big causes to know:

* right ventricular dilation
* pulmonary hypertension
* infective endocarditis, especially in IV drug use
* carcinoid syndrome
* Ebstein anomaly

Pulmonary hypertension = high pressure in the lung circulation.

IV drug use = intravenous drug use.

Carcinoid syndrome = serotonin-secreting neuroendocrine tumor effects that can damage right-sided heart valves.

---

## One-line intuition

Tricuspid regurgitation is a leaky right-sided AV (atrioventricular) valve, so blood shoots backward into the right atrium and backs up into the veins.

---

## HY pearl

Right-sided murmurs, like tricuspid regurgitation, get louder with inspiration because inspiration increases venous return to the right side of the heart.
