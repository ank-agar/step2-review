## Gender dysphoria

![Image](https://www.itspronouncedmetrosexual.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Genderbread-Person-v4.png)

![Image](https://www.itspronouncedmetrosexual.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Genderbread-Person-3.31.jpg)

![Image](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2023/04/12/00/09/transgender-7918518_1280.jpg)

Gender dysphoria is distress caused by a mismatch between:

* gender identity (a person's internal sense of being male, female, both, neither, or another gender)
* sex assigned at birth (the label given at birth, usually based on external anatomy)

Important:

* being transgender (gender identity differs from sex assigned at birth) is not itself a disorder
* the dysphoria part means the distress/impairment

---

### Core idea

The brain's sense of self does not match the body/social role the person was assigned.

So the suffering comes from:

* body features feeling wrong
* being treated as the wrong gender
* puberty causing unwanted secondary sex characteristics (breasts, facial hair, voice deepening, body shape changes)

---

### How it can show up

Someone may have:

* strong desire to be another gender
* strong dislike of their primary sex characteristics (genitals) or secondary sex characteristics (breasts, facial hair, voice)
* desire to be treated as another gender
* anxiety/depression from the mismatch
* social withdrawal or school/work impairment

---

### Why puberty can worsen it

Before puberty, the mismatch may be mostly social.

During puberty, sex hormones cause visible body changes, so the mismatch becomes harder to ignore.

Example:

* estrogen (main female sex hormone) causes breast development
* testosterone (main male sex hormone) causes facial hair and voice deepening

If those changes feel opposite to the person's gender identity, distress can spike.

---

## One-line intuition

Gender dysphoria is not "confusion"; it is clinically significant distress from the body/social gender not matching the person's internal gender identity.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Differentiate gender dysphoria from body dysmorphic disorder (distress over a perceived flaw in appearance). In gender dysphoria, the distress is specifically about gender incongruence (mismatch between experienced gender and assigned sex).
