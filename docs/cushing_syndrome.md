### Core idea

Cushing syndrome = too much cortisol effect in the body.

Cortisol is a glucocorticoid hormone (steroid hormone from the adrenal cortex that helps with stress, glucose, blood pressure, and inflammation).

So think:

> chronic high cortisol = body stuck in "stress hormone mode."

---

### Classic findings

* central obesity
* moon facies (round face)
* buffalo hump (fat pad on upper back/neck)
* thin skin and easy bruising
* purple striae (stretch marks)
* proximal muscle weakness (weak hips/shoulders)
* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* hyperglycemia (high blood glucose)
* infections

---

### Why the findings happen

* **High glucose:** cortisol increases gluconeogenesis (making new glucose), so blood sugar rises
* **Muscle weakness:** cortisol breaks down protein in muscle
* **Thin skin/striae:** cortisol decreases collagen, so skin becomes fragile and tears more easily
* **Central obesity/moon face/buffalo hump:** cortisol redistributes fat toward the trunk, face, and upper back
* **Hypertension:** cortisol increases vascular sensitivity to catecholamines (epinephrine/norepinephrine stress hormones)
* **Infections:** cortisol suppresses immune function

---

### Causes

Cushing syndrome can be from:

* exogenous steroids (steroids given as medication, like prednisone)
* pituitary adenoma making too much ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone)
* adrenal tumor making too much cortisol
* ectopic ACTH production (ACTH made by a non-pituitary tumor, classically small cell lung carcinoma)

ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone) normally tells the adrenal cortex to make cortisol.

---

### Cushing syndrome vs Cushing disease

**Cushing syndrome** = any cause of excess cortisol.

**Cushing disease** = specifically a pituitary adenoma making too much ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone).

So:

* syndrome = broad category
* disease = pituitary cause

---

## One-line intuition

Cushing syndrome is chronic cortisol excess, so the body breaks down protein, raises glucose/BP, suppresses immunity, and redistributes fat to the face/trunk.

---

## Pearl

Most common cause of Cushing syndrome is exogenous glucocorticoids (steroid medications). For Step 1, if ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone) is low, think adrenal/exogenous source; if ACTH is high, think pituitary or ectopic ACTH.
