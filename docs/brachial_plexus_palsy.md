Brachial plexus palsy means weakness/paralysis of the arm because the **brachial plexus** is injured.

Break it down:

* **brachial** = arm
* **plexus** = network of nerves
* **palsy** = weakness/paralysis

So:

> injury to the nerve network that controls the shoulder, arm, forearm, and hand.

---

### Core anatomy

The brachial plexus is made from spinal nerve roots:

* **C5**
* **C6**
* **C7**
* **C8**
* **T1**

These roots combine and branch into nerves that move/sense the upper limb.

So if the plexus is stretched, compressed, or torn, the arm loses motor function and sometimes sensation.

---

### Classic cause

In newborns, this often happens during difficult delivery, especially with:

* shoulder dystocia
* traction on the neck/shoulder

**Shoulder dystocia** = the baby's shoulder gets stuck behind the mother's pubic bone during delivery.

Why this injures the plexus:

* the head/neck may be pulled one way
* the shoulder stays stuck
* that stretches the nerves running from neck to arm

---

### Upper vs lower lesions

Most testable version:

## Erb-Duchenne palsy

This is an upper brachial plexus injury:

* **C5-C6**

Weak muscles include:

* deltoid
* biceps
* supraspinatus/infraspinatus

So the arm rests in the classic:

* adducted shoulder
* internally rotated arm
* extended elbow
* pronated forearm

This is called **waiter's tip** posture.

Intuition:

* biceps is weak → elbow cannot flex well
* deltoid/supraspinatus are weak → shoulder cannot abduct well
* lateral rotators are weak → arm falls into internal rotation

---

## Klumpke palsy

This is a lower brachial plexus injury:

* **C8-T1**

It affects hand muscles more.

Classic finding:

* claw hand

Can also cause **Horner syndrome** if T1 sympathetic fibers are involved.

**Horner syndrome** = ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis.

* **ptosis** = droopy eyelid
* **miosis** = constricted pupil
* **anhidrosis** = decreased sweating

---

### One-line intuition

Brachial plexus palsy is basically a stretched/torn arm nerve network, so the exact arm weakness depends on which roots were injured.

---

## Pearl 🔥

**Erb palsy = C5-C6 = waiter's tip.**

**Klumpke palsy = C8-T1 = claw hand ± Horner syndrome.**
