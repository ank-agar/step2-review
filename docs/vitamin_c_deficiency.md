### Core idea

Vitamin C deficiency causes **scurvy** (disease from low vitamin C).

Vitamin C is needed to make strong **collagen** (main structural protein in skin, blood vessels, gums, bones, and connective tissue).

So the big idea is:

> low vitamin C = weak collagen = bleeding + poor wound healing.

---

### Why collagen gets weak

Vitamin C is a **cofactor** (helper molecule needed for an enzyme to work) for:

* **prolyl hydroxylase** (enzyme that modifies proline in collagen)
* **lysyl hydroxylase** (enzyme that modifies lysine in collagen)

These enzymes do **hydroxylation** (adding an -OH group).

That -OH helps collagen form a stable triple helix and cross-link properly.

Without vitamin C:

* collagen is underhydroxylated
* collagen becomes unstable
* connective tissue becomes fragile

---

### Classic findings

Weak collagen explains:

* bleeding gums
* loose teeth
* poor wound healing
* easy bruising
* petechiae (tiny pinpoint skin bleeds)
* perifollicular hemorrhages (bleeding around hair follicles)
* corkscrew hairs
* bone pain

Blood vessels are basically leaking because their collagen support is weak.

---

### One-line intuition

Vitamin C is like the collagen-strengthening vitamin; without it, vessels, gums, skin, and wounds lose structural support and start bleeding/breaking down.
