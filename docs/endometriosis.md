## Endometriosis

### Core idea

Endometriosis = endometrial-like tissue outside the uterus.

Endometrium = the inner lining of the uterus that normally grows and sheds during the menstrual cycle.

So in endometriosis, that kind of tissue is in places like:

* ovaries
* fallopian tubes
* pelvic peritoneum (thin lining of the abdominal/pelvic cavity)
* uterosacral ligaments
* bowel or bladder surface

---

### Why it causes pain

The ectopic tissue is still hormone-responsive.

Ectopic = in the wrong location.

So during the menstrual cycle, it can:

* thicken
* break down
* bleed
* trigger inflammation

But unlike normal menstrual blood, this blood/tissue has nowhere easy to exit.

That causes:

* chronic pelvic pain
* severe dysmenorrhea (painful periods)
* dyspareunia (pain with sex)
* dyschezia (pain with bowel movements)
* adhesions (scar-like bands between organs)

---

### Why infertility can happen

Inflammation + scarring can distort pelvic anatomy.

For example:

* ovary gets stuck to nearby tissue
* fallopian tube movement is impaired
* egg pickup by the tube becomes harder

So the issue is not just pain; the pelvic organs can become mechanically trapped or distorted.

---

### Classic ovary finding

If endometriosis involves the ovary, it can form an endometrioma.

Endometrioma = ovarian cyst filled with old blood.

This is called a chocolate cyst because old blood looks thick and brown.

---

### Diagnosis

Definitive diagnosis is usually by laparoscopy (camera surgery looking inside the abdomen/pelvis) with biopsy.

Biopsy = taking tissue to look at under a microscope.

Microscopy can show:

* endometrial glands
* endometrial stroma (supportive tissue)
* hemosiderin-laden macrophages

Hemosiderin = iron pigment from old blood.

Macrophages = immune cells that eat debris.

So hemosiderin-laden macrophages basically mean:

> immune cells cleaned up old bleeding.

---

## One-line intuition

Endometriosis is uterus-lining-like tissue in the wrong place, still responding to hormones, so it bleeds cyclically and causes inflammation, pain, scars, and sometimes infertility.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1 pearl: endometriosis is estrogen-dependent, so symptoms often improve with pregnancy, menopause, or ovarian hormone suppression using combined oral contraceptives, progestins, or GnRH agonists.

GnRH = gonadotropin-releasing hormone.
