## Rheumatic heart disease

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/18/Mitr_Stenosis.svg/960px-Mitr_Stenosis.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/97/Rheumatic_heart_disease%2C_gross_pathology_20G0013_lores.jpg)

Rheumatic heart disease is chronic heart valve damage after rheumatic fever.

Rheumatic fever happens after group A Streptococcus pharyngitis, meaning strep throat caused by Streptococcus pyogenes.

Core idea:

* strep throat happens
* the immune system makes antibodies against strep
* those antibodies cross-react with heart tissue
* repeated inflammation scars the valves

This is called molecular mimicry, meaning the bacteria and your own tissue look similar enough that the immune response accidentally attacks both.

---

### What gets damaged

Most commonly:

* mitral valve

Then:

* aortic valve

The valve leaflets become:

* thickened
* fibrotic
* fused at the commissures, meaning the valve edges stick together

This makes the valve opening narrow.

That narrowing is called stenosis.

So chronic rheumatic heart disease classically causes:

* mitral stenosis

---

### Why the murmur happens

The mitral valve sits between:

* left atrium
* left ventricle

In mitral stenosis, blood has trouble moving from the left atrium into the left ventricle during diastole, meaning the filling phase of the heart.

So you hear:

* diastolic rumbling murmur
* opening snap after S2, meaning after the second heart sound

Why opening snap?

The stiff mitral valve suddenly opens under high left atrial pressure, so it makes a snapping sound.

---

### Why symptoms happen

If blood cannot leave the left atrium easily:

* left atrial pressure rises
* blood backs up into the lungs
* pulmonary edema can happen, meaning fluid in the lungs
* shortness of breath develops

Over time, the enlarged left atrium can cause:

* atrial fibrillation, meaning irregular atrial rhythm
* thromboembolism, meaning clots can form and travel

---

## One-line intuition

Rheumatic heart disease is old strep-triggered immune damage that scars the mitral valve until it becomes a tight, stiff doorway.

---
