Abnormal uterine bleeding, or **AUB (abnormal uterine bleeding)**, means uterine bleeding that is abnormal in:

* amount
* timing
* frequency
* duration

So basically:

> bleeding from the uterus that does not follow a normal menstrual pattern.

---

### Core idea

Normal periods happen because hormones make the **endometrium (inner lining of the uterus)** grow, stabilize, then shed.

AUB happens when that process is disrupted by either:

* a structural problem in the uterus
* a hormone/ovulation problem
* a bleeding/clotting problem
* medication or another systemic issue

---

### High-yield causes: PALM-COEIN

Use **PALM-COEIN (classification system for causes of abnormal uterine bleeding)**:

**PALM = structural causes**

* **P:** polyp
* **A:** adenomyosis
* **L:** leiomyoma, aka fibroid
* **M:** malignancy/hyperplasia

**COEIN = non-structural causes**

* **C:** coagulopathy
* **O:** ovulatory dysfunction
* **E:** endometrial
* **I:** iatrogenic, meaning treatment/medication-caused
* **N:** not otherwise classified

---

### Why the bleeding happens

Think of the uterus like a lining that needs two things:

* good structure
* stable hormonal control

If there is a **polyp or fibroid**, the uterine cavity is distorted, so the lining sheds irregularly.

If there is **anovulation (no ovulation)**, there is often unopposed **estrogen (hormone that grows the endometrium)** without enough **progesterone (hormone that stabilizes the endometrium)**.

So the endometrium keeps proliferating, becomes unstable, then breaks down unpredictably:

* irregular bleeding
* heavy bleeding
* prolonged bleeding

---

## One-line intuition

AUB is abnormal period-like bleeding because the uterine lining is either structurally disrupted or hormonally unstable.

---

## Pearl 🔥

On Step 1, chronic **anovulation (no ovulation)** can cause **endometrial hyperplasia (overgrowth of the uterine lining)** because estrogen keeps stimulating the endometrium without progesterone to organize and stabilize it.
