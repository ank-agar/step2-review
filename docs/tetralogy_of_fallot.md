### Core idea

Tetralogy of Fallot is a cyanotic congenital heart disease (blue baby heart defect present from birth) with 4 problems.

Tetralogy = 4 things:

* pulmonary stenosis (narrowing of the path from right ventricle to lungs)
* ventricular septal defect, or VSD (hole between the right and left ventricles)
* overriding aorta (aorta sits over the VSD and receives blood from both ventricles)
* right ventricular hypertrophy, or RVH (thickened right ventricle muscle)

---

### Why the baby turns blue

The key problem is:

* blood has trouble getting to the lungs because of pulmonary stenosis

So right-sided pressure rises, and blood crosses the VSD from right to left.

That means:

* deoxygenated blood (low-oxygen blood) skips the lungs
* enters the aorta
* goes to the body
* causes cyanosis (bluish skin from low oxygen)

One-line intuition:

The right ventricle cannot push blood easily into the lungs, so blood takes the shortcut through the VSD into the body.

---

### Why each finding happens

* **Pulmonary stenosis:** blocks blood flow to the lungs
* **VSD:** gives blood a hole to cross between ventricles
* **Overriding aorta:** aorta is positioned to receive mixed blood from both sides
* **RVH:** right ventricle thickens because it is pumping against obstruction

So the stenosis is what drives the cyanosis.

More pulmonary stenosis = more right-to-left shunting = more cyanosis.

---

### Classic findings

* cyanosis (blue discoloration from low oxygen)
* harsh systolic murmur (murmur during heart contraction), usually from pulmonary stenosis
* boot-shaped heart on chest X-ray
* tet spells (sudden episodes of severe cyanosis)

Tet spells often happen when systemic vascular resistance, or SVR (resistance in the body blood vessels), falls.

Why?

Lower SVR makes it easier for blood to flow right-to-left across the VSD into the aorta instead of going to the lungs.

---

### Squatting helps

Kids with Tetralogy of Fallot may squat during a cyanotic spell.

Squatting increases SVR (systemic vascular resistance).

Higher SVR pushes against right-to-left shunting, so more blood is forced toward the lungs.

So:

* squat = increase SVR
* increase SVR = less right-to-left shunt
* less right-to-left shunt = better oxygenation

---

## One-line intuition

Tetralogy of Fallot is a 4-part heart defect where blocked lung outflow makes blue blood bypass the lungs and enter the body.

---

## HY pearl

Tetralogy of Fallot is associated with DiGeorge syndrome, caused by 22q11 deletion (deletion on chromosome 22), due to abnormal neural crest cell development.
