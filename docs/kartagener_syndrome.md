### Core idea

Kartagener syndrome is **primary ciliary dyskinesia (PCD; defective movement of cilia)** plus **situs inversus (organs reversed left-to-right)**.

Classic triad:

* chronic sinusitis
* bronchiectasis
* situs inversus

---

### Why the findings happen

Cilia are tiny hair-like structures that normally sweep mucus out of the airways.

If cilia do not move properly:

* mucus sits in the sinuses → chronic sinusitis
* mucus sits in bronchi → recurrent infections
* repeated infections damage/dilate bronchi → bronchiectasis

During embryologic development, nodal cilia help set the left-right body axis.

If those cilia are defective:

* left-right positioning becomes random
* about half get situs inversus

---

### Reproductive association

Sperm tails are basically flagella (tail-like motile structures), which use similar movement machinery as cilia.

So defective motility can cause:

* male infertility
* sometimes female subfertility from abnormal fallopian tube cilia

---

## One-line intuition

Kartagener syndrome = broken cilia, so mucus cannot clear and organ laterality can flip.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Kartagener syndrome is usually **autosomal recessive (need two defective copies of a gene)** and classically involves defective **dynein arms (motor proteins that help cilia beat)**.
