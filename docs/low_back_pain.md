### Core idea

Low back pain means pain in the lumbar region (lower back), usually from irritation of:

* muscles
* ligaments (connective tissue that connects bone to bone)
* intervertebral discs (cushions between vertebrae)
* facet joints (small joints between vertebrae)
* nerve roots (nerves exiting the spinal cord)

Most low back pain is mechanical, meaning it comes from strain, posture, movement, joints, or discs rather than a systemic disease.

---

### Why it hurts

The lumbar spine carries a lot of body weight and still has to bend, twist, and stabilize the trunk.

So it is vulnerable to:

* **Muscle strain:** tiny tears/inflammation after lifting, twisting, or overuse
* **Disc herniation:** disc material pushes out and can irritate/compress a nerve root
* **Degenerative disc disease:** disc loses water/height with age, increasing stress on nearby joints
* **Spinal stenosis:** narrowing of the spinal canal, which can compress nerves
* **Facet joint pain:** small spine joints get irritated, especially with extension

Intuition:

> The lower back is both the body’s support column and movement hinge, so it takes a lot of mechanical stress.

---

### Pain pattern clue

If pain stays in the back:

* think muscle strain, ligament strain, disc pain, or facet joint pain

If pain shoots down the leg:

* think radiculopathy (nerve root irritation/compression)

Classic example:

* sciatic pain = pain radiating down the leg from irritation of lumbosacral nerve roots (lower spine nerve roots), often from a herniated disc

---

### Red flags

Low back pain becomes more concerning if there is:

* fever
* cancer history
* unexplained weight loss
* trauma
* IV drug use (intravenous drug use)
* saddle anesthesia (numbness around the groin/butt area)
* urinary retention or bowel/bladder incontinence
* progressive leg weakness

These suggest possible infection, malignancy (cancer), fracture, or cauda equina syndrome (compression of the bundle of nerves at the bottom of the spinal canal).

---

## One-line intuition

Low back pain is usually mechanical stress/inflammation in the lumbar spine, but leg radiation or neurologic deficits point toward nerve root involvement.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Disc herniation classically compresses the nerve root below the disc level:

* L4-L5 disc herniation → L5 nerve root symptoms
* L5-S1 disc herniation → S1 nerve root symptoms
