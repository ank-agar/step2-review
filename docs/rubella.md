Rubella is **German measles**, a mild viral rash illness caused by rubella virus.

Classic adult/child findings:

* low-grade fever
* tender posterior auricular/suboccipital lymphadenopathy
* fine pink maculopapular rash
* rash starts on face, then spreads downward
* Forchheimer spots = small red spots on the soft palate

Maculopapular means:

* macule = flat color change
* papule = raised bump

So a maculopapular rash is a mixed flat + raised red rash.

---

### Core idea

Rubella itself is usually mild.

The big danger is **pregnancy**, especially first trimester infection.

Why?

Rubella can cross the placenta and infect the fetus, causing **CRS (congenital rubella syndrome)**.

---

### Congenital rubella syndrome

Classic triad:

* sensorineural deafness
* cataracts
* PDA (patent ductus arteriosus)

PDA means the ductus arteriosus stays open after birth, causing abnormal blood flow between the aorta and pulmonary artery.

Why these findings happen:

* **Deafness:** rubella damages fetal inner ear development
* **Cataracts:** rubella disrupts fetal lens development
* **PDA:** rubella interferes with normal fetal heart/vessel development

Other possible findings:

* blueberry muffin rash
* microcephaly
* growth restriction
* hepatosplenomegaly

Hepatosplenomegaly means enlarged liver and spleen.

---

### Vaccine

Rubella is prevented by the **MMR vaccine**.

MMR means:

* measles
* mumps
* rubella

It is a live attenuated vaccine, meaning it contains a weakened version of the virus.

Important:

* give before pregnancy if not immune
* do not give during pregnancy because it is live attenuated

---

## One-line intuition

Rubella is a usually mild rash illness, but fetal infection can cause the classic congenital triad: **deafness + cataracts + PDA**.
