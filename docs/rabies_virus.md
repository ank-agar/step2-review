## Rabies virus

Rabies virus is a bullet-shaped, enveloped, negative-sense ssRNA virus (single-stranded RNA virus) in the Rhabdoviridae family.

It causes fatal encephalitis (brain inflammation), usually after an infected animal bite.

---

### Core idea

Rabies is scary because it travels through nerves to the brain.

Basic path:

* animal bite deposits virus in muscle/tissue
* virus binds near the neuromuscular junction (where nerve meets muscle)
* enters peripheral nerves
* travels retrograde up axons toward the CNS (central nervous system)
* infects brain
* spreads to salivary glands so it can be transmitted in saliva

Retrograde means:

> moving backward along the nerve toward the cell body/spinal cord/brain.

---

### Why symptoms happen

Rabies mainly targets neurons (nerve cells), especially in the brain.

That causes:

* fever/headache early: nonspecific viral prodrome (early infection symptoms)
* paresthesia at bite site: nerve irritation near the wound
* agitation/confusion: encephalitis affecting behavior circuits
* hydrophobia: painful throat/pharyngeal spasms when trying to swallow water
* hypersalivation: brainstem dysfunction plus difficulty swallowing saliva
* coma/death: progressive CNS (central nervous system) failure

Hydrophobia does not mean the virus "hates water."

It means swallowing triggers painful spasms, so the patient appears afraid of water.

---

### Classic pathology finding

Rabies forms Negri bodies.

Negri bodies are eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions (pink/red viral clumps inside the cell cytoplasm) in neurons.

High-yield locations:

* hippocampus
* cerebellar Purkinje cells

So if Step 1 gives:

* animal bite
* encephalitis
* hydrophobia
* eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions in neurons

think rabies.

---

### Prevention/treatment idea

Before symptoms:

* wound washing
* HRIG (human rabies immune globulin)
* rabies vaccine

HRIG gives immediate passive antibodies.

Vaccine makes your own immune system produce antibodies.

After symptoms start, rabies is almost always fatal.

That is why PEP (post-exposure prophylaxis, meaning prevention after exposure) is urgent.

---

## One-line intuition

Rabies is a bullet-shaped RNA virus that enters through a bite, crawls backward up nerves to the brain, then causes fatal encephalitis and spreads to saliva.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Rabies virus is a negative-sense RNA virus, so it must carry its own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (enzyme that copies RNA from an RNA template) inside the virion.
