### Core idea

Anthrax is an infection caused by **Bacillus anthracis**, a:

* gram-positive rod
* spore-forming bacterium
* encapsulated bacterium

The spores are the key: they survive in soil/animal products for a long time, then enter humans through skin, lungs, or GI tract (gastrointestinal tract).

---

### Why it causes disease

Bacillus anthracis has 2 big virulence factors:

* **poly-D-glutamate capsule** = blocks phagocytosis (immune cells eating bacteria)
* **toxins** = cause edema (swelling), necrosis (tissue death), and systemic shock

The toxin system has 3 parts:

* **PA (protective antigen)** = helps toxins enter cells
* **EF (edema factor)** = increases cAMP (cyclic adenosine monophosphate) → edema
* **LF (lethal factor)** = tissue injury/cell death

Think:

> PA is the doorway, EF causes swelling, LF causes death.

---

### Classic presentations

**Cutaneous anthrax** is most common:

* painless papule → vesicle → black eschar (dead scab)
* lots of surrounding edema

**Inhalational anthrax** is the scary one:

* flu-like symptoms first
* then severe respiratory distress/shock
* classically causes widened mediastinum from hemorrhagic mediastinitis (bleeding/inflammation in the central chest)

**GI anthrax** can cause:

* abdominal pain
* vomiting
* bloody diarrhea

---

## One-line intuition

Anthrax is a spore-forming gram-positive rod whose capsule helps it hide from immune cells, while its toxins cause massive edema and tissue destruction.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Bacillus anthracis is classically described as **nonhemolytic on blood agar** and has a **Medusa head colony** appearance.
