# Premature rupture of membranes

![Image](https://leaflets.ekhuft.nhs.uk/s3/assets/waters-breaking-with-labels-1733826953.jpg)

![Image](https://healthlibrary.vidanthealth.com/RelatedItems/533882.img)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/ba/Positive_Fern_Test.jpg/250px-Positive_Fern_Test.jpg)

Premature rupture of membranes means the amniotic sac breaks before labor starts.

The amniotic sac is the fluid-filled membrane around the fetus.

So basically:

* membrane breaks
* amniotic fluid leaks out through the vagina
* but true labor has not started yet

---

### Important wording

PROM (premature rupture of membranes) = rupture before labor.

PPROM (preterm premature rupture of membranes) = rupture before labor and before 37 weeks gestation (pregnancy age).

So:

* PROM = timing relative to labor
* PPROM = timing relative to labor + too early in pregnancy

---

### Why this matters

The membranes normally act like a sealed protective barrier.

Once they rupture:

* bacteria can ascend from the vagina into the uterus
* amniotic fluid can decrease
* labor may start early

This is why PROM raises risk of:

* chorioamnionitis (infection of the amniotic fluid and fetal membranes)
* preterm birth
* umbilical cord compression
* placental abruption (placenta separates from uterus too early)

---

### Classic presentation

Patient says:

* sudden painless gush of fluid
* continuous trickle of clear fluid
* fluid leaking from vagina before contractions

Key idea:

> Water breaks before labor begins.

---

### Diagnosis clues

On speculum exam (looking into the vagina/cervix with a speculum), you may see pooling of fluid in the vagina.

Tests can show:

* nitrazine test positive because amniotic fluid is more alkaline (basic) than normal vaginal fluid
* ferning on microscopy because dried amniotic fluid forms a fern-like crystallization pattern

---

## One-line intuition

PROM is when the protective water balloon around the baby pops too early, creating a route for infection and often triggering early delivery.

---

## Pearl 🔥

After PROM, avoid digital cervical exams (checking the cervix with fingers) unless delivery is imminent, because they increase infection risk.
