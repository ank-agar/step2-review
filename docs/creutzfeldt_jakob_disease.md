# Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17146.jpg)

![Image](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/genoms-brains.jpg)

![Image](https://case.edu/medicine/pathology/sites/default/files/2020-10/Brain%20MRI%202.png)

Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease, or **CJD (Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease)**, is a fatal **prion disease**.

A **prion** is an infectious misfolded protein. It is not a bacteria, virus, fungus, or parasite.

Core idea:

* normal prion protein misfolds
* misfolded prion protein makes other prion proteins misfold too
* misfolded proteins accumulate in the brain
* neurons die
* the brain develops tiny holes, called **spongiform change**

Spongiform means "sponge-like."

So CJD is basically:

> misfolded protein spreads its bad shape to other proteins, causing rapidly progressive brain degeneration.

---

### Classic presentation

CJD causes **rapidly progressive dementia**.

Meaning:

* memory/cognition worsens quickly
* neurologic deficits appear over weeks to months
* death usually occurs within months

Common findings:

* dementia
* myoclonus, meaning sudden brief muscle jerks
* ataxia, meaning poor coordination
* visual problems
* akinetic mutism late, meaning awake-looking but not moving or speaking

---

### Why the findings happen

The prions damage gray matter in the brain.

That explains:

* **dementia:** cortical neuron damage
* **myoclonus:** irritated/damaged cortex causes abnormal motor firing
* **ataxia:** cerebellar involvement disrupts coordination
* **spongiform brain tissue:** neurons die and leave vacuoles, meaning small empty spaces

Think:

> protein misfolding chain reaction -> brain tissue becomes sponge-like -> rapidly progressive dementia.

---

### Diagnosis clues

High-yield clues include:

* **MRI (magnetic resonance imaging):** cortical ribboning or basal ganglia hyperintensity
* **EEG (electroencephalogram):** periodic sharp wave complexes
* **CSF (cerebrospinal fluid):** 14-3-3 protein can be positive
* definitive diagnosis is brain biopsy/autopsy

**Cortical ribboning** means the cortex lights up in a ribbon-like pattern on MRI.

---

### Forms

Most cases are sporadic, meaning they happen randomly.

Other forms:

* familial CJD: inherited PRNP gene mutation
* iatrogenic CJD: transmitted by medical exposure, like contaminated neurosurgical instruments
* variant CJD: linked to bovine spongiform encephalopathy, also called mad cow disease

PRNP means prion protein gene.

---

## One-line intuition

CJD is a rapidly fatal dementia where a misfolded protein makes other proteins misfold, destroying the brain into a sponge-like pattern.
