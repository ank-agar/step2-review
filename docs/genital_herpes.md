# Genital herpes

Genital herpes is a sexually transmitted infection caused by HSV (herpes simplex virus), usually:

* HSV-2 (herpes simplex virus type 2)
* sometimes HSV-1 (herpes simplex virus type 1), especially from oral-genital contact

Classic finding:

* painful grouped vesicles on an erythematous base

Vesicles = small fluid-filled blisters.

Erythematous = red/inflamed.

---

### Core idea

HSV infects genital mucosa or abraded skin, causes painful blisters/ulcers, then hides in sensory nerve ganglia.

For genital herpes, it classically becomes latent in the sacral dorsal root ganglia.

Dorsal root ganglia = clusters of sensory nerve cell bodies near the spinal cord.

Latency = virus is present but mostly dormant.

---

### Why it recurs

HSV is a DNA virus that can travel up sensory nerves after the first infection.

Then later, triggers can reactivate it:

* stress
* illness/fever
* immunosuppression
* local trauma

When it reactivates, it travels back down the nerve to the skin/mucosa and causes another outbreak.

So the intuition is:

> herpes does not just live on the skin; it hides in nerves, then comes back to the same general area.

---

### Symptoms

First episode may cause:

* painful genital vesicles/ulcers
* dysuria (pain with urination)
* tender inguinal lymphadenopathy (swollen groin lymph nodes)
* fever, malaise, myalgias

Myalgias = muscle aches.

Recurrent episodes are usually:

* milder
* shorter
* often preceded by tingling/burning

That tingling/burning before lesions is called a prodrome.

---

### Transmission

HSV spreads by direct contact with infected skin, mucosa, or secretions.

Important:

* transmission can happen even without visible sores
* this is called asymptomatic shedding

Asymptomatic shedding = virus is being released from skin/mucosa even though the person has no symptoms.

---

### Diagnosis

Best test if lesions are present:

* PCR (polymerase chain reaction) swab from the lesion

PCR detects viral DNA.

Tzanck smear can show multinucleated giant cells, but it is older and less specific.

Multinucleated giant cells = infected cells fused together with multiple nuclei.

---

### Treatment

Treat with:

* acyclovir
* valacyclovir
* famciclovir

These inhibit viral DNA polymerase.

Viral DNA polymerase = the enzyme HSV uses to copy its DNA.

Why it helps:

* less viral DNA copying
* shorter outbreaks
* less shedding/transmission risk if used suppressively

---

## One-line intuition

Genital herpes is painful recurrent genital vesicles because HSV hides in sacral sensory nerves and periodically reactivates back to the skin.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Acyclovir is activated by viral thymidine kinase, so HSV strains that lose thymidine kinase can become acyclovir-resistant.
