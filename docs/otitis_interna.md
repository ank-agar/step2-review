## Otitis interna

Otitis interna = inflammation/infection of the inner ear.

Basically:

* otitis externa = outer ear canal
* otitis media = middle ear
* otitis interna = inner ear

The inner ear contains the:

* cochlea, which helps with hearing
* vestibular system, which helps with balance

So when the inner ear gets inflamed, you get problems with:

* hearing
* balance

---

### Core symptoms

Otitis interna can cause:

* vertigo, which means a spinning sensation
* nausea/vomiting
* loss of balance
* tinnitus, which means ringing in the ear
* hearing loss
* sometimes nystagmus, which means involuntary repetitive eye movements

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The inner ear is like a tiny sensor system.

The semicircular canals detect head rotation.

The cochlea detects sound.

So inflammation messes up those signals:

* bad vestibular signals → vertigo, nausea, imbalance, nystagmus
* bad cochlear signals → hearing loss and tinnitus

That is why inner ear disease feels more like “the room is spinning” than just “my ear hurts.”

---

### Labyrinthitis connection

Otitis interna is often discussed as labyrinthitis.

Labyrinthitis = inflammation of the labyrinth, which is the inner ear structure containing the cochlea and vestibular organs.

If both balance and hearing are affected, think labyrinthitis/otitis interna.

If only balance is affected with no hearing loss, think vestibular neuritis, which is inflammation of the vestibular nerve.

---

## One-line intuition

Otitis interna is an inner ear infection, so it disrupts the ear’s hearing-and-balance machinery.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 clue: inner ear inflammation can cause vertigo + hearing loss, while vestibular neuritis usually causes vertigo without hearing loss because the cochlea is spared.
