### Core idea

Erythema nodosum is inflammation of the subcutaneous fat, especially on the anterior shins.

Subcutaneous = under the skin.

Panniculitis = inflammation of fat tissue.

So erythema nodosum is basically:

> tender red nodules because the fat under the skin is inflamed.

---

### What it looks like

Classically:

* painful/tender red bumps
* usually on both shins
* may turn bruise-like as they heal
* usually does not ulcerate or scar

Why no ulceration?

Because the main problem is deep in the fat layer, not destruction of the surface epidermis (outer skin layer).

---

### Why it happens

It is usually a hypersensitivity reaction.

Hypersensitivity = the immune system is overreacting to some trigger.

Common triggers:

* strep throat
* sarcoidosis
* tuberculosis
* inflammatory bowel disease
* pregnancy
* oral contraceptive pills
* sulfonamide drugs

The immune system reacts to the trigger, and the inflammation shows up in the septa of subcutaneous fat.

Septa = connective tissue walls between fat lobules.

That is why pathology shows:

* septal panniculitis
* no true vasculitis

Vasculitis = inflammation of blood vessels.

---

### One-line intuition

Erythema nodosum is an immune reaction that makes the fat under the shin skin inflamed, causing tender red bumps.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Erythema nodosum + bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy + ankle arthritis suggests Lofgren syndrome, an acute form of sarcoidosis.

Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy = enlarged lymph nodes at both lung hila, where the bronchi and vessels enter the lungs.
