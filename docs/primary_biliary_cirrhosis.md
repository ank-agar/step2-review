# Primary biliary cirrhosis

![Image](https://www.mayoclinic.org/-/media/kcms/gbs/patient-consumer/images/2026/02/20/18/48/primary-biliary-cholangitis-3150922-004-2-8col.jpg)

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/18244/N01647-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/Primary_biliary_cirrhosis_low_mag.jpg/960px-Primary_biliary_cirrhosis_low_mag.jpg)

Primary biliary cirrhosis is now called **primary biliary cholangitis**.

It is an autoimmune disease where the body attacks the **small intrahepatic bile ducts** (small bile ducts inside the liver).

Break the name apart:

* primary = not caused by another obstruction
* biliary = involving bile ducts
* cholangitis = inflammation of bile ducts
* cirrhosis = scarring of the liver

---

### Core idea

The immune system destroys small bile ducts, so bile cannot drain well.

That causes **cholestasis** (impaired bile flow), which leads to:

* bile buildup in liver
* inflammation
* fibrosis (scar tissue formation)
* eventual cirrhosis

---

### Why the findings happen

* **High ALP (alkaline phosphatase):** bile duct injury/cholestasis pattern
* **High GGT (gamma-glutamyl transferase):** supports biliary tract injury
* **Positive AMA (anti-mitochondrial antibody):** classic autoimmune marker
* **High IgM (immunoglobulin M):** commonly increased in PBC (primary biliary cholangitis)
* **Pruritus (itching):** bile salts build up in skin
* **Fatigue:** common autoimmune/cholestatic symptom
* **Xanthomas (yellow cholesterol deposits):** impaired bile flow causes lipid abnormalities

---

### Classic patient

Think:

* middle-aged woman
* itching + fatigue
* elevated ALP (alkaline phosphatase)
* positive AMA (anti-mitochondrial antibody)

---

## One-line intuition

PBC (primary biliary cholangitis) is autoimmune destruction of tiny bile ducts, causing bile backup and slow liver scarring.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PBC (primary biliary cholangitis) is associated with other autoimmune diseases, especially **Sjögren syndrome** (autoimmune dry eyes and dry mouth).
