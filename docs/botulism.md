## Botulism

### Core idea

Botulism is **flaccid paralysis** caused by botulinum toxin from **Clostridium botulinum**.

Clostridium botulinum is a:

* gram-positive rod
* anaerobe (grows without oxygen)
* spore-former

The toxin blocks release of **ACh (acetylcholine)** at the **NMJ (neuromuscular junction)**.

So the muscle does not get the “contract” signal.

Result:

* weakness
* hypotonia (low muscle tone)
* flaccid paralysis

---

### Mechanism

Normally:

* nerve terminal releases ACh (acetylcholine)
* ACh binds muscle receptors
* muscle contracts

Botulinum toxin cleaves **SNARE proteins (soluble NSF attachment protein receptor proteins)**.

SNARE proteins help vesicles fuse with the presynaptic membrane so ACh can be released.

So:

> botulinum toxin cuts the vesicle-fusion machinery → no ACh release → no muscle contraction.

That is why botulism causes **flaccid paralysis**, not spasticity.

---

### Classic symptoms

Botulism causes **descending paralysis**:

* cranial nerves first
* then arms/trunk
* then respiratory muscles

Classic findings:

* diplopia (double vision)
* ptosis (droopy eyelids)
* dysphagia (trouble swallowing)
* dysarthria (trouble speaking)
* dry mouth
* constipation
* weakness with normal sensation

Mental status is usually normal because this is mainly a peripheral nerve terminal problem.

---

### Infant botulism

Infant botulism happens when spores are ingested and then produce toxin in the gut.

Classic source:

* honey

Classic presentation:

* constipation first
* poor feeding
* weak cry
* “floppy baby” from hypotonia (low tone)

This is why infants under 1 year should not get honey.

---

### Treatment idea

Botulism is a medical emergency because respiratory muscles can fail.

Treatment is:

* antitoxin
* respiratory support if needed
* wound care/debridement if wound botulism

Antitoxin blocks circulating toxin, but it does not instantly reverse toxin already bound to nerve terminals.

---

## One-line intuition

Botulism = botulinum toxin blocks ACh (acetylcholine) release at the NMJ (neuromuscular junction), so muscles cannot contract → descending flaccid paralysis.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Botulinum toxin and tetanus toxin both cleave SNARE proteins, but:

* botulinum blocks ACh (acetylcholine) release at the NMJ (neuromuscular junction) → flaccid paralysis
* tetanus blocks GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid) and glycine release from inhibitory interneurons → spastic paralysis
