# Hypothyroidism

### Core idea

Hypothyroidism = too little thyroid hormone.

Thyroid hormone mainly helps keep the body’s metabolic “speed” up, so low thyroid hormone makes everything slow down.

---

### Key hormone pattern

Most Step 1 cases are **primary hypothyroidism**:

* thyroid gland fails
* T3/T4 are low
* pituitary responds by increasing TSH

TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone) is the pituitary hormone that tells the thyroid to make thyroid hormone.

T3/T4 (triiodothyronine/thyroxine) are the thyroid hormones.

So the classic lab pattern is:

* low free T4
* high TSH

Why?

Because low T4 removes negative feedback, so the pituitary keeps yelling at the thyroid with more TSH.

---

### Symptoms

Think “low metabolism”:

* fatigue
* weight gain
* cold intolerance
* constipation
* bradycardia (slow heart rate)
* dry skin
* hair loss
* slowed reflexes
* depression/brain fog
* heavy menstrual bleeding

The delayed relaxation phase of deep tendon reflexes is a classic testable clue.

---

### Common causes

In the US, the most common cause is:

* Hashimoto thyroiditis

Hashimoto thyroiditis = autoimmune destruction of the thyroid.

Autoimmune means the immune system attacks self tissue.

High-yield antibodies:

* anti-TPO antibodies
* anti-thyroglobulin antibodies

TPO (thyroid peroxidase) is the enzyme needed to organify iodine and build thyroid hormone.

---

### Why the thyroid can get big

If the thyroid is failing, TSH rises.

TSH does two things:

* stimulates thyroid hormone production
* stimulates thyroid growth

So chronic high TSH can cause a goiter (enlarged thyroid), even though thyroid hormone output is low.

---

## One-line intuition

Hypothyroidism is the body running in “low power mode” because there is not enough thyroid hormone.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In primary hypothyroidism:

* TSH is high
* free T4 is low

In secondary hypothyroidism, the pituitary fails, so TSH is low or inappropriately normal.
