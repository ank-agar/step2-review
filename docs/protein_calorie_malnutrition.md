## Protein-calorie malnutrition

![Image](https://med.libretexts.org/@api/deki/files/23166/clipboard_e369040ccdf64076d6637e134c30f3220.png?revision=1&size=bestfit&width=471&height=323)

![Image](https://med.libretexts.org/@api/deki/files/23165/clipboard_e553f55b7cdba1e34d237ae34396ed98d.png?revision=1)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/47/Starved_girl.jpg)

Protein-calorie malnutrition means the body is not getting enough:

* protein
* calories
* often other nutrients too

It is also called **protein-energy malnutrition**.

---

### Two classic forms

### 1. Marasmus

Marasmus = severe lack of **total calories and protein**.

So the body thinks:

> “I have no fuel coming in, so I need to burn my own fat and muscle.”

That causes:

* severe wasting
* very thin appearance
* loss of subcutaneous fat (fat under the skin)
* muscle wasting
* growth failure

Intuition:

Marasmus = **overall starvation**.

---

### 2. Kwashiorkor

Kwashiorkor = especially severe **protein deficiency**, often with relatively more carbohydrate intake.

Classic findings:

* edema (fluid swelling)
* swollen belly
* fatty liver
* flaky skin/dermatitis (skin inflammation)
* thin or discolored hair
* poor growth

---

### Why does low protein cause edema?

Protein is needed to make **albumin** (major blood protein that holds water inside blood vessels).

Low protein intake →
low albumin →
low oncotic pressure (protein “pull” that keeps fluid in blood vessels) →
fluid leaks into tissues →
edema.

That is why kwashiorkor can look “puffy” even though the patient is malnourished.

---

### Why fatty liver?

The liver needs protein to make apolipoproteins (proteins that help export fat from the liver).

Low protein →
can’t package/export fat well →
fat builds up in liver →
fatty liver.

---

## One-line intuition

Protein-calorie malnutrition is starvation on a spectrum: **marasmus is dry wasting**, while **kwashiorkor is protein-starvation with edema**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 distinction:

* **Marasmus:** low calories + low protein → severe wasting, no edema
* **Kwashiorkor:** low protein → low albumin → edema + fatty liver
