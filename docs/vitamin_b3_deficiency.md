Vitamin B3 deficiency = **niacin deficiency**.

Severe niacin deficiency causes **pellagra**.

Classic **4 D’s**:

* dermatitis
* diarrhea
* dementia
* death, if untreated

---

### Core idea

Niacin is used to make:

* **NAD+** (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide)
* **NADP+** (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate)

These are electron carriers used in metabolism.

So if vitamin B3 is low, cells have trouble making energy and doing repair.

---

### Why the findings happen

High-turnover or high-energy tissues get hit hardest:

* **Skin:** photosensitive dermatitis, especially on sun-exposed areas
* **GI tract** (gastrointestinal tract): diarrhea because intestinal lining cells are injured
* **Brain:** confusion, irritability, dementia-like symptoms because neurons are energy-sensitive

The skin rash is classically:

* bilateral and symmetric
* worse in sun-exposed areas
* hyperpigmented, rough, and scaly

Classic named rash:

* **Casal necklace** = hyperpigmented rash around the neck

---

### Important causes

Think of anything that lowers niacin or its precursor **tryptophan** (amino acid used to make niacin):

* poor diet or severe malnutrition
* alcoholism
* malabsorption
* Hartnup disease
* carcinoid syndrome
* isoniazid

Why carcinoid syndrome?

Tryptophan gets diverted into making **serotonin** (neurotransmitter), so less tryptophan is available to make niacin.

Why Hartnup disease?

Defective neutral amino acid transport means decreased tryptophan absorption, so less niacin can be made.

---

## One-line intuition

Vitamin B3 deficiency means low niacin → low NAD+/NADP+ → energy failure in skin, gut, and brain → dermatitis, diarrhea, dementia.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Pellagra can be triggered by **carcinoid syndrome** because tryptophan is “stolen” to make serotonin instead of niacin.
