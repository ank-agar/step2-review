## Pancytopenia

![Image](https://kids.kiddle.co/images/thumb/f/f0/Hematopoiesis_simple.svg/800px-Hematopoiesis_simple.svg.png)

![Image](https://www.immunology.org/sites/default/files/inline-images/Bone-marrow-figure-4.jpg)

![Image](https://www.immunology.org/sites/default/files/inline-images/Bone-marrow-figure-1.jpg)

Pancytopenia means decreased levels of all 3 major blood cell lines:

* RBCs (red blood cells)
* WBCs (white blood cells)
* platelets

Break it down:

* pan- = all
* cyto- = cells
* -penia = low amount

So:

> pancytopenia = low all blood cells.

---

### Core idea

If all 3 cell lines are low, think:

> the problem is probably at the bone marrow level.

Why?

Because RBCs (red blood cells), WBCs (white blood cells), and platelets all come from hematopoietic stem cells (blood-forming stem cells) in the bone marrow.

So if the marrow gets damaged, crowded out, or suppressed, all the downstream blood cells can drop together.

---

### What symptoms happen

Each low cell line gives different symptoms:

* Low RBCs (red blood cells) → anemia, fatigue, pallor, shortness of breath
* Low WBCs (white blood cells), especially neutrophils (bacteria-fighting white blood cells) → infections
* Low platelets → bleeding, bruising, petechiae (tiny red-purple skin spots from bleeding)

---

### Big causes to think about

Pancytopenia can happen from:

* aplastic anemia = empty/hypocellular bone marrow that cannot make blood cells
* leukemia = malignant WBC precursors crowd out normal marrow
* myelodysplastic syndrome = abnormal marrow makes defective blood cells
* megaloblastic anemia = impaired DNA synthesis causes ineffective blood cell production
* chemotherapy/radiation = kills rapidly dividing marrow cells
* hypersplenism = enlarged spleen sequesters/traps blood cells

---

### Why megaloblastic anemia can cause pancytopenia

In megaloblastic anemia, DNA synthesis is impaired.

That means rapidly dividing bone marrow precursors cannot divide normally.

So the marrow tries to make cells, but many precursors die inside the marrow before becoming mature blood cells.

That is called ineffective hematopoiesis (ineffective blood cell production).

Result:

* low RBCs
* low WBCs
* low platelets

---

## One-line intuition

Pancytopenia means the blood cell factory is failing, blocked, poisoned, or being over-cleared.

---

## HY pearl

Aplastic anemia classically causes pancytopenia with a hypocellular bone marrow, meaning the marrow is mostly fat instead of blood-forming cells.
