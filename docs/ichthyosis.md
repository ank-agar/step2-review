### Core idea

Ichthyosis is a group of skin disorders where the skin becomes:

* very dry
* thick
* scaly

Think: **fish-scale skin**.

The problem is usually in the **stratum corneum** (outermost dead keratin layer of the epidermis), where dead skin cells do not shed normally.

---

### Why the skin gets scaly

Normally:

* skin cells mature
* move upward
* die
* shed off smoothly

In ichthyosis:

* shedding is impaired
* dead keratinized cells build up
* the outer layer becomes thick and dry

So the scale is basically **retained dead keratin layer**.

---

### Classic form

**Ichthyosis vulgaris** is the most common inherited form.

Often due to mutation in **FLG** (filaggrin gene).

Filaggrin helps make the skin barrier and keeps moisture in.

So if filaggrin is defective:

* weak skin barrier
* water escapes
* skin becomes dry
* scales accumulate

---

### What it looks like

Usually worse on:

* legs
* arms
* trunk

Often spares:

* skin folds

Can be associated with:

* atopic dermatitis (eczema)
* asthma
* allergic rhinitis

because a weak skin barrier predisposes to allergic disease.

---

## One-line intuition

Ichthyosis = the outer dead skin layer does not shed properly, so dry keratin piles up into fish-like scales.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: **ichthyosis vulgaris = FLG (filaggrin gene) mutation**, causing impaired epidermal barrier and increased risk of atopic dermatitis.
