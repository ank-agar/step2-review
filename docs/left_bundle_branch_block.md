## Left bundle branch block

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ae/Left_bundle_branch_block_ECG_characteristics.svg/960px-Left_bundle_branch_block_ECG_characteristics.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/05/ConductionsystemoftheheartwithouttheHeart.png/1280px-ConductionsystemoftheheartwithouttheHeart.png)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482167/bin/StatPearls__ECG.jpg)

![Image](https://www.wikidoc.org/images/8/89/LBBB04.jpg)

Left bundle branch block (LBBB) means the left bundle branch of the heart's electrical conduction system is blocked/delayed.

So the impulse cannot quickly travel down the normal fast pathway to activate the left ventricle.

Instead:

* right ventricle activates first
* then the electrical signal slowly spreads cell-to-cell toward the left ventricle

That slow spread makes ventricular depolarization take longer.

Depolarization = electrical activation/contraction setup.

---

### ECG idea

ECG (electrocardiogram) shows:

* wide QRS complex
* QRS duration greater than 120 ms
* broad/notched R waves in lateral leads
* deep S waves in V1

QRS complex = ventricular depolarization on ECG.

Why wide QRS?

Because the ventricles are no longer activated through both bundle branches quickly at the same time. The left ventricle gets activated late, so the QRS spreads out.

---

### One-line intuition

LBBB = the left ventricle gets the electrical signal late, so ventricular activation is slow and the QRS gets wide.

---

### Why it matters

LBBB often suggests underlying heart disease, like:

* hypertension-induced heart disease
* coronary artery disease
* cardiomyopathy
* aortic stenosis

It can also make myocardial infarction (MI, heart attack) harder to diagnose on ECG because the abnormal depolarization also causes abnormal repolarization.

Repolarization = electrical reset after contraction.

---

## Pearl

LBBB has a classic ECG pattern:

* broad/notched R waves in lateral leads I, aVL, V5, V6
* deep S wave in V1

Quick memory:

> WiLLiaM = LBBB has a W-like pattern in V1 and M-like pattern in V6.
