### Core idea

Ehrlichiosis is a tick-borne bacterial infection.

Classic Step 1 organism:

* **Ehrlichia chaffeensis**

It is transmitted mainly by the:

* **lone star tick** (*Amblyomma americanum*)

And it likes to infect:

* **monocytes** (a type of white blood cell)

---

### What happens

The bacteria enter white blood cells and live inside them.

Inside the cell, they form little clusters called:

* **morulae** (mulberry-like clusters of bacteria inside a cell)

So on a blood smear, the key clue is:

* morulae inside monocytes

Think:

> Ehrlichia = intracellular bacteria hiding inside monocytes.

---

### Symptoms

Usually presents like a vague flu-like illness after a tick bite:

* fever
* headache
* muscle aches
* malaise (feeling very tired/unwell)

Labs can show:

* leukopenia (low white blood cells)
* thrombocytopenia (low platelets)
* elevated LFTs (liver function tests)

---

### Why the labs happen

The organism infects immune cells and causes systemic inflammation.

So:

* infected white cells get damaged -> leukopenia
* inflammation/consumption affects platelets -> thrombocytopenia
* systemic infection stresses the liver -> elevated LFTs (liver function tests)

---

### Diagnosis/treatment

Diagnosis can be by:

* PCR (polymerase chain reaction) testing
* blood smear showing morulae

Treatment:

* **doxycycline**

Even in kids, doxycycline is used for serious suspected tick-borne rickettsial-type infections.

---

## One-line intuition

Ehrlichiosis is a lone-star-tick infection where bacteria hide inside monocytes and form morulae.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Ehrlichia infects **monocytes**, while Anaplasma infects **granulocytes** (especially neutrophils).

Memory:

* **Ehrlichia = monocytes**
* **Anaplasma = granulocytes**
