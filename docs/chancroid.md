### Core idea

Chancroid is a sexually transmitted infection (STI = sexually transmitted infection) caused by **Haemophilus ducreyi**.

Classic presentation:

* painful genital ulcer
* soft/ragged ulcer edge
* tender inguinal lymphadenopathy (swollen groin lymph nodes)
* sometimes suppurative buboes (pus-filled swollen lymph nodes)

---

### Why the findings happen

**H. ducreyi** infects genital skin/mucosa through tiny breaks in the surface.

Then:

* local tissue destruction → painful ulcer
* inflammation + necrosis (cell death) → dirty gray/yellow base
* lymphatic drainage to groin nodes → painful inguinal lymph node swelling
* severe node infection → bubo formation

So the big picture is:

> genital ulcer + angry painful groin nodes.

---

### How to distinguish it

Chancroid vs syphilis:

* **chancroid:** painful, soft ulcer
* **syphilis:** painless, firm/indurated chancre (chancre = primary syphilis ulcer)

Chancroid vs herpes:

* **chancroid:** painful ulcer, usually no vesicles
* **HSV (herpes simplex virus):** painful grouped vesicles that ulcerate

---

### Treatment idea

Treat with antibiotics, classically:

* azithromycin
* ceftriaxone
* ciprofloxacin
* erythromycin

Also test for other genital ulcer causes, especially syphilis, HSV (herpes simplex virus), and HIV (human immunodeficiency virus), because these can overlap.

---

## One-line intuition

Chancroid is **H. ducreyi causing a painful soft genital ulcer with painful pus-forming groin lymph nodes**.
