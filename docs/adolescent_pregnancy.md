### Core idea

Adolescent pregnancy = pregnancy in a teen, usually age 10-19.

It is not a special type of pregnancy biologically; it is pregnancy happening in a younger patient whose body, brain, support system, and access to care may still be developing.

---

### Why it matters medically

Teen pregnancy is higher risk for:

* **preterm birth** = delivery before 37 weeks
* **low birth weight** = baby weighs less than 2500 g
* **anemia** = low red blood cell/hemoglobin level
* **hypertensive disorders of pregnancy** = high blood pressure problems like preeclampsia
* **STIs** = sexually transmitted infections
* delayed prenatal care

---

### Why these risks happen

* **Late/no prenatal care:** fewer chances to catch anemia, infection, hypertension, fetal growth problems
* **Nutritional competition:** the teen may still be growing, so iron/calorie demands are higher
* **Higher STI risk:** untreated infection can cause cervicitis/PID (pelvic inflammatory disease) history, chorioamnionitis, preterm labor, or neonatal infection
* **Psychosocial stress:** school, family, housing, partner violence, and cost barriers can worsen adherence and follow-up

So the big intuition is:

> pregnancy is already physiologically demanding, and adolescence adds medical + social vulnerability.

---

### Prevention/management idea

For Step 1, think:

* pregnancy test when appropriate
* screen for STIs
* start prenatal vitamins/folate
* assess safety, support, mental health, and substance use
* discuss contraception after delivery

Contraception pearl:

* LARC (long-acting reversible contraception), like an IUD (intrauterine device) or implant, is very effective because it does not depend on remembering a pill every day.

---

## One-line intuition

Adolescent pregnancy is high risk because the patient is pregnant while still biologically and psychosocially developing.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Adolescent pregnancy is associated with **preterm birth and low birth weight**, and the best outcomes come from early, confidential, nonjudgmental prenatal care.
