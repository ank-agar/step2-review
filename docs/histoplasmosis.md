### Core idea

Histoplasmosis is a fungal lung infection caused by **Histoplasma capsulatum**.

Classic exposure:

* soil contaminated with bird or bat droppings
* especially Ohio and Mississippi River valleys

You inhale the spores, so it starts in the lungs.

---

### The organism

Histoplasma is a **dimorphic fungus** (a fungus that changes form depending on temperature):

* cold/environment: mold
* warm/body: yeast

High-yield phrase:

> mold in the cold, yeast in the heat

---

### Why it hides in macrophages

After inhalation:

* spores enter alveoli (tiny lung air sacs)
* convert into yeast
* macrophages (immune cells that eat pathogens) phagocytose them

But Histoplasma survives inside macrophages instead of being killed.

So on pathology, think:

* tiny yeast inside macrophages
* granulomas (organized immune walls around something hard to clear)
* possible calcified lung nodules later

---

### Symptoms

Most cases are mild or asymptomatic (no symptoms).

If symptomatic:

* fever
* cough
* fatigue
* chest pain
* flu-like illness

Severe disease can disseminate (spread through blood) especially in immunocompromised patients (patients with weakened immune systems), like AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome).

---

### Why dissemination happens

Macrophages normally travel through lymph and blood.

So if Histoplasma is living inside macrophages, it can hitch a ride to:

* liver
* spleen
* bone marrow
* adrenal glands

That is why disseminated histoplasmosis can cause hepatosplenomegaly (enlarged liver and spleen), pancytopenia (low red cells, white cells, and platelets), and adrenal involvement.

---

### Diagnosis and treatment

Useful tests:

* urine antigen - especially good for disseminated disease
* fungal culture
* tissue biopsy showing intracellular yeast in macrophages

Treatment:

* mild cases may need no treatment
* itraconazole for many symptomatic cases
* amphotericin B for severe/disseminated disease

---

## One-line intuition

Histoplasmosis is a soil fungus inhaled into the lungs that turns into yeast and hides inside macrophages.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Histoplasma is an **intracellular yeast in macrophages**, while Blastomyces is a **broad-based budding yeast** and Cryptococcus has a **thick capsule**.
