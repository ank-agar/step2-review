## Bicornuate uterus

### Core idea

A **bicornuate uterus** is a congenital uterine anomaly (uterus shape problem present from birth) where the uterus has **two upper horns** instead of one smooth rounded top.

Break the word apart:

* **bi-** = two
* **cornuate** = horns

So bicornuate uterus literally means:

> a two-horned uterus.

---

### Why it happens

The uterus forms from two **Mullerian ducts** (embryologic tubes that become the female reproductive tract).

Normally:

* the two ducts fuse into one uterus
* the middle dividing wall gets resorbed
* the top becomes one smooth fundus (top of the uterus)

In bicornuate uterus:

* the Mullerian ducts only partially fuse
* the top of the uterus stays split
* this creates a heart-shaped uterus with two horns

---

### Why it matters clinically

Many patients are asymptomatic.

But in pregnancy, the abnormal shape can cause:

* recurrent pregnancy loss
* preterm labor
* breech presentation
* cervical insufficiency (cervix opens too early during pregnancy)

Reason:

The fetus may have less normal uterine space to grow, and the uterus may not stretch/contract as normally.

---

### Key distinction

Do not confuse it with a **septate uterus** (uterus with an internal wall/septum but a normal outer uterine shape).

The difference:

* **Bicornuate uterus:** abnormal outer contour; deep dip at the fundus
* **Septate uterus:** normal outer contour; internal septum remains

This matters because septate uterus can often be treated hysteroscopically (through the cervix with a camera), but bicornuate uterus usually is not treated that way.

---

## One-line intuition

Bicornuate uterus = the two embryologic uterus halves started to fuse, but the top stayed split into two horns.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Bicornuate uterus is a **Mullerian duct anomaly**, so always remember the association with **renal anomalies** (kidney/urinary tract abnormalities), because the reproductive and urinary systems develop close together embryologically.
