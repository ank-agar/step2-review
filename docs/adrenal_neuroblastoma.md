### Core idea

Adrenal neuroblastoma is a malignant childhood tumor from immature sympathetic nervous system cells in the adrenal medulla.

Break it down:

* neuro = nerve
* blast = immature cell
* oma = tumor

So neuroblastoma = tumor of immature nerve-type cells.

The adrenal medulla is the inner part of the adrenal gland that comes from neural crest cells.

That is why this tumor often arises above the kidney, in the adrenal gland.

---

### Why it happens there

During development, neural crest cells migrate and become parts of the sympathetic nervous system.

Some become adrenal medulla cells.

If those immature cells fail to mature normally and start proliferating, they can form neuroblastoma.

So the intuition is:

> adrenal medulla = sympathetic nervous tissue sitting on top of the kidney.

That is why a "nerve tumor" can show up as an abdominal adrenal mass.

---

### Classic findings

Most common presentation:

* child under age 5
* abdominal mass
* weight loss, fever, irritability
* bone pain if it spreads to bone
* periorbital ecchymoses, meaning bruising around the eyes, if it spreads to the orbit

It can also cause:

* hypertension, meaning high blood pressure
* tachycardia, meaning fast heart rate
* sweating

because the tumor may produce catecholamines, which are sympathetic stress hormones like epinephrine and norepinephrine.

---

### Labs

Neuroblastoma often increases urine:

* VMA (vanillylmandelic acid)
* HVA (homovanillic acid)

These are breakdown products of catecholamines.

Reasoning:

Catecholamines get metabolized into VMA and HVA, so a catecholamine-producing tumor can raise these in urine.

---

### Imaging / pathology clue

On imaging, adrenal neuroblastoma often appears as a large adrenal/retroperitoneal mass that may cross the midline and calcify.

Histology can show small round blue cells with Homer Wright rosettes.

Homer Wright rosettes = tumor cells arranged in a circle around central pink fibrillary nerve material.

---

## One-line intuition

Adrenal neuroblastoma is an aggressive childhood tumor of immature sympathetic nerve cells in the adrenal medulla.

---

## Pearl

HY: Neuroblastoma can cross the midline, while Wilms tumor usually does not. Also remember elevated urine VMA/HVA for neuroblastoma.
