### Core idea

Erythema multiforme is an acute immune-mediated skin reaction.

Break it down:

* erythema = redness
* multiforme = many forms/shapes

So it is a red rash with lesions that can look different, but the classic one is a target lesion.

Target lesion = bullseye-looking skin lesion with concentric rings.

---

### Classic finding

Think:

* target lesions
* usually symmetric
* often on hands, feet, arms, legs
* may involve oral mucosa

Mucosa = moist lining surfaces, like mouth, eyes, genitals.

---

### Why it happens

Most classically, erythema multiforme is triggered by:

* HSV (herpes simplex virus)
* Mycoplasma pneumoniae
* sometimes medications

The immune system reacts to the trigger and attacks keratinocytes.

Keratinocytes = main skin cells in the epidermis.

That injury creates the ringed appearance:

* center = damaged/dusky skin
* middle ring = edema/swelling
* outer ring = inflammation/redness

---

### Minor vs major

* EM minor = mostly skin lesions
* EM major = skin lesions plus mucosal involvement

EM = erythema multiforme.

Important: EM is not the same thing as SJS/TEN.

SJS = Stevens-Johnson syndrome.

TEN = toxic epidermal necrolysis.

SJS/TEN is usually more severe, drug-triggered, and has much more widespread epidermal detachment.

---

## One-line intuition

Erythema multiforme is the immune system making bullseye rashes on the skin, usually after HSV infection.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Most HY Step 1 association: erythema multiforme is classically linked to HSV, and the buzzword lesion is a target lesion.
