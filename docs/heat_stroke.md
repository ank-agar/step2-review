# Heat stroke

![Image](https://www.weather.gov/images/wrn/Infographics/2022/heat-symptoms-2022-final.png)

![Image](https://www.weather.gov/images/wrn/social_media/2017/heat_symptoms.jpg)

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/media/images/2024/04/heat-related-illnesses.JPG)

Heat stroke is a life-threatening heat illness where the body can no longer control its temperature.

Classic idea:

* core body temperature is very high, usually >104 F
* CNS (central nervous system) dysfunction is present
* the patient may be confused, delirious, seizing, or unconscious

So the key difference from heat exhaustion is:

* heat exhaustion = hot + sweaty + weak/dizzy, but brain function mostly intact
* heat stroke = hot + altered mental status

---

### Why it happens

Normally, the hypothalamus (brain temperature-control center) cools the body by:

* sweating
* increasing skin blood flow
* making you seek shade/rest

In heat stroke, heat production or heat exposure overwhelms these cooling systems.

Then:

* proteins and cell membranes get damaged by heat
* inflammation increases
* blood vessels become leaky
* organs can start failing

That is why heat stroke can cause:

* confusion/seizures from brain injury
* rhabdomyolysis (skeletal muscle breakdown)
* AKI (acute kidney injury) from myoglobin (muscle protein that can injure kidney tubules)
* DIC (disseminated intravascular coagulation), which is widespread abnormal clotting and bleeding
* liver injury

---

### Treatment intuition

This is not a normal fever.

Fever is a regulated increase in body temperature set by the hypothalamus.

Heat stroke is unregulated overheating because the body cannot dump heat fast enough.

So antipyretics (fever reducers), like acetaminophen, do not fix the problem.

Treatment is:

* call emergency services
* rapid cooling, especially ice-water/cold-water immersion if available
* IV (intravenous) fluids/supportive care
* monitor for organ damage

---

## One-line intuition

Heat stroke = the body overheats so badly that the brain malfunctions and organs start getting injured.
