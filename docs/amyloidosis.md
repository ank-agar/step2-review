### Core idea

Amyloidosis is when misfolded proteins stack into insoluble beta-pleated sheets and deposit outside cells.

Break the word apart:

* amyloid = abnormal protein deposit
* -osis = disease process

So:

> amyloidosis = disease from abnormal protein deposits building up in tissues.

---

### Why it damages organs

Amyloid deposits are extracellular (outside the cells), so they slowly fill the tissue space and compress normal cells.

That causes:

* **kidney disease:** deposits in glomeruli (kidney filtering tufts) cause proteinuria (protein in urine)
* **heart disease:** deposits make the heart stiff, causing restrictive cardiomyopathy (stiff heart filling problem)
* **tongue enlargement:** soft tissue deposition can cause macroglossia (large tongue)
* **easy bruising:** vessel wall deposition makes vessels fragile

The intuition:

Amyloid is like waxy protein gunk accumulating between cells, so organs become stiff, crowded, and dysfunctional.

---

### Classic types

* **AL amyloidosis (amyloid light-chain amyloidosis):** from plasma cells making abnormal immunoglobulin light chains (antibody fragments)
* **AA amyloidosis (amyloid A amyloidosis):** from chronic inflammation increasing serum amyloid A protein
* **ATTR amyloidosis (transthyretin amyloidosis):** from transthyretin protein, often affecting heart and nerves

AL is classically associated with plasma cell dyscrasias (abnormal plasma cell disorders), like multiple myeloma.

AA is classically associated with chronic inflammatory diseases, like rheumatoid arthritis (autoimmune joint inflammation).

---

### Diagnosis clue

Amyloid stains with Congo red dye and shows apple-green birefringence under polarized light.

* Congo red = the special stain
* birefringence = light splitting/shimmering effect under polarized microscopy

That apple-green birefringence is the board-style buzzword.

---

## One-line intuition

Amyloidosis is misfolded protein stacking into beta-pleated sheets, depositing outside cells, and making organs stiff and dysfunctional.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Amyloid is classically **Congo red positive with apple-green birefringence**, and the protein structure is **beta-pleated sheet**.
