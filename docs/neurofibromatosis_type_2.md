Neurofibromatosis type 2, or NF2 (neurofibromatosis type 2), is an autosomal dominant (inherited with one mutated copy of a gene) tumor syndrome.

Classic finding:

* bilateral vestibular schwannomas

Vestibular schwannomas are benign tumors of Schwann cells (cells that make myelin around peripheral nerves) on CN VIII (cranial nerve 8, the vestibulocochlear nerve).

---

### Core idea

NF2 causes tumors in the nervous system, especially:

* vestibular schwannomas
* meningiomas
* ependymomas

The biggest Step 1 clue is:

> bilateral acoustic neuromas = NF2

Acoustic neuroma is another name for vestibular schwannoma.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

CN VIII carries:

* hearing
* balance

So tumors on CN VIII cause:

* hearing loss
* tinnitus (ringing in the ear)
* vertigo/imbalance

If the tumor gets bigger, it can compress nearby cranial nerves (nerves coming directly from the brain/brainstem), especially:

* CN VII (cranial nerve 7, facial nerve) -> facial weakness
* CN V (cranial nerve 5, trigeminal nerve) -> facial numbness

So the pattern is:

> ear symptoms first, then nearby cranial nerve symptoms if the tumor grows.

---

### Genetics

NF2 is due to mutation of the NF2 tumor suppressor gene on chromosome 22.

This gene makes merlin (a tumor suppressor protein).

Tumor suppressor means it normally acts like a brake on cell growth.

So if NF2 is lost:

* Schwann cells grow too much -> schwannomas
* meningeal cells grow too much -> meningiomas
* ependymal cells can grow too much -> ependymomas

---

### NF1 vs NF2

NF1 (neurofibromatosis type 1):

* cafe-au-lait spots
* neurofibromas
* optic gliomas
* chromosome 17

NF2 (neurofibromatosis type 2):

* bilateral vestibular schwannomas
* meningiomas
* ependymomas
* chromosome 22

Easy memory:

> NF2 = 2 ears affected.

---

## One-line intuition

NF2 is a chromosome 22 tumor suppressor disorder where Schwann cells form bilateral CN VIII tumors, causing hearing loss, tinnitus, and balance problems.

---

## Pearl

USMLE loves this association:

> bilateral vestibular schwannomas + meningiomas/ependymomas = NF2.

Do not mix it up with NF1, which is the cafe-au-lait and cutaneous neurofibroma disease.
