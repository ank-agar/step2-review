### Core idea

Postpartum blues = "baby blues" after delivery.

It is a very common, mild, temporary mood disturbance after childbirth.

Typical symptoms:

* crying easily
* mood swings
* irritability
* anxiety
* fatigue
* trouble sleeping

Key timing:

* starts around postpartum day 2-3
* peaks around day 4-5
* resolves within 2 weeks

Postpartum means after childbirth.

---

### Why it happens

After delivery, the placenta is gone.

The placenta was making lots of estrogen and progesterone.

So after birth:

* estrogen drops
* progesterone drops
* sleep deprivation increases
* stress of newborn care increases

That sudden hormone crash + exhaustion can temporarily destabilize mood.

Intuition:

> the body goes from "high pregnancy hormone state" to "sudden withdrawal state," so emotions become extra labile.

Labile means rapidly changing.

---

### Important distinction

Postpartum blues is mild and self-limited.

Postpartum depression is more severe.

Worry about postpartum depression if:

* symptoms last more than 2 weeks
* symptoms worsen instead of improving
* patient cannot care for self or baby
* hopelessness is prominent
* suicidal thoughts are present
* thoughts of harming the baby are present

Postpartum psychosis is an emergency.

Psychosis means loss of reality testing, like delusions or hallucinations.

Red flags for postpartum psychosis:

* hallucinations
* delusions
* severe confusion
* bizarre behavior
* thoughts of harming self or baby

---

## One-line intuition

Postpartum blues is the normal short-lived emotional crash after delivery from sudden hormone withdrawal + exhaustion.

---
