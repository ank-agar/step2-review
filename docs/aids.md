### Core idea

AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome) is the severe late stage of HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) infection.

HIV destroys CD4 T cells (helper T cells), which are immune cells that coordinate the immune response.

So AIDS = HIV has weakened cell-mediated immunity enough that the body becomes vulnerable to opportunistic infections (infections that take advantage of a weak immune system).

---

### Why CD4 loss matters

CD4 T cells are like the immune system's "command center."

They help activate:

* macrophages (phagocytic cells that eat microbes)
* B cells (antibody-producing cells)
* CD8 T cells (cytotoxic T cells that kill infected cells)

So when HIV knocks down CD4 T cells, the immune system does not just lose one cell type.

It loses coordination.

---

### How HIV causes AIDS

HIV enters CD4 T cells using:

* CD4 receptor
* CCR5 or CXCR4 coreceptor (extra entry receptor)

Then HIV uses reverse transcriptase (enzyme that converts RNA into DNA) to turn viral RNA into DNA.

That viral DNA integrates into the host genome, so the infected cell becomes a virus-producing cell.

Over time:

* CD4 count falls
* cell-mediated immunity weakens
* opportunistic infections appear

---

### AIDS-defining idea

AIDS is diagnosed when someone with HIV has either:

* CD4 count < 200 cells/mm3
* an AIDS-defining illness

Classic AIDS-defining illnesses include:

* Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (fungal pneumonia)
* esophageal candidiasis (Candida infection in the esophagus)
* toxoplasmosis (parasite infection, often brain lesions)
* cryptococcal meningitis (fungal infection of the meninges)
* CMV retinitis (cytomegalovirus infection of the retina)
* Kaposi sarcoma (HHV-8-associated vascular tumor)

---

### Intuition

HIV attacks the immune system's helper cells.

Once CD4 cells drop too low, the body can no longer organize a strong defense, so "rare" infections and cancers start showing up.

---

## One-line intuition

AIDS is what happens when HIV destroys enough CD4 helper T cells that the immune system loses command and opportunistic diseases take over.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia is classically associated with CD4 count < 200 cells/mm3, and prophylaxis (preventive treatment) is usually TMP-SMX (trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole).
