### Core idea

Myelofibrosis = scarring/fibrosis of the bone marrow.

Break it down:

* myelo = bone marrow
* fibrosis = scar tissue formation

So:

> bone marrow gets replaced by scar tissue.

---

### What causes it?

Usually it is a myeloproliferative neoplasm, or MPN (bone marrow stem cell cancer where myeloid cells overgrow).

Common mutations:

* JAK2 (Janus kinase 2)
* CALR (calreticulin)
* MPL (thrombopoietin receptor)

These mutations make marrow cells, especially megakaryocytes (platelet-producing cells), abnormally active.

---

### Why fibrosis happens

Abnormal megakaryocytes release cytokines (cell signaling proteins), especially:

* TGF-beta (transforming growth factor beta)
* PDGF (platelet-derived growth factor)

These cytokines stimulate fibroblasts (scar-producing cells).

So the logic is:

abnormal megakaryocytes -> cytokines -> fibroblast activation -> marrow fibrosis

---

### Why blood cells look abnormal

If the marrow is scarred, blood cells have trouble getting out normally.

Red blood cells get physically squeezed through a fibrotic marrow, so they become:

* teardrop-shaped RBCs (red blood cells)
* also called dacrocytes (teardrop cells)

This is the classic smear clue.

---

### Why spleen gets big

If bone marrow cannot make blood well, the body tries to make blood somewhere else.

This is called extramedullary hematopoiesis:

* extra = outside
* medullary = marrow
* hematopoiesis = blood cell production

Main site:

* spleen

So patients often get splenomegaly (enlarged spleen).

---

### Classic findings

* anemia (low red blood cells)
* fatigue
* splenomegaly (enlarged spleen)
* teardrop RBCs (red blood cells)
* dry tap on bone marrow aspiration

Dry tap means you try to aspirate marrow, but little/no fluid comes out because the marrow is fibrotic and packed with scar tissue.

---

## One-line intuition

Myelofibrosis is when abnormal marrow cells make the bone marrow scar down, so blood production moves to the spleen and RBCs get squeezed into teardrops.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, think:

**myelofibrosis = JAK2 mutation + marrow fibrosis + dry tap + teardrop RBCs + massive splenomegaly.**
