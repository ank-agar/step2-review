### Core idea

Oophoritis = inflammation of the ovary.

Break the word apart:

* oophor/oophoro- = ovary
* -itis = inflammation

So:

> oophoritis = inflamed ovary.

Most commonly, think of it as part of PID (pelvic inflammatory disease), where infection ascends from the lower genital tract into the upper reproductive tract.

---

### Usual pathway

Infection can spread upward:

* vagina/cervix
* uterus
* fallopian tubes
* ovaries

When the fallopian tube is inflamed, that is salpingitis.

When the ovary is inflamed, that is oophoritis.

Together, this can be called salpingo-oophoritis.

---

### Common causes

Usually bacterial, especially organisms associated with PID (pelvic inflammatory disease):

* Neisseria gonorrhoeae
* Chlamydia trachomatis
* mixed anaerobes from vaginal flora

Less commonly:

* mumps virus
* autoimmune oophoritis, meaning immune attack against ovarian tissue

---

### Why symptoms happen

Inflammation in/around the ovary irritates pelvic tissues, causing:

* lower abdominal or pelvic pain
* fever
* cervical motion tenderness
* adnexal tenderness, meaning pain near the ovary/fallopian tube area
* abnormal vaginal discharge if part of PID (pelvic inflammatory disease)

If pus collects around the tube and ovary, it can form a TOA (tubo-ovarian abscess).

That is basically:

> infected fluid/pus trapped around the fallopian tube + ovary.

---

### Why fertility can be affected

The big danger is scarring.

Inflammation damages the fallopian tubes and nearby pelvic structures, so the egg and sperm have a harder time meeting normally.

This can cause:

* infertility
* ectopic pregnancy, meaning implantation outside the uterus
* chronic pelvic pain

---

## One-line intuition

Oophoritis is an inflamed ovary, usually because PID (pelvic inflammatory disease) infection climbed upward and involved the ovary.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: PID (pelvic inflammatory disease) classically involves salpingitis first, and scarring of the fallopian tubes increases risk of infertility and ectopic pregnancy.
