## Anaplastic thyroid cancer

### Core idea

Anaplastic thyroid cancer is a very aggressive thyroid carcinoma (malignant tumor from epithelial cells).

**Anaplastic** means the cells are very poorly differentiated, meaning they no longer look or behave like normal thyroid cells.

So instead of being a slow thyroid nodule, it acts like a rapidly growing neck mass.

---

### Classic presentation

Usually in an older patient with:

* rapidly enlarging neck mass
* dysphagia (trouble swallowing)
* hoarseness
* dyspnea (trouble breathing)
* hard fixed thyroid mass

Why?

The thyroid sits right in front of the trachea (windpipe) and near the esophagus (food tube) and recurrent laryngeal nerve (nerve to the vocal cords).

So when the tumor grows fast and invades locally:

* trachea compression → dyspnea
* esophagus compression → dysphagia
* recurrent laryngeal nerve involvement → hoarseness

---

### Why it is so aggressive

The tumor cells are undifferentiated.

That means they have lost normal thyroid features, so they:

* divide rapidly
* invade nearby structures early
* metastasize (spread to distant sites) early
* usually do not take up iodine well

That last point matters because radioactive iodine works best on differentiated thyroid cancers that still behave like thyroid tissue.

Anaplastic thyroid cancer is too dedifferentiated, so radioactive iodine is usually not very helpful.

---

### Histology idea

On histology (microscope appearance), anaplastic thyroid cancer looks very ugly and pleomorphic.

**Pleomorphic** means the cells vary a lot in size and shape.

You may see:

* giant cells
* spindle cells
* bizarre nuclei
* necrosis (dead tumor tissue)
* lots of mitoses (cell divisions)

Intuition:

> The cells have lost their thyroid identity, so the microscope looks chaotic.

---

### Important association

It can arise from a pre-existing differentiated thyroid cancer, especially papillary thyroid carcinoma.

So think:

> well-differentiated thyroid cancer can sometimes dedifferentiate into a much more aggressive anaplastic cancer.

---

## One-line intuition

Anaplastic thyroid cancer is the “wild, undifferentiated” thyroid cancer: older patient, rapidly enlarging hard neck mass, local invasion, poor prognosis.

---

## Pearl 🔥

High-yield Step 1: anaplastic thyroid carcinoma is associated with **TP53 mutation** and is the worst-prognosis thyroid cancer.
