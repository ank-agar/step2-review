# Leukocytosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b8/White_Blood_Cells.svg/1280px-White_Blood_Cells.svg.png)

![Image](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/PHIL_Images/6048/6048_lores.jpg)

Leukocytosis means increased white blood cells in the blood.

Break it down:

* leuko- = white
* cyto- = cell
* -osis = increased/abnormal condition

So:

> leukocytosis = too many white blood cells.

White blood cells are also called leukocytes.

---

### Core idea

Your body is usually reacting to something, so the bone marrow releases more white blood cells.

Common causes:

* infection, especially bacterial infection
* inflammation
* stress, trauma, burns, or surgery
* corticosteroids
* leukemia

Corticosteroids are steroid drugs like prednisone.

---

### Why it happens

White blood cells are immune cells.

If the body senses infection or inflammation, cytokines (immune signaling proteins) tell the bone marrow:

> make and release more defense cells.

That raises the white blood cell count on a CBC (complete blood count).

---

### Step 1 way to think

The most important question is:

> which white blood cell is increased?

Examples:

* neutrophilia = increased neutrophils, usually bacterial infection or acute inflammation
* lymphocytosis = increased lymphocytes, often viral infection or chronic lymphocytic leukemia
* eosinophilia = increased eosinophils, think parasites, allergies, asthma, drug reactions
* basophilia = increased basophils, classically associated with chronic myeloid leukemia
* monocytosis = increased monocytes, chronic inflammation or some infections

Neutrophils are the main acute bacterial infection white blood cells.

---

### Left shift

A left shift means immature neutrophils, especially bands, are increased.

Why?

The marrow is rushing to meet demand, so it releases young neutrophils early.

Intuition:

> bacterial infection is like calling in extra soldiers before they are fully mature.

---

## One-line intuition

Leukocytosis means the immune system is turned up, so more white blood cells are showing up in the blood.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Corticosteroids can cause neutrophilic leukocytosis because they demarginate neutrophils, meaning neutrophils that were stuck to blood vessel walls detach and enter the circulating blood.
