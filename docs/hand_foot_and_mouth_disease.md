### Core idea

Hand-foot-and-mouth disease, or HFMD (hand-foot-and-mouth disease), is a contagious viral illness that classically causes:

* fever
* painful mouth sores
* vesicular rash on the hands and feet

Vesicles = small fluid-filled blisters.

Most common cause:

* Coxsackievirus A16

Other important cause:

* EV-A71 (enterovirus A71), which is more associated with neurologic complications

---

### Why it affects the mouth, hands, and feet

The virus is an enterovirus (a virus that replicates in the gastrointestinal tract).

It spreads through:

* saliva / respiratory droplets
* blister fluid
* fecal-oral spread

After infection, it causes a brief viremia (virus in the blood), then seeds skin and oral mucosa.

That gives:

* mouth ulcers because oral mucosa gets inflamed and blistered
* palm/sole rash because the virus commonly targets acral skin
* poor oral intake because the mouth sores hurt
* dehydration risk because kids may refuse to drink

Acral = distal body parts like hands and feet.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* child under 5 years old
* daycare exposure
* fever + sore throat
* painful oral ulcers
* red spots or blisters on palms and soles

It usually resolves on its own in about 7-10 days.

---

## One-line intuition

HFMD is a contagious enterovirus infection that makes kids febrile, then puts painful blisters in the mouth and on the hands/feet.

---

## Pearl

Do not confuse hand-foot-and-mouth disease with foot-and-mouth disease.

* hand-foot-and-mouth disease = human enterovirus infection, usually Coxsackievirus A16
* foot-and-mouth disease = animal disease caused by aphthovirus

Also HY: Coxsackievirus is a positive-sense single-stranded RNA virus.
