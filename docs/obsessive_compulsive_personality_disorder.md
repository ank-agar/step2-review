Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder, or **OCPD** (obsessive-compulsive personality disorder), is a **Cluster C personality disorder** (anxious/fearful personality disorder group) where the person is excessively:

* perfectionistic
* rule-focused
* controlling
* rigid
* devoted to work/productivity

The key is:

> They care so much about doing things the “right” way that flexibility and efficiency get sacrificed.

---

### Core idea

OCPD is not mainly about intrusive thoughts or rituals.

It is a **personality style** where the person’s baseline way of living is:

* “There is one correct way.”
* “I should control the details.”
* “If it is not perfect, it is wrong.”

So they may spend so much time perfecting a task that they do not finish it.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Perfectionism delays completion:** they keep revising because “good enough” feels unacceptable
* **Poor delegation:** other people will not do it exactly their way
* **Rigid morality:** rules feel absolute, not flexible
* **Work over relationships:** productivity feels safer/more important than leisure or intimacy
* **Frugality/hoarding:** saving things or money can feel like responsible preparation

---

### OCPD vs OCD

**OCD** (obsessive-compulsive disorder):

* intrusive, unwanted thoughts = obsessions
* repetitive behaviors to reduce anxiety = compulsions
* usually **ego-dystonic** (feels wrong/unwanted to the person)

**OCPD**:

* rigid perfectionism/control
* no true required obsessions or compulsions
* usually **ego-syntonic** (feels consistent with the person’s values)

So:

> OCD = “I hate these thoughts/rituals, but I feel forced.”

> OCPD = “My way is the correct way.”

---

## One-line intuition

OCPD is “pathologic perfectionism + control” where rules and details become more important than flexibility, relationships, and finishing the task.

---

## Pearl 🔥

OCPD is a **Cluster C personality disorder**, and the big Step 1 distinction from OCD is **ego-syntonic in OCPD vs ego-dystonic in OCD**.
