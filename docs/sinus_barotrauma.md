# Sinus barotrauma

### Core idea

Sinus barotrauma = injury to the paranasal sinuses (air-filled spaces in the skull around the nose) from a pressure mismatch.

Think:

* airplane descent
* scuba diving descent/ascent
* hyperbaric oxygen therapy

The sinus is supposed to equalize pressure through a tiny opening called the ostium (opening between a sinus and the nasal cavity).

If that opening is blocked, pressure cannot equalize.

---

### Why it hurts

Normally:

* outside pressure changes
* sinus air moves in or out through the ostium
* pressure stays balanced

But if the ostium is blocked by edema (swelling), mucus, allergies, URI (upper respiratory infection), or congestion:

* trapped sinus air cannot equilibrate
* pressure pulls or pushes on the mucosa (sinus lining)
* small vessels can rupture
* blood/fluid can fill the sinus

That causes sudden facial pain or headache.

---

### Classic symptoms

* sharp facial pain during ascent or descent
* frontal headache if frontal sinus is involved
* pain behind the eyes if ethmoid/sphenoid sinuses are involved
* cheek or upper tooth pain if maxillary sinus is involved
* epistaxis (nosebleed)
* bloody postnasal drip

Postnasal drip = mucus/blood draining from the nose backward into the throat.

---

## One-line intuition

Sinus barotrauma is basically a “sinus squeeze” because trapped air cannot match the outside pressure.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do **not** dive or fly with bad congestion/URI if you cannot equalize, because swollen nasal mucosa can block the sinus ostia and predispose to barotrauma.
