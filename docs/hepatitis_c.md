# Hepatitis C

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/HCV_structure.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/HepatitisC.jpg/960px-HepatitisC.jpg)

Hepatitis C is liver inflammation caused by HCV (hepatitis C virus).

Core idea:

* HCV is an enveloped, positive-sense ssRNA virus (single-stranded ribonucleic acid virus)
* it spreads mainly through blood
* it often becomes chronic
* chronic infection can lead to cirrhosis and HCC (hepatocellular carcinoma)

---

### Transmission

Think blood exposure:

* IV drug use (intravenous drug use)
* needle-stick injury
* transfusion before screening was common
* birth from infected mother
* sexual transmission is possible, but less efficient than HBV (hepatitis B virus)

---

### Why it becomes chronic

HCV RNA polymerase (viral enzyme that copies RNA) makes lots of errors.

So the virus keeps changing its surface proteins.

That means:

* immune system recognizes one version
* virus mutates
* immune system falls behind
* chronic liver inflammation continues

This is why hepatitis C is famous for chronic infection.

---

### What it does to the liver

Chronic inflammation damages hepatocytes (liver cells).

Over years:

* inflammation
* fibrosis (scar tissue formation)
* cirrhosis (severe liver scarring with distorted architecture)
* increased risk of HCC (hepatocellular carcinoma)

So the danger is usually not dramatic acute hepatitis.

The danger is quiet chronic liver injury.

---

### Labs and diagnosis

Common findings:

* increased AST (aspartate aminotransferase)
* increased ALT (alanine aminotransferase)

Testing:

* anti-HCV antibody = exposure to HCV
* HCV RNA = active infection

Important:

Antibody can stay positive even after cleared infection, so HCV RNA tells you if virus is actually present.

---

### Extrahepatic associations

Hepatitis C can also cause immune-complex disease.

Classic Step 1 associations:

* mixed cryoglobulinemia (antibodies that precipitate in the cold)
* MPGN (membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis, a kidney inflammation pattern)
* porphyria cutanea tarda (blistering photosensitivity from abnormal heme metabolism)

---

## One-line intuition

Hepatitis C is a bloodborne RNA virus that mutates a lot, evades immunity, and quietly causes chronic liver inflammation.

---

## HY pearl

Hepatitis C is strongly associated with mixed cryoglobulinemia and MPGN (membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis), so kidney disease + purpura + hepatitis history should make you think HCV.
