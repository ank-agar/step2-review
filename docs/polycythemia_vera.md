## Polycythemia vera

![Image](https://med.libretexts.org/@api/deki/files/20675/0359PV2-Gloria-Kwon-1024x768.jpg?revision=1)

Polycythemia vera is a myeloproliferative neoplasm (bone marrow cancer where marrow makes too many blood cells).

Main idea:

* too many RBCs (red blood cells)
* often too many platelets
* often too many WBCs (white blood cells)

But the big Step 1 idea is:

> too many RBCs make the blood thick.

---

### Why it happens

Most cases have a JAK2 mutation.

JAK2 (Janus kinase 2) is part of a growth-signaling pathway inside bone marrow cells.

Normally:

* EPO (erythropoietin) tells marrow to make RBCs
* EPO comes from the kidney when oxygen is low

In polycythemia vera:

* mutated JAK2 is stuck "on"
* marrow makes RBCs even without needing EPO
* so EPO is low

That is why this is primary polycythemia (problem starts in bone marrow), not secondary polycythemia (high EPO from hypoxia, tumors, high altitude, etc.).

---

### Why the symptoms happen

More RBCs = higher hematocrit (percent of blood volume made of RBCs).

Higher hematocrit makes blood more viscous (thicker/sludgier).

Thick blood causes:

* headache
* dizziness
* blurry vision
* thrombosis (blood clots)
* hypertension sometimes

The spleen can enlarge because it helps handle excess blood cells and may do extramedullary hematopoiesis (blood cell production outside the marrow).

---

### Classic clues

Polycythemia vera can cause:

* plethoric face = red/ruddy face
* aquagenic pruritus = itching after hot shower/water exposure
* erythromelalgia = burning red pain in hands/feet due to platelet-mediated microthrombi (tiny clots)
* splenomegaly = enlarged spleen

---

### Labs

Typical findings:

* increased hemoglobin
* increased hematocrit
* low EPO (erythropoietin)
* JAK2 mutation positive
* increased RBC mass

Treatment often includes phlebotomy (removing blood) to lower hematocrit and low-dose aspirin to reduce clot risk.

---

## One-line intuition

Polycythemia vera = JAK2 mutation makes bone marrow overproduce RBCs, so blood gets thick and clots more easily.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Low EPO helps distinguish polycythemia vera from secondary polycythemia, where EPO is usually high because the body is responding to hypoxia (low oxygen).
