### Core idea

Meconium aspiration syndrome is when a newborn breathes in meconium-stained amniotic fluid.

* meconium = the baby's first stool
* aspiration = breathing something into the airway/lungs
* syndrome = a group of findings that happen together

So the baby inhales stool-contaminated fluid, and that irritates + blocks the lungs.

---

### Why it happens

Usually this is in term or post-term babies.

Fetal stress/hypoxia (low oxygen) can cause:

* increased gut movement
* anal sphincter relaxation
* meconium passage into amniotic fluid

Then the baby gasps before or during delivery and inhales that meconium-stained fluid.

---

### Why the baby has respiratory distress

Meconium hurts the lungs in a few ways:

* **Airway plugging:** thick meconium blocks bronchioles
* **Ball-valve air trapping:** air gets in but cannot get out well, causing hyperinflation
* **Atelectasis:** complete obstruction collapses parts of lung
* **Chemical pneumonitis:** meconium irritates lung tissue and causes inflammation
* **Surfactant inactivation:** surfactant normally keeps alveoli open; meconium makes it work poorly

Surfactant = substance that lowers alveolar surface tension and prevents alveolar collapse.

---

### Classic findings

Think:

* term/post-term newborn
* meconium-stained fluid
* tachypnea (fast breathing)
* grunting/retractions
* cyanosis (bluish discoloration from low oxygen)
* barrel-shaped hyperinflated chest

Chest X-ray can show:

* patchy infiltrates
* hyperinflation
* areas of atelectasis mixed with air trapping

---

### Big complication

MAS (meconium aspiration syndrome) can cause PPHN (persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn).

Why?

Low oxygen + acidosis (excess acid in blood) make pulmonary vessels constrict.

That keeps pressure high in the lungs, so blood can shunt right-to-left and skip oxygenation.

---

## One-line intuition

The baby breathes in thick stool-contaminated fluid, which plugs airways, irritates lung tissue, knocks out surfactant, and causes hypoxic respiratory distress.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Meconium aspiration syndrome is classically a disease of **term/post-term infants**, not very premature infants, because passage of meconium into amniotic fluid usually requires a more mature GI tract.
