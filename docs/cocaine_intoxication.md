## Cocaine intoxication

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/14/Cocaine_on_Serotonin_Dopamine_Noradrenaline_reuptake.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Mydriasis_and_miosis.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5e/CocaineHydrochloridePowder_cropped.jpg)

Cocaine intoxication = acute toxic effects from cocaine causing a big sympathetic nervous system surge.

Think:

* very awake
* very stimulated
* very vasoconstricted
* very tachycardic

---

### Core mechanism

Cocaine blocks reuptake of:

* dopamine
* norepinephrine
* serotonin

Reuptake = the presynaptic neuron taking neurotransmitter back up from the synapse.

So cocaine leaves these neurotransmitters sitting in the synapse longer.

More norepinephrine = sympathetic overdrive.

More dopamine/serotonin = central nervous system stimulation.

---

### Classic findings

* tachycardia = fast heart rate
* hypertension = high blood pressure
* mydriasis = dilated pupils
* diaphoresis = sweating
* agitation/anxiety/paranoia
* hyperthermia = high body temperature
* seizures

Severe cases can cause:

* myocardial infarction (heart attack)
* stroke
* arrhythmias (abnormal heart rhythms)
* rhabdomyolysis (muscle breakdown)

---

### Why heart attack can happen

Cocaine causes coronary vasospasm.

Coronary = heart blood vessels.

Vasospasm = sudden blood vessel tightening.

So even if the vessel is not fully blocked by plaque, cocaine can clamp it down and reduce oxygen delivery to the heart.

At the same time, cocaine increases heart rate and blood pressure, so the heart needs more oxygen.

Bad combo:

* less oxygen supply
* more oxygen demand

---

### Treatment idea

First-line treatment is usually benzodiazepines.

Benzodiazepines = sedative drugs that increase GABA (gamma-aminobutyric acid), the main inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brain.

Why they help:

* calm agitation
* reduce sympathetic outflow
* help prevent/treat seizures
* lower heart rate and blood pressure indirectly

For chest pain, nitrates can help because they dilate coronary vessels.

---

## One-line intuition

Cocaine intoxication is basically a massive catecholamine surge from blocked reuptake, causing a hot, sweaty, hypertensive, tachycardic, agitated patient.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cocaine also blocks fast sodium channels in the heart, like a local anesthetic, so overdose can cause widened QRS (wide ventricular depolarization on electrocardiogram) and dangerous arrhythmias.
