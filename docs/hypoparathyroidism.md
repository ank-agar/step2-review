## Hypoparathyroidism

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/8890.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK540972/bin/Gray1177.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/Trousseau%27s_Sign_of_Latent_Tetany.jpg)

Hypoparathyroidism = low parathyroid hormone (PTH, parathyroid hormone).

PTH normally raises blood calcium.

So if PTH is low:

* calcium goes down
* phosphate goes up

---

### Core idea

The parathyroid glands are underactive, so the body cannot maintain calcium in the blood.

Think:

> low PTH = low calcium + high phosphate

---

### Why calcium is low

PTH normally increases calcium by:

* pulling calcium out of bone
* increasing calcium reabsorption in the kidney
* activating vitamin D, which increases calcium absorption from the gut

So without enough PTH, all of those calcium-raising effects are weaker.

Result:

* hypocalcemia (low calcium in the blood)

---

### Why phosphate is high

PTH normally makes the kidney waste phosphate in urine.

So if PTH is low, the kidney holds onto phosphate.

Result:

* hyperphosphatemia (high phosphate in the blood)

---

### Symptoms

Low calcium makes nerves and muscles too excitable.

That can cause:

* perioral numbness (numbness around the mouth)
* paresthesias (tingling sensations)
* muscle cramps
* tetany (involuntary muscle spasm)
* seizures
* prolonged QT interval on ECG (electrocardiogram)

Classic signs:

* Chvostek sign = facial twitching when tapping the facial nerve
* Trousseau sign = carpopedal spasm (hand/wrist spasm) after inflating a blood pressure cuff

---

### Common cause

Most classic cause:

* accidental parathyroid removal or damage during thyroid surgery

The parathyroids sit right behind the thyroid, so thyroidectomy (surgical removal of the thyroid) can injure them.

---

## One-line intuition

Hypoparathyroidism means the calcium-raising hormone is missing, so calcium falls, phosphate rises, and nerves/muscles start firing too easily.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 loves the contrast:

* hypoparathyroidism: low PTH, low calcium, high phosphate
* pseudohypoparathyroidism: high PTH, low calcium, high phosphate

Pseudo- means the hormone is present, but the body is resistant to it.
