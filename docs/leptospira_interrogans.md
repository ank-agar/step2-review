# Leptospira interrogans

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4b/Leptospira_interrogans_strain_RGA_01.png)

**Leptospira interrogans** is a thin, spiral-shaped **spirochete** (corkscrew-shaped bacterium) that causes **leptospirosis**.

Classic source:

* animal urine, especially rats
* contaminated freshwater, soil, or floodwater
* enters through broken skin or mucous membranes (wet surfaces like eyes/mouth)

So the classic Step 1 setup is:

> swimmer/triathlete/floodwater exposure + fever + calf pain + conjunctival suffusion.

**Conjunctival suffusion** = red eyes without pus.

---

### Why the findings happen

Leptospira spreads through the blood, then likes to injure:

* **kidney** → acute kidney injury
* **liver** → jaundice
* **muscle** → severe calf/back pain
* **blood vessels** → hemorrhage sometimes

Severe leptospirosis is called **Weil disease**:

* jaundice
* renal failure
* hemorrhage

Think:

> Leptospira = rat pee in water → corkscrew bacteria enter skin → liver + kidney damage.

---

### Diagnosis / treatment

Diagnosis can use:

* serology (antibody testing)
* PCR (polymerase chain reaction; detects bacterial DNA)

Treatment:

* doxycycline for mild disease
* penicillin or ceftriaxone for severe disease

---

## One-line intuition

Leptospira interrogans is a motile corkscrew bacterium from animal urine-contaminated water that causes fever, calf pain, red eyes, jaundice, and kidney injury.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Leptospira interrogans is an **aerobic spirochete** and is classically associated with **rat urine exposure**, unlike **Borrelia burgdorferi** (Lyme disease; tick-borne spirochete) and **Treponema pallidum** (syphilis; sexually transmitted spirochete).
