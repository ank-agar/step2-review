### Core idea

Orthostatic hypotension is a drop in blood pressure when a person stands up.

Orthostatic = standing upright  
Hypotension = low blood pressure

Diagnostic idea:

* systolic blood pressure drops at least **20 mm Hg**
* or diastolic blood pressure drops at least **10 mm Hg**
* within about **3 minutes** of standing

Systolic blood pressure means the top number.  
Diastolic blood pressure means the bottom number.

---

### What normally happens when you stand

When you stand up, gravity pulls blood down into the legs and abdomen.

That briefly lowers venous return.

Venous return means blood returning to the heart.

Less venous return causes:

* less ventricular filling
* lower stroke volume
* lower cardiac output
* lower blood pressure

Stroke volume means blood pumped per heartbeat.  
Cardiac output means blood pumped per minute.

Normally, baroreceptors sense this drop and activate the sympathetic nervous system.

Baroreceptors are pressure sensors in the carotid sinus and aortic arch.

The sympathetic response causes:

* increased heart rate
* increased contractility
* arteriolar vasoconstriction
* venous constriction

That pushes blood pressure back up.

---

### Why orthostatic hypotension happens

Orthostatic hypotension happens when that compensation is too weak.

Common causes:

* dehydration or blood loss
* diuretics
* antihypertensives
* prolonged bed rest
* autonomic neuropathy
* Parkinson disease or other autonomic failure syndromes

Autonomic neuropathy means damage to nerves that control automatic body functions, like blood vessel tone and heart rate.

So when the patient stands:

* blood pools in the legs
* not enough blood returns to the heart
* cardiac output falls
* brain perfusion falls
* patient gets dizzy, lightheaded, blurry vision, or syncope

Syncope means fainting.

---

### Step 1 contrast

Orthostatic hypotension is different from **POTS**.

POTS means postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome.

In POTS:

* heart rate rises a lot after standing
* blood pressure does **not** drop enough to meet orthostatic hypotension criteria

In orthostatic hypotension:

* blood pressure drops significantly after standing

---

## One-line intuition

Standing makes blood fall into the legs; if the body cannot squeeze vessels and boost heart output fast enough, the brain briefly gets underperfused.

---

## Pearl

Diabetic autonomic neuropathy can cause orthostatic hypotension because damaged autonomic nerves cannot properly vasoconstrict blood vessels when the patient stands.
