# Dissociative disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bb/5_core_components_of_Dissociative_disorders.jpg)

![Image](https://oupblog.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Feeling-Unreal-infographic-.jpg)

![Image](https://lindsaybraman.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/lb-derealization.depersonalization-pin-683x1024.jpg)

Dissociative disorders are psychiatric conditions where normal mental functions get disconnected from each other, especially:

* memory
* identity
* consciousness
* perception
* sense of self

So the core idea is:

> parts of the mind that usually feel integrated start feeling separated.

---

### Main types

* **Dissociative amnesia:** inability to remember important personal information, usually after trauma
* **Depersonalization/derealization disorder:** feeling detached from yourself or from the world
* **Dissociative identity disorder:** two or more distinct identity states with gaps in memory

Definitions:

* **Depersonalization** = feeling detached from yourself, like watching yourself from outside your body
* **Derealization** = feeling detached from the world, like the environment is dreamlike, fake, or foggy
* **Amnesia** = memory loss

---

### Why it happens

Often, dissociation is the brain's protective response to overwhelming stress or trauma.

Intuition:

If an experience is too intense to process normally, the brain may separate pieces of the experience:

* memory gets separated
* emotions get separated
* identity/sense of self gets separated

That is why trauma can lead to memory gaps or feeling disconnected from your own body.

---

### Key exam distinction

In depersonalization/derealization disorder, **reality testing is intact**.

That means the patient feels like things are unreal, but they know the feeling is not actually true.

This helps separate it from psychosis (loss of contact with reality), where the patient may truly believe a delusion or hallucination is real.

---

## One-line intuition

Dissociative disorders are when memory, identity, perception, or consciousness become disconnected, usually as a defense against severe stress or trauma.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Depersonalization/derealization disorder is ego-dystonic (distressing and unwanted), and reality testing stays intact.
