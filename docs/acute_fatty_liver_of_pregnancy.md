### Core idea

Acute fatty liver of pregnancy is a rare, dangerous liver failure syndrome that happens in late pregnancy, usually the 3rd trimester.

The liver fills with tiny fat droplets inside hepatocytes (liver cells), called microvesicular steatosis (many small fat droplets in cells).

---

### Why it happens

Think:

* fetus has a fatty acid oxidation defect
* fatty acid metabolites build up
* those toxic metabolites cross the placenta into mom
* mom's liver gets overwhelmed
* hepatocytes fill with tiny fat droplets and stop working well

Fatty acid oxidation means breaking down fatty acids in mitochondria (cell energy organelles).

Classic association:

* LCHAD deficiency (long-chain 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase deficiency)

This is why the baby can be the trigger, but the mom gets liver failure.

---

### What you see

Late pregnancy patient with:

* nausea/vomiting
* RUQ pain (right upper quadrant pain)
* jaundice (yellow skin/eyes from bilirubin buildup)
* hypoglycemia (low blood glucose)
* coagulopathy (poor clotting)
* elevated AST/ALT (aspartate aminotransferase/alanine aminotransferase, liver injury enzymes)
* elevated bilirubin
* renal dysfunction (kidney dysfunction)
* encephalopathy (confusion from liver failure toxins)

---

### Why hypoglycemia happens

The liver normally makes glucose during fasting by gluconeogenesis (making new glucose).

In acute fatty liver of pregnancy, the liver is failing, so it cannot make enough glucose.

So:

> failing liver = low glucose + poor clotting factor production.

That combo is very important.

---

### Distinguish from HELLP

HELLP syndrome means:

* Hemolysis (red blood cell destruction)
* Elevated Liver enzymes
* Low Platelets

Both HELLP and acute fatty liver of pregnancy can have RUQ pain and elevated liver enzymes.

But acute fatty liver of pregnancy is more associated with:

* hypoglycemia
* encephalopathy
* coagulopathy
* more obvious liver failure

---

### Treatment intuition

The trigger is pregnancy/placenta/fetal fatty acid metabolism, so treatment is:

* stabilize mom
* urgent delivery
* supportive care for liver failure

Delivery removes the ongoing source of toxic fatty acid metabolites.

---

## One-line intuition

Acute fatty liver of pregnancy is mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation failure in late pregnancy causing microvesicular fat buildup in mom's liver and acute liver failure.

---
