## Malingering

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikiversity/en/4/45/Malingering%2C_Factitious_Disorder%2C_Somatization_chart.jpg)

Malingering = intentionally faking or exaggerating symptoms for an external reward.

External reward means something practical, like:

* money/disability benefits
* avoiding work or school
* avoiding jail or military duty
* getting drugs
* avoiding responsibility

---

### Core idea

The symptoms are consciously produced.

The motivation is outside gain.

So the equation is:

> intentional fake symptoms + external benefit = malingering

---

### Why it is different from factitious disorder

Both can involve lying about symptoms.

But the motivation is different:

* **Malingering:** wants external gain, like money, drugs, or avoiding punishment
* **Factitious disorder:** wants to be in the sick role, meaning they want attention/care as a patient

So:

> malingering = outside reward  
> factitious disorder = patient role

---

### Why it is different from somatic symptom disorder

In somatic symptom disorder, the patient is not intentionally faking.

They truly feel distressed about symptoms.

In malingering, the person is intentionally producing or exaggerating symptoms to get something.

---

## One-line intuition

Malingering is “pretending to be sick because it gets me something.”

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step questions, look for a clear external incentive:

* prisoner says they are suicidal to avoid jail
* patient exaggerates pain to get opioids
* person claims disability symptoms for money

That external reward is the giveaway.
