Measles virus is an **enveloped, negative-sense single-stranded RNA virus**.

Break that down:

* **enveloped** = has a lipid membrane coating
* **negative-sense RNA** = its RNA (ribonucleic acid) must be copied into usable mRNA (messenger RNA) before proteins can be made
* **single-stranded** = one RNA strand, not double-stranded

It is in the:

* **Paramyxoviridae** family
* **Morbillivirus** genus

---

### Core idea

Measles virus causes **measles**, also called **rubeola**.

It spreads by the respiratory route and is extremely contagious.

Classic symptoms:

* fever
* cough
* coryza (runny nose)
* conjunctivitis (red/inflamed eyes)
* Koplik spots (small white-blue spots on the buccal mucosa, meaning inner cheek)
* maculopapular rash (flat + raised red rash)

The easy memory:

> Measles = cough + coryza + conjunctivitis + Koplik spots + descending rash.

---

### Why the findings happen

The virus enters through the respiratory tract.

Then it spreads through lymphoid tissue and blood, so it becomes a **systemic infection** rather than just a local throat infection.

That explains why you see:

* **respiratory symptoms:** virus enters through airway mucosa
* **conjunctivitis:** mucosal inflammation also affects the eyes
* **Koplik spots:** infected/inflamed oral mucosa
* **rash:** immune T cells attack virus-infected endothelial cells (cells lining blood vessels) in the skin
* **immunosuppression:** measles disrupts immune memory, so patients become more vulnerable to other infections

---

### Virus structure

Important surface proteins:

* **H protein** = hemagglutinin protein; helps the virus attach to host cells
* **F protein** = fusion protein; helps the viral envelope fuse with the host cell membrane

Intuition:

* H = hold on
* F = fuse in

So the virus first attaches, then fuses, then releases its RNA into the cell.

---

### Complications

Important complications include:

* pneumonia
* otitis media (middle ear infection)
* encephalitis (brain inflammation)
* SSPE (subacute sclerosing panencephalitis), a rare delayed fatal brain disease years after infection

---

## One-line intuition

Measles virus is a very contagious enveloped RNA virus that enters through the respiratory tract, spreads systemically, and causes the classic 3 C's plus Koplik spots and a descending rash.

---

## Pearl

Measles causes transient immune suppression, so vitamin A deficiency and secondary infections make it much more dangerous, especially in children.
