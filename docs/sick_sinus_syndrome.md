# Sick sinus syndrome

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/heart-conduction-system)

![Image](https://uark.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/88/2024/05/Sick-sinus-syndrome.jpg)

![Image](https://nl.ecgpedia.org/images/thumb/7/7c/SSS_ecg_001.jpg/600px-SSS_ecg_001.jpg)

Sick sinus syndrome is dysfunction of the SA node (sinoatrial node), which is the heart's normal pacemaker.

So the SA node cannot reliably set the rhythm.

That can cause:

* sinus bradycardia (slow sinus rhythm)
* sinus pauses/arrest (temporary failure of the SA node to fire)
* tachy-brady syndrome (alternating fast and slow rhythms)

---

### Core idea

The heart's natural pacemaker is "sick," so the rhythm becomes too slow, pauses, or flips between fast and slow.

Think:

> broken pacemaker = unreliable heart timing

---

### Why symptoms happen

If the SA node fires too slowly or pauses:

* cardiac output drops
* brain perfusion drops
* patient gets dizziness, syncope (fainting), fatigue, or confusion

If it alternates with tachyarrhythmias (fast abnormal rhythms), especially atrial fibrillation (irregular atrial rhythm):

* palpitations can happen
* embolic stroke risk can increase

---

### Classic patient

Usually older adults because aging causes fibrosis (scarring) of the SA node and nearby conduction tissue.

Also can be worsened by:

* beta blockers (drugs that block beta-adrenergic receptors and slow heart rate)
* calcium channel blockers (drugs that reduce calcium entry into cardiac/smooth muscle cells)
* digoxin (drug that increases vagal tone and can slow AV node conduction)

---

### Diagnosis intuition

A single ECG (electrocardiogram) can miss it because the rhythm problem may come and go.

So diagnosis often needs Holter monitoring (portable ECG monitoring over time).

---

### Treatment intuition

If symptomatic bradycardia or pauses are present:

* permanent pacemaker

Why?

Because medications can slow or speed the heart, but they do not fix the unreliable SA node.

---

## One-line intuition

Sick sinus syndrome = an unreliable SA node causing slow beats, pauses, or tachy-brady swings.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Tachy-brady syndrome commonly alternates between atrial fibrillation (fast irregular atrial rhythm) and long sinus pauses after the atrial fibrillation stops.
