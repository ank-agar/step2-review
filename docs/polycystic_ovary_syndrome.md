### Core idea

Polycystic ovary syndrome, or PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome), is a hormonal disorder where the ovaries make too much androgen (male-type sex hormone), causing:

* irregular periods
* infertility/subfertility
* hirsutism (excess male-pattern hair growth)
* acne
* polycystic ovaries on ultrasound

Important: the "cysts" are usually not true ovarian cysts.

They are small immature follicles (egg-containing sacs) that did not fully mature and ovulate.

---

### Why the findings happen

In PCOS, there is often increased LH (luteinizing hormone) relative to FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone).

LH stimulates theca cells (ovarian cells that make androgen) to make more androgen.

That androgen causes:

* **hirsutism/acne:** androgen acts on hair follicles and sebaceous glands
* **irregular periods:** follicles do not mature normally, so ovulation is inconsistent
* **multiple small follicles:** follicles get "stuck" instead of one dominant follicle ovulating
* **infertility:** no ovulation means no egg release

Insulin resistance (body tissues respond poorly to insulin) also worsens PCOS because high insulin increases ovarian androgen production.

---

### Classic diagnosis idea

Think:

* hyperandrogenism (high androgen effect)
* ovulatory dysfunction (irregular or absent ovulation)
* polycystic ovaries on ultrasound

You usually need 2 of these 3 after excluding other causes.

---

## One-line intuition

PCOS is basically "too much ovarian androgen + insulin resistance," which blocks normal follicle maturation and ovulation.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PCOS increases risk of endometrial hyperplasia (thickened uterine lining) because chronic anovulation (lack of ovulation) means estrogen is not opposed by progesterone.
