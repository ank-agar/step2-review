### Core idea

Parainfluenza viruses are respiratory viruses that classically cause:

* croup
* bronchiolitis
* pneumonia
* cold-like upper respiratory infections

They are **not influenza viruses**.

Parainfluenza belongs to the **Paramyxoviridae** family.

Break it down:

* para- = beside / similar to
* influenza = flu-like illness

So parainfluenza means:

> a flu-like respiratory virus, but not actual influenza.

---

### What kind of virus is it?

Parainfluenza is:

* enveloped
* negative-sense single-stranded RNA (ribonucleic acid)
* nonsegmented
* Paramyxovirus family

Important proteins:

* F protein = fusion protein
* HN protein = hemagglutinin-neuraminidase protein

The **F protein** helps infected cells fuse together, which can make **syncytia** (multinucleated giant cells).

---

### Classic disease: croup

Parainfluenza is the classic cause of **croup**.

Croup = inflammation of the:

* larynx
* trachea
* bronchi

This is why croup is also called **laryngotracheobronchitis**.

Symptoms:

* barking cough
* inspiratory stridor
* hoarseness
* low-grade fever

**Inspiratory stridor** means a harsh sound while breathing in, usually from upper airway narrowing.

---

### Why the barking cough/stridor happens

Parainfluenza infects the upper airway and causes swelling under the vocal cords.

That area is called the **subglottic region**.

When it swells:

* airway gets narrower
* air rushes through a tight space
* you hear stridor
* cough sounds “barky”

One-line intuition:

> Parainfluenza makes the upper airway swollen and tight, so breathing becomes noisy and the cough sounds barky.

---

### X-ray finding

Croup can show the **steeple sign** on neck X-ray.

Steeple sign = narrowed upper trachea that looks like a church steeple.

This happens because the subglottic airway is swollen and narrowed.

---

### Treatment idea

Most cases are clinical diagnosis and supportive care.

Treatment can include:

* humidified air/supportive care
* dexamethasone, a corticosteroid that decreases inflammation
* nebulized epinephrine for more severe airway swelling

Reasoning:

* steroid decreases airway edema
* epinephrine causes vasoconstriction, which quickly shrinks swollen mucosa

---

## One-line intuition

Parainfluenza is the Paramyxovirus that classically causes croup: barky cough + inspiratory stridor from subglottic airway swelling.

---

## Pearl

Parainfluenza causes **croup**, while **influenza** is an Orthomyxovirus with a segmented genome.

HY Step 1 contrast:

* Paramyxovirus = nonsegmented negative-sense RNA
* Orthomyxovirus = segmented negative-sense RNA
