### Core idea

Schizoid personality disorder is a **Cluster A personality disorder** (odd/eccentric personality disorder group) where the person has:

* little desire for close relationships
* preference for being alone
* restricted emotional expression
* indifference to praise or criticism

The key word is **detached**.

---

### What it looks like

A patient may:

* choose solitary activities
* have few or no close friends
* seem emotionally cold or flat
* not care much about social approval
* have little interest in sexual or romantic relationships

This is not just being introverted. It becomes a disorder when the pattern is persistent and causes impairment in functioning.

---

### Why the findings happen

The core issue is **low drive for interpersonal closeness**.

So:

* they avoid relationships because they do not strongly desire them
* they seem flat because emotional expression toward others is limited
* criticism/praise does not affect them much because social validation is not a major motivator

Intuition:

> Schizoid = socially detached and emotionally flat, but not psychotic.

---

### High-yield differentiations

**Schizoid vs avoidant personality disorder**

* Schizoid: does **not want** close relationships
* Avoidant: **wants** relationships but avoids them because of fear of rejection

**Schizoid vs schizotypal personality disorder**

* Schizoid: detached, flat, solitary
* Schizotypal: odd beliefs, magical thinking, unusual perceptions

**Schizoid vs schizophrenia**

* Schizoid: no hallucinations or delusions
* Schizophrenia: psychosis (loss of reality testing), hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thought

---

## One-line intuition

Schizoid personality disorder is a lifelong pattern of preferring distance from people, with little emotional expression or desire for close relationships.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Schizoid personality disorder is **ego-syntonic** (the person usually sees the behavior as normal), so patients often do **not** seek treatment on their own.
