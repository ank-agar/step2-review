## Euthyroid sick syndrome

![Image](https://dm5migu4zj3pb.cloudfront.net/manuscripts/60000/60047/medium/JCI60047.f1.jpg)

![Image](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/image/imgsrv.fcgi?cid=644280&t=l)

![Image](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/image/imgsrv.fcgi?cid=5920&t=l)

Euthyroid sick syndrome = abnormal thyroid labs during severe illness, but the thyroid gland itself is not primarily broken.

Also called:

* nonthyroidal illness syndrome

Nonthyroidal = the problem is outside the thyroid.

---

### Core lab pattern

Most classic:

* low T3 (triiodothyronine)
* increased rT3 (reverse T3)
* normal or low TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
* T4 (thyroxine) usually normal, but can become low in severe illness

So Step 1 shortcut:

> sick patient + low T3 + high reverse T3 = euthyroid sick syndrome.

---

### Why this happens

Normally:

* T4 gets converted into T3
* T3 is the active thyroid hormone

During severe illness, starvation, trauma, sepsis, MI (myocardial infarction), surgery, etc:

* the body decreases conversion of T4 to active T3
* more T4 gets shunted toward rT3

rT3 (reverse T3) is inactive.

So the body is basically lowering metabolism during stress.

---

### Intuition

The thyroid is not failing.

The body is just saying:

> “We are critically sick, so stop spending energy.”

That is why T3 drops.

---

### Why TSH is not high

In true primary hypothyroidism:

* thyroid hormone is low
* pituitary senses this
* TSH goes up

But in euthyroid sick syndrome:

* illness/cytokines suppress the hypothalamus-pituitary-thyroid axis
* hypothalamus = brain region controlling pituitary hormones
* pituitary = gland that releases TSH

So TSH is often normal or low instead of appropriately high.

---

### Treatment idea

Treat the underlying illness, not the thyroid labs.

Usually do not give thyroid hormone unless there is real hypothyroidism too.

---

## One-line intuition

Euthyroid sick syndrome is a “sick body energy-saving mode”: low active T3, high inactive rT3, and no primary thyroid disease.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do not confuse euthyroid sick syndrome with primary hypothyroidism:

* primary hypothyroidism: high TSH
* euthyroid sick syndrome: normal/low TSH with low T3 and high rT3
