### Core idea

Pernicious anemia is autoimmune vitamin B12 deficiency.

The body makes antibodies against:

* intrinsic factor (protein from stomach parietal cells that helps absorb vitamin B12)
* parietal cells (stomach cells that make intrinsic factor and acid)

So the patient cannot absorb vitamin B12 well in the terminal ileum (last part of the small intestine).

---

### Why anemia happens

Vitamin B12 is needed for DNA synthesis (making DNA).

If DNA synthesis is low:

* the nucleus matures slowly
* the cytoplasm keeps growing
* cells become big and immature

That gives megaloblastic anemia (large immature RBC precursor problem from impaired DNA synthesis).

Classic smear findings:

* macro-ovalocytes (large oval red blood cells)
* hypersegmented neutrophils (neutrophils with too many nuclear lobes)

---

### Why neurologic symptoms happen

Vitamin B12 is also needed for myelin maintenance.

Myelin is the insulation around nerves.

Low B12 can cause subacute combined degeneration (demyelination of dorsal columns and lateral corticospinal tracts in the spinal cord).

That can cause:

* paresthesias (tingling/numbness)
* loss of vibration/proprioception (position sense)
* weakness/spasticity

This is a big difference from folate deficiency: folate deficiency causes megaloblastic anemia too, but not the neurologic deficits.

---

### Labs

Expected labs:

* low vitamin B12
* high methylmalonic acid, or MMA (marker that rises in B12 deficiency)
* high homocysteine (amino acid that rises in B12 or folate deficiency)
* increased MCV (mean corpuscular volume; RBC size)
* anti-intrinsic factor antibodies

MMA helps separate B12 from folate:

* B12 deficiency: high MMA + high homocysteine
* folate deficiency: normal MMA + high homocysteine

---

## One-line intuition

The stomach stops making usable intrinsic factor, so B12 cannot get absorbed, so DNA synthesis fails and the RBC precursors get huge.

---

## Pearl

Pernicious anemia is associated with chronic autoimmune gastritis and increased risk of gastric adenocarcinoma, so remember:

**pernicious anemia = autoimmune stomach problem causing B12 malabsorption.**
