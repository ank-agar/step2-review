### Core idea

Minimal change disease is a kidney disease where the glomeruli (tiny kidney filters) look almost normal on light microscopy, but the podocyte foot processes (little “feet” that help keep protein in the blood) are damaged on electron microscopy.

Classic presentation:

* child
* sudden periorbital edema (swelling around the eyes)
* heavy proteinuria (protein in urine)
* nephrotic syndrome (protein loss in urine causing low blood albumin, edema, and high lipids)

---

### Why it is called “minimal change”

On light microscopy:

* glomeruli look normal
* no obvious inflammation
* no obvious immune deposits

So it looks like there is “minimal change.”

But on electron microscopy:

* podocyte foot processes are effaced

Effaced means flattened/fused together.

---

### Why protein leaks out

Normally, podocyte foot processes act like a fine filter around the glomerular basement membrane (GBM, the filtration barrier of the glomerulus).

In minimal change disease:

* T-cell cytokines (immune signaling proteins) injure podocytes
* podocyte foot processes flatten
* the filtration barrier becomes leaky to albumin

Albumin is a major blood protein.

So:

> damaged podocytes = albumin leaks into urine = edema

---

### Why edema happens

Albumin normally holds water inside blood vessels using oncotic pressure (protein-pulling pressure).

If albumin is lost in urine:

* blood albumin falls
* oncotic pressure falls
* fluid leaves blood vessels
* swelling develops

This is why kids can wake up with puffy eyes.

---

### Key associations

Minimal change disease can be triggered by:

* recent infection
* NSAIDs (nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs)
* Hodgkin lymphoma

But in children, it is often idiopathic (no clear cause).

---

## One-line intuition

Minimal change disease is “normal-looking glomeruli, but podocyte feet are flattened, so albumin spills into urine.”

---

## Pearl

Minimal change disease is the most common cause of nephrotic syndrome in children and usually responds well to corticosteroids (anti-inflammatory steroid medications like prednisone).
