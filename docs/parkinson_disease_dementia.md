Parkinson disease dementia is dementia that develops **after established Parkinson disease**.

Parkinson disease = movement disorder from degeneration of dopaminergic neurons (dopamine-producing neurons) in the substantia nigra (midbrain region that helps control movement).

Dementia = decline in cognition severe enough to impair daily function.

So Parkinson disease dementia means:

> Parkinson motor symptoms come first, then cognition progressively worsens later.

---

### Core idea

The same Lewy body pathology that causes Parkinson motor symptoms spreads/affects more brain regions involved in:

* attention
* executive function
* visuospatial processing
* memory
* behavior

Lewy bodies are abnormal intracellular aggregates (clumps inside cells) made mostly of alpha-synuclein (a presynaptic neuronal protein).

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia (slow movement):** loss of dopamine (movement neurotransmitter) from substantia nigra neurons
* **Dementia:** Lewy body disease affects cortical and limbic areas (thinking, memory, emotion)
* **Visual hallucinations:** Lewy body dementias commonly disrupt visual processing networks
* **Fluctuating cognition:** attention/arousal circuits are affected, so alertness can vary
* **REM sleep behavior disorder:** REM sleep paralysis is impaired, so patients may act out dreams

---

### Key distinction

Timing is the testable point:

* **Parkinson disease dementia:** Parkinson motor symptoms for at least 1 year before dementia
* **Dementia with Lewy bodies:** dementia occurs before or within 1 year of parkinsonism

Same general Lewy body family, different timing.

---

## One-line intuition

Parkinson disease dementia is Parkinson disease that has spread from mainly movement circuitry into cognition circuitry.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Lewy body dementias can have severe sensitivity to typical antipsychotics (dopamine-blocking drugs), which may worsen parkinsonism and confusion.
