## Testicular torsion

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c8/Testicular_torsion.svg/960px-Testicular_torsion.svg.png?utm_campaign=index&utm_content=thumbnail&utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Scrotal_ultrasonography_of_testicular_torsion.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ac/Torsion_teste_aguda_%28cropped%29.jpg)

Testicular torsion is when the testicle twists around the spermatic cord (the cord containing the blood vessels, nerves, and vas deferens that connect to the testicle).

That twist cuts off blood flow.

So the big danger is:

* ischemia (low blood supply)
* infarction (tissue death)
* loss of the testicle if not treated fast

---

### Core idea

The testicle is basically hanging by a cord.

If it rotates, the cord gets kinked like a twisted garden hose.

Less blood gets in and out, so the testicle becomes:

* suddenly very painful
* swollen
* high-riding
* sometimes horizontally lying

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Sudden severe pain:** acute ischemia irritates pain fibers
* **Nausea/vomiting:** severe visceral pain can trigger autonomic symptoms
* **Absent cremasteric reflex:** cremasteric reflex means stroking the inner thigh normally makes the testicle rise; torsion often knocks this reflex out
* **High-riding testis:** the twisted spermatic cord shortens/pulls the testicle upward
* **Decreased Doppler flow:** Doppler ultrasound (ultrasound that checks blood flow) shows reduced or absent blood flow

---

### Classic risk factor

Bell clapper deformity = abnormal fixation of the testicle inside the scrotum, so it swings too freely.

Intuition:

> the testicle can rotate like a clapper inside a bell.

That makes torsion more likely.

---

### Treatment

This is a surgical emergency.

Definitive treatment is orchiopexy (surgically fixing the testicle in place).

Usually both sides are fixed because the anatomic risk is often bilateral (on both sides).

---

## One-line intuition

Testicular torsion is a twisted spermatic cord cutting off blood flow to the testicle.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Torsion is usually **sudden severe testicular pain with absent cremasteric reflex**, while epididymitis (inflammation of the epididymis, the tube behind the testicle that stores sperm) is usually more gradual and may improve with elevation of the scrotum.
