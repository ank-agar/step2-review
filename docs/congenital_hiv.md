### Core idea

Congenital HIV means a baby gets HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) from the mother around birth.

More exact term:

* **perinatal HIV transmission** = HIV passed from mother to baby during pregnancy, delivery, or breastfeeding

So the baby is not “born with a genetic HIV problem.”

The baby acquires an infection from maternal blood/secretions/breast milk.

---

### How transmission happens

HIV can pass:

* **in utero** = across the placenta during pregnancy
* **intrapartum** = during delivery, from exposure to maternal blood/genital secretions
* **postpartum** = after birth through breastfeeding

Biggest risk factor:

* high maternal viral load

Viral load means the amount of HIV RNA (ribonucleic acid) in the blood.

More virus in mom = more chance the baby gets exposed to enough virus to become infected.

---

### Why babies get sick

HIV infects **CD4 T cells** (helper T lymphocytes).

CD4 T cells normally coordinate immune responses, especially against infections.

So when HIV destroys/impairs them, the child becomes prone to:

* recurrent infections
* failure to thrive
* chronic diarrhea
* pneumonia
* oral thrush
* lymphadenopathy
* hepatosplenomegaly

Lymphadenopathy means enlarged lymph nodes.

Hepatosplenomegaly means enlarged liver and spleen.

---

### Diagnosis intuition

In adults, HIV screening often uses antibody tests.

But in newborns, maternal IgG (immunoglobulin G) antibodies cross the placenta.

So an HIV antibody test in a newborn can be positive just because mom’s antibodies crossed over.

That does **not** prove the baby is infected.

Instead, use:

* HIV nucleic acid testing
* HIV RNA/DNA PCR (polymerase chain reaction)

These look for the virus itself, not just antibodies.

---

### Prevention

Risk drops a lot with:

* maternal ART (antiretroviral therapy)
* low/undetectable maternal viral load
* neonatal ART prophylaxis
* avoiding breastfeeding when safe alternatives are available
* C-section in selected high viral load cases

ART means antiretroviral therapy, which is medication that blocks HIV replication.

---

## One-line intuition

Congenital HIV is really vertical transmission of HIV from mother to baby, and the key driver is how much virus the baby is exposed to.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1: do **not** rely on HIV antibody testing to diagnose HIV in a newborn because maternal IgG crosses the placenta; use HIV RNA/DNA PCR instead.
