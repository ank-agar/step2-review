# Severe combined immunodeficiency

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/502e/7874439/b0ed8cfb8c20/nihms-1659521-f0001.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK22254/bin/ADA.jpg)

Severe combined immunodeficiency, or **SCID (severe combined immunodeficiency)**, is a genetic disease where the immune system is basically missing its strongest soldiers:

* **T cells (T lymphocytes)**
* **B cells (B lymphocytes)**
* sometimes **NK cells (natural killer cells)**

So the baby cannot fight infections well.

---

### Core idea

**Combined** means both major adaptive immune arms are affected:

* **cell-mediated immunity** = mainly T cells
* **humoral immunity** = B cells making antibodies

So SCID = severe failure of both T-cell and B-cell immunity.

Even if B cells are present in some types, they often do not work well because B cells need T-cell help.

---

### Why infections happen

T cells normally:

* kill virus-infected cells
* activate macrophages
* help B cells class switch and make good antibodies

B cells normally:

* become plasma cells
* make antibodies

So if T cells are absent or nonfunctional:

* viral infections become severe
* fungal/opportunistic infections happen
* B cells cannot make strong antibody responses

Classic infections:

* recurrent pneumonia
* chronic diarrhea
* oral thrush from Candida
* severe viral infections
* failure to thrive

---

### Important causes

Common high-yield causes:

* **IL2RG (interleukin-2 receptor gamma gene) mutation** = X-linked SCID (X chromosome-linked severe combined immunodeficiency)
* **ADA (adenosine deaminase) deficiency**
* **RAG1/RAG2 (recombination activating gene 1 and 2) defects**

Why ADA deficiency causes SCID:

* ADA normally breaks down purines
* without ADA, toxic purine metabolites build up
* lymphocytes are very sensitive to this toxicity
* T cells, B cells, and NK cells die or fail to mature

So ADA deficiency is like poisoning the lymphocyte factory.

---

### Newborn screening idea

Newborn screening often checks **TREC (T-cell receptor excision circle)** levels.

TREC forms when new T cells mature in the thymus.

Low TREC means:

* not enough new T cells are being made
* think SCID until proven otherwise

---

## One-line intuition

SCID is the “no functional adaptive immune system” disease: no useful T-cell help, weak/absent antibody defense, and life-threatening infections early in infancy.

---

## Pearl

For Step 1, remember: **X-linked SCID from IL2RG mutation = low T cells, low NK cells, but B cells may be present and nonfunctional**.
