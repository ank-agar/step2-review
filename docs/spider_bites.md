### Core idea

A spider bite is skin injury from spider fangs, sometimes with venom injection.

Most spider bites are mild and look like:

* small red bump
* local pain
* itching
* swelling

The Step 1 relevant spiders are mainly:

* **brown recluse**
* **black widow**

---

### Brown recluse

Brown recluse venom causes local tissue injury.

Mechanism:

* venom contains **sphingomyelinase D** (enzyme that damages cell membranes)
* this injures endothelial cells (blood vessel lining cells)
* causes inflammation + thrombosis (clotting in small vessels)
* decreased blood flow leads to skin necrosis (tissue death)

So the classic lesion can become:

* painful
* blistering
* blue/purple
* necrotic ulcer

Intuition:

> brown recluse = local skin destruction.

---

### Black widow

Black widow venom mainly affects nerves.

Mechanism:

* venom has **alpha-latrotoxin** (toxin that triggers neurotransmitter release)
* causes massive release of acetylcholine and catecholamines
* leads to painful muscle cramping and autonomic symptoms

Findings:

* severe muscle cramps
* abdominal rigidity/pain
* sweating
* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* nausea/vomiting

Intuition:

> black widow = neurotoxic muscle cramping.

---

### How to tell them apart

* **Brown recluse:** skin necrosis
* **Black widow:** muscle cramps + autonomic symptoms

So if the question stem says a bite turns into an ulcer, think brown recluse.

If it says painful cramps, sweating, or abdominal rigidity, think black widow.

---

### First aid

Basic management:

* wash with soap and water
* cold pack
* elevate the area
* pain control
* seek care for severe symptoms, spreading redness, fever, necrosis, or suspected black widow/brown recluse bite

Do **not** try to suck out venom.

---

## One-line intuition

Most spider bites are just local inflammation, but brown recluse destroys skin and black widow overstimulates nerves.

---

## Pearl 🔥

**Brown recluse = sphingomyelinase D → necrotic skin ulcer.**

**Black widow = alpha-latrotoxin → neurotransmitter release → painful muscle cramps.**
