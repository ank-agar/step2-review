# Legionella

![Image](https://www.cdc.gov/legionella/water-system-maintenance/images/people-at-risk-graphic.png)

Legionella is a Gram-negative rod bacteria that causes:

* Legionnaires disease = severe pneumonia (lung infection)
* Pontiac fever = mild flu-like illness without pneumonia

The main Step 1 species is:

* Legionella pneumophila

---

### Core idea

Think:

> Legionella lives in water, gets aerosolized, then hides inside macrophages.

Macrophages are immune cells that normally eat and kill bacteria.

But Legionella survives inside them by blocking phagolysosome fusion.

Phagolysosome fusion means:

* phagosome = vesicle that swallowed the bug
* lysosome = enzyme-filled killing compartment
* fusion = combining them so the bug gets destroyed

So if fusion is blocked, the macrophage becomes a protected apartment for the bacteria.

---

### How people get it

You get Legionella by inhaling aerosolized water droplets.

Common sources:

* air-conditioning cooling towers
* hot tubs
* showers
* plumbing systems
* fountains

Important:

* not usually spread person-to-person

So the logic is:

water system grows bacteria -> mist forms -> mist is inhaled -> bacteria reaches alveolar macrophages.

Alveolar macrophages are macrophages in the alveoli (tiny air sacs of the lung).

---

### Symptoms

Legionella causes atypical pneumonia.

Atypical pneumonia means pneumonia with symptoms that may not look like classic lobar pneumonia.

Classic findings:

* fever
* cough
* shortness of breath
* diarrhea
* confusion
* hyponatremia

Hyponatremia means low sodium in the blood.

Diarrhea + confusion + pneumonia is a big clue.

---

### Why diarrhea and confusion happen

Legionella is not just sitting in the lung like a simple local infection.

It causes a systemic inflammatory illness, meaning the immune response affects the whole body.

So you can get:

* GI (gastrointestinal) symptoms like diarrhea
* CNS (central nervous system) symptoms like confusion
* SIADH (syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone), causing hyponatremia

SIADH means too much ADH (antidiuretic hormone), so the body holds onto water and dilutes sodium.

---

### Diagnosis

Best quick test:

* urine antigen test

Antigen means a piece of the organism that the immune system can recognize.

Legionella also grows on:

* charcoal yeast extract agar

Agar means the jelly-like plate used to grow bacteria in the lab.

It does not grow well on routine culture media, so the lab needs special media.

---

### Treatment

Use antibiotics that get inside cells because Legionella lives intracellularly.

Intracellular means inside cells.

Good choices:

* azithromycin
* fluoroquinolones, like levofloxacin

Why?

Because beta-lactams (penicillin-like drugs) mostly work outside cells and are weaker for intracellular organisms.

---

## One-line intuition

Legionella is a water-loving intracellular bug that causes atypical pneumonia with diarrhea, confusion, and low sodium.

---
