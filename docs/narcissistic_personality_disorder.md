# Narcissistic personality disorder

![Image](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/-/scassets/images/org/health/articles/9742-narcissistic-personality-disorder)

Narcissistic personality disorder, or **NPD (narcissistic personality disorder)**, is a **Cluster B personality disorder**.

Cluster B means dramatic, emotional, or erratic personality disorders.

Core triad:

* grandiosity
* need for admiration
* lack of empathy

In simple terms:

> The person’s self-esteem depends heavily on feeling superior, special, or admired by others.

---

### Core idea

NPD is not just “high confidence.”

It is more like:

* fragile self-esteem underneath
* overcompensating with superiority
* needing other people to validate that superiority
* becoming angry, ashamed, or defensive when criticized

So the outside may look like arrogance, but the inside is often unstable self-worth.

---

### Common features

Patients may show:

* exaggerated sense of self-importance
* fantasies of success, power, beauty, or brilliance
* belief that they are special or unique
* need for excessive admiration
* sense of entitlement
* exploiting others
* lack of empathy
* envy of others, or belief others envy them
* arrogant behavior

Diagnosis usually requires a persistent pattern starting by early adulthood and present across many situations.

---

### Why the behavior happens

The key mechanism is:

> fragile self-esteem + need for admiration = defensive grandiosity.

So if someone criticizes them, it can feel like a direct threat to their identity.

That can lead to:

* rage
* dismissing the critic
* blaming others
* trying to regain status or control

---

### Step 1 association

NPD is a **Cluster B personality disorder**, along with:

* antisocial personality disorder
* borderline personality disorder
* histrionic personality disorder

Quick contrast:

* **Narcissistic:** “I am special; admire me.”
* **Histrionic:** “Look at me.”
* **Borderline:** unstable relationships, identity, mood, and fear of abandonment.
* **Antisocial:** violates rights of others, lacks remorse.

---

## One-line intuition

NPD is pathologic self-importance where the person needs admiration to protect fragile self-esteem and has poor empathy for others.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Narcissistic personality disorder is **ego-syntonic**.

**Ego-syntonic** means the person usually sees their thoughts/behavior as acceptable or justified, so they often do **not** think they are the problem.
