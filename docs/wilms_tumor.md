### Core idea

Wilms tumor is a malignant kidney tumor in children.

Another name is nephroblastoma:

* nephro- = kidney
* blastoma = tumor made of immature embryonic cells

So the intuition is:

> fetal kidney cells failed to mature properly, then later grew into a tumor.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* child age 2 to 5
* painless abdominal mass
* hematuria (blood in urine)
* hypertension (high blood pressure)

Why hypertension?

The tumor can compress kidney tissue and renal blood flow. The kidney senses “low flow” and releases renin (enzyme that activates the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system), which raises blood pressure.

---

### Genetics/associations

Wilms tumor is linked to problems on chromosome 11, especially:

* WT1 gene (Wilms tumor 1 gene) on chromosome 11p13
* WT2 region on chromosome 11p15

Associated syndromes:

* WAGR syndrome = Wilms tumor, aniridia (absent iris), genitourinary abnormalities, intellectual disability
* Denys-Drash syndrome = Wilms tumor + gonadal dysgenesis (abnormal gonad development) + nephropathy (kidney disease)
* Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome = organ overgrowth, macroglossia (large tongue), hemihyperplasia (one side/body part overgrowth), ↑ risk of Wilms tumor

---

### Histology

Classic Wilms tumor is triphasic, meaning 3 tissue patterns:

* blastemal cells = primitive small blue cells
* epithelial cells = tubules/glomeruloid structures
* stromal cells = connective tissue-like component

One-line intuition:

It looks like a kidney trying to develop, but in a disorganized malignant way.

---

### Important distinction

Wilms tumor:

* kidney origin
* usually smooth abdominal mass
* usually does **not** cross midline

Neuroblastoma:

* adrenal/sympathetic nervous system origin
* often crosses midline
* can calcify
* can increase catecholamines (epinephrine/norepinephrine-like stress hormones)

---

## One-line intuition

Wilms tumor is a pediatric kidney cancer from immature embryonic kidney cells that presents as a painless abdominal mass and can cause hypertension.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do **not** aggressively palpate a suspected Wilms tumor because rupture/spillage can spread tumor cells and worsen staging.
