## MUTYH-associated polyposis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/72/BER_basic_pathway.svg/960px-BER_basic_pathway.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/Tubular_adenoma_in_MUTYH_polyposis_--_very_low_mag.jpg/960px-Tubular_adenoma_in_MUTYH_polyposis_--_very_low_mag.jpg)

MUTYH-associated polyposis is an inherited colon cancer syndrome caused by **biallelic MUTYH mutations**.

Break that down:

* **MUTYH** = a DNA repair gene
* **biallelic** = both copies of the gene are mutated
* **polyposis** = lots of polyps, which are abnormal tissue growths from mucosa

Inheritance is:

* **autosomal recessive** = you need 2 bad copies to get the disease

---

### Core idea

MUTYH normally helps with **base excision repair**.

Base excision repair means:

> removing and replacing one damaged DNA base.

So if MUTYH is broken, oxidative DNA damage is not repaired well.

That lets mutations build up in colon cells, especially in growth-control genes, leading to:

* many adenomatous polyps
* increased colorectal cancer risk

---

### What it looks like clinically

Patients can develop:

* 10s to 100s of colon adenomas
* colorectal cancer at a younger age
* sometimes duodenal adenomas

**Adenoma** = benign glandular tumor, but in the colon it is premalignant.

Premalignant means it is not cancer yet, but it can become cancer over time.

---

### Why this causes colon cancer

Think:

DNA damage keeps happening normally.

MUTYH is supposed to fix small base-level mistakes.

If both MUTYH copies are defective:

* small DNA errors persist
* mutations accumulate
* colon epithelial cells gain growth advantages
* adenomas form
* adenomas can progress to adenocarcinoma

So the intuition is:

> broken DNA typo-correction system → mutation buildup → colon polyps → colon cancer risk.

---

### How to distinguish from FAP

**FAP** = familial adenomatous polyposis.

Both can cause many adenomatous colon polyps.

But:

* **FAP:** autosomal dominant, APC mutation
* **MUTYH-associated polyposis:** autosomal recessive, MUTYH mutation

So MUTYH-associated polyposis can look like attenuated FAP, but the inheritance pattern is different.

---

## One-line intuition

MUTYH-associated polyposis is **autosomal recessive failure of base excision repair**, causing lots of colon adenomas and high colorectal cancer risk.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association:

**MUTYH = base excision repair defect**, not nucleotide excision repair.

Base excision repair fixes small base damage; nucleotide excision repair fixes bulky DNA lesions like thymine dimers from UV light.
