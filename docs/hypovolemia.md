# Hypovolemia

![Image](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f%3Dwebp/apps/archive/20260407.195030/resources/e08b3fa56ffe450c120fde3e7cbfe301d2fdc45b)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17223.jpg)

Hypovolemia means low circulating blood volume.

Break it down:

* hypo- = low
* vol- = volume
* -emia = in the blood

So hypovolemia = not enough effective fluid inside the blood vessels.

---

### Core idea

The body does not have enough intravascular volume (fluid inside blood vessels), so less blood returns to the heart.

Less venous return means:

* decreased preload (ventricular filling before contraction)
* decreased stroke volume (blood pumped per beat)
* decreased cardiac output (blood pumped per minute)
* decreased tissue perfusion (blood delivery to organs)

---

### Common causes

Hypovolemia can happen from losing:

* blood: trauma, GI (gastrointestinal) bleed, surgery
* water/salt: vomiting, diarrhea, sweating, burns
* fluid into "third spaces": pancreatitis, bowel obstruction, ascites (fluid in the abdomen)

The key is: fluid is either leaving the body or leaving the blood vessels.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Tachycardia (fast heart rate):** sympathetic nervous system tries to maintain cardiac output
* **Hypotension (low blood pressure):** not enough volume to fill the vascular system
* **Cool, clammy skin:** vasoconstriction shunts blood to brain/heart
* **Low urine output:** kidneys sense low perfusion and conserve fluid
* **Thirst/dry mucous membranes:** body is trying to restore volume
* **Altered mental status:** brain is not getting enough perfusion

---

### Hypovolemia vs dehydration

Dehydration = low total body water.

Hypovolemia = low circulating vascular volume.

They often overlap, but they are not exactly the same.

Example:

* diarrhea can cause dehydration + hypovolemia
* hemorrhage causes hypovolemia even if total body water is not the main problem

---

## One-line intuition

Hypovolemia is when the blood vessels are underfilled, so the heart has less to pump and organs get less perfusion.

---

## Pearl 🔥

In early hypovolemic shock, tachycardia can show up before hypotension because the body compensates with sympathetic activation before blood pressure finally crashes.
