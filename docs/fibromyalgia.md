# Fibromyalgia

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279092/bin/fibromyalgia-Image002.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/38/Fibromyalgia_pain_sites_APS_2019.svg/1920px-Fibromyalgia_pain_sites_APS_2019.svg.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/9758.jpg)

Fibromyalgia is chronic widespread pain caused mainly by abnormal pain processing in the CNS (central nervous system).

Key idea:

* the muscles hurt
* but the muscles are not being destroyed
* labs and imaging are usually normal

So think:

> the body's pain volume knob is turned up too high.

---

### Break down the word

* fibro = fibrous tissue/connective tissue
* myalgia = muscle pain

So fibromyalgia literally sounds like:

> pain in muscles and connective tissue.

But the real mechanism is more about pain amplification than actual tissue inflammation.

---

### Classic symptoms

* widespread muscle pain
* fatigue
* poor sleep
* brain fog
* headaches
* IBS (irritable bowel syndrome)
* mood symptoms like anxiety/depression

---

### Why the pain happens

The nervous system becomes sensitized.

Sensitized = it responds too strongly to normal input.

So mild pressure or normal body signals can be interpreted as pain.

This can cause:

* hyperalgesia (increased pain from a painful stimulus)
* allodynia (pain from something that should not be painful)

That is why the patient feels real pain even though muscle enzymes, X-rays, and inflammatory markers can be normal.

---

### Diagnosis idea

Fibromyalgia is clinical, meaning based on symptoms and exam.

Older teaching emphasized tender points, but modern diagnosis focuses more on:

* widespread pain
* symptom severity
* duration at least 3 months
* no better explanation

Important:

* CK (creatine kinase) is usually normal
* ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate) is usually normal
* muscle strength is usually normal

So it is not like polymyositis/dermatomyositis, where true muscle inflammation causes proximal weakness.

---

## One-line intuition

Fibromyalgia is chronic widespread pain where the CNS (central nervous system) amplifies pain signals despite no clear tissue damage.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 distinction:

Fibromyalgia causes pain without objective muscle weakness or inflammation.

Polymyositis causes proximal muscle weakness with increased CK (creatine kinase).
