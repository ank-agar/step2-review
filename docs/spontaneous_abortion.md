### Core idea

Spontaneous abortion means miscarriage:

* loss of pregnancy before 20 weeks gestation
* not intentionally induced

So the word "abortion" here just means the pregnancy ended early, and "spontaneous" means it happened naturally.

---

### Why it happens

Most early miscarriages happen because the embryo has a chromosomal abnormality.

Chromosomal abnormality = wrong number or structure of chromosomes (DNA packages).

Intuition:

* embryo has major genetic problem
* development cannot continue normally
* uterus eventually sheds the pregnancy tissue

This is why the most common cause is usually fetal/embryonic, not something the mother "did."

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* vaginal bleeding
* crampy pelvic pain
* passage of tissue

Diagnosis is usually with:

* ultrasound
* beta-hCG (beta human chorionic gonadotropin, a pregnancy hormone)
* pelvic exam

---

### Types to know

* Threatened abortion: bleeding, closed cervix, pregnancy may still continue
* Inevitable abortion: bleeding + open cervix, pregnancy loss is going to happen
* Incomplete abortion: some POC (products of conception, pregnancy tissue) passed, some still inside
* Complete abortion: all POC passed
* Missed abortion: embryo/fetus has died but POC have not passed yet

---

## One-line intuition

Spontaneous abortion is when an early pregnancy fails, most often from chromosomal problems, and the uterus starts expelling the pregnancy tissue.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Most spontaneous abortions occur in the first trimester, and the most common cause is fetal chromosomal abnormality.
