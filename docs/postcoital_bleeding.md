# Postcoital bleeding

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17036.jpg)

Postcoital bleeding means:

* bleeding after sex
* not just normal menstrual bleeding

Break it down:

* post = after
* coital = intercourse/sex
* bleeding = blood coming from the genital tract

So the core idea is:

> sex mechanically irritates something fragile, inflamed, infected, or abnormal, and that area bleeds.

---

### Most common source

Usually the bleeding is coming from the:

* cervix
* vagina

The cervix is especially important because it gets contacted during sex and has a fragile transformation zone.

Transformation zone = the area of the cervix where squamous cells and columnar cells meet.

---

### Common causes

Postcoital bleeding can happen from:

* cervical ectropion
* cervicitis
* cervical polyp
* vaginal dryness/atrophy
* trauma/friction
* cervical cancer or precancer

Cervicitis = inflammation of the cervix, often from sexually transmitted infections like chlamydia or gonorrhea.

Cervical ectropion = inner cervical glandular cells are exposed on the outside of the cervix.

Those glandular cells are delicate, so they bleed more easily when touched.

---

### Why cervical ectropion bleeds

Normally:

* outer cervix has tougher squamous epithelium
* inner cervical canal has softer glandular epithelium

In ectropion:

* the softer glandular tissue is exposed outside
* sex can rub against it
* the tissue bleeds easily

Intuition:

It is like having delicate inner lining exposed on the outside surface.

---

### Why this matters clinically

Postcoital bleeding is often benign, but it should not be ignored because it can be a warning sign of cervical cancer.

That is why evaluation often includes:

* pelvic exam
* Pap smear if due
* HPV testing if appropriate
* STI testing

HPV = human papillomavirus.

STI = sexually transmitted infection.

---

## One-line intuition

Postcoital bleeding usually means the cervix or vagina is irritated and fragile, but cervical cancer must be ruled out.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: postcoital bleeding is a classic red flag symptom for cervical cancer, which is strongly linked to high-risk HPV types 16 and 18.
