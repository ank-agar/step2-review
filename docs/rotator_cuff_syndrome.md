## Rotator cuff syndrome

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Rotator_cuff_muscles.svg/1280px-Rotator_cuff_muscles.svg.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/21755.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5a/Rotator_cuff_tear.jpg)

Rotator cuff syndrome means pain/weakness from irritation or injury of the rotator cuff.

The rotator cuff is the group of 4 muscles/tendons that hold the humeral head (top of the upper arm bone) in the glenoid (shoulder socket).

Think:

* the shoulder is very mobile
* so it is also unstable
* the rotator cuff acts like a dynamic stabilizing cuff around it

The 4 rotator cuff muscles are **SITS**:

* **Supraspinatus**: starts abduction (moving arm away from body)
* **Infraspinatus**: external rotation (rotating arm outward)
* **Teres minor**: external rotation
* **Subscapularis**: internal rotation (rotating arm inward)

---

### Core idea

Rotator cuff syndrome usually means the tendon is:

* inflamed
* irritated/pinched
* degenerated
* partially or fully torn

Most commonly, the **supraspinatus tendon** is involved.

Why?

It runs under the acromion (bony roof of the shoulder), so it can get compressed during overhead motion.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Shoulder pain:** inflamed tendon/bursa gets irritated with movement
* **Pain with overhead activity:** raising the arm narrows the subacromial space (space under the acromion)
* **Weakness with abduction:** supraspinatus helps start arm abduction
* **Night pain:** lying on that shoulder compresses the irritated tendon
* **Limited range of motion:** pain makes the patient avoid movement

---

### Classic exam clue

Painful arc:

* pain when abducting the arm around **60-120 degrees**

That range is where the supraspinatus tendon is getting squeezed under the acromion.

---

## One-line intuition

Rotator cuff syndrome is when the shoulder's stabilizing tendon cuff gets irritated or torn, especially the supraspinatus, causing pain with overhead movement.

---

## Pearl

The **supraspinatus** is tested with the **empty can test**: arms forward/abducted with thumbs down, then resist downward pressure. Pain or weakness suggests supraspinatus/rotator cuff pathology.
