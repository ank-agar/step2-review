### Core idea

Anaphylaxis is a severe, rapid, whole-body allergic reaction.

Think:

* allergen enters body
* mast cells and basophils activate
* massive mediator release
* airway tightens + blood vessels leak + blood pressure drops

Main mediators:

* histamine
* leukotrienes
* prostaglandins
* platelet-activating factor

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Hives/itching/flushing:** histamine causes skin blood vessels to dilate and leak fluid
* **Angioedema:** deeper tissue swelling, especially lips, tongue, eyelids, or throat
* **Wheezing/shortness of breath:** bronchoconstriction, meaning airway smooth muscle tightens
* **Vomiting/diarrhea/cramps:** gut smooth muscle contracts and gut vessels leak fluid
* **Hypotension:** blood vessels dilate and leak plasma, so effective circulating volume drops

So the dangerous combo is:

> airway swelling + bronchoconstriction + shock

---

### Classic triggers

Common triggers include:

* foods, especially peanuts, shellfish, eggs
* insect stings
* medications, especially penicillin
* latex

---

### Treatment idea

First-line treatment is **epinephrine**.

Epinephrine helps because it:

* opens airways through beta-2 receptors
* raises blood pressure through alpha-1 receptors
* decreases further mast cell mediator release through beta-2 receptors

Antihistamines can help itching/hives, but they do **not** fix airway swelling or shock.

---

## One-line intuition

Anaphylaxis is your allergy cells dumping mediators everywhere at once, causing swelling, wheezing, and circulatory collapse.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Anaphylaxis is a **type I hypersensitivity reaction**, usually mediated by **IgE (immunoglobulin E)** bound to mast cells.
