## Postpartum hemorrhage

![Image](https://dgnvxbcc3-res.cloudinary.com/image/upload/v1773105330/Journals/Automation/AFP/2007/0315/p875-f2-jpg.jpg)

![Image](https://www.netterimages.com/images/vpv/000/000/009/9107-0550x0475.jpg)

Postpartum hemorrhage means excessive bleeding after delivery.

Classically:

* more than 500 mL after vaginal delivery
* more than 1000 mL after C-section (cesarean section)

But clinically, think:

> bleeding after birth that is enough to cause instability or require urgent treatment.

---

### Core idea

After the placenta separates, it leaves open maternal blood vessels in the uterus.

Normally, the uterus contracts hard and squeezes those vessels shut.

So the uterus acts like a living tourniquet.

If the uterus does not contract, the vessels stay open and blood pours out.

---

### Most common cause

The most common cause is uterine atony.

Uterine atony means the uterus is soft, boggy, and not contracting well.

Break it down:

* uterine = uterus
* a- = without
* tone = muscle firmness/contraction

So:

> uterine atony = uterus without good muscle tone.

---

### The 4 T's

Causes of postpartum hemorrhage are remembered as the 4 T's:

* **Tone:** uterine atony, most common
* **Trauma:** lacerations (tears), uterine rupture, hematoma (blood collection)
* **Tissue:** retained placenta or placental fragments
* **Thrombin:** coagulation problem, meaning the blood cannot clot well

---

### Why bleeding happens

Normally:

* placenta detaches
* uterine spiral arteries are exposed
* myometrium (uterine smooth muscle layer) contracts
* vessels get compressed
* bleeding stops

In uterine atony:

* myometrium stays relaxed
* spiral arteries stay open
* uterus feels boggy
* postpartum bleeding continues

---

### Classic clue

Heavy vaginal bleeding after delivery + boggy enlarged uterus = uterine atony until proven otherwise.

If the uterus is firm but bleeding continues, think trauma, especially cervical or vaginal laceration.

---

### Treatment intuition

Goal: make the uterus clamp down.

First steps usually include:

* fundal massage
* oxytocin (uterotonic drug that contracts the uterus)
* other uterotonics if needed
* remove retained tissue if present
* repair lacerations if trauma is the cause

Uterotonic means a medication that increases uterine tone.

---

## One-line intuition

Postpartum hemorrhage is usually the uterus failing to squeeze shut the bleeding placental vessels after delivery.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Uterine atony is the most common cause of postpartum hemorrhage, and a boggy uterus points to **Tone** in the 4 T's.
