### Core idea

A backache just means pain in the back.

Most commonly, it is **mechanical low back pain**, meaning pain from the muscles, ligaments, joints, discs, or vertebrae (spine bones) rather than from a dangerous systemic disease.

Common causes:

* muscle strain
* ligament sprain
* poor posture/prolonged sitting
* degenerative disc disease
* herniated disc pressing on a nerve root

---

### Why it hurts

The lower back carries a lot of body weight.

So small problems there can trigger pain:

* **muscle strain:** overstretched muscle fibers become inflamed
* **disc problem:** intervertebral disc (cushion between spine bones) can bulge/herniate and irritate a nerve
* **facet joint irritation:** small spine joints get inflamed with movement/arthritis
* **nerve compression:** pain can shoot down the leg, called sciatica (pain along the sciatic nerve pathway)

---

### When to worry

Back pain is more concerning if there is:

* fever
* weight loss
* history of cancer
* trauma
* weakness or numbness in the legs
* bowel/bladder dysfunction
* saddle anesthesia (numbness around the groin/butt area)

Those suggest something beyond simple muscle strain.

---

## One-line intuition

Backache is usually the spine/muscles saying, "this mechanical structure is irritated," but nerve symptoms or systemic symptoms make it more serious.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Back pain + urinary retention/incontinence + saddle anesthesia = think **cauda equina syndrome** (compression of the lower spinal nerve roots), which is a surgical emergency.
