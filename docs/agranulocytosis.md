# Agranulocytosis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1f/Hematopoiesis_%28human%29_diagram_en.svg/1280px-Hematopoiesis_%28human%29_diagram_en.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/39/Neutrophil.svg/1280px-Neutrophil.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f2/Neutrophil_with_anthrax_copy.jpg/1280px-Neutrophil_with_anthrax_copy.jpg)

Agranulocytosis = dangerously low granulocytes, especially neutrophils.

Granulocytes are white blood cells with cytoplasmic granules, mainly:

* neutrophils
* eosinophils
* basophils

But clinically, agranulocytosis usually means severe neutropenia (low neutrophil count).

ANC (absolute neutrophil count) is very low, classically:

* ANC less than 500 cells/uL = severe neutropenia
* ANC less than 100 cells/uL = agranulocytosis

---

### Why it matters

Neutrophils are the fast bacterial/fungal infection cells.

So if neutrophils drop really low:

* bacteria are not killed well
* fungal infections become more dangerous
* fever can be the only early sign
* infections can progress quickly to sepsis

Sepsis = life-threatening organ dysfunction from infection.

---

### Common causes

Big Step 1 causes:

* clozapine, an antipsychotic medication
* chemotherapy, which suppresses bone marrow
* antithyroid drugs like propylthiouracil and methimazole
* sulfonamides, which are sulfa antibiotics
* aplastic anemia, which is bone marrow failure

The idea is either:

* bone marrow does not make enough neutrophils
* neutrophils are destroyed faster than they are made

---

### Why clozapine is tested

Clozapine can rarely cause agranulocytosis.

So patients on clozapine need regular CBC (complete blood count) monitoring.

CBC = complete blood count, a blood test that measures red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

If neutrophils crash, the drug is stopped because the patient can become severely immunocompromised.

Immunocompromised = immune system is weakened.

---

## One-line intuition

Agranulocytosis is basically "no neutrophil soldiers," so bacterial and fungal infections can become dangerous fast.

---

## Pearl

Clozapine is famous for agranulocytosis, so it requires routine ANC (absolute neutrophil count) monitoring.
