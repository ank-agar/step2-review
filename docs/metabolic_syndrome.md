## Metabolic syndrome

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/8709.jpg)

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17969/N01201-H.jpg)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/images/MetabolicSyndrome.jpg)

![Image](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/sites/default/files/inline-images/MetabolicSyndrome_1188769671_03032022.jpg)

Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of metabolic risk factors that travel together and increase risk of:

* type 2 diabetes mellitus
* coronary artery disease
* stroke

The main idea:

> abdominal obesity + insulin resistance = bad glucose, lipid, and blood pressure physiology.

---

### Diagnostic idea

Think **3 out of 5**:

* increased waist circumference
* high triglycerides (fat carried in blood)
* low HDL (high-density lipoprotein, “good cholesterol”)
* high blood pressure
* high fasting glucose (blood sugar after not eating)

So metabolic syndrome is not one single lab abnormality.

It is a pattern.

---

### Why abdominal obesity matters

Visceral fat (fat around abdominal organs) is metabolically active.

It releases:

* free fatty acids
* inflammatory cytokines (inflammation signaling proteins)

These promote insulin resistance.

Insulin resistance means insulin is present, but muscle, fat, and liver cells do not respond to it well.

So the pancreas has to make more insulin to keep glucose normal.

Eventually:

* glucose rises
* triglycerides rise
* HDL falls
* blood pressure rises

---

### Why the lipid pattern happens

Insulin resistance makes the liver produce more VLDL (very-low-density lipoprotein, a triglyceride-rich lipoprotein).

That gives:

* increased triglycerides
* decreased HDL

That is why metabolic syndrome is more about **high triglycerides + low HDL**, not just high LDL (low-density lipoprotein, “bad cholesterol”).

---

### One-line intuition

Metabolic syndrome is the body saying:

> “Too much visceral fat is making insulin signaling worse, and now glucose, lipids, and blood pressure are all drifting in the wrong direction.”

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember:

**Metabolic syndrome = insulin resistance syndrome.**

The classic buzzword pattern is:

* abdominal obesity
* hyperglycemia (high blood glucose)
* hypertriglyceridemia (high triglycerides)
* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* low HDL
