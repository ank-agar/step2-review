## Polymyalgia rheumatica

![Image](https://medicinepods.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/GCA26PMR28229.png)

![Image](https://res.cloudinary.com/da1molee1/image/upload/c_fill,g_center,h_439,w_780/q_auto:eco/causes-shoulder-blade-pain-snapping-scapula-syndrome.png?_a=BAAAV6E0)

Polymyalgia rheumatica is an inflammatory disease causing pain and stiffness in the proximal muscles.

Proximal means close to the trunk, so think:

* shoulders
* neck
* hips
* thighs

It usually happens in older adults, especially age >50.

---

### Core idea

The name breaks down:

* poly- = many
* myalgia = muscle pain
* rheumatica = inflammatory/rheumatic condition

So:

> many muscle aches from an inflammatory rheumatic disease.

But the key is that it is more about inflammation around joints/bursae than true muscle destruction.

Bursae are small fluid-filled sacs that reduce friction near joints.

---

### Classic presentation

Usually:

* older patient
* bilateral shoulder and hip aching
* morning stiffness
* elevated ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate) and CRP (C-reactive protein)
* normal CK (creatine kinase)

Normal CK matters because CK rises when muscle is actually being damaged, like in myositis.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Inflammation around the shoulder and hip girdles makes big movements painful.

So patients may have trouble:

* getting out of a chair
* combing hair
* lifting arms
* getting dressed

But the muscle itself is not primarily weak.

It feels like weakness because movement hurts and stiffness limits range of motion.

---

### Big association

Polymyalgia rheumatica is associated with GCA (giant cell arteritis).

GCA is inflammation of large/medium arteries, especially branches of the carotid artery near the temple.

Ask about:

* new headache
* jaw claudication, meaning jaw pain with chewing
* scalp tenderness
* vision changes

Vision changes are an emergency because GCA can cause blindness.

---

### Treatment clue

PMR (polymyalgia rheumatica) classically improves quickly with low-dose corticosteroids.

Corticosteroids are anti-inflammatory steroid medications, like prednisone.

---

## One-line intuition

An older patient wakes up stiff and achy in both shoulders/hips because inflammation around the big proximal joints makes movement painful, not because the muscles are being destroyed.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PMR (polymyalgia rheumatica) has proximal pain/stiffness with normal CK (creatine kinase); polymyositis has true proximal muscle weakness with elevated CK.
