## DiGeorge syndrome

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cf/Illu_thymus.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a3/Illu_thyroid_parathyroid.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cb/DiGeorge_syndrome1.jpg)

DiGeorge syndrome is a congenital syndrome (present from birth) caused by a **22q11.2 microdeletion**.

Break that down:

* **22** = chromosome 22
* **q** = long arm of the chromosome
* **11.2** = the specific location
* **microdeletion** = a tiny missing piece of DNA

So the core idea is:

> a tiny missing piece of chromosome 22 messes up development of the pharyngeal pouches.

The big Step 1 association is abnormal development of the **3rd and 4th pharyngeal pouches** (embryologic structures in the neck region).

These normally help form:

* thymus
* parathyroid glands

---

### Why the findings happen

**Low/absent thymus**

The thymus is where T cells (T lymphocytes, immune cells important for cell-mediated immunity) mature.

So if the thymus is underdeveloped:

* low T cells
* recurrent viral/fungal infections
* weak cell-mediated immunity

---

**Low parathyroids**

The parathyroid glands make PTH (parathyroid hormone), which raises blood calcium.

So if the parathyroids are underdeveloped:

* low PTH
* low calcium
* tetany/seizures

Tetany means involuntary muscle spasms from low calcium making nerves more excitable.

---

**Heart defects**

The same embryologic development problem can affect neural crest cells (embryonic cells that help form parts of the heart outflow tract).

So you can see conotruncal defects (heart outflow tract defects), especially:

* truncus arteriosus
* tetralogy of Fallot
* interrupted aortic arch

---

### Classic memory hook

**CATCH-22**

* **C** = cardiac defects
* **A** = abnormal facies (facies means facial appearance)
* **T** = thymic aplasia (aplasia means absent development)
* **C** = cleft palate
* **H** = hypocalcemia
* **22** = chromosome 22 deletion

---

## One-line intuition

DiGeorge syndrome is basically: **22q11.2 deletion → bad 3rd/4th pharyngeal pouch development → low thymus + low parathyroids + heart defects.**

---

## Pearl 🔥

DiGeorge syndrome causes a **T-cell deficiency**, so live vaccines may be dangerous in severe cases because the patient may not control the weakened vaccine organism.
