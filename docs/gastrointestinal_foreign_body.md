## Gastrointestinal foreign body

![Image](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/media-assets/17817/N00852-H_thumbnail.jpg)

![Image](https://medicine.uams.edu/radiology/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2022/07/image-27-1024x666.jpg)

![Image](https://medicine.uams.edu/radiology/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2022/07/image-28.jpg)

A **gastrointestinal foreign body** means an object is inside the **GI tract (gastrointestinal tract: mouth → esophagus → stomach → intestines → anus)** when it should not be there.

Common examples:

* coins
* button batteries
* magnets
* bones
* toys
* sharp objects

---

### Core idea

Most swallowed objects pass through the GI tract without major damage.

The dangerous ones are objects that:

* get stuck
* burn tissue
* puncture the bowel
* obstruct the lumen
* trap bowel between them

**Lumen** means the hollow inside space of a tube, like the inside of the intestine.

---

### Where they get stuck

Foreign bodies often lodge at narrow points, especially the **esophagus**.

Classic esophageal narrowing sites:

* upper esophageal sphincter
* aortic arch level
* lower esophageal sphincter / GE junction

**GE junction** means gastroesophageal junction, where the esophagus meets the stomach.

Intuition:

> A swallowed object moves through a tube, so it gets stuck where the tube naturally narrows.

---

### Why complications happen

* **Obstruction:** object blocks the lumen, so food/gas/fluid cannot pass
* **Perforation:** sharp object pierces through the wall
* **Ulceration/necrosis:** pressure or chemical injury kills tissue
* **Fistula:** abnormal connection forms between two spaces

**Necrosis** means tissue death.

**Fistula** means an abnormal connection between two body surfaces/organs.

---

### High-yield dangerous objects

**Button batteries** are dangerous because they can generate caustic injury.

**Caustic injury** means chemical burn-type tissue damage.

Why:

* battery gets lodged, especially in the esophagus
* local electrical current produces hydroxide
* hydroxide causes liquefactive necrosis
* this can rapidly cause perforation or fistula

**Liquefactive necrosis** means tissue death where tissue becomes soft/liquidy from enzymatic digestion.

---

**Multiple magnets** are dangerous because they can attract each other across bowel walls.

Why:

* one magnet may be in one loop of bowel
* another magnet may be in a nearby loop
* they clamp bowel wall between them
* trapped bowel can become ischemic, necrotic, perforated, or fistulized

**Ischemic** means not enough blood flow.

---

### One-line intuition

A GI foreign body is a swallowed object in the digestive tube; most pass, but batteries, magnets, sharp objects, or stuck objects can burn, block, trap, or puncture the bowel.

---

## Pearl

On X-ray, a **coin in the esophagus** usually appears face-on on the **AP view (anteroposterior view: front-to-back X-ray)** because the esophagus is behind the trachea, while an aspirated airway object often turns sideways on AP view.
