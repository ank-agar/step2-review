### Core idea

Vulvovaginitis means inflammation of the:

* vulva = external female genital area
* vagina = internal canal
* -itis = inflammation

So it literally means:

> inflamed vulva + vagina.

Main symptoms:

* itching
* burning
* pain
* redness/swelling
* abnormal discharge
* odor sometimes

---

### Why it happens

The vagina normally has **Lactobacillus** bacteria, which make acid and keep vaginal **pH (acidity level)** low.

When that balance is disrupted, organisms can overgrow or irritants can inflame the tissue.

Big causes:

* **Candida** = yeast/fungus
* **Bacterial vaginosis** = loss of Lactobacillus + overgrowth of anaerobes (bacteria that grow without oxygen), especially Gardnerella
* **Trichomonas vaginalis** = flagellated protozoan (motile parasite)
* irritants/allergy = soaps, douches, spermicides
* low estrogen = thin, dry vaginal mucosa after menopause

---

### Classic Step 1 associations

**Candida vulvovaginitis**

* thick white "cottage cheese" discharge
* itching/burning
* normal vaginal pH
* KOH (potassium hydroxide) prep shows budding yeast + pseudohyphae

**Bacterial vaginosis**

* thin gray discharge
* fishy odor
* high vaginal pH
* clue cells = vaginal epithelial cells coated with bacteria

**Trichomoniasis**

* frothy green-yellow discharge
* high vaginal pH
* strawberry cervix
* motile flagellated organisms on wet mount

---

## One-line intuition

Vulvovaginitis is what happens when the normal acidic vaginal environment gets irritated or biologically unbalanced, causing inflammation and discharge.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Candida usually has **normal pH**, while bacterial vaginosis and Trichomonas usually have **increased pH**.
