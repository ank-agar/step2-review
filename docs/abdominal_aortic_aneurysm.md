## Abdominal aortic aneurysm

An abdominal aortic aneurysm, or AAA (abdominal aortic aneurysm), is a balloon-like dilation of the abdominal aorta.

The aorta is the main artery carrying blood from the heart to the body.

So AAA means:

> the main artery in the abdomen gets weak and bulges outward.

---

### Core idea

Think:

* normal aorta = strong hose
* aneurysm = weak spot in the hose wall that balloons out

The danger is not just that it is big.

The danger is:

* the wall is stretched
* the wall is weakened
* it can rupture and cause massive internal bleeding

---

### Why it happens

Most AAAs are due to weakening of the vessel wall from atherosclerosis.

Atherosclerosis means plaque buildup and chronic damage inside arteries.

That damage causes inflammation and breakdown of elastic tissue in the aortic wall.

Less elastic tissue means the aorta cannot recoil and resist pressure well, so it slowly dilates.

---

### Classic risk factors

Big ones:

* older age
* male sex
* smoking
* hypertension (high blood pressure)
* atherosclerosis
* family history

Smoking is especially important.

It damages vessels and increases inflammation, which makes the aortic wall weaker.

---

### Symptoms

Usually asymptomatic, meaning no symptoms.

If symptoms happen, think:

* deep abdominal pain
* back pain
* pulsatile abdominal mass

Pulsatile means it beats with the pulse.

---

### Rupture

Ruptured AAA is a medical emergency.

Classic rupture picture:

* severe abdominal or back pain
* hypotension (low blood pressure)
* pulsatile abdominal mass

Why hypotension?

Because blood is leaving the circulation and bleeding into the abdomen or retroperitoneum.

Retroperitoneum means the space behind the abdominal cavity.

---

### Diagnosis

Ultrasound is commonly used for screening because it is fast, noninvasive, and good at measuring aortic diameter.

CT (computed tomography) is useful if you need more anatomy, especially before repair or if rupture is suspected.

AAA is generally defined as abdominal aorta diameter greater than 3 cm.

---

## One-line intuition

AAA is a weakened, ballooned-out abdominal aorta that is often silent until it gets large or ruptures.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Screen men age 65-75 who have ever smoked with one-time abdominal ultrasound.
