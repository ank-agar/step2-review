# Familial Mediterranean fever

Familial Mediterranean fever is an **autosomal recessive autoinflammatory disease**.

Break it down:

* **familial** = inherited
* **Mediterranean** = classically seen in people of Mediterranean ancestry
* **fever** = recurrent fever attacks

Autoinflammatory means the **innate immune system** is overactive without needing an infection or autoantibody trigger.

---

### Core cause

It is caused by mutations in **MEFV** (Mediterranean fever gene), which affects **pyrin**.

Pyrin normally helps regulate the **inflammasome** (innate immune protein complex that activates inflammation).

When pyrin regulation is abnormal:

* inflammasome activity increases
* **IL-1** (interleukin-1, a pro-inflammatory cytokine) increases
* neutrophil-driven inflammation flares

So the body gets repeated episodes of inflammation even when there is no infection.

---

### Classic findings

Attacks are usually short and recurrent:

* fever
* abdominal pain from peritonitis (inflammation of the peritoneum, the lining around abdominal organs)
* chest pain from pleuritis (inflammation of the pleura, the lining around lungs)
* arthritis (joint inflammation)
* erysipelas-like rash, usually on the lower legs

The pain is from **serositis** (inflammation of serous membranes, which are smooth linings around organs).

---

### Why abdominal/chest pain happens

The peritoneum and pleura are normally thin slippery linings.

During an attack, they become inflamed.

Inflamed lining rubbing with movement = pain.

So:

* peritoneum inflamed = abdominal pain
* pleura inflamed = pleuritic chest pain, worse with breathing

---

### Main complication

Repeated inflammation can cause **AA amyloidosis** (amyloid A protein deposition), especially in the kidney.

This can lead to:

* proteinuria (protein in urine)
* nephrotic syndrome (heavy protein loss in urine causing edema and low albumin)
* kidney failure

---

### Treatment

Classic treatment is **colchicine**.

Colchicine decreases neutrophil activity, so it reduces inflammatory attacks and helps prevent AA amyloidosis.

---

## One-line intuition

Familial Mediterranean fever is an inherited pyrin/inflammasome problem that causes random IL-1-driven fever + serositis attacks.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Familial Mediterranean fever is classically:

* autosomal recessive
* MEFV mutation
* recurrent fever + serositis
* treated with colchicine
* dangerous long-term because of AA amyloidosis
