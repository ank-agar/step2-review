## Hyperaldosteronism

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/15/Adrenal_glands.jpg/1280px-Adrenal_glands.jpg)

Hyperaldosteronism = too much aldosterone.

Aldosterone is a mineralocorticoid hormone from the zona glomerulosa of the adrenal cortex that tells the kidney:

* reabsorb sodium
* dump potassium
* dump hydrogen ions

So the classic findings are:

* hypertension
* hypokalemia
* metabolic alkalosis

Metabolic alkalosis means the blood is too basic because hydrogen ions are being lost.

---

### Core idea

Aldosterone makes the body hold onto salt.

Where sodium goes, water follows.

So:

> increased aldosterone -> increased sodium/water retention -> increased blood volume -> hypertension

At the same time, the kidney gets rid of potassium and hydrogen ions:

> increased aldosterone -> potassium loss + hydrogen ion loss -> hypokalemia + metabolic alkalosis

---

### Primary vs secondary

**Primary hyperaldosteronism** means the adrenal gland itself is overproducing aldosterone.

Common causes:

* adrenal adenoma
* bilateral adrenal hyperplasia

Labs:

* high aldosterone
* low renin

Renin is low because the body senses high blood pressure/volume and tries to shut off the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS; hormone system that raises blood pressure and volume).

---

**Secondary hyperaldosteronism** means something outside the adrenal gland is driving aldosterone up through renin.

Examples:

* renal artery stenosis
* heart failure
* cirrhosis

Labs:

* high aldosterone
* high renin

The adrenal gland is responding normally to increased renin stimulation.

---

### Conn syndrome

Conn syndrome = primary hyperaldosteronism from an aldosterone-producing adrenal adenoma.

Think:

> adrenal tumor autonomously secretes aldosterone

Autonomous means it keeps secreting even when the body wants it to stop.

---

## One-line intuition

Hyperaldosteronism is a salt-retaining state: the body holds sodium/water, loses potassium/hydrogen, and becomes hypertensive, hypokalemic, and alkalotic.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Primary hyperaldosteronism has an increased aldosterone-to-renin ratio because aldosterone is high but renin is suppressed.
