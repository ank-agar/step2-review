### Core idea

Goodpasture syndrome is an autoimmune disease where antibodies attack basement membrane in:

* lungs
* kidneys

More specifically, it is anti-GBM disease.

GBM = glomerular basement membrane, which is the thin filtration support layer in kidney glomeruli.

The antibody target is:

* type IV collagen
* especially the alpha-3 chain

---

### Why lungs + kidneys?

Both organs have basement membranes with the target collagen.

So antibodies bind in:

* alveoli (lung air sacs) → bleeding into lungs
* glomeruli (kidney filters) → rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis

Glomerulonephritis = inflammation of the kidney glomeruli, which are the filtering units.

---

### Classic presentation

Think:

* hemoptysis = coughing up blood
* hematuria = blood in urine
* rapidly rising creatinine = worsening kidney function

So the Step 1 phrase is:

> young man with hemoptysis + hematuria

That combo should make you think pulmonary-renal syndrome.

Pulmonary-renal syndrome = lung bleeding plus kidney inflammation/failure.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

* **Hemoptysis:** antibodies damage alveolar basement membrane, so blood leaks into air spaces
* **Hematuria/proteinuria:** antibodies damage glomerular basement membrane, so blood/protein leak into urine
* **Rapid renal failure:** glomerular injury can be severe and form crescents
* **Linear immunofluorescence:** antibodies bind evenly along the basement membrane

Immunofluorescence = staining technique that shows where antibodies/complement are deposited.

---

### Key biopsy finding

Goodpasture syndrome shows:

* linear IgG deposition along the GBM

IgG = immunoglobulin G, a type of antibody.

Why linear?

Because the antibody is binding directly along the basement membrane itself, like painting a smooth line.

Compare:

* Goodpasture = linear
* post-strep glomerulonephritis = granular

Granular means immune complexes are scattered/lumpy instead of smoothly coating the membrane.

---

## One-line intuition

Goodpasture is your own IgG attacking type IV collagen basement membrane in lungs and kidneys, causing coughing blood + peeing blood.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Goodpasture syndrome is a type II hypersensitivity reaction because antibodies directly bind a fixed tissue antigen: type IV collagen in basement membrane.
