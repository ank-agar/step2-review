## Premature ejaculation

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/88/Ejaculation_anatomy_en.svg/1280px-Ejaculation_anatomy_en.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/Human_male_reproductive_system_en.svg/1280px-Human_male_reproductive_system_en.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/Male_pelvic_structures.svg/1280px-Male_pelvic_structures.svg.png)

Premature ejaculation is ejaculation that happens earlier than desired, usually with poor control and distress.

Basically:

* ejaculation happens too quickly
* the person feels unable to delay it
* it causes bother/distress for the patient or partner

---

### Core idea

Ejaculation is a reflex controlled by the nervous system.

The key Step 1 idea:

* **Parasympathetic** = erection
* **Sympathetic** = ejaculation

Mnemonic:

> Point and shoot

Meaning:

* **Point** = parasympathetic causes erection
* **Shoot** = sympathetic causes ejaculation

---

### Why it happens

Premature ejaculation is usually not an anatomic blockage problem.

It is more like the ejaculation reflex threshold is reached too easily or too quickly.

Common contributors:

* anxiety/performance pressure
* increased sympathetic tone
* hypersensitivity
* relationship or psychologic stress
* sometimes prostatitis, thyroid disease, or medication/substance effects

Why anxiety can worsen it:

* anxiety increases sympathetic nervous system activity
* sympathetic activity drives emission/ejaculation
* so the body reaches the “shoot” reflex faster

---

### Treatment idea

Treatment is aimed at increasing control and delaying the reflex.

Common options:

* behavioral techniques
* topical anesthetics
* SSRIs (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors)

Why SSRIs help:

Serotonin generally delays ejaculation, so SSRIs can increase ejaculation latency.

---

## One-line intuition

Premature ejaculation is when the sympathetic ejaculation reflex fires too early.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Step 1 HY: erection is parasympathetic, ejaculation is sympathetic.

So remember:

> Point = parasympathetic, shoot = sympathetic.
