### Core idea

Diaper rash = inflammation of skin in the diaper area, usually from:

* moisture
* friction
* urine
* stool

Most common type is **irritant contact dermatitis**:

* irritant = something directly damages/annoys skin
* contact = touching the skin
* dermatitis = skin inflammation

So diaper rash is basically:

> irritated, inflamed baby skin because the diaper environment is wet, warm, and full of irritants.

---

### Why it happens

The diaper traps moisture against the skin.

That causes **maceration** (softening/breakdown from wetness) of the **stratum corneum** (outer protective skin layer).

Then:

* wet skin breaks down more easily
* diaper rubbing causes friction injury
* stool enzymes irritate the skin
* urine + stool raise skin pH (pH = acidity level)
* higher pH activates stool enzymes even more

So the cycle is:

> wet skin → weaker barrier → more friction/irritation → rash → even weaker barrier.

---

### What it looks like

Typical irritant diaper rash:

* red or pink patches
* on convex surfaces, like buttocks, thighs, genital area
* usually **spares the skin folds**

Why folds are spared:

The folds are less directly exposed to rubbing + urine/stool contact, so they are relatively protected.

---

### Candida clue

**Candida** (yeast/fungus) diaper rash is different.

It tends to involve:

* skin folds
* beefy red plaques
* satellite lesions

**Satellite lesions** = little red bumps/pustules around the main rash.

Candida likes warm, moist areas, so the diaper area is perfect for it.

---

### Treatment intuition

Treatment is mostly about restoring the barrier:

* frequent diaper changes
* keep area dry
* gentle cleaning
* barrier cream like zinc oxide or petrolatum

If Candida is suspected:

* add topical antifungal cream

**Topical** = applied to the skin.

---

## One-line intuition

Diaper rash is skin barrier breakdown from a wet, warm, high-friction diaper environment, sometimes complicated by Candida yeast.

---

## Pearl

Irritant diaper dermatitis usually **spares the skin folds**, while Candida diaper dermatitis often **involves the folds** and has **satellite pustules**.
