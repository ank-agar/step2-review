# Otitis externa

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19697.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ca/Gray904.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c9/Otitis_externa.jpg/960px-Otitis_externa.jpg)

Otitis externa = inflammation/infection of the external auditory canal (ear canal outside the tympanic membrane, aka eardrum).

It is also called swimmer's ear.

### Core idea

Water or trauma damages the ear canal skin barrier.

Then bacteria can grow in that warm, wet canal.

Most common causes:

* Pseudomonas aeruginosa
* Staphylococcus aureus

### Why it hurts

The ear canal skin is inflamed and swollen.

So anything that moves the outer ear pulls on that inflamed canal:

* pulling the pinna (outer ear) hurts
* pressing the tragus (small flap in front of ear canal) hurts

That is a classic clue for otitis externa.

### Symptoms

* ear pain
* itching
* ear canal redness/swelling
* discharge from the ear
* muffled hearing

Muffled hearing happens because swelling/debris narrows the canal, so sound has a harder time reaching the tympanic membrane (eardrum).

### Otitis externa vs otitis media

Otitis externa:

* problem is in the external auditory canal (outer ear canal)
* pain with tragus pressure or pinna pulling

Otitis media:

* problem is behind the tympanic membrane (middle ear)
* often follows upper respiratory infection
* tympanic membrane may look bulging

## One-line intuition

Otitis externa is basically infected, swollen ear canal skin, usually after moisture or scratching breaks the normal barrier.

## Pearl 🔥

In diabetics or immunocompromised patients, severe Pseudomonas otitis externa can become malignant otitis externa, which is skull base osteomyelitis (bone infection at the base of the skull).
