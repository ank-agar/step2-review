### Core idea

Adenosine deaminase deficiency is a cause of **SCID (severe combined immunodeficiency)**.

ADA (adenosine deaminase) normally helps break down **deoxyadenosine**.

If ADA is missing:

* deoxyadenosine builds up
* dATP (deoxyadenosine triphosphate) builds up
* lymphocytes get poisoned
* T cells and B cells fail

So the immune system is severely weak.

---

### Why lymphocytes die

dATP buildup inhibits **ribonucleotide reductase** (enzyme needed to make DNA building blocks).

So cells cannot make DNA well.

This especially hurts lymphocytes because they need to divide rapidly during immune development.

Result:

* low T cells
* low B cells
* low antibodies
* recurrent severe infections

---

### Presentation

Think sick infant with:

* recurrent bacterial, viral, and fungal infections
* chronic diarrhea
* failure to thrive
* absent tonsils/lymph nodes
* absent thymic shadow on chest x-ray

Why so many infection types?

Because **combined** means both:

* cellular immunity = T cells
* humoral immunity = B cells/antibodies

are impaired.

---

### Inheritance

ADA deficiency is usually **autosomal recessive**.

Autosomal recessive means the child needs 2 bad copies of the gene, one from each carrier parent.

---

## One-line intuition

ADA deficiency = toxic purine metabolites build up and kill developing lymphocytes, causing SCID.

---

## Pearl 🔥

ADA deficiency causes SCID with impaired T cells and B cells, but the key biochem clue is **increased dATP**, which inhibits **ribonucleotide reductase**.
