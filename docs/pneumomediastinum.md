### Core idea

Pneumomediastinum means air in the mediastinum.

The mediastinum is the central space in the chest between the lungs that contains:

* heart
* trachea
* esophagus
* great vessels

So pneumomediastinum is basically:

> free air collecting around the central chest structures.

---

### Why it happens

Most Step 1-style mechanism:

* sudden increased pressure inside alveoli
* alveoli rupture
* air escapes into lung interstitium
* air tracks along bronchovascular sheaths
* air reaches the mediastinum

Bronchovascular sheaths means the connective tissue pathways around bronchi and blood vessels.

This air-tracking mechanism is called the Macklin effect.

---

### Common triggers

Anything that suddenly increases intrathoracic pressure can do it:

* severe coughing
* asthma exacerbation
* vomiting
* Valsalva maneuver
* trauma
* mechanical ventilation/barotrauma

Barotrauma means pressure-related tissue injury.

---

### Symptoms/signs

Can cause:

* sharp retrosternal chest pain
* shortness of breath
* neck pain
* subcutaneous emphysema
* Hamman sign

Retrosternal means behind the sternum.

Subcutaneous emphysema means air trapped under the skin, often felt as crackly crepitus.

Hamman sign is a crunching/clicking sound over the chest that is synchronized with the heartbeat.

---

## One-line intuition

Pneumomediastinum is air that leaked from an airway/alveolus and traveled into the middle of the chest around the heart and great vessels.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY distinction:

* pneumomediastinum = air in the mediastinum, around central chest structures
* pneumothorax = air in the pleural space, causing lung collapse

Both can happen together, but they are not the same location.
