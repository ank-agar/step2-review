### Core idea

Scleroderma literally means **hard skin**.

It is an autoimmune connective tissue disease where the body makes too much collagen, causing:

* skin thickening/tightening
* blood vessel damage
* fibrosis of internal organs sometimes

Fibrosis means scar-like tissue buildup.

---

### Why the skin gets hard

The immune system injures small blood vessels and activates fibroblasts.

Fibroblasts are connective tissue cells that make collagen.

So:

> immune injury + vascular damage → fibroblast activation → excess collagen → thick, tight, hard tissue

That is why the fingers can become shiny, tight, and claw-like.

This finger skin tightening is called **sclerodactyly**.

---

### Systemic sclerosis

Systemic sclerosis means scleroderma affecting the body systemically, not just one skin patch.

It can involve:

* skin
* lungs
* GI tract (gastrointestinal tract)
* kidneys
* heart
* small blood vessels

Important: the vascular problem is not random. Small vessels narrow/spasm, so patients often get **Raynaud phenomenon**.

Raynaud phenomenon means fingers/toes turn white or blue with cold or stress because small arteries spasm.

---

### Limited vs diffuse

**Limited systemic sclerosis**:

* mainly fingers, hands, face
* slower progression
* associated with anti-centromere antibodies
* classic CREST findings

CREST means:

* **C**alcinosis: calcium deposits in skin
* **R**aynaud phenomenon: cold-induced vessel spasm
* **E**sophageal dysmotility: weak esophageal movement → reflux/dysphagia
* **S**clerodactyly: tight thick fingers
* **T**elangiectasias: dilated small blood vessels on skin

**Diffuse systemic sclerosis**:

* more widespread skin involvement, including trunk/proximal limbs
* faster progression
* more internal organ fibrosis
* associated with anti-Scl-70 antibodies

Anti-Scl-70 is an antibody against DNA topoisomerase I.

---

### Why organ problems happen

Same basic mechanism: too much collagen makes organs stiff.

* Esophagus: fibrosis/weak smooth muscle → dysphagia and reflux
* Lungs: interstitial lung disease (fibrosis between alveoli) → dyspnea
* Pulmonary arteries: vascular remodeling → pulmonary hypertension
* Kidneys: renal vessel injury → severe hypertension/renal crisis

Renal crisis means sudden severe kidney dysfunction with very high blood pressure.

---

## One-line intuition

Scleroderma is basically **autoimmune small-vessel injury that turns on fibroblasts, causing excess collagen and scar-like hardening of skin and organs**.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Anti-centromere antibody → limited systemic sclerosis/CREST.

Anti-Scl-70 antibody → diffuse systemic sclerosis with higher risk of pulmonary fibrosis.
