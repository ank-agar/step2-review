## Congenital chlamydia

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4a/Chlamydae_Life_Cycle.svg/960px-Chlamydae_Life_Cycle.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5e/Chlamydia.jpg/960px-Chlamydia.jpg)

![Image](https://eyewiki-images.s3.us-east-va.perf.cloud.ovh.us/d/d9/Ophthalmia_neonatorum.jpg)

Congenital chlamydia usually means **neonatal chlamydial infection** from **Chlamydia trachomatis**.

Important wording:

* it is usually **not truly congenital** from placenta-crossing infection
* it is usually **perinatal**, meaning acquired around birth
* baby gets exposed while passing through an infected birth canal

So think:

> maternal cervical chlamydia → baby exposed during vaginal delivery → eye/lung infection

---

### Classic presentations

**1. Neonatal conjunctivitis**

Conjunctivitis means inflammation of the conjunctiva, the thin membrane over the eye.

Timing:

* about **5-14 days after birth**

Findings:

* red eyes
* swollen eyelids
* watery or mucopurulent discharge

Mucopurulent means mucus + pus-like drainage.

---

**2. Afebrile pneumonia**

Afebrile means no fever.

Timing:

* around **1-3 months of age**

Findings:

* cough
* tachypnea, meaning fast breathing
* diffuse lung infiltrates
* often eosinophilia, meaning increased eosinophils (allergy/parasite-type white blood cells)

---

### Why the symptoms happen

Chlamydia is an **obligate intracellular bacterium**.

Obligate intracellular means it has to live inside host cells.

It infects mucosal surfaces, especially:

* conjunctiva of the eye
* nasopharynx, meaning the back of the nose/throat
* lungs

So the baby does not usually have a big bloodstream infection. Instead, it gets irritated/infected mucosal surfaces:

* eye infection first
* lung infection later

---

### Treatment idea

Treat with a **systemic macrolide**, like erythromycin or azithromycin.

Systemic means oral/whole-body treatment.

Why not just eye ointment?

Because the baby may also have chlamydia in the nasopharynx or lungs, so topical eye treatment alone can miss the real infection.

---

## One-line intuition

Congenital chlamydia is really birth-canal-acquired **Chlamydia trachomatis** causing newborn conjunctivitis at 1-2 weeks and afebrile pneumonia at 1-3 months.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Differentiate neonatal conjunctivitis timing:

* **Gonorrhea:** 2-5 days after birth, more severe purulent conjunctivitis
* **Chlamydia:** 5-14 days after birth, conjunctivitis + later afebrile pneumonia
