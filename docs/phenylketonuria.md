## Phenylketonuria

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/Inborn_errors_of_metabolism_of_phenylalanine_and_tyrosine.svg/1280px-Inborn_errors_of_metabolism_of_phenylalanine_and_tyrosine.svg.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/16/Phenylketonuria_testing.jpg)

Phenylketonuria, or **PKU (phenylketonuria)**, is an **autosomal recessive (need 2 bad copies of the gene)** disorder where the body cannot break down **phenylalanine (an amino acid from dietary protein)**.

The problem is usually deficiency of:

* **phenylalanine hydroxylase**, or **PAH (phenylalanine hydroxylase)**

Normal pathway:

* phenylalanine → tyrosine

In PKU:

* phenylalanine cannot become tyrosine
* phenylalanine builds up
* extra phenylalanine gets shunted into toxic metabolites called **phenylketones (ketone-like breakdown products from phenylalanine)**

That is why it is called phenyl-keton-uria:

* phenyl = phenylalanine-related
* keton = ketone metabolites
* uria = in urine

---

### Why the findings happen

High phenylalanine is toxic to the developing brain, so untreated PKU causes:

* intellectual disability
* seizures
* developmental delay
* microcephaly (small head size)

Low tyrosine also matters because **tyrosine (amino acid made from phenylalanine)** is used to make **melanin (pigment in skin/hair)**.

So PKU can cause:

* fair skin
* light hair
* blue eyes

because less tyrosine → less melanin.

Classic smell:

* musty or mousy odor

because phenylalanine metabolites build up in sweat and urine.

---

### Diagnosis and treatment

PKU is found on newborn screening with a heel-prick blood test.

Treatment:

* low-phenylalanine diet
* avoid aspartame because it contains phenylalanine
* give tyrosine, because tyrosine becomes an essential amino acid in PKU

Essential amino acid means the body needs it from diet because it cannot make enough of it.

---

## One-line intuition

PKU is a blocked phenylalanine → tyrosine pathway, so phenylalanine poisons the brain and low tyrosine causes hypopigmentation.

---

## Pearl 🔥

PKU is associated with **maternal PKU syndrome**: if a pregnant patient has poorly controlled phenylalanine levels, the baby can develop intellectual disability, microcephaly, growth restriction, and congenital heart defects even if the baby does not inherit PKU.
