# Morning sickness

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/05/Morning_sickness.svg/960px-Morning_sickness.svg.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19673.jpg)

Morning sickness = nausea and vomiting during pregnancy.

Despite the name, it can happen:

* morning
* afternoon
* night
* all day

It usually starts early in the first trimester and often improves by around weeks 14-16.

---

### Core idea

Early pregnancy causes a big hormone shift, especially from the placenta.

Important hormones involved:

* hCG (human chorionic gonadotropin)
* estrogen
* GDF15 (growth differentiation factor 15), a placenta-related nausea signal

These can stimulate nausea/vomiting pathways in the brain and gut.

So the intuition is:

> early placenta hormone surge -> nausea center gets triggered -> nausea/vomiting.

---

### Why it is worse in some pregnancies

Morning sickness tends to be worse when pregnancy hormone levels are higher, like:

* multiple gestation = twins/triplets
* molar pregnancy = abnormal placental tissue overgrowth

Why?

More placental tissue can make more hCG (human chorionic gonadotropin), so the nausea signal can be stronger.

---

### When it becomes dangerous

Severe morning sickness is called hyperemesis gravidarum.

Hyperemesis gravidarum = severe nausea/vomiting in pregnancy causing:

* weight loss
* dehydration
* ketonuria (ketones in urine from starvation/fat breakdown)
* electrolyte abnormalities

If vomiting is prolonged, think:

* hypokalemia (low potassium)
* hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis (low chloride with high blood pH from vomiting stomach acid)

---

### Treatment intuition

First-line meds are often:

* pyridoxine = vitamin B6
* doxylamine = antihistamine used for nausea/sleep

If the patient is very dehydrated, they may need IV (intravenous) fluids.

Important: if vomiting has been prolonged and they need glucose, give thiamine (vitamin B1) first to prevent Wernicke encephalopathy (confusion, ataxia, eye movement problems).

---

## One-line intuition

Morning sickness is early-pregnancy nausea from placental/hormonal signaling, and the severe version is hyperemesis gravidarum.

---

## HY pearl

Severe vomiting in pregnancy + weight loss + ketonuria = hyperemesis gravidarum; give thiamine before glucose to prevent Wernicke encephalopathy.
