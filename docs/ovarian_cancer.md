### Core idea

Ovarian cancer is malignant growth of cells in/near the ovary.

Most Step 1-relevant ovarian cancers are **epithelial tumors** (tumors from surface-lining cells), especially serous tumors.

Important modern idea:

* many “ovarian” high-grade serous cancers may actually start in the fallopian tube epithelium
* then involve the ovary and spread through the peritoneal cavity (abdominal lining space)

---

### Why it presents late

The ovary sits inside the peritoneal cavity, so a tumor has room to grow before causing obvious symptoms.

That is why symptoms are often vague:

* bloating
* early satiety
* abdominal/pelvic pain
* increased urinary frequency
* ascites (fluid in the abdomen)

So the intuition is:

> deep pelvic tumor + vague GI/GU symptoms = often found late.

GI means gastrointestinal.  
GU means genitourinary.

---

### How it spreads

Ovarian cancer commonly spreads by **peritoneal seeding**.

Meaning:

* cancer cells shed off the ovary
* float through peritoneal fluid
* implant on abdominal/peritoneal surfaces

This explains:

* ascites
* omental caking (tumor spread to the omentum, a fatty peritoneal apron)
* abdominal distension

---

### Risk factors

Higher risk:

* BRCA1/BRCA2 mutations (breast cancer gene 1/2 mutations)
* Lynch syndrome (inherited DNA mismatch repair defect)
* nulliparity (never giving birth)
* infertility
* endometriosis

Protective:

* oral contraceptive pills
* pregnancy
* breastfeeding
* tubal ligation

Why?

Less ovulation = less repeated surface injury/repair of ovarian epithelium, so lower mutation risk.

---

### Tumor marker

CA-125 (cancer antigen 125) can be elevated.

But it is not a great screening test because it can also rise in non-cancer conditions, like:

* endometriosis
* pelvic inflammatory disease
* menstruation
* pregnancy

So CA-125 is more useful for following known disease than for screening everyone.

---

## One-line intuition

Ovarian cancer is a sneaky pelvic/peritoneal cancer: it grows quietly, causes vague bloating-type symptoms, and spreads by sprinkling cells across the peritoneum.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Serous cystadenocarcinoma is the classic malignant epithelial ovarian tumor and can show **psammoma bodies** (round laminated calcifications).
