# Aortic dissection

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538173/bin/Picture1.jpg)

### Core idea

Aortic dissection = a tear in the intima (inner lining) of the aorta (largest artery leaving the heart).

Blood enters the wall of the aorta and splits the layers apart.

So instead of blood staying only in the normal tube, it creates a second fake pathway inside the wall.

* true lumen = normal blood channel
* false lumen = new abnormal channel inside the vessel wall
* intimal flap = torn inner layer separating the true lumen from the false lumen

---

### Why it is dangerous

The aorta is under very high pressure.

So once blood gets into the wall, that pressure can rip the dissection farther and farther.

This can cause:

* rupture = bleeding out
* branch vessel blockage = ischemia (poor blood flow) to organs
* stroke if carotid arteries are involved
* MI (myocardial infarction/heart attack) if coronary arteries are involved
* aortic regurgitation if the ascending aorta/root is involved
* cardiac tamponade (blood/fluid compressing the heart) if it ruptures into the pericardial sac

---

### Classic presentation

Sudden severe tearing chest pain radiating to the back.

Other clues:

* unequal arm blood pressures
* pulse deficits
* neurologic symptoms
* syncope (fainting)
* widened mediastinum on CXR (chest x-ray)

But CT angiography (computed tomography angiography) is the big diagnostic test.

---

### Big classification

Stanford system:

* Type A = involves ascending aorta
* Type B = does not involve ascending aorta

Easy intuition:

* A = Ascending aorta = scary/surgery
* B = Beyond ascending aorta = blood pressure control unless complicated

Type A is more dangerous because it is close to the heart, aortic valve, coronary arteries, and pericardial sac.

---

### Risk factors

Anything that weakens the aortic wall or increases pressure:

* hypertension (high blood pressure), most common
* Marfan syndrome
* Ehlers-Danlos syndrome
* bicuspid aortic valve
* aortic coarctation
* pregnancy
* trauma
* cocaine use

---

## One-line intuition

Aortic dissection is like high-pressure blood finding a tear in the aorta’s inner lining and ripping a new tunnel through the wall.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For USMLE (United States Medical Licensing Examination), think:

* aortic dissection + cystic medial degeneration in Marfan syndrome
* tearing chest pain radiating to back
* widened mediastinum
* unequal pulses/BP
* Type A needs surgery
* Type B usually gets beta-blockers first
