Unexpected weight loss means losing weight without trying.

Clinically, it is concerning when someone loses about:

* **>5% of body weight over 6-12 months**

So if someone was 160 lb and unintentionally loses 8+ lb, that counts.

---

### Core idea

Weight loss happens when:

> calories used > calories taken in or absorbed

So think of 3 big buckets:

* **not eating enough**
* **not absorbing enough**
* **burning/breaking down too much**

---

### Why it happens

Common causes include:

* **Cancer:** tumors and inflammation increase catabolism (breakdown of body tissue), causing cachexia (disease-related wasting with fat and muscle loss)
* **Hyperthyroidism:** excess thyroid hormone increases basal metabolic rate (baseline calorie burning), so the patient burns calories too fast
* **Diabetes mellitus:** glucose is lost in urine, so the body breaks down fat and muscle for fuel
* **GI (gastrointestinal) disease:** malabsorption (poor nutrient absorption) means food enters but nutrients do not fully get into the body
* **Chronic infection:** like tuberculosis or HIV (human immunodeficiency virus), which increases inflammatory energy use
* **Depression:** appetite drops, so intake drops
* **Medications:** some decrease appetite, cause nausea, or increase metabolism

---

### Intuition

Unexpected weight loss is not one disease.

It is a sign that the body is either:

* not getting fuel
* not absorbing fuel
* wasting fuel
* breaking itself down for fuel

That is why the differential diagnosis (list of possible causes) is broad.

---

### Classic Step 1 framing

If the stem says:

* weight loss + heat intolerance + tremor + palpitations -> think **hyperthyroidism**
* weight loss + night sweats + lymph nodes -> think **lymphoma**
* weight loss + chronic cough + hemoptysis (coughing blood) -> think **tuberculosis or lung cancer**
* weight loss + diarrhea + greasy stools -> think **malabsorption**
* weight loss + polyuria (excess urination) + polydipsia (excess thirst) -> think **diabetes mellitus**

---

## One-line intuition

Unexpected weight loss means the body is in a negative energy state, usually from decreased intake, poor absorption, or increased catabolism.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cancer cachexia is driven largely by inflammatory cytokines (immune signaling proteins), especially TNF-alpha (tumor necrosis factor-alpha), IL-1 (interleukin-1), and IL-6 (interleukin-6), causing muscle wasting even when calorie intake is not extremely low.
