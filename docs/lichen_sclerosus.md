### Core idea

Lichen sclerosus is a chronic inflammatory skin disease that causes:

* thin, white, shiny skin
* intense itching
* scarring
* narrowing/tightening of genital skin

It most often affects the anogenital area, meaning the genital and anal region.

---

### What the name means

Break it apart:

* lichen = skin disease with patchy/rough plaques
* sclerosus = hardened/scarred

So lichen sclerosus basically means:

> a chronic skin condition where inflammation makes the skin white, thin, and scarred.

---

### Why the findings happen

The key process is chronic inflammation in the dermis, meaning the deeper layer of skin.

That inflammation causes:

* **white plaques:** loss/thinning of normal skin structure makes the area look pale
* **itching:** inflammation irritates local nerves
* **fragile skin:** the epidermis (top skin layer) becomes thin, so it tears easily
* **scarring:** repeated inflammation leads to fibrosis, meaning excess collagen/scar tissue
* **pain with sex or urination:** scarred genital skin can narrow or crack

On histology, meaning microscope appearance, you can see:

* thin epidermis
* hyperkeratosis, meaning thick keratin layer
* pale/homogenized collagen in the dermis
* band-like inflammatory cells

---

### Classic locations

Common sites:

* vulva
* perianal skin, meaning skin around the anus
* glans penis, meaning the head of the penis
* foreskin

In vulvar disease, the vagina itself is usually spared.

That is useful because lichen planus can involve the vagina, but lichen sclerosus usually does not.

---

### Classic appearance

Think:

* porcelain-white plaques
* thin “cigarette paper” skin
* figure-eight pattern around the vulva and anus
* scarring/architectural distortion in chronic disease

In males, it can cause phimosis, meaning inability to retract the foreskin.

---

### Treatment

First-line treatment is high-potency topical corticosteroids, meaning strong anti-inflammatory steroid creams applied to the skin.

Classic example:

* clobetasol

This works because the main problem is immune-driven inflammation causing skin damage and scarring.

---

## One-line intuition

Lichen sclerosus is chronic genital skin inflammation that makes the skin white, thin, itchy, fragile, and scarred.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Lichen sclerosus increases risk of squamous cell carcinoma (SCC, a cancer of squamous epithelial cells), especially vulvar SCC, so persistent thickened, ulcerated, or changing lesions should be biopsied.
