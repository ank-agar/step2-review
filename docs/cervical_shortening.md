### Core idea

Cervical shortening means the cervix is getting shorter/thinner earlier than expected during pregnancy.

The cervix is the lower "neck" of the uterus that normally stays long and closed to keep the pregnancy inside.

So cervical shortening = the cervix is losing its structural support too early.

---

### Why it matters

A short cervix can increase risk of:

* preterm labor
* preterm delivery
* pregnancy loss in the second trimester

because the cervix is supposed to act like a closed plug until late pregnancy.

---

### Mechanism

Normally:

* cervix stays long, firm, and closed
* fetus stays contained in uterus

With cervical shortening:

* cervix becomes shorter
* internal os may open
* fetal membranes can funnel downward

Internal os = the upper opening of the cervix toward the uterus.

External os = the lower opening of the cervix toward the vagina.

Funneling = membranes start bulging into the cervical canal from above.

---

### Intuition

Think of the cervix like the long neck of a tied balloon.

If the neck gets shorter and starts opening, the balloon contents are less supported.

That is why shortening can lead to early delivery.

---

### How it is measured

Usually by transvaginal ultrasound.

Transvaginal ultrasound = ultrasound probe placed in the vagina to get a close view of pelvic organs.

Cervical length is measured from:

* internal os
* to external os

A common cutoff for a short cervix is:

* less than 25 mm before 24 weeks

---

### Related term

Cervical insufficiency means the cervix cannot stay closed during pregnancy without contractions.

So:

* cervical shortening = ultrasound finding
* cervical insufficiency = clinical problem where the cervix opens/shortens too early

---

## One-line intuition

Cervical shortening means the cervix is becoming too short too early, so it may stop acting like a strong closed gate for the pregnancy.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association: cervical insufficiency classically causes painless cervical dilation in the second trimester.
