## Cervical dystonia

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/19090.jpg)

![Image](https://www.botoxone.com/content/dam/botoxplus/images/cddad-img-muscles-lateral.jpg)

![Image](https://www.botoxone.com/content/dam/botoxplus/images/cddad-img-muscles-anterior.jpg)

Cervical dystonia = involuntary contraction of neck muscles causing abnormal head/neck posture.

Break it down:

* cervical = neck region
* dystonia = sustained or intermittent involuntary muscle contraction

So cervical dystonia is basically:

> neck muscles fire when they should not, pulling the head into an abnormal position.

---

### Core idea

The head may:

* rotate to one side = torticollis
* tilt to one side = laterocollis
* flex forward = anterocollis
* extend backward = retrocollis

This can also cause:

* neck pain
* shoulder elevation
* head tremor
* limited range of motion

---

### Why the findings happen

The problem is not usually the muscle itself.

It is abnormal motor signaling from the nervous system, especially basal ganglia circuits (movement-control circuits in the brain), causing certain neck muscles to contract too much.

If the sternocleidomastoid muscle (SCM; neck muscle from sternum/clavicle to mastoid process behind the ear) contracts abnormally, it can pull the head into rotation/tilt.

So the posture depends on which neck muscles are overactive.

---

### Treatment intuition

Main treatment:

* botulinum toxin injection

Botulinum toxin blocks acetylcholine (ACh; neurotransmitter used at the neuromuscular junction) release.

Less ACh reaching muscle = less unwanted muscle contraction.

---

## One-line intuition

Cervical dystonia is a brain signaling problem where neck muscles contract involuntarily and pull the head into twisted/tilted postures.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Botulinum toxin cleaves SNARE proteins (vesicle-fusion proteins), preventing acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction, which weakens the overactive muscle.
