Psoriatic arthritis is an inflammatory arthritis associated with psoriasis.

Think:

* psoriasis = scaly skin plaques
* arthritis = inflamed painful joints
* together = psoriatic arthritis

It is a seronegative spondyloarthropathy.

Seronegative means:

* rheumatoid factor (RF) is usually negative

Spondyloarthropathy means:

* inflammatory arthritis that can affect the spine, sacroiliac joints, tendons, and peripheral joints

---

### Classic findings

Look for:

* psoriasis plaques
* nail pitting
* distal interphalangeal (DIP) joint arthritis
* dactylitis
* enthesitis
* asymmetric joint involvement

DIP joint means the joint closest to the fingertip.

Dactylitis means diffuse swelling of an entire finger or toe.

That is why it is called a:

* sausage digit

Enthesitis means inflammation where a tendon or ligament inserts into bone.

Common example:

* Achilles tendon pain at the heel

---

### Why this happens

The immune system is overactive in skin and joints.

Key inflammatory signals include:

* tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-alpha)
* interleukin-17 (IL-17)
* interleukin-23 (IL-23)

These cytokines (immune signaling proteins) drive inflammation in:

* skin → psoriasis plaques
* nail bed → nail pitting
* synovium → joint swelling and pain
* entheses → tendon/ligament insertion pain

So the same immune problem explains both:

* skin disease
* joint disease

---

### X-ray association

Psoriatic arthritis can cause:

* erosions
* new bone formation
* pencil-in-cup deformity

Pencil-in-cup means one bone end becomes narrowed like a pencil, while the adjacent bone becomes eroded like a cup.

---

## One-line intuition

Psoriatic arthritis is psoriasis plus inflammatory joint disease, especially involving DIP joints, nails, entheses, and sausage digits.

---

## Pearl

Psoriatic arthritis is classically:

* RF-negative
* DIP-predominant
* associated with nail pitting

That helps distinguish it from rheumatoid arthritis (RA), which usually affects metacarpophalangeal (MCP) and proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joints while sparing DIP joints.
