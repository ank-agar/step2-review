## Osteomyelitis

Osteomyelitis = infection of bone and bone marrow.

Break the word apart:

* osteo- = bone
* myel- = marrow
* -itis = inflammation

So osteomyelitis literally means:

> inflammation/infection of the bone marrow.

---

### Core idea

Bacteria get into bone and cause:

* inflammation
* pus
* increased pressure inside bone
* reduced blood flow
* bone necrosis (bone death)

Bone has poor space to swell, so infection can compress blood vessels and make dead bone hard for antibiotics to reach.

---

### How bacteria reach the bone

Main routes:

* hematogenous spread (spread through blood)
* direct inoculation (bacteria directly enter from trauma, surgery, or puncture wound)
* contiguous spread (spread from nearby infected tissue)

Classic examples:

* child with fever + bone pain = hematogenous osteomyelitis
* diabetic foot ulcer reaching bone = contiguous spread
* puncture wound through sneaker = Pseudomonas osteomyelitis

---

### Most common organism

Most common overall:

* Staphylococcus aureus

Special associations:

* sickle cell disease: Salmonella
* puncture wound through shoe: Pseudomonas
* neonates: group B Streptococcus and Staphylococcus aureus

---

### Why it often affects the metaphysis in children

Metaphysis = widened part of a long bone near the growth plate.

In kids, blood flow in the metaphysis is slow and has sharp turns.

That lets bacteria circulating in blood "park" there, exit the bloodstream, and infect bone.

---

### Findings

Think:

* fever
* localized bone pain
* warmth/swelling
* refusal to bear weight in children
* elevated ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate)
* elevated CRP (C-reactive protein)

MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) is very sensitive early.

X-ray can be normal early because bone destruction takes time to become visible.

---

### Chronic osteomyelitis terms

Sequestrum = dead infected bone fragment.

Involucrum = new bone that forms around the dead infected bone.

Intuition:

The body tries to wall off the infection, but dead bone has poor blood supply, so antibiotics and immune cells cannot penetrate well.

That is why chronic osteomyelitis often needs surgical debridement (removal of infected/dead tissue), not just antibiotics.

---

## One-line intuition

Osteomyelitis is a bone infection where bacteria create pus inside rigid bone, cutting off blood supply and causing dead bone that is hard to sterilize.
