# Conversion disorder

![Image](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/692d/7930164/be7598996b88/nihms-1621988-f0001.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3c/Functional_neurologic_disorder.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f2/Conversion_Disorder_Image.jpg)

Conversion disorder is now usually called **functional neurologic symptom disorder**.

It means:

* real neurologic symptoms
* like weakness, paralysis, tremor, blindness, numbness, or seizure-like episodes
* but the symptoms do **not match** a known structural neurologic disease

So the key idea is:

> the nervous system hardware is not destroyed, but the signaling/function is abnormal.

---

### Classic examples

Patients can present with:

* limb weakness or paralysis
* non-epileptic seizure-like episodes
* sensory loss
* abnormal gait
* tremor
* aphonia, meaning inability to speak
* blindness or visual symptoms

These symptoms are **not intentionally produced**.

That is important.

---

### Conversion disorder vs malingering

**Conversion disorder:**

* symptoms are unconscious
* patient is not faking
* no obvious external reward

**Malingering:**

* symptoms are intentionally produced
* there is external gain
* example: money, avoiding work, avoiding jail, getting drugs

---

### Why does it happen?

Old idea:

* psychological stress gets “converted” into physical neurologic symptoms

Modern idea:

* abnormal brain network function affects movement, sensation, attention, emotion, and sense of control

So it is not “made up.”

It is more like:

> the brain can still move/sense, but the normal voluntary control pathway is not working correctly.

---

### Important exam clue

Symptoms are often **inconsistent** with neuroanatomy.

For example:

* weakness that changes with distraction
* sensory loss that does not follow a dermatome, meaning a skin area supplied by one spinal nerve root
* seizure-like episodes with normal EEG, meaning electroencephalogram, which records brain electrical activity

---

### One-line intuition

Conversion disorder is a real neurologic-looking symptom caused by abnormal nervous system function, not structural nerve damage and not intentional faking.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, distinguish:

* **conversion disorder:** unconscious symptoms, no external gain
* **factitious disorder:** intentional symptoms, desire to be a patient
* **malingering:** intentional symptoms, external gain
