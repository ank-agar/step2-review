## Prader-Willi syndrome

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK1330/bin/pws-Image002.jpg)

![Image](https://pub.mdpi-res.com/ijms/ijms-24-04271/article_deploy/html/images/ijms-24-04271-g002.png)

Prader-Willi syndrome is a genetic disorder caused by loss of **paternal** gene expression on chromosome 15q11-q13.

Key idea:

* the dad's chromosome 15 region should be active
* the mom's copy is normally imprinted
* imprinting = epigenetic silencing, meaning the gene is present but turned off

So if the paternal copy is deleted or not working, there is no active copy.

---

### Classic findings

In infancy:

* hypotonia (low muscle tone)
* poor feeding
* weak cry
* failure to thrive (poor growth/weight gain)

Later childhood:

* hyperphagia (excessive hunger)
* obesity
* short stature
* intellectual disability
* hypogonadism (underdeveloped sex organs/decreased sex hormone function)
* small hands and feet

---

### Why the findings happen

The main problem is hypothalamic dysfunction.

Hypothalamus = brain region that controls hunger, hormones, temperature, and autonomic functions.

So:

* bad satiety signaling -> hyperphagia and obesity
* low GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone) signaling -> hypogonadism
* low GH (growth hormone) axis function -> short stature
* abnormal neurologic development -> hypotonia and intellectual disability

---

### Genetics intuition

Think:

> Prader-Willi = missing dad signal.

Most common mechanisms:

* paternal deletion of chromosome 15q11-q13
* maternal uniparental disomy, meaning both chromosome 15 copies come from mom
* imprinting defect, meaning the dad's copy is present but incorrectly silenced

---

## One-line intuition

Prader-Willi syndrome is when the child loses the active dad copy of chromosome 15q11-q13, causing hypothalamic dysfunction with infant hypotonia followed by uncontrollable hunger and obesity.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Prader-Willi and Angelman syndrome involve the same chromosome region:

* **Prader-Willi:** paternal deletion or loss of paternal expression
* **Angelman:** maternal deletion or loss of maternal UBE3A expression

Shortcut:

> Prader-Willi = hungry, hypotonic kid from missing dad chromosome 15 signal.
