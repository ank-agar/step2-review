# Holoprosencephaly

### Core idea

Holoprosencephaly is a congenital brain malformation where the **prosencephalon** (embryonic forebrain) fails to properly split into two cerebral hemispheres.

Break the word apart:

* holo- = whole/undivided
* prosencephalon = embryonic forebrain
* -cephaly = head/brain condition

So:

> the forebrain stays too fused instead of separating into left and right halves.

---

### Types

From most severe to least severe:

* **Alobar:** no real separation; one fused forebrain/ventricle
* **Semilobar:** partial separation, usually posterior brain separates more than anterior brain
* **Lobar:** mostly separated, but some midline frontal fusion remains

Intuition:

The more the brain separates, the milder the type.

---

### Why the findings happen

The forebrain normally splits early in development. If that midline splitting fails:

* **single ventricle/monoventricle:** the ventricular cavities do not divide normally
* **fused thalami:** deep midline brain structures stay stuck together
* **absent corpus callosum:** the corpus callosum (nerve fiber tract connecting the hemispheres) cannot form normally if the hemispheres do not separate normally
* **seizures/developmental delay:** abnormal cortical brain structure irritates and impairs brain function
* **facial abnormalities:** the face and forebrain develop together at the midline

That is why severe holoprosencephaly can cause midline facial defects like:

* cyclopia (single eye or fused eye field)
* hypotelorism (closely spaced eyes)
* cleft lip/palate
* single central incisor

---

### Important associations

Classically associated with:

* **trisomy 13** (Patau syndrome; three copies of chromosome 13)
* **SHH mutation** (Sonic hedgehog gene mutation)
* maternal diabetes

SHH is important for midline patterning, so if SHH signaling is abnormal, the brain/face midline can fail to separate properly.

---

## One-line intuition

Holoprosencephaly = the embryonic forebrain fails to split into two hemispheres, so the brain and often the midline face stay fused.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, connect **holoprosencephaly + cleft lip/palate or cyclopia + polydactyly** with **trisomy 13**.
