## Paranoid personality disorder

![Image](https://psychicare.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Personality-Disorders.jpg)

![Image](https://cdn-prod.medicalnewstoday.com/content/images/articles/327/327003/a-man-with-paranoid-personality-disorder-looking-suspiciously-at-a-coworker.jpg)

![Image](https://www.goodtherapy.org/dbimages/ckupload_20180906210222_man_sulks_while_worried_friends_look_on.jpg)

Paranoid personality disorder (PPD) is a Cluster A personality disorder.

Cluster A = odd/eccentric personality disorders:

* paranoid
* schizoid
* schizotypal

Core idea:

> chronic distrust and suspiciousness of other people, even when there is no good evidence.

The person often interprets neutral things as threatening.

Examples:

* “They complimented me, but they’re probably mocking me.”
* “My friend is late, so they must not respect me.”
* “My partner is talking to someone, so they must be cheating.”

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The brain’s “threat detector” is basically turned up too high.

So normal social ambiguity gets interpreted as danger:

* harmless comment → insult
* small mistake → betrayal
* privacy → secrecy
* disagreement → attack

That leads to:

* guardedness
* jealousy
* holding grudges
* reluctance to confide in others
* hypersensitivity to criticism

---

### Key distinction

PPD usually does **not** mean frank psychosis.

Psychosis = loss of reality testing, such as delusions (fixed false beliefs) or hallucinations (sensory experiences without external stimulus).

In PPD, the patient is suspicious and mistrustful, but usually does not have clear hallucinations or bizarre fixed delusions.

---

### Compare

* **Paranoid personality disorder:** “I don’t trust people.”
* **Schizoid personality disorder:** “I don’t really want close relationships.”
* **Schizotypal personality disorder:** “I have odd beliefs/magical thinking.”

---

## One-line intuition

Paranoid personality disorder is a lifelong pattern of seeing other people’s motives as hostile or untrustworthy.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1: Paranoid personality disorder is **ego-syntonic**.

Ego-syntonic = the person sees the behavior as reasonable and part of who they are, not as a problem.

So they often do **not** think, “I am too suspicious.”

They think, “Other people really are dangerous/untrustworthy.”
