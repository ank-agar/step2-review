### Core idea

Methamphetamine use disorder = continued methamphetamine use despite harm, cravings, inability to cut down, tolerance, or withdrawal.

Methamphetamine is a powerful CNS (central nervous system) stimulant.

Think:

* very high dopamine
* very high norepinephrine
* intense reward/reinforcement
* sympathetic overdrive

Sympathetic = fight-or-flight system.

---

### Mechanism

Methamphetamine increases release of:

* dopamine
* norepinephrine
* serotonin

Big Step 1 mechanism:

* enters presynaptic neuron
* enters vesicles through VMAT2 (vesicular monoamine transporter 2)
* pushes dopamine out of vesicles into the cytoplasm
* reverses DAT (dopamine transporter), sending dopamine into the synapse

Synapse = space between neurons.

So instead of just blocking dopamine cleanup, meth actively dumps dopamine out.

---

### Why it is addictive

Dopamine is the brain's reward/learning signal.

Meth causes a huge dopamine surge in the mesolimbic pathway.

Mesolimbic pathway = reward pathway from the VTA (ventral tegmental area) to the nucleus accumbens.

So the brain learns:

> this drug is extremely important, repeat it.

That is why cravings and compulsive use happen.

---

### Findings

During use/intoxication:

* euphoria = intense pleasure
* increased energy and alertness
* decreased appetite
* insomnia = inability to sleep
* agitation/paranoia
* tachycardia = fast heart rate
* hypertension = high blood pressure
* mydriasis = dilated pupils
* diaphoresis = sweating

Long-term use can cause:

* weight loss
* dental caries/tooth decay, called "meth mouth"
* skin picking/excoriations
* anxiety, paranoia, or psychosis
* cognitive problems
* cardiomyopathy (weakened heart muscle)

Psychosis = loss of contact with reality, often hallucinations or delusions.

---

### Withdrawal

Withdrawal is mostly a dopamine-crash picture:

* depression
* fatigue
* increased sleep
* increased appetite
* strong cravings
* anhedonia

Anhedonia = inability to feel pleasure.

Reason:

After repeated huge dopamine surges, the brain becomes less responsive to normal rewards.

---

### Treatment idea

Main treatment is behavioral therapy, especially contingency management.

Contingency management = rewards for objective evidence of not using, like negative urine drug tests.

There is no classic first-line FDA-approved medication specifically for methamphetamine use disorder.

FDA = Food and Drug Administration.

---

## One-line intuition

Methamphetamine use disorder is the brain getting trained by massive dopamine release to keep chasing meth despite worsening health, sleep, mood, teeth, heart, and life function.
