## Graves disease

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17067.jpg)

![Image](https://dm5migu4zj3pb.cloudfront.net/manuscripts/15000/15745/medium/JCI0215745.f1.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/25/Myxedema.jpg)

Graves disease is an autoimmune cause of hyperthyroidism.

Autoimmune = your immune system attacks your own tissue.

Hyperthyroidism = too much thyroid hormone.

---

### Core idea

In Graves disease, the body makes TSI (thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulin).

TSI acts like TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone), meaning it stimulates the TSH receptor on thyroid follicular cells.

So the thyroid gets a fake "make hormone" signal nonstop.

Result:

* increased T3 (triiodothyronine)
* increased T4 (thyroxine)
* decreased TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)

Why decreased TSH?

Because high T3/T4 tells the pituitary gland:

> we already have enough thyroid hormone, stop releasing TSH.

But the thyroid keeps making hormone anyway because TSI is still stimulating it.

---

### Classic findings

Graves causes hyperthyroid symptoms:

* weight loss
* heat intolerance
* sweating
* tremor
* palpitations
* anxiety
* diarrhea

Think:

> too much thyroid hormone = body metabolism is turned up.

---

### Big Graves-specific clues

Graves classically has:

* diffuse goiter
* exophthalmos
* pretibial myxedema

Goiter = enlarged thyroid gland.

Exophthalmos = bulging eyes.

Pretibial myxedema = thick, waxy swelling of skin over the shins.

---

### Why the eyes bulge

TSH receptors are also found on fibroblasts in the orbit.

Fibroblasts = connective tissue cells.

Orbit = eye socket.

TSI stimulates these fibroblasts, causing inflammation and glycosaminoglycan buildup.

Glycosaminoglycans = water-holding molecules in connective tissue.

That pulls water into the tissue behind the eye, so the eye gets pushed forward.

That is why Graves causes exophthalmos.

---

### Why pretibial myxedema happens

Same basic idea:

TSI activates fibroblasts in skin, especially over the shins.

Those fibroblasts deposit glycosaminoglycans, which hold water and make the skin thick/swollen.

So pretibial myxedema is not from low thyroid hormone.

It is from autoimmune fibroblast activation.

---

### Labs

Typical Graves labs:

* decreased TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
* increased free T4 (thyroxine)
* increased T3 (triiodothyronine)
* positive TSI (thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulin)

Radioactive iodine uptake is diffusely increased.

Diffuse = the whole thyroid lights up, not just one nodule.

---

## One-line intuition

Graves is when antibodies pretend to be TSH, overstimulating the whole thyroid and causing hyperthyroidism plus eye/skin findings.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Graves disease is the most common cause of hyperthyroidism, and the key antibody is TSI (thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulin), which stimulates the TSH receptor.
