# Rheumatoid arthritis

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Rheumatoid_Arthritis_%28Swan_Neck_Deformity%29.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/83/Swan_neck_deformity_in_a_65_year_old_Rheumatoid_Arthritis_patient-_2014-05-27_01-49.jpg)

Rheumatoid arthritis is a chronic autoimmune inflammatory disease where the immune system attacks the synovium (joint lining).

Classic joint pattern:

* symmetric joint pain/swelling
* MCP (metacarpophalangeal joints = knuckles) involvement
* PIP (proximal interphalangeal joints = middle finger joints) involvement
* wrist involvement
* DIP (distal interphalangeal joints = joints closest to fingernails) usually spared

---

### Core idea

The immune system inflames the synovium, which becomes thick and destructive.

That thick inflammatory tissue is called pannus (abnormal inflamed synovial tissue that grows over cartilage).

Pannus destroys:

* cartilage
* bone
* tendons/ligaments

So RA causes painful swollen joints early, then deformities later.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Morning stiffness:** inflammatory fluid builds up overnight
* **Symmetric small joint disease:** autoimmune inflammation tends to hit similar joints on both sides
* **Ulnar deviation:** chronic MCP joint/tendon damage pulls fingers toward the ulna (pinky side)
* **Swan-neck deformity:** PIP hyperextension + DIP flexion from tendon/joint damage
* **Boutonniere deformity:** PIP flexion + DIP hyperextension from extensor tendon damage

---

### Important antibodies

* RF (rheumatoid factor) = antibody against Fc portion of IgG (immunoglobulin G)
* anti-CCP (anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide antibody) = more specific for RA

Anti-CCP is especially useful because it is more specific and predicts more erosive disease.

---

## One-line intuition

RA is autoimmune synovitis (inflammation of the joint lining) that forms pannus, which eats cartilage/bone and deforms the joint.

---

## Pearl 🔥

RA is associated with HLA-DR4 (human leukocyte antigen DR4), and the classic Step 1 drug association is methotrexate causing folate deficiency, so give folinic acid/leucovorin rescue when needed.
