# Septic abortion

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/Blausen_0719_PelvicInflammatoryDisease.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c3/Incomplete_miscarriage.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Endometritis_-_2_-_high_mag.jpg)

Septic abortion = infection of the uterus around the time of an abortion or miscarriage.

Key idea:

* abortion/miscarriage happens
* infected tissue may remain in the uterus
* bacteria ascend from the vagina/cervix into the uterus
* infection can spread into the blood
* patient can develop sepsis (life-threatening body-wide inflammatory response to infection)

---

### Classic findings

Think:

* fever/chills
* pelvic or lower abdominal pain
* uterine tenderness
* vaginal bleeding
* foul-smelling vaginal discharge
* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* hypotension (low blood pressure) if septic shock develops

The big danger is not just local uterine infection.

The danger is:

> infected retained products of conception → bacteria/toxins enter blood → sepsis/shock.

Products of conception means pregnancy tissue, like placental or fetal tissue.

---

### Why retained tissue matters

Retained tissue acts like a nutrient-rich surface for bacteria.

So if pregnancy tissue stays in the uterus after miscarriage/abortion, bacteria can grow there and cause endometritis (inflammation/infection of the uterine lining).

That is why treatment is usually:

* IV antibiotics (intravenous antibiotics, meaning antibiotics through a vein)
* uterine evacuation/removal of retained tissue

Antibiotics kill the bacteria, but if infected tissue remains, it can keep feeding the infection.

---

### Organisms

Usually polymicrobial.

Polymicrobial means multiple organisms together.

Common coverage needs:

* gram-positive bacteria
* gram-negative bacteria
* anaerobes (bacteria that grow without oxygen)

Anaerobes matter because the uterus/pelvis can become a low-oxygen infected space.

---

## One-line intuition

Septic abortion is an infected miscarriage/abortion where retained pregnancy tissue lets bacteria grow in the uterus and potentially spill into the bloodstream.

---

## Pearl

HY Step 1: septic abortion is a gynecologic emergency; management is broad-spectrum IV antibiotics plus prompt evacuation of retained products of conception.
