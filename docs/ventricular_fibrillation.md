## Ventricular fibrillation

![Image](https://assets.mayoclinic.org/content/dam/media/en/images/2023/02/09/ventricular-fibrillation.jpg)

![Image](https://nl.ecgpedia.org/images/9/9e/Rhythm_ventricular_fibrillation.png)

![Image](https://nl.ecgpedia.org/images/3/35/ECG_SR_to_VF_in_INF_MI.jpg)

Ventricular fibrillation, or VF (ventricular fibrillation), is a lethal arrhythmia (abnormal heart rhythm) where the ventricles (lower heart chambers) fire chaotically.

Instead of squeezing together as one strong pump, the ventricles just quiver.

So:

* no coordinated ventricular contraction
* no cardiac output (blood pumped forward)
* no pulse
* sudden cardiac arrest

---

### Core idea

The ventricles are electrically active, but mechanically useless.

That is why the ECG (electrocardiogram) looks chaotic:

* no clear P waves
* no organized QRS complexes
* irregular squiggly baseline

Break it down:

* ventricular = from the ventricles
* fibrillation = rapid, disorganized twitching

So ventricular fibrillation literally means:

> the ventricles are twitching chaotically instead of pumping.

---

### Why it is so dangerous

The ventricles are the main pumping chambers.

If they do not pump:

* brain gets no blood → loss of consciousness
* coronary arteries (heart arteries) get poor blood flow → worse ischemia (low oxygen)
* death can occur within minutes

This is why VF (ventricular fibrillation) is treated immediately with:

* CPR (cardiopulmonary resuscitation) to manually keep blood moving
* defibrillation (electric shock to reset chaotic cardiac electrical activity)

---

### Common causes

Often triggered by ischemic heart disease (poor blood flow to heart muscle), especially:

* MI (myocardial infarction/heart attack)
* scarred myocardium (heart muscle)
* electrolyte problems, especially abnormal potassium or magnesium
* inherited channelopathies (ion channel disorders), like long QT syndrome

---

## One-line intuition

VF (ventricular fibrillation) = the ventricles are electrically screaming but not mechanically pumping.

---

## Pearl 🔥

VF (ventricular fibrillation) is a shockable cardiac arrest rhythm.

Shock:

* VF (ventricular fibrillation)
* pulseless VT (ventricular tachycardia, fast ventricular rhythm)

Do not shock:

* asystole (flatline)
* PEA (pulseless electrical activity, organized electrical rhythm without a pulse)
