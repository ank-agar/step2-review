# Antisocial personality disorder

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/70/Signs_of_AsPD_1.png)

Antisocial personality disorder (ASPD) is a Cluster B personality disorder (dramatic/emotional/erratic personality disorder group) where the person has a long-term pattern of:

* violating other people's rights
* breaking rules/laws
* lying/manipulating
* impulsive or aggressive behavior
* lack of remorse

Important: **antisocial does NOT mean shy or introverted.**

That is **asocial** (prefers less social interaction).

Antisocial = against society/rules/rights of others.

---

### Core idea

ASPD is basically:

> "I know the rule exists, but I do not care if I break it or hurt someone."

So the issue is not usually psychosis (loss of reality testing).

It is more about:

* low empathy
* poor impulse control
* poor guilt/remorse
* repeated harmful behavior

---

### Diagnosis clue

You need:

* age **18 or older**
* evidence of **conduct disorder** before age 15

Conduct disorder (childhood pattern of violating rules/rights) includes things like:

* aggression toward people/animals
* destruction of property
* deceit/theft
* serious rule violations

Why this matters:

Personality disorders are diagnosed in adults, but ASPD usually has roots in childhood behavior.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

If someone has reduced empathy/remorse, then harming others does not create the normal internal "stop signal."

So they may:

* lie because it gets them what they want
* manipulate because other people are treated like tools
* break laws because consequences feel less important
* act impulsively because long-term harm is not weighed strongly

---

### High-yield associations

ASPD is associated with:

* substance use disorder (SUD; problematic use of drugs/alcohol)
* incarceration/legal problems
* aggression/violence
* risky behavior
* childhood conduct disorder

Also, people with ASPD often do **not** seek treatment on their own because they may not view their behavior as wrong.

---

## One-line intuition

ASPD = chronic disregard for rules and other people's rights, usually starting as conduct disorder in childhood.

---

## Pearl 🔥

For Step 1, remember:

**Conduct disorder before 15 + adult age 18+ = antisocial personality disorder.**

And do not confuse:

* **antisocial** = violates others' rights/social rules
* **asocial** = socially withdrawn
