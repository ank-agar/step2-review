Inhalant use disorder means compulsive use of inhaled chemical vapors despite harm.

These are not usually "drugs" in the classic sense. They are often household/workplace chemicals like:

* glue
* paint thinner
* spray paint
* gasoline
* nail polish remover
* aerosol propellants

The person inhales the fumes to get intoxicated.

---

### Core idea

Volatile chemicals enter through the lungs, rapidly cross into blood, and reach the brain within seconds.

Why so fast?

* lungs have huge surface area
* chemicals are lipid-soluble (fat-soluble)
* lipid-soluble substances cross the blood-brain barrier easily

So the intuition is:

> inhale chemical vapor -> rapid brain entry -> short high -> repeated use -> addiction/toxicity.

---

### What the high feels like

Inhalants often act like CNS depressants.

CNS = central nervous system, meaning brain + spinal cord.

So symptoms can look like alcohol intoxication:

* euphoria
* dizziness
* slurred speech
* poor coordination
* confusion
* impaired judgment

---

### Why repeated use is dangerous

Inhalants can injure many organs:

* brain: cognitive problems, ataxia (poor coordination), white matter damage
* heart: arrhythmias (abnormal rhythms)
* liver/kidney: toxic injury
* lungs: irritation, hypoxia

Hypoxia = low oxygen.

This happens because inhaled chemicals can displace oxygen in the lungs, so the brain and heart may not get enough oxygen.

---

### Big testable danger

Sudden sniffing death = sudden fatal arrhythmia after inhalant use.

Arrhythmia = abnormal heart rhythm.

This can happen even in a first-time user.

Reason:

Some inhalants sensitize the myocardium (heart muscle) to catecholamines.

Catecholamines = epinephrine/norepinephrine-type stress hormones.

So if the person gets scared/runs/exerts themselves, the heart can flip into a lethal rhythm.

---

### One-line intuition

Inhalants are fast-acting lipid-soluble vapors that give a brief CNS depressant high but can suddenly kill via cardiac arrhythmia or chronically damage brain, liver, kidney, and lungs.

---

### HY pearl

For Step 1, remember inhalants are classically associated with **sudden sniffing death** from ventricular arrhythmia.

Ventricular arrhythmia = abnormal rhythm starting in the ventricles (main pumping chambers of the heart).
