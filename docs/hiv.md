HIV (human immunodeficiency virus) is an enveloped RNA (ribonucleic acid) retrovirus that infects CD4 T cells (helper T cells).

Core idea:

* HIV attacks CD4 T cells
* CD4 T cells coordinate the immune system
* so losing them causes immunodeficiency (weak immune defense)

---

### Why HIV is a retrovirus

Most viruses go:

* DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) → RNA → protein

HIV goes backward:

* RNA → DNA

That backward step is done by reverse transcriptase (enzyme that converts RNA into DNA).

Then integrase (enzyme that inserts viral DNA into host DNA) puts the viral DNA into the human cell genome.

So the virus basically hides inside the host cell's DNA instructions.

---

### How HIV enters cells

HIV binds:

* gp120 (viral attachment protein) to CD4 (helper T-cell receptor)
* then a coreceptor, usually CCR5 or CXCR4 (chemokine receptors)

Then gp41 (viral fusion protein) helps HIV fuse with the cell membrane.

One-line intuition:

> gp120 docks, gp41 fuses.

---

### Why CD4 count drops

HIV infects CD4 T cells and causes their loss through:

* direct viral damage
* immune killing of infected cells
* chronic immune activation

As CD4 count falls, the patient becomes vulnerable to opportunistic infections (infections that take advantage of weak immunity).

Classic major cutoff:

* CD4 < 200 cells/mm3 = AIDS-defining level

AIDS (acquired immunodeficiency syndrome) is the late stage of HIV with severe immune suppression or AIDS-defining illness.

---

### Treatment logic

ART (antiretroviral therapy) blocks key HIV steps:

* entry/fusion
* reverse transcription
* integration
* protease processing

Protease (viral enzyme that cuts viral proteins into mature functional pieces) is needed for new viruses to become infectious.

So protease inhibitors make immature, nonfunctional viral particles.

---

## One-line intuition

HIV is an RNA retrovirus that enters CD4 T cells, reverse-transcribes into DNA, integrates into the host genome, and slowly destroys helper T-cell immunity.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HIV is a lentivirus (slow retrovirus), and the Step 1 buzzword enzymes are:

* reverse transcriptase
* integrase
* protease
