### Core idea

Myositis = inflammation of muscle.

Break the word apart:

* myo- = muscle
* -itis = inflammation

So myositis literally means:

> inflamed muscle.

Usually on Step 1, this refers to inflammatory myopathies (muscle diseases caused by inflammation), especially:

* polymyositis
* dermatomyositis
* inclusion body myositis

---

### What happens

The immune system attacks skeletal muscle, causing:

* muscle fiber injury
* inflammatory cell infiltration
* muscle weakness
* elevated CK (creatine kinase), because damaged muscle leaks muscle enzymes into blood

CK (creatine kinase) is high because muscle cells are breaking open and spilling their intracellular enzymes.

---

### Classic symptom

The big pattern is:

* proximal muscle weakness

Proximal means close to the trunk, so think:

* shoulders
* hips
* thighs

So patients may have trouble:

* climbing stairs
* standing from a chair
* lifting arms overhead

Why proximal weakness?

Because autoimmune inflammatory myopathies often target large limb-girdle muscles first. Limb-girdle means muscles around the shoulders and hips.

---

### Dermatomyositis

Dermatomyositis = skin + muscle inflammation.

Dermato- means skin.

Classic findings:

* proximal muscle weakness
* heliotrope rash around the eyes
* Gottron papules over knuckles

Mechanism:

* complement-mediated damage to small blood vessels in muscle
* perifascicular atrophy

Perifascicular atrophy means muscle fibers shrink near the edge of a muscle fascicle (bundle of muscle fibers).

---

### Polymyositis

Polymyositis = many muscles inflamed.

Classically:

* proximal muscle weakness
* no skin rash

Mechanism:

* CD8+ T cells attack muscle fibers

CD8+ T cells are cytotoxic T cells, meaning they directly kill infected or abnormal cells.

---

### One-line intuition

Myositis is when muscle becomes an immune target, so inflamed muscle fibers get damaged and weak.

---

## Pearl

Dermatomyositis in adults is associated with increased malignancy risk, especially ovarian, lung, pancreatic, stomach, and colorectal cancers.
