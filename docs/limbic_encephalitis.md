### Core idea

Limbic encephalitis is inflammation of the limbic system, especially the:

* hippocampus
* amygdala
* medial temporal lobes

The limbic system controls memory, emotion, behavior, and seizure tendency.

So if it gets inflamed, the big symptoms are:

* memory problems
* confusion
* personality/behavior changes
* seizures
* hallucinations or psychiatric symptoms

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Memory loss:** the hippocampus helps form new memories
* **Emotional/behavior changes:** the amygdala helps regulate fear/emotion
* **Seizures:** inflamed temporal lobe tissue becomes electrically irritable
* **Psych symptoms:** limbic circuits connect emotion, memory, and perception

MRI (magnetic resonance imaging) classically shows increased T2/FLAIR signal in the medial temporal lobes.

FLAIR (fluid-attenuated inversion recovery) is an MRI sequence that makes edema/inflammation look bright.

---

### Causes to know

Two big buckets:

* **Autoimmune/paraneoplastic:** antibodies attack limbic brain tissue
* **Infectious:** especially HSV (herpes simplex virus) encephalitis

Paraneoplastic means caused indirectly by cancer, where the immune system attacks tumor antigens and accidentally cross-reacts with nervous tissue.

---

## One-line intuition

Limbic encephalitis is “brain inflammation in the memory/emotion center,” so patients get memory loss, behavior changes, and seizures.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HSV (herpes simplex virus) encephalitis loves the temporal lobes and can mimic limbic encephalitis, so temporal lobe inflammation + fever/seizures = think HSV and treat quickly with acyclovir.
