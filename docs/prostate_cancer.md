## Prostate cancer

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/Knochenszintigramm_multiple_Filiae.jpg)

Prostate cancer is malignant growth of prostate gland cells.

Most prostate cancers are:

* adenocarcinomas (cancers from gland-forming epithelial cells)
* in the peripheral zone (outer/posterior part of the prostate)

That is why it may be felt on DRE (digital rectal exam), because the posterior prostate is near the rectum.

---

### Core idea

Think:

> older man + peripheral posterior prostate mass + elevated PSA (prostate-specific antigen) + osteoblastic bone metastases.

Osteoblastic means the cancer stimulates new bone formation, so bone lesions look denser/sclerotic rather than punched-out.

---

### Why symptoms happen

Early prostate cancer often has no symptoms because it starts in the peripheral zone, away from the urethra.

If it grows enough, it can cause urinary obstruction:

* hesitancy
* weak stream
* nocturia (waking up at night to urinate)
* retention

But urinary symptoms are more classic for BPH (benign prostatic hyperplasia), which grows in the transition zone around the urethra.

---

### Diagnosis/staging clues

PSA (prostate-specific antigen) can be elevated, but it is not perfectly specific because it can also rise with:

* BPH (benign prostatic hyperplasia)
* prostatitis (prostate inflammation)
* recent prostate manipulation

Biopsy gives the Gleason score, which grades how abnormal the gland architecture looks.

Intuition:

* more organized glands = lower grade
* fused/irregular/no glands = higher grade
* higher Gleason score = more aggressive cancer

---

### Spread

Prostate cancer classically metastasizes to bone, especially the spine and pelvis.

Why?

The prostate drains through venous plexuses that communicate with vertebral veins, so tumor cells can reach the axial skeleton.

Bone mets can cause:

* back pain
* high ALP (alkaline phosphatase), because bone formation is increased
* spinal cord compression in advanced disease

---

## One-line intuition

Prostate cancer is usually a posterior peripheral-zone adenocarcinoma that is often silent early, tracked with PSA, graded by Gleason score, and loves to make osteoblastic bone mets.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Prostate cancer = peripheral zone; BPH (benign prostatic hyperplasia) = transition zone.
