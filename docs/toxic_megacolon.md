# Toxic megacolon

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17189.jpg)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4b/Toxic_Megacolon_in_Ulcerative_Colitis.jpg)

Toxic megacolon is a life-threatening dilation of the colon due to severe colitis (inflammation of the colon).

Break the term apart:

* toxic = systemic illness, like fever/tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* mega = big
* colon = large intestine

So:

> inflamed colon gets huge + the patient looks very sick.

---

### Core idea

Severe inflammation damages the colon wall and paralyzes smooth muscle.

That causes:

* poor motility
* trapped gas/stool
* massive colon dilation
* risk of perforation (colon wall rupture)

Classically, the colon is dilated >6 cm on abdominal x-ray.

---

### Common causes

Most important Step 1 associations:

* ulcerative colitis
* Crohn disease
* C. difficile colitis (Clostridioides difficile infection)

Ulcerative colitis is especially classic because inflammation is continuous and limited to the colon.

---

### Why it is dangerous

As the colon stretches, the wall gets thinner.

Thin inflamed wall = easy rupture.

If it perforates, stool/bacteria can spill into the abdomen and cause peritonitis (inflammation/infection of the abdominal lining) and sepsis (life-threatening systemic infection).

---

### Presentation

Think:

* abdominal distension
* abdominal pain
* fever
* tachycardia (fast heart rate)
* diarrhea, often bloody if due to inflammatory bowel disease
* dehydration

---

## One-line intuition

Toxic megacolon is when severe colitis paralyzes and balloons the colon, making it a rupture risk.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Do **not** do colonoscopy in suspected toxic megacolon because insufflation (pumping air into the colon) can trigger perforation.
