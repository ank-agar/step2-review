### Core idea

Hepatitis C can cause kidney disease by forming immune complexes that get stuck in the glomerulus.

Glomerulus = the tiny kidney filtering ball.

The classic glomerular disease is:

* MPGN (membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis)
* often due to mixed cryoglobulinemia

Cryoglobulins = antibodies that precipitate in the cold and dissolve when warm.

---

### What hepatitis C does

Chronic hepatitis C infection keeps stimulating B cells.

B cells = immune cells that make antibodies.

This can lead to production of abnormal antibody complexes, especially:

* IgM (immunoglobulin M)
* IgG (immunoglobulin G)
* complement proteins

These immune complexes circulate in blood and deposit in glomerular capillaries.

---

### Why kidney findings happen

The immune complexes trigger inflammation in the glomerulus.

That causes:

* hematuria = blood in urine
* proteinuria = protein in urine
* hypertension = high blood pressure
* decreased kidney function

Reasoning:

The glomerulus is normally a delicate filter. If immune complexes deposit there, the filter gets inflamed and leaky, so red blood cells and protein escape into urine.

---

### Why it looks like MPGN

MPGN (membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis) means:

* membrano- = thickened glomerular basement membrane
* proliferative = increased cells/inflammation
* glomerulonephritis = inflamed glomeruli

The classic biopsy pattern is:

* thickened capillary walls
* hypercellular glomeruli
* tram-track appearance

Tram-track = duplicated/split glomerular basement membrane.

This happens because immune deposits sit under the endothelium, and the kidney tries to build new basement membrane around them.

Endothelium = inner lining of blood vessels.

---

### Key lab clue

Hepatitis C cryoglobulinemic disease often has low complement, especially:

* low C4

Complement = immune proteins that help inflammation.

Why low C4?

The immune complexes activate and consume complement, so blood complement levels drop.

---

## One-line intuition

Hepatitis C chronically stimulates antibody production, those antibody complexes clog/inflame the glomerular filter, causing MPGN with hematuria + proteinuria.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 association:

Hepatitis C → mixed cryoglobulinemia → MPGN (membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis) with low C4.

Also remember the cryoglobulinemia triad:

* palpable purpura
* arthralgia
* weakness

Arthralgia = joint pain.
