## Human papillomavirus infection

Human papillomavirus infection means infection with **HPV (human papillomavirus)**, a **non-enveloped double-stranded DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) virus** that infects **squamous epithelium**.

Squamous epithelium = flat surface-lining cells, like in:

* cervix
* vagina/vulva
* penis
* anus
* oropharynx (back of throat)
* skin

---

### Core idea

HPV likes epithelial cells and makes them proliferate.

That can cause:

* warts
* genital warts
* cervical dysplasia
* cervical cancer
* anal, penile, vulvar, vaginal, and oropharyngeal cancers

The key distinction is:

* **low-risk HPV:** causes warts
* **high-risk HPV:** can cause cancer

---

### High-yield types

* **HPV 6 and 11:** genital warts
* **HPV 16 and 18:** cervical cancer and other anogenital/oropharyngeal cancers

Think:

> 6 and 11 give you warts.  
> 16 and 18 are the scary cancer ones.

---

### Why HPV causes warts

HPV infects basal epithelial cells through tiny breaks in the skin or mucosa.

Then it pushes infected cells to keep dividing.

More epithelial cell growth = a raised lesion = wart.

So the wart is basically:

> virus-driven overgrowth of squamous epithelium.

---

### Why high-risk HPV can cause cancer

High-risk HPV makes viral proteins:

* **E6**
* **E7**

These disable tumor suppressors:

* **E6 inhibits p53**
* **E7 inhibits Rb (retinoblastoma protein)**

p53 and Rb normally act like brakes on the cell cycle.

So if HPV knocks out the brakes:

* damaged cells do not die when they should
* cells keep dividing
* mutations accumulate
* dysplasia can progress to cancer

---

### Cervical screening connection

HPV infection can cause **koilocytes**.

Koilocytes = squamous cells with:

* enlarged irregular nuclei
* a clear halo around the nucleus

This is a classic cytology clue for HPV infection.

Pap smear = test that samples cervical cells to look for abnormal cells.

HPV testing = test that looks for high-risk HPV DNA.

---

### Symptoms

Most HPV infections are asymptomatic.

When symptomatic, HPV can cause:

* painless genital warts
* abnormal Pap smear
* cervical dysplasia
* cancer years later if high-risk infection persists

Important: the infection itself is common, and most people clear it naturally.

The danger is **persistent high-risk HPV infection**, especially HPV 16 or 18.

---

### Prevention

HPV vaccine protects against the most important high-risk and wart-causing HPV types.

Why it works:

* it trains the immune system before exposure
* antibodies block HPV from infecting basal epithelial cells
* preventing infection prevents the downstream dysplasia/cancer pathway

---

## One-line intuition

HPV is a squamous-epithelium-loving DNA virus that causes warts by making cells grow, and causes cancer when high-risk types disable p53 and Rb.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HPV E6 knocks out **p53**, and HPV E7 knocks out **Rb**.

That is the Step 1 mechanism for high-risk HPV-associated cancers.
