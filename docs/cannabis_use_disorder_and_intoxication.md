### Core idea

Cannabis intoxication = the acute effects after using cannabis.

Cannabis use disorder = repeated cannabis use despite problems in life, school/work, health, or relationships.

The main psychoactive chemical is THC (tetrahydrocannabinol).

THC activates CB1 receptors (cannabinoid type 1 receptors) in the brain.

---

### Cannabis intoxication

Classic findings:

* euphoria or relaxation
* impaired judgment
* impaired coordination
* slowed reaction time
* increased appetite
* anxiety, panic, or paranoia
* conjunctival injection, meaning red eyes
* tachycardia, meaning fast heart rate
* dry mouth

Intuition:

THC alters brain signaling in areas for reward, memory, coordination, and perception, so the patient may feel relaxed or euphoric but has worse attention, memory, and motor control.

---

### Why red eyes happen

Cannabis causes vasodilation, meaning blood vessels widen.

So conjunctival vessels in the eye become more visible:

* vessel dilation
* more redness
* classic “red eyes”

---

### Cannabis use disorder

This is basically addiction to cannabis.

Look for:

* craving
* tolerance, meaning needing more for the same effect
* withdrawal, meaning symptoms after stopping
* unsuccessful attempts to cut down
* continued use despite social, school, work, or health problems
* lots of time spent getting, using, or recovering from cannabis

Withdrawal can cause:

* irritability
* anxiety
* insomnia, meaning trouble sleeping
* decreased appetite
* restlessness
* depressed mood
* abdominal pain, sweating, fever, chills, or headache

---

### Why dependence happens

The brain adapts to repeated THC stimulation.

Think:

* THC repeatedly stimulates CB1 receptors
* brain turns down its own normal cannabinoid signaling
* stopping THC suddenly leaves the system under-stimulated
* that causes withdrawal symptoms like irritability, poor sleep, and low appetite

So the patient keeps using cannabis partly to avoid feeling bad.

---

## One-line intuition

Cannabis intoxication is the short-term “high” from THC, while cannabis use disorder is when repeated THC use rewires reward/withdrawal circuits enough that the person keeps using despite harm.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Cannabis intoxication classically causes conjunctival injection plus increased appetite; on Step 1, red eyes + hunger + impaired coordination points strongly toward cannabis.
