### Core idea

Head and neck cancer = cancers that arise in the upper aerodigestive tract:

* oral cavity
* pharynx (throat)
* larynx (voice box)
* nasal cavity/paranasal sinuses
* salivary glands

Most are **SCC (squamous cell carcinoma)**, meaning cancer from squamous epithelial cells lining the mouth/throat.

---

### Main risk factors

* **Tobacco + alcohol:** classic risk combo
* **HPV (human papillomavirus):** especially oropharyngeal cancer, like tonsil/base of tongue
* **EBV (Epstein-Barr virus):** associated with nasopharyngeal carcinoma

Why tobacco/alcohol matter:

* they chronically damage mucosa
* repeated injury causes mutations
* mutated squamous cells can become dysplastic, then malignant

---

### How it presents

Think: a persistent local problem in the mouth/throat/voice area.

Common findings:

* nonhealing mouth ulcer
* neck mass from lymph node spread
* hoarseness
* trouble swallowing
* ear pain without ear disease
* weight loss

The neck mass happens because head and neck cancers often spread first through lymphatics to cervical lymph nodes (neck lymph nodes).

---

### HPV-related intuition

HPV-positive oropharyngeal cancer is often in:

* tonsils
* base of tongue

HPV makes viral proteins that inactivate tumor suppressors:

* E6 inhibits p53
* E7 inhibits Rb (retinoblastoma protein)

So the cell loses its “stop dividing / repair DNA / undergo apoptosis” controls.

---

## One-line intuition

Head and neck cancer is usually squamous cancer of the mouth/throat/voice-box lining, classically from tobacco/alcohol or HPV, and it commonly shows up as a mouth lesion, hoarseness, dysphagia, or neck lymph node mass.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HPV-positive oropharyngeal SCC (squamous cell carcinoma) is associated with **p16 positivity**, because HPV E7 knocks out Rb (retinoblastoma protein), causing compensatory p16 overexpression.
