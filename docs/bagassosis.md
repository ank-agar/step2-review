# Bagassosis

Bagassosis is an occupational lung disease from inhaling moldy sugarcane dust.

More specifically:

* bagasse = leftover fibrous sugarcane pulp after juice extraction
* -osis = disease process

So bagassosis literally means:

> lung disease from bagasse dust.

---

### Core idea

Bagassosis is a type of hypersensitivity pneumonitis (immune-mediated inflammation of alveoli and small airways after inhaling an antigen).

The usual trigger is thermophilic actinomycetes (heat-loving filamentous bacteria) growing in moldy sugarcane bagasse.

---

### Why the symptoms happen

The dust particles get inhaled deep into the lung:

* they reach alveoli (tiny air sacs where gas exchange happens)
* the immune system recognizes the organic antigen
* inflammation builds in the interstitium (thin tissue between alveoli)
* gas exchange gets harder

That gives:

* cough
* dyspnea (shortness of breath)
* fever
* malaise
* restrictive lung findings sometimes

If exposure continues, repeated inflammation can become fibrosis (scarring).

---

## One-line intuition

Bagassosis is basically “farmer’s lung,” but the antigen source is moldy sugarcane bagasse instead of moldy hay.

---

## Pearl 🔥

HY Step 1 link: hypersensitivity pneumonitis is mainly a type III hypersensitivity reaction (immune complex-mediated reaction), with chronic cases also involving type IV hypersensitivity (T-cell-mediated delayed reaction).
