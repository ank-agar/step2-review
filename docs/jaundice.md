### Core idea

Jaundice is yellow discoloration of the skin/eyes due to increased bilirubin.

Bilirubin = yellow breakdown product of heme from red blood cells.

So the intuition is:

> too much RBC (red blood cell) breakdown product builds up → tissues look yellow.

---

### Normal bilirubin path

RBCs break down → heme becomes unconjugated bilirubin.

Unconjugated bilirubin:

* is not water-soluble
* travels in blood bound to albumin
* goes to the liver

Then the liver conjugates it.

Conjugated bilirubin = bilirubin made water-soluble by the liver so it can go into bile and leave through stool.

---

### Why jaundice happens

Think of it as 3 big categories:

* **Prehepatic:** before the liver
* **Hepatic:** inside the liver
* **Posthepatic:** after the liver

---

### Prehepatic jaundice

Problem:

* too much RBC breakdown

Example:

* hemolytic anemia

Why:

* more RBCs destroyed → more heme → more unconjugated bilirubin
* liver is normal, but it gets overwhelmed

Main bilirubin type:

* unconjugated bilirubin

---

### Hepatic jaundice

Problem:

* liver cells cannot process or excrete bilirubin well

Examples:

* hepatitis
* cirrhosis
* alcoholic liver disease

Why:

* damaged hepatocytes (liver cells) cannot conjugate bilirubin normally
* or they leak/excrete conjugated bilirubin poorly

Main bilirubin type:

* mixed unconjugated + conjugated bilirubin

---

### Posthepatic jaundice

Problem:

* bile cannot drain

Examples:

* gallstone blocking the common bile duct
* pancreatic head cancer compressing the bile duct

Why:

* liver conjugates bilirubin fine
* but the drainage pipe is blocked
* conjugated bilirubin backs up into blood

Main bilirubin type:

* conjugated bilirubin

---

### Classic clues

Jaundice can show up as:

* yellow skin
* yellow eyes
* dark urine
* pale stools
* itching

Why dark urine?

* conjugated bilirubin is water-soluble, so kidneys can pee it out.

Why pale stools?

* bilirubin normally enters gut and becomes stercobilin, which makes stool brown
* if bile is blocked, less pigment reaches stool

Why itching?

* bile salts can build up in skin during cholestasis

Cholestasis = reduced or blocked bile flow.

---

## One-line intuition

Jaundice means bilirubin is building up, usually because RBC breakdown is too high, the liver is sick, or bile drainage is blocked.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Unconjugated bilirubin cannot enter urine because it is albumin-bound and not water-soluble; conjugated bilirubin can enter urine because it is water-soluble.
