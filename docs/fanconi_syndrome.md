## Fanconi syndrome

### Core idea

Fanconi syndrome = generalized failure of the PCT (proximal convoluted tubule) to reabsorb useful stuff.

So instead of keeping things in the blood, the kidney dumps them into urine:

* glucose
* amino acids
* phosphate
* bicarbonate
* uric acid
* potassium

Think:

> PCT is the “bulk reabsorption” segment, and Fanconi syndrome breaks the bulk reabsorber.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Glucosuria with normal blood glucose:** glucose is filtered normally, but the damaged PCT cannot reabsorb it
* **Aminoaciduria:** amino acids are lost in urine
* **Phosphaturia:** phosphate is lost in urine
* **Hypophosphatemic rickets/osteomalacia:** low phosphate means poor bone mineralization  
  * rickets = defective bone mineralization in children
  * osteomalacia = defective bone mineralization in adults
* **Proximal RTA (renal tubular acidosis, type 2):** bicarbonate is lost in urine, so blood becomes acidotic
* **Polyuria/polydipsia:** solute wasting pulls water into urine, causing lots of urination and thirst

---

### Common causes

Inherited:

* cystinosis
* Wilson disease
* galactosemia
* tyrosinemia

Acquired:

* multiple myeloma
* tenofovir
* ifosfamide
* cisplatin
* valproic acid
* expired tetracyclines
* heavy metals

---

## One-line intuition

Fanconi syndrome is when the proximal tubule becomes leaky, so the body pees out “good stuff” it was supposed to keep.

---

## Pearl

Fanconi syndrome is **not** Fanconi anemia.

* Fanconi syndrome = kidney PCT wasting problem
* Fanconi anemia = DNA repair defect causing aplastic anemia, thumb/radius abnormalities, and cancer risk
