## Acute bronchiolitis

![Image](https://ddcontent.digitaldoorway.org.za/modules/medline_plus/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17098.jpg)

![Image](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK442240/bin/ch5f3.jpg)

![Image](https://doctorlib.org/medical/thoracic-pathology/thoracic-pathology.files/image136.jpg)

Acute bronchiolitis = viral inflammation of the bronchioles (tiny airways near the end of the respiratory tree), classically in infants.

Most common cause:

* RSV (respiratory syncytial virus)

Other viruses can do it too, but for Step 1, think:

> baby + winter + wheezing after URI = RSV bronchiolitis

URI (upper respiratory infection) means infection of the nose/throat area.

---

### Core idea

The virus infects small airway epithelium (lining cells).

That causes:

* edema (swelling from fluid)
* mucus production
* epithelial sloughing (dead lining cells falling off)
* small airway obstruction

So the baby is basically trying to breathe through tiny tubes that are swollen and clogged.

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Wheezing:** air is forced through narrowed bronchioles
* **Tachypnea:** fast breathing because oxygen exchange is harder
* **Retractions/nasal flaring:** increased work of breathing
* **Hyperinflation:** air gets in but has trouble getting out, so air trapping happens
* **Poor feeding:** babies cannot breathe and feed comfortably at the same time

Retractions = chest wall pulling inward during breathing.

---

### Important distinction

Bronchiolitis is not mainly bronchospasm (smooth muscle tightening), like asthma.

It is mainly:

* mucus
* edema
* dead epithelial debris

That is why bronchodilators (meds that open airways by relaxing smooth muscle) usually do not help much.

---

## One-line intuition

Acute bronchiolitis is a viral “clogging and swelling” disease of the smallest airways in infants.

---

## Pearl 🔥

RSV (respiratory syncytial virus) causes bronchiolitis by making infected respiratory epithelial cells fuse into syncytia (multinucleated giant cells formed by cell fusion).
