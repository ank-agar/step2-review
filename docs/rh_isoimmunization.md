Rh isoimmunization means an Rh-negative mother makes antibodies against Rh-positive fetal red blood cells.

Rh = Rhesus factor, a red blood cell surface antigen.

Isoimmunization = making antibodies against blood cell antigens from another person of the same species.

So the classic setup is:

* mother is Rh-negative
* fetus is Rh-positive
* fetal blood enters maternal blood
* mother makes anti-D antibodies

D antigen = the most important Rh antigen.

---

### Core idea

The mother’s immune system sees fetal Rh-positive red blood cells as foreign and makes anti-D IgG antibodies.

IgG (immunoglobulin G) can cross the placenta.

So in a later Rh-positive pregnancy, maternal anti-D IgG crosses into the fetus and destroys fetal red blood cells.

---

### Why first pregnancy is usually okay

The first Rh-positive fetus usually sensitizes the mother during delivery, when fetal and maternal blood mix.

So the sequence is:

* first Rh-positive baby exposes mom to Rh antigen
* mom makes anti-D antibodies after exposure
* next Rh-positive fetus gets attacked by those maternal IgG antibodies

That is why the second Rh-positive pregnancy is classically at higher risk.

---

### What happens to the fetus

Maternal anti-D IgG causes hemolysis, meaning red blood cell destruction.

This causes:

* fetal anemia
* jaundice from increased bilirubin
* erythroblastosis fetalis
* hydrops fetalis in severe cases

Erythroblastosis fetalis = fetal blood has lots of immature red blood cells because the fetus is desperately trying to replace destroyed red blood cells.

Hydrops fetalis = severe fetal edema from high-output heart failure due to profound anemia.

---

### Prevention

Give Rho(D) immune globulin, also called RhoGAM, to an unsensitized Rh-negative mother.

It binds fetal Rh-positive red blood cells before the mother’s immune system can notice them.

So:

> RhoGAM hides the fetal Rh-positive cells from mom’s immune system.

Important: RhoGAM prevents sensitization, but it does not help if the mother is already sensitized.

---

## One-line intuition

Rh isoimmunization is when an Rh-negative mom gets trained to attack Rh-positive fetal red blood cells, causing hemolytic disease in a future Rh-positive fetus.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Rh disease is mediated by IgG, because IgG crosses the placenta; IgM (immunoglobulin M) does not cross the placenta.
