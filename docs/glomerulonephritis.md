## Glomerulonephritis

Glomerulonephritis means inflammation of the glomeruli.

Break the word apart:

* glomerulo- = glomerulus, the kidney's blood-filtering capillary tuft
* nephritis = kidney inflammation

So:

> inflammation of the kidney's filtering units.

---

### Core idea

The glomerulus is supposed to filter blood while keeping big stuff inside the blood, like:

* red blood cells
* protein

In glomerulonephritis, immune/inflammatory damage makes the filter leaky and swollen.

So you get:

* hematuria (blood in urine)
* RBC casts (red blood cell casts)
* some proteinuria (protein in urine)
* decreased GFR (glomerular filtration rate)
* hypertension
* edema

---

### Why the findings happen

* **Blood in urine:** inflamed glomerular capillaries leak red blood cells
* **RBC casts:** red blood cells enter tubules and get molded into casts
* **Low urine output:** swollen/inflamed glomeruli cannot filter well
* **Hypertension/edema:** lower GFR causes salt and water retention
* **Dark/cola-colored urine:** red blood cells break down in urine

---

### Nephritic vs nephrotic intuition

Glomerulonephritis usually gives a nephritic picture.

Nephritic syndrome = inflamed glomerulus.

Think:

* blood
* RBC casts
* hypertension
* decreased GFR

Nephrotic syndrome = very leaky protein filter.

Think:

* massive proteinuria
* hypoalbuminemia (low albumin in blood)
* edema
* hyperlipidemia (high blood lipids)

---

## One-line intuition

Glomerulonephritis is an inflamed kidney filter, so blood leaks through and filtration drops.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis often happens after a throat or skin infection with group A Streptococcus, with low C3 (complement component 3) and granular immune complex deposits.
