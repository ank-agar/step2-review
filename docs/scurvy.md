Scurvy = severe vitamin C deficiency.

Vitamin C is also called ascorbic acid.

Core idea:

* vitamin C is needed to make strong collagen
* collagen is the main structural protein in connective tissue
* without good collagen, blood vessels, gums, skin, bone, and wounds become fragile

---

### Why vitamin C deficiency causes these findings

Vitamin C is a cofactor (helper molecule for an enzyme) for:

* prolyl hydroxylase
* lysyl hydroxylase

These enzymes hydroxylate (add an -OH group to) proline and lysine in collagen.

That hydroxylation helps collagen form a strong stable triple helix.

So:

* low vitamin C
* decreased hydroxylation of proline/lysine
* weak collagen
* weak connective tissue

---

### Classic findings

Weak collagen in capillaries causes bleeding:

* petechiae (tiny red-purple spots from small bleeding)
* purpura (larger purple bleeding spots)
* ecchymoses (bruises)
* perifollicular hemorrhage (bleeding around hair follicles)

Weak collagen in gums causes:

* swollen bleeding gums
* loose teeth

Weak collagen in skin and wounds causes:

* poor wound healing
* reopening of old wounds

Hair finding:

* corkscrew hairs

Bone finding, especially in kids:

* subperiosteal hemorrhage (bleeding under the periosteum, the outer bone covering)
* bone pain

---

## One-line intuition

Scurvy is when lack of vitamin C makes collagen weak, so the body basically loses connective tissue strength and starts bleeding/bruising easily.

---

## HY pearl 🔥

Vitamin C also increases iron absorption by keeping iron in the Fe2+ ferrous form, so scurvy can be associated with anemia (low red blood cells) from both bleeding and decreased iron absorption.
