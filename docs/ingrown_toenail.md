## Ingrown toenail

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/Ingrown_toenail_-_Paronychia.png)

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/03/Ingrown_Toenail%2C_Onychocryptosis%2C_Wedge_Resection_01.JPG)

An ingrown toenail is when the edge/corner of the toenail grows into the nearby skin.

Medical name:

* onychocryptosis

Break it down:

* onycho- = nail
* crypt = hidden/buried
* -osis = condition

So onychocryptosis basically means:

> the nail edge is buried into the skin.

---

### Core idea

The nail edge acts like a tiny sharp wedge digging into the lateral nail fold (skin on the side of the nail).

This causes:

* pain
* redness
* swelling
* tenderness
* sometimes pus/infection

Most commonly:

* big toe
* lateral edge of the nail

---

### Why it happens

Usually from mechanical pressure:

* cutting nails too short or rounded
* tight shoes
* trauma
* curved/thick nail shape

Why rounded trimming matters:

If you cut the corner too far down, the nail grows forward and the corner can spear into the skin instead of gliding over it.

---

### Why it gets inflamed

The body sees the nail edge like a foreign body.

So you get:

* local tissue injury
* inflammation
* swelling
* sometimes bacterial infection

And swelling makes the problem worse because the skin bulges over the nail edge even more.

---

## One-line intuition

An ingrown toenail is the nail corner physically digging into the side skin, causing a painful local inflammatory reaction.

---

## HY pearl

Paronychia (infection/inflammation of the nail fold) can happen from an ingrown toenail, and Staphylococcus aureus is a common bacterial cause.
