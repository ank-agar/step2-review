## Galactorrhea

![Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93prolactin_%28HPP%29_axis_diagram.svg/960px-Hypothalamic%E2%80%93pituitary%E2%80%93prolactin_%28HPP%29_axis_diagram.svg.png)

![Image](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/images/ency/fullsize/17093.jpg)

Galactorrhea means milky nipple discharge when someone is not normally breastfeeding.

Break the word apart:

* galacto- = milk
* -rrhea = flow/discharge

So:

> milk flow from the nipple outside of normal lactation.

---

### Core idea

Galactorrhea usually means prolactin is too high.

Prolactin is the anterior pituitary hormone that tells the breast to make milk.

Anterior pituitary = the front part of the pituitary gland, which releases hormones into blood.

---

### Why prolactin gets high

Normally, dopamine inhibits prolactin.

Dopamine = a hypothalamic neurotransmitter that acts like the brake on prolactin release.

So if dopamine goes down, prolactin goes up.

Common causes:

* prolactinoma, which is a pituitary tumor that secretes prolactin
* antipsychotics, because they block dopamine receptors
* hypothyroidism, because increased TRH (thyrotropin-releasing hormone) can stimulate prolactin
* pregnancy
* nipple/chest wall stimulation
* kidney disease, because prolactin clearance can decrease

---

### Why symptoms happen

High prolactin causes:

* milk production from breast tissue
* decreased GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone)
* decreased LH (luteinizing hormone) and FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
* amenorrhea, infertility, and low libido

Amenorrhea = absence of menstrual periods.

Intuition:

The body thinks it is in a breastfeeding-like state, so it makes milk and suppresses ovulation.

---

### Step 1 distinction

Galactorrhea is usually:

* bilateral
* milky
* sometimes expressible

More concerning nipple discharge is:

* unilateral
* bloody or clear/serous
* spontaneous
* from one duct

That pattern makes you think about intraductal papilloma or malignancy.

---

## One-line intuition

Galactorrhea = prolactin effect on the breast, often because dopamine inhibition of prolactin is lost.

---

## Pearl 🔥

Antipsychotics can cause galactorrhea because dopamine normally inhibits prolactin, so blocking dopamine removes the prolactin brake.
