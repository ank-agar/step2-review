# Orchitis

Orchitis = inflammation of the testis.

Break it down:

* orchis = testis
* -itis = inflammation

So orchitis literally means:

> inflamed testicle.

### Core idea

The testis becomes swollen, painful, and inflamed, usually because of infection.

Most commonly:

* viral infection, especially mumps
* bacterial spread from epididymitis

Epididymitis means inflammation of the epididymis, which is the coiled tube sitting behind the testis that stores and carries sperm.

When both are inflamed, it is called epididymo-orchitis.

### Why it hurts/swells

Inflammation brings:

* vasodilation, meaning blood vessels widen
* increased blood flow
* immune cells
* tissue edema, meaning swelling from extra fluid

The testis is inside the scrotum, so swelling stretches sensitive tissue and causes pain.

### Causes to remember

In kids/young adults:

* mumps virus

In sexually active men:

* Chlamydia trachomatis
* Neisseria gonorrhoeae

In older men:

* urinary tract bacteria, like E. coli

UTI means urinary tract infection.

### Classic presentation

* testicular pain
* scrotal swelling
* tenderness
* fever sometimes
* dysuria sometimes, meaning pain with urination

### Why mumps causes orchitis

Mumps is a paramyxovirus, meaning an enveloped negative-sense RNA (ribonucleic acid) virus.

It classically infects the parotid glands, which are salivary glands near the jaw, causing parotitis (parotid gland inflammation).

But it can also spread to the testes, especially after puberty, causing orchitis.

### Important distinction

Orchitis can look like testicular torsion.

Testicular torsion means the spermatic cord twists and cuts off blood flow to the testis.

That is an emergency.

Ultrasound with Doppler, meaning ultrasound that checks blood flow, helps distinguish them:

* orchitis: increased blood flow from inflammation
* torsion: decreased blood flow from twisting

## One-line intuition

Orchitis is an inflamed testis: infection brings immune cells and extra blood flow, so the testis becomes painful, swollen, and tender.

## Pearl

Mumps orchitis after puberty can cause testicular atrophy, meaning shrinkage of the testis, but sterility is uncommon because it is often unilateral, meaning only one testis is affected.
